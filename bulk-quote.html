Frequently asked questions

How do I get a price for a larger order?

Fill in the form on this page. It opens WhatsApp with your request already written. We reply with the price, the delivery fee and the delivery time for your location.

Who can request a quote?

Anyone buying more than a single item: farms, agrovet shops, hostels, hotels, landlords, schools, offices and households.

How do I pay?

We accept M-Pesa. We confirm the total, including delivery, before you pay. We never ask for your M-Pesa PIN.

Is the quote a promise of a discount?

No. A quote gives you the exact price and delivery fee for what you asked for. Prices and stock are confirmed on WhatsApp.
