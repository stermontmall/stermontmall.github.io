Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ordering, payment, delivery and returns.


How do I order?

Add items to your cart on our website and place your order, or message us on WhatsApp. We confirm stock, the delivery fee and the delivery time, then you pay by M-Pesa.

How do I pay?

We accept M-Pesa. Only pay using the details we give you on our website or in our chat. We never ask for your M-Pesa PIN.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes. We deliver to all 47 counties in Kenya, usually within 1 to 3 days. Remote areas can take longer.

How much is delivery?

The fee depends on your location and the size and weight of your items. We tell you the exact fee before you pay. See shipping and delivery.

Can I collect my order from your shop?

Yes. Our shop is at The Sarit Centre (Westlands), Karuna Road, Nairobi, open 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM every day. Message us first so we can confirm your items are ready.

What if my item is faulty or damaged?

Message us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos. See returns and refunds.

How can I track my order?

Use the track order page, or message us on WhatsApp with your order number.

Are pest control products safe to use?

Always read the label and follow the directions exactly. Keep products away from children, pets and food, and ventilate the room. If you are unsure, ask us before you buy.

Still have a question?

We are happy to help, every day from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
