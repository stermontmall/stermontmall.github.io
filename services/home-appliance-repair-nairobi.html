Signs your appliance needs attention

Act early. A fridge that is warm inside, ices up or runs without stopping, a washing machine that leaks, shakes hard or leaves water in the drum, and a microwave that sparks or stops heating are all signs to stop using the appliance and call a technician. Running a faulty appliance can turn a small repair into a big one.

Strange smells, breakers that trip when the appliance starts and loud grinding noises also deserve a check.

Care tips between repairs

Leave a small gap behind the fridge so air can move, clean the door seals and do not overfill it. Clean the washing machine filter and run an empty hot cycle now and then. Keep microwaves clean inside and never run them empty. A voltage guard helps protect appliances where the power supply is unstable.

Is it worth repairing an old fridge or washing machine?

Often yes, if the fault is a part that is cheap compared with a new appliance. If the repair will cost close to a replacement, we tell you honestly.

Do you use genuine spare parts?

Do you service laundromat machines?

How long does an appliance repair take?

Simple faults can often be fixed during the visit. If a part has to be sourced, we tell you the likely timing in the quote before you decide.

What should I do before the technician arrives?

Switch the appliance off, note what it was doing when it failed and any error code, and clear space around it. A short video sent on WhatsApp helps us prepare.
