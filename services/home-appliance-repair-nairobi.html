When a fridge stops cooling or a washing machine will not drain, you want it fixed correctly, not patched. Our technicians diagnose the fault first, explain what failed and what it costs, and only then repair it. That avoids paying for parts you do not need.

We repair and service washing machines, fridges and freezers, microwaves and ovens for households and for commercial laundromats, using genuine spare parts where available. Regular servicing also extends the life of your appliances and keeps energy use down.

What is included

  • Diagnosis of the fault with a clear explanation
  • Quote before any repair or parts purchase
  • Repair using genuine spare parts where available
  • Testing of the appliance after repair
  • Servicing and cleaning to prevent repeat faults
  • Advice on whether repair or replacement makes more sense

How it works

  1. Message us the appliance brand, model and what it is doing.
  2. A technician visits and diagnoses the fault.
  3. You approve the quote and we repair, using the correct parts.
  4. We test the appliance with you and share care tips.

What decides the price

  • Type and brand of appliance
  • The fault: a clogged drain is cheaper than a compressor or control board
  • Cost and availability of spare parts
  • Commercial machines used all day versus household units

Frequently asked questions

How much does appliance repair cost in Nairobi?

Appliance repair at Stermont Arcade ranges from KSh 2,500 to KSh 12,000. The fault and the spare part decide the final price, and you approve it before work starts.

Is it worth repairing an old fridge or washing machine?

Often yes, if the fault is a part that is cheap compared with a new appliance. If the repair will cost close to a replacement, we tell you honestly.

Do you use genuine spare parts?

We use genuine spare parts where they are available and tell you when only a compatible part can be sourced.

Do you service laundromat machines?
