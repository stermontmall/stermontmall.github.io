Planning a system that works at night

Incidents often happen after dark, so the picture at night matters more than the picture at noon. We test every camera after dark and adjust the angle and the lighting so faces and gates are readable, not just shapes.

We also plan for power and internet. A small backup unit keeps the recorder and router running during outages, and a stable connection keeps phone viewing working.

Privacy and signage

Cameras that record people are covered by Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Put up clear notices that CCTV is in use, point cameras at your own property rather than at neighbours' windows or private areas, and limit who can view or copy the recordings. If your property is large or you record the public, take advice on what the law requires.
