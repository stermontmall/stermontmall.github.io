A camera that records nothing useful is wasted money. Our security installers plan where each camera goes so it covers the gate, the entrances and the blind spots, then set up the recorder and your phone so you can watch live from anywhere with internet.

We install IP camera systems for apartments, estates, shops, schools and warehouses, and we add electric fencing and smart locks where they make sense. Cables are routed neatly and protected from weather, and we explain the system to you before we leave.

What is included

  • Site survey and camera placement plan
  • IP cameras and recorder installation with neat, protected cabling
  • Remote viewing set up on your phone and tablet
  • Motion alerts and recording schedules configured
  • Electric fence and smart lock installation where requested
  • Handover, training and a support contact for questions

How it works

  1. Tell us the property type, the number of entrances and what you want to watch.
  2. We visit or review photos, plan the cameras and send a quote.
  3. We install, test the picture day and night and connect your phone.
  4. You get a short training and our contact for support.

What decides the price

  • Number of cameras and their resolution
  • Storage: how many days of recording you want to keep
  • Cable distance and whether walls or ceilings must be drilled
  • Extras such as electric fencing, smart locks and access control

Who we serve

Estates and apartment blocks, shops and supermarkets, schools, warehouses, offices and homes.

Where we work

Nairobi and nearby towns, with other counties handled by quote.

Frequently asked questions

How much does CCTV installation cost in Nairobi?

Our CCTV and smart security installations range from KSh 8,500 to KSh 85,000. A small home kit sits near the lower end while a warehouse or estate with many cameras sits higher.

Can I watch the cameras on my phone?

Yes. We set up remote viewing so you can see live footage and playback from your phone anywhere you have internet.

Do you install electric fences?

Yes. We install electric fencing for perimeter walls and gates, and we explain safety signage and maintenance before handover.

What happens if the power goes off?

We can add a small backup power unit so the recorder and router stay on during outages. Ask for it in your quote.
