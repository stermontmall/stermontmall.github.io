Signs you need pest control now

Do not wait for a full infestation. Cockroaches seen in daylight usually mean a large hidden population. Small dark spots on mattress seams, rusty stains on sheets and itchy bites in a line point to bed bugs. Gnaw marks, droppings along walls and noises in the ceiling at night point to rodents. Mud tubes on walls and hollow-sounding timber point to termites.

If you manage rental units or a restaurant, one complaint from a tenant or customer is a signal to inspect the whole property, because pests move through shared walls, drains and ceilings. Send us a photo on WhatsApp and we tell you what we think it is before the inspection.

Bed bug treatment: inspection, targeted treatment and follow-up

Bed bugs hide in more places than the mattress. Check seams, bed frames, headboards, bedside furniture, upholstered chairs and cracks near sleeping areas. Small dark spots, shed skins or live insects can be signs, but bites alone do not confirm bed bugs because other causes can look similar.

Before treatment, reduce clutter around the bed and follow the preparation instructions we provide. The treatment plan depends on the level of infestation and the room. Bed bug control may require more than one visit because eggs and hidden insects can be missed during a single treatment. We explain the follow-up plan and what to monitor after the service.

Cockroach control for kitchens, shops and food premises

Cockroaches often gather around water, warmth, food residue and small gaps. Kitchens, cupboards, drains, appliance spaces and waste-storage areas are common inspection points. A useful treatment addresses hiding places and the conditions that allow cockroaches to survive—not just the insects visible on a counter.

Depending on the site, the plan may use targeted baiting, treatment of suitable cracks and crevices, and practical sanitation or exclusion advice. Keep food covered, repair leaks, empty bins regularly and seal gaps where possible. Food businesses should coordinate the visit with their operating schedule and follow all preparation and re-entry instructions.

Termite inspection and treatment for timber and buildings

Termite activity may show as mud tubes, damaged timber, discarded wings or wood that sounds hollow. These signs deserve an inspection because termites can remain hidden inside timber or below ground. Not every damaged board is caused by termites, so diagnosis should come before selecting a treatment.

The treatment method depends on whether activity is in structural timber, furniture, soil or another location, as well as the construction of the building. Ask for the inspection findings, the treatment scope, any access or drilling requirements and the prevention advice in the written quote. Do not cover suspected activity before it has been assessed.

Rodent control: remove access, food and nesting opportunities

Rats and mice can contaminate food, damage stored goods and chew cables. Droppings, gnaw marks, rub marks along walls and noises in ceilings are common clues. Effective control starts with identifying entry routes and food sources as well as the locations where rodents travel.

Depending on the property, the plan may include suitable traps or bait stations, monitoring and exclusion recommendations. Store food in closed containers, manage waste, clear dense clutter and seal openings where practical. Bait stations must be placed and maintained so children, pets and non-target animals cannot access them; the technician will explain site-specific precautions.

Mosquito, flea, ant and other nuisance-pest management

Mosquito problems are often connected to standing water, blocked drainage or vegetation around a property. Fleas may need attention to both the affected room and the animals or areas associated with them. Ants can follow food trails and enter through small gaps, while flies are often linked to waste or other attractants.

Treatment choices depend on the pest and the site. We inspect the conditions, explain which areas need attention and provide practical prevention steps. For mosquitoes, remove standing water where possible; for fleas, follow any pet-care advice from a qualified veterinary professional; and for all treatments, follow the product-specific instructions given by the technician.

What to expect before and after a fumigation visit

Before the visit, tell us the pest, the property type, your location and whether children, pets, food preparation or sensitive equipment are present. If activity continues, report where and when you see it so the team can assess whether a follow-up is needed.

Integrated pest management: fix the cause, not only the visible pests

Long-lasting pest management usually combines inspection, targeted treatment, monitoring and prevention. A technician should look for the conditions that support the infestation: leaking taps, food residue, uncovered waste, standing water, gaps around pipes, damaged door seals, clutter and access points. Treating insects or rodents without addressing those conditions can allow the problem to return.

Where appropriate, the service plan can include practical recommendations for sanitation, waste handling, proofing and monitoring alongside the selected treatment. The measures depend on the pest and the property; no single spray or method is right for every infestation.

Fumigation for hotels, Airbnb apartments and rental properties

Guest accommodation needs careful coordination because pest reports can affect bookings, reviews and room availability. For bed bugs, inspect beds, headboards, bedside furniture, upholstered seating and nearby cracks rather than relying on mattress spraying alone. For cockroaches, include kitchens, food-storage areas, drains and waste points in the inspection.

Hosts and property managers should share room numbers, recent sightings, guest changeover times and any adjoining rooms with suspected activity. The written scope should state which rooms are included, what preparation is required, when occupants may safely return and whether monitoring or a follow-up visit is recommended.

Pest control for restaurants, food stores and commercial kitchens

Food premises need a treatment plan that considers food handling, staff movement, operating hours and sensitive equipment. Inspection should cover food stores, preparation areas, under and behind appliances, waste holding points, delivery entrances and possible pest entry routes. The scope should identify the target pest and the exact areas included.

Before a visit, arrange safe storage or removal of exposed food, utensils and food-contact items as instructed by the service provider. After treatment, follow the product label and technician's site-specific directions for ventilation, cleaning and re-entry. Keep service records and report recurring sightings promptly.

A practical preparation checklist before pest treatment

Before the appointment, tell the team what pest you have seen, where and when it appears, how long the problem has lasted, the property type and whether anyone has allergies, respiratory sensitivities, pets, aquariums or food-preparation areas that need special consideration. Share clear photos of the pest or affected area if you can do so safely.

Wait for the treatment-specific instructions before moving furniture, emptying cupboards, laundering bedding or covering equipment. Preparation differs between gel baiting, crack-and-crevice treatment, bed bug work, rodent monitoring and outdoor mosquito management. Do not mix chemicals, apply household pesticides on top of a professional treatment or re-enter until the technician confirms it is safe.

What a professional pest-control service report should record

For a home, rental property or commercial site, ask for a written record of the inspection and work completed. Useful details include the date, property or rooms inspected, pest evidence observed, treatment areas, product information where applicable, precautions given, monitoring points and any recommended follow-up. A report helps property managers track recurring issues and gives the next technician useful history.

For recurring contracts, agree the visit frequency, reporting format, escalation contact and how urgent sightings are handled before the first scheduled visit. The exact documentation available should be confirmed in the quotation rather than assumed.

Why bed bugs can require follow-up treatment

Bed bugs hide in narrow seams and joints around sleeping areas and can be transported in luggage, clothing and used furniture. A room that looks clear immediately after treatment may still need monitoring. Follow the preparation instructions, avoid moving untreated items into other rooms and tell the technician if neighbouring rooms or units have reported activity.

The number and timing of visits depend on the infestation, the treatment selected and the product instructions. Ask the technician to explain the follow-up plan, what signs to monitor and what to do if you find live insects after the stated monitoring period.

Termite control starts with identifying the source of activity

Termite damage can be mistaken for ordinary wood decay or other insect damage. An inspection should establish where activity is present, whether it is in timber or connected to soil, what parts of the structure may be affected and whether moisture or building conditions are contributing to the problem.

Before approving work, ask for the inspection findings, proposed treatment method, areas included, access requirements, limitations and any warranty terms in writing. Do not rely on a general surface spray as proof that a concealed termite colony or source has been dealt with.

Frequently asked questions

How much does fumigation cost in Nairobi?

Pest control and fumigation at Stermont Arcade ranges from KSh 3,500 to KSh 25,000. A small cockroach or ant treatment sits at the lower end, while whole-property treatments such as termites or large bed bug infestations sit higher. You get the exact price after inspection.

How long before I can go back inside after fumigation?

It depends on the product and the room, so the technician tells you on the day. Usually you stay out for a few hours, air the rooms well and wipe food-contact surfaces before use. Children and pets should be out until the technician says it is safe.

Do you treat bed bugs in one visit?

Some infestations need more than one visit because bed bugs and eggs can remain hidden in seams, bed frames and nearby furniture. The technician should explain the expected treatment plan, monitoring period and whether a follow-up is recommended for your property.

Can you treat an Airbnb between guests?

Yes. Tell us your check-out and check-in times and we plan the treatment so the unit is ready on time. Hosts often keep a regular service for bed bug and cockroach prevention.

Is fumigation safe for children and pets?

Keep children, pets and fish tanks out of the treated area until the technician says it is safe to return. After that, airing the rooms and wiping the surfaces where food is prepared are the usual steps. The technician explains the exact steps for the product used.

Do you serve restaurants and offices on a regular schedule?

Yes. Restaurants, offices and rental blocks can arrange regular visits so problems are caught early. Tell us the size of the premises and how often you want visits and we quote a schedule.

What is the difference between pest control and fumigation?

Pest control is the broader process of inspecting, treating, monitoring and preventing pests. Fumigation is a specific treatment method and is not appropriate for every pest or building. The right approach depends on the pest, site conditions and approved product directions; an inspection should determine the suitable method.

Do I need to leave the house during pest treatment?

That depends on the product and treatment area. Before work begins, ask the technician for the exact evacuation, re-entry and ventilation instructions for the treatment being used. Keep children, pets and anyone not authorised to be present away from the treated area until it is declared safe.

Can I clean immediately after fumigation?

Do not assume that immediate cleaning is suitable. Cleaning too soon can remove a treatment intended to remain in place, while food-contact surfaces may need specific cleaning before use. Follow the written, product-specific instructions given by the technician.

How do I prepare for bed bug treatment?

Preparation varies by method and property. The technician may ask you to reduce clutter, prepare bedding or move selected items, but wait for the exact instructions before doing so. Avoid carrying potentially infested items into other rooms or another property.

Do you provide pest-control services for businesses and institutions?

We accept enquiries for offices, restaurants, hotels, schools, warehouses, rental properties and other commercial premises. Share the site location, property type, pest sightings, approximate size and any tender or reporting requirements so availability and the proposed scope can be confirmed.

What information should I send to get a fumigation quotation?

Send your town or estate, property type, approximate number of rooms or floor area, the pest you have seen, how long it has been present and a photo if available. Mention children, pets, food areas and any time restrictions. We can then advise on the next step and confirm whether an inspection is needed for a firm quote.

Does one fumigation treatment eliminate every pest permanently?

No responsible provider should promise that one treatment permanently prevents every future infestation. Results depend on the pest, its life cycle, the extent of activity, building conditions and follow-up prevention. The service plan should explain expected outcomes, monitoring and any return-visit terms in writing.
