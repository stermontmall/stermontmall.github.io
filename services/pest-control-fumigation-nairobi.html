Pests do not stay small for long. A few cockroaches in a kitchen become an infestation in weeks, bed bugs travel from one bedroom to the next, rats chew through wiring, and termites can weaken roof timber before anyone sees a sign. Our pest control and fumigation service deals with the problem at the source instead of only spraying what you can see.

We work from Sarit Centre in Westlands and serve homes, rental units, offices, restaurants, schools and short-stay apartments across Nairobi and the rest of Kenya. Every job starts with an inspection, so the treatment fits the pest and the building: gel baiting for cockroaches, targeted treatment for bed bugs, soil treatment for termites and bait stations and proofing advice for rodents.

What decides the price

  • Type of pest: a single-room cockroach treatment costs far less than termite soil treatment around a building
  • Size of the property: bedsitter, apartment, house, office floor or restaurant kitchen
  • How bad the infestation is and how many visits it needs
  • Access: gardens, roof spaces and crawl spaces take longer to treat

Signs you need pest control now

Do not wait for a full infestation. Cockroaches seen in daylight usually mean a large hidden population. Small dark spots on mattress seams, rusty stains on sheets and itchy bites in a line point to bed bugs. Gnaw marks, droppings along walls and noises in the ceiling at night point to rodents. Mud tubes on walls and hollow-sounding timber point to termites.

If you manage rental units or a restaurant, one complaint from a tenant or customer is a signal to inspect the whole property, because pests move through shared walls, drains and ceilings. Send us a photo on WhatsApp and we tell you what we think it is before the inspection.

Frequently asked questions

How much does fumigation cost in Nairobi?

Pest control and fumigation at Stermont Arcade ranges from KSh 3,500 to KSh 25,000. A small cockroach or ant treatment sits at the lower end, while whole-property treatments such as termites or large bed bug infestations sit higher. You get the exact price after inspection.

How long before I can go back inside after fumigation?

It depends on the product and the room, so the technician tells you on the day. Usually you stay out for a few hours, air the rooms well and wipe food-contact surfaces before use. Children and pets should be out until the technician says it is safe.

Do you treat bed bugs in one visit?

Light infestations are often cleared in one visit, but bed bugs hide deep in mattresses and furniture and many jobs need a second treatment. That is why we plan a follow-up and back it with the 90-day guarantee.

Can you treat an Airbnb between guests?

Yes. Tell us your check-out and check-in times and we plan the treatment so the unit is ready on time. Hosts often keep a regular service for bed bug and cockroach prevention.

Is fumigation safe for children and pets?

Keep children, pets and fish tanks out of the treated area until the technician says it is safe to return. After that, airing the rooms and wiping the surfaces where food is prepared are the usual steps. The technician explains the exact steps for the product used.

Do you serve restaurants and offices on a regular schedule?

Yes. Restaurants, offices and rental blocks can arrange regular visits so problems are caught early. Tell us the size of the premises and how often you want visits and we quote a schedule.
