## Decisions
- Canonical is `https://stermontarcade.co.ke/services/`, not the home page: a canonical pointing at `/` would tell Google this page is a duplicate and keep it out of results.
- No address, rating or review markup: none exist in the repo, so none was invented. Add a street address to the `LocalBusiness` block if there is a public one (needed for the Local Pack).
- No prices; package tiers are generic names (Standard, Deep or extended, Premium with follow-up).
