A TV mounted a little crooked or on the wrong wall plug is a risk to the screen and to anyone below it. We mount televisions the safe way: we check the wall type, pick the right fixings and bracket for the screen size and weight, and level it with a spirit level.

Beyond mounting, we hide the cables in the wall or in trunking, connect your decoder, soundbar, console and streaming box, and set up surround sound for home cinemas, sports bars and boardrooms.

What is included

  • Wall check for concrete, block or drywall and the right plugs and fixings
  • Bracket selection: fixed, tilting or full motion
  • Levelled and secured mounting at a comfortable viewing height
  • Cable management: in-wall concealment or neat trunking
  • Connection and setup of decoder, soundbar, console or streaming devices
  • Basic picture and sound settings

How it works

  1. Send the TV size, the wall type and a photo of the wall.
  2. We confirm the price and book a time.
  3. We mount, level, hide the cables and connect your devices.
  4. We test sound and picture with you before we leave.

What decides the price

  • Screen size and weight
  • Bracket type: fixed, tilting or full motion
  • Concealed cabling inside the wall versus surface trunking
  • Extras such as soundbars, surround systems and projector setup

Who we serve

Homeowners and tenants, sports bars and restaurants, offices and boardrooms, hotels and Airbnb hosts.

Where we work

Nairobi and nearby towns.

Frequently asked questions

How much does TV mounting cost in Nairobi?

TV mounting and audio visual setup at Stermont Arcade ranges from KSh 1,500 to KSh 7,500. A standard TV on a solid wall is at the lower end, while large screens, hidden cables and sound systems cost more.

Do you supply the wall bracket?

We can supply a bracket that matches your TV or fit the one you already have, as long as it suits the screen size and weight. Tell us the model when you book.

Can you hide the cables in the wall?

Yes. Where the wall allows, we run cables inside it. Otherwise we use neat trunking painted to match.

Can you mount a TV on a drywall partition?

Yes, with the right fixings and a bracket rated for the weight. We check the partition on site before mounting.
