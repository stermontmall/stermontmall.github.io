Bed bug fumigation in Nairobi: inspection before treatment

Bed bugs hide close to where people rest. They may shelter in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, bedside furniture, skirting gaps, upholstery and cracks around sleeping areas. A reliable service begins by confirming the pest and mapping the affected rooms rather than applying a blanket spray without inspection.

Stermont Arcade handles enquiries from homeowners, tenants, landlords, property managers, hostels, hotels and short-stay operators. During the assessment, the technician checks likely harbourages, the number of sleeping spaces, adjoining rooms and signs of movement. This helps determine the treatment scope and whether neighbouring units need inspection.

How a professional bed bug treatment plan works

After inspection, the technician explains the proposed method, preparation requirements, rooms included, expected downtime and whether a follow-up visit is recommended. Treatment may combine carefully targeted residual applications, physical measures and monitoring, selected according to the site and approved product labels. No method should be described as universally effective for every infestation.

A written plan should identify the rooms treated, any items that need special handling, re-entry instructions and what to do if activity continues. Occupants should never re-enter until the applicator's stated safety interval has passed.

Preparing homes, hostels and hotels

Before the visit, reduce clutter without moving untreated belongings into clean rooms. Bag bedding and washable textiles for laundering as directed, empty accessible drawers if requested, and make bed frames and skirting areas reachable. Do not apply additional household pesticides before the inspection unless the technician specifically advises it; uncoordinated sprays can scatter insects or complicate treatment.

For hotels and hostels, record room numbers, occupancy and recent complaints. Avoid moving guests or furniture from an affected room into an untreated room without a controlled plan. A room-by-room record helps maintenance teams monitor activity and schedule any necessary follow-up.

What affects cost and how to prevent recurrence

The quotation depends on the number and size of rooms, level of infestation, furniture complexity, access, preparation and repeat visits. A bedsitter is not comparable with a multi-room hostel block or hotel floor. Stermont Arcade confirms the scope and final quotation after learning about the premises and carrying out an inspection where required.

After treatment, use mattress and pillow encasements where appropriate, inspect second-hand furniture before bringing it indoors, check luggage after travel and respond early to new signs. Prevention reduces risk but cannot guarantee that bed bugs will never be introduced again.

Frequently asked questions

How much does bed bug fumigation cost in Nairobi?

The cost depends on room count, infestation severity, furniture, access and whether repeat visits are needed. Share the property type and location for an inspection and a written quotation; avoid relying on a flat price before the scope is known.

Can bed bugs be eliminated in one visit?

Some light infestations may respond quickly, but eggs and hidden harbourages can make follow-up treatment or monitoring necessary. The technician should explain the expected treatment sequence and revisit criteria.

Should I throw away my mattress?

Usually not. An inspection can determine whether the mattress can be treated or protected with an appropriate encasement. Discarding furniture without controlling the infestation can spread bed bugs through the building.

How should I prepare a room?

Follow the technician's written preparation instructions. Common steps include reducing clutter, bagging washable textiles for laundering, making bed frames accessible and keeping children, pets and occupants away during application and until the stated re-entry time.

Do you serve apartments, hotels and hostels?

Yes. We take enquiries for homes, apartments, hostels, hotels, furnished rentals and other premises in Nairobi, and can discuss requests from other Kenyan towns subject to scheduling.
