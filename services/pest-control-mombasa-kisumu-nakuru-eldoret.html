Stermont Arcade supports commercial buyers, agrovets, farms, facility managers, hospitality operators and pest-control teams sourcing pest-management products and application equipment. Enquiries are welcome from Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret and other county towns, including bulk and repeat procurement requests.

Delivery speed varies by destination, courier availability and product handling restrictions; we do not promise an unverified delivery time.

Bulk chemicals and pest-management products

For commercial orders, specify the active ingredient or exact product name, formulation, pack size, quantity and intended site. Product labels and applicable Kenyan registration requirements determine permitted uses, target pests, rates, protective equipment and storage. We do not substitute a different formulation or pack size without your agreement.

Some products may have transport or handling limitations. Share the delivery county and town before checkout so the team can confirm whether the requested item can be shipped to that destination and what arrangements apply.

Knapsack sprayers and ULV foggers

Choose sprayers and fogging equipment by application method, tank capacity, power source, nozzle or droplet requirements, duty cycle, spares availability and operator training. A knapsack sprayer for targeted residual application is not interchangeable with a ULV fogger intended for controlled aerosol applications. Check the equipment manual and product label before selecting a machine or applying any chemical.

For larger procurement, provide the number of units, required accessories, operating environment and whether you need manual, battery or engine-powered equipment. We can confirm available models and current pricing before you place a bulk order.
