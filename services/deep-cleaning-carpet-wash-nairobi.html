A normal clean keeps a home tidy. A deep clean resets it. We scrub what daily cleaning misses: grease in the kitchen, limescale in bathrooms, dust in corners and vents, and dirt trapped deep in carpets, sofas and mattresses.

Our team uses steam vacuuming, extraction machines and floor scrubbers, and we handle move-in and move-out cleans, post-construction cleaning and regular deep cleans for homes, offices and rental units.

What is included

  • Deep steam vacuuming of carpets and rugs
  • Sofa and upholstery extraction cleaning
  • Mattress sanitation to lift dust, stains and odour
  • Kitchen and bathroom descaling and degreasing
  • Floor scrubbing and polishing for tiles and hard floors
  • Post-construction cleaning: paint splashes, cement dust and debris

Move-out cleaning and your deposit

Landlords usually inspect a house before returning a deposit, so a thorough move-out clean helps you avoid disputes. We scrub kitchens, bathrooms, floors and the surfaces where dirt shows in the final check. Tell us which areas your landlord cares about most.

Airbnb, office and post-construction jobs

Short-stay hosts need fast, repeatable cleans between guests, and offices need floors and carpets refreshed with little disruption. We plan the team size around your deadline.

After building or renovation work, we remove cement dust, paint splashes and debris that ordinary cleaning cannot lift. Tell us the size of the space and how dusty it is, and send photos if you can, so our quote fits the job.

How to prepare for the team

Move small valuables and fragile items to a safe place, clear the surfaces you want cleaned and tell the team about delicate fabrics or floors. If you want certain areas done first, say so at the start.

Cleaning contracts for offices, schools, hospitals, estates and tenders

Facility managers need a cleaning partner who works to a written scope: which areas, how often, which tasks, and who supervises. We quote per area or per visit with the scope, frequency, equipment and supplies set out clearly, and we agree how completed work is reported. That makes it easy to compare bids and to hold the work to a standard.

Tell us which compliance documents your tender requires and we will confirm what we can supply before you commit. Typical clients include offices, schools, clinics, apartment blocks, hotels, short-stay rentals and property managers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does deep cleaning cost in Nairobi?

Deep cleaning and carpet washing at Stermont Arcade ranges from KSh 4,000 to KSh 20,000. A single sofa set or a small apartment is near the lower end while a large house or post-construction job costs more.

How long does a carpet take to dry?

It depends on the carpet and the weather, but extraction cleaning usually leaves carpets damp for several hours. Open windows and keep foot traffic off until dry.

Can you remove stains from mattresses and sofas?

We can lift most fresh and many old stains, but results depend on the fabric and the stain. We tell you what to expect before we start.

Do you clean after construction?

Yes. We remove cement dust, paint splashes and debris so the property is ready to move in.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes. The team uses steam vacuuming, extraction machines and floor scrubbers. If you want a product avoided because of allergies or a sensitive surface, tell us when you book.

Can you add a sofa and mattress to a house clean?

Yes. Sofa extraction and mattress sanitation can be added to a house clean. Tell us how many items so the quote is accurate.
