Problems that should not wait

Call a technician quickly for burning smells at sockets or the distribution board, sparks, warm or discoloured plugs, shocks from taps or appliances, and water leaking near wiring. Switch off the affected circuit if it is safe to do so and stay clear until it is checked.

For plumbing, a burst pipe, a leak that keeps getting worse or water coming through a ceiling needs the main valve closed first, then a call.

Planned maintenance for landlords and managers

Routine checks on taps, toilets, water heaters, breakers and sockets catch small faults before they become emergencies, and tenants notice the difference. If you manage several units, tell us how many and we plan visits instead of repeated call-outs.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an electrician or plumber cost in Nairobi?

Electrical and plumbing maintenance at Stermont Arcade ranges from KSh 2,000 to KSh 15,000 depending on the job and the materials.

Can you install an instant water heater?

Yes. We install instant water heaters with the correct cable, breaker and earthing, and test the unit before handover.

Why does my circuit breaker keep tripping?

It may be an overloaded circuit, a faulty appliance or damaged wiring. Do not keep resetting it. Call us and we trace the cause.

Can you find a hidden leak?

Yes. We use inspection and testing methods to locate leaks in walls and floors, then repair them with as little damage as possible.

Do you supply the materials?

Materials are quoted with the job. For water heaters we do both supply-and-fit and fit-only, and we confirm which in your quote.

Can you come after hours or in an emergency?

Message us on WhatsApp with the problem, your location and a photo. We tell you the fastest visit available and whether an after-hours charge applies.
