Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you serve?

We take bookings in Nairobi and Kitale and arrange visits in other Kenyan towns on request. Tell us your town in the booking form and we confirm availability.

How do I book and pay?

Choose a service, fill in the booking form and send it on WhatsApp. We confirm the date and give you a quote, and you can pay with M-Pesa.

Do I need to leave my home during pest control treatment?

Gel baiting for cockroaches usually does not need evacuation. Other treatments may need you to stay out for a few hours, and we tell you before the visit.
