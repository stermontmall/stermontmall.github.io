How Much Does Pest Control Cost in Nairobi? A 2026 Price Guide

Quick answer: Pest control in Nairobi typically costs between KSh 3,500 and KSh 25,000. Small cockroach or ant jobs are at the low end, while termites and large bed bug infestations cost more. The pest, the size of the property and the number of visits decide the price.

What decides the price

The first factor is the pest. Cockroaches and ants in one kitchen are quick to treat with gel baits. Bed bugs need treatment of mattresses, bed frames and cracks. Termites need soil treatment around the building. Rodents need bait stations and a check of how they get in.

The second factor is size: a bedsitter, a three-bedroom house and a restaurant kitchen are very different jobs. The third is the number of visits, because some pests need a follow-up treatment to break the breeding cycle.

How to prepare for fumigation

Clear the areas to be treated, cover or store food, and remove pets, fish tanks and children from the property for the time the technician advises. Wash bedding at a high temperature before bed bug treatment.

After the treatment, air the rooms and wipe food-contact surfaces. Do not mop treated floors until the technician says so, because that can remove the product.

Stopping pests from coming back

Seal gaps around pipes and doors, fix leaks, keep food in closed containers and take out rubbish daily. Rental hosts should inspect mattresses and furniture between guests. A regular service for restaurants and Airbnbs costs less than repeated emergency treatments.

Questions to ask before you book

Ask what pest the technician expects, what the price includes, how many visits are planned and what happens if the pest returns. Clear answers on all four show that the provider has looked at your problem instead of quoting a flat number.

Ask also how long you must stay out, what to cover or remove, and whether follow-up is included. Get the answers in writing, even if it is only a WhatsApp message.

Which pests are most common in Nairobi homes

Cockroaches thrive in kitchens, drains and warm bathrooms. Bed bugs spread through luggage, second-hand furniture and shared bedding, which is why short-stay units are often affected. Rodents enter through gaps around pipes and roofs. Termites live in soil and attack timber and sometimes wall plaster. Ants, fleas and flies are usually linked to food waste, pets and drains.

What a good pest control quotation should include

Ask for the pests to be treated, the method (gel, spray, heat, fogging or bait stations), the number of visits, what follow-up is included and what the guarantee covers. A low price that covers one spray and no follow-up often costs more later. Ask whether furniture must be moved, how long you must stay out and when children and pets can return.

Annual pest control contracts and tenders

Offices, schools, hotels, clinics and estates usually save money and risk with a planned schedule instead of emergency call-outs. A tender-ready quotation lists the scope per building, the visit frequency, the treatment methods, the response time for call-outs and the reporting you receive after each visit. Questions people ask

Is a pre-inspection really needed?

Yes. It lets the technician identify the pest and plan the right treatment, so you pay for what works.

What does the 90-day guarantee cover?

If the same pest returns in the treated area within 90 days, we treat it again. Terms are confirmed in your quote.

Should I treat when I only see one cockroach?

A single cockroach seen at night may be a visitor, but one seen in daylight, or several in a week, usually means a nest nearby. A quick inspection tells you which it is.

How long before the pests are gone after treatment?

Some pests die within hours, while others need days as baits work through the colony or a second visit finishes the job. Your technician explains what to expect for your pest.
