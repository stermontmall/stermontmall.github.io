Deep Cleaning Checklist for Nairobi Homes and Move-Out Cleans

Quick answer: Professional deep cleaning in Nairobi costs about KSh 4,000 to KSh 20,000 depending on size and dirt level. A proper deep clean covers kitchen grease, bathroom limescale, floors, carpets, sofas and mattresses.

Kitchen and bathrooms

Degrease the cooker hood, oven, tiles and cupboards. In bathrooms, remove limescale from taps, showers and tiles and disinfect toilets and drains.

Floors, carpets and upholstery

Scrub tile grout, vacuum and extract carpets and rugs, and clean sofas with extraction machines to lift dirt from deep in the fabric. Mattresses benefit from sanitation to reduce dust and odour.

Move-in, move-out and post-construction

Move-out cleans help tenants get deposits back. Move-in cleans give you a fresh start. After construction, cement dust and paint splashes need special removal before the home is safe to live in.

Questions people ask

How often should I deep clean?

Most homes benefit from a deep clean once or twice a year, and before and after moving.

Do I need to be at home?

Not necessarily, but it helps to be available at the start and the end to point out priorities and check the result.
