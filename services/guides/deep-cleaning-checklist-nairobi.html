Kitchen and bathrooms

Degrease the cooker hood, oven, tiles and cupboards. In bathrooms, remove limescale from taps, showers and tiles and disinfect toilets and drains.

Floors, carpets and upholstery

Scrub tile grout, vacuum and extract carpets and rugs, and clean sofas with extraction machines to lift dirt from deep in the fabric. Mattresses benefit from sanitation to reduce dust and odour.

Move-in, move-out and post-construction

Move-out cleans help tenants get deposits back. Move-in cleans give you a fresh start. After construction, cement dust and paint splashes need special removal before the home is safe to live in.

Bedrooms and living areas

Dust ceiling corners, fans and vents, wipe skirting boards and doors, vacuum under beds and sofas, and wash curtains if they are washable. Vacuum mattresses and air the room well.

What to do before the cleaners arrive

Pack away valuables and small items, empty the fridge if the clean includes it, and tell the team where the water and power are. Point out stains and problem areas at the start so they get attention.

When to book a professional

Book professional cleaning when you move in or out, after renovation, before hosting guests, or when carpets and sofas look dull despite regular cleaning. Machines lift dirt that a brush and a bucket cannot reach.

Kitchen and bathroom checklist

In the kitchen, clean inside cabinets and drawers, degrease the oven, hob and extractor, wipe the fridge inside and behind, and descale taps and the sink. In bathrooms, scrub grout, descale shower screens and taps, and sanitise toilets, basins and floor drains.

Floors, windows and soft furnishings

Vacuum and mop every floor, polish hard floors where needed, wash windows and tracks, and wipe doors, switches and skirting. Extract sofas, carpets and mattresses where they are dirty or smell, and allow time for them to dry.

Questions people ask

How often should I deep clean?

Most homes benefit from a deep clean once or twice a year, and before and after moving.

Do I need to be at home?

Not necessarily, but it helps to be available at the start and the end to point out priorities and check the result.

Is the price per room or per house?

It depends on the job. We give a range first and confirm after we know the size, the dirt level and the items to be cleaned.

Can you clean while we are still living in the house?

Yes. Tell us which rooms are in use and we plan the order of work.
