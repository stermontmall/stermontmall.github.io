Farm Spraying in Kenya: Safe Application, Timing and Cost

Quick answer: Farm spraying and crop advice in Kenya costs about KSh 4,500 to KSh 35,000 depending on farm size and crop. Safe spraying means diagnosing first, following the label dose and respecting the waiting period before harvest.

Diagnose before you spray

Yellow leaves can come from disease, insects, nutrient shortage or too much water. Spraying a fungicide on an insect problem wastes money. A farm visit or clear photos help identify the cause.

Follow the label

The label gives the dose, the crops the product is meant for, the mixing method and the days to wait before harvest. Using more does not work better and can burn the crop or leave residues. Wear gloves, a mask and long clothing when mixing and spraying.

Time your spray

Spray early in the morning or late afternoon when wind is light and it is cooler. Avoid spraying before rain, which washes the product off. Rotate products with different modes of action to slow resistance.

Questions people ask

Can I mix two chemicals in one tank?

Only if the labels allow it. Some mixtures damage crops or reduce effect, so ask us before mixing.
