CCTV Installation in Nairobi: Costs, Camera Types and Placement

Quick answer: CCTV installation in Nairobi usually costs between KSh 8,500 and KSh 85,000. The number of cameras, storage and cable distance set the price, and extras such as electric fencing or smart locks add to it.

Choosing the cameras

IP cameras send digital video over a network and are easy to view on a phone. Choose higher resolution for gates and number plates, and wider lenses for yards and corridors. Cameras with night vision matter because most incidents happen after dark.

Where to place them

Cover entrances, gates, parking, stores and blind corners. Mount cameras high enough to be out of easy reach and angled to avoid direct sunlight, which washes out the picture.

Storage and remote viewing

The recorder decides how many days of footage you keep. More cameras and higher resolution need bigger storage. Remote viewing needs a stable internet connection at the site.

Adding fences and smart locks

Electric fencing deters entry along walls and gates, and smart locks remove the risk of lost keys. They work best together with cameras that record what happens at the entry points.

What affects the price

Camera count, resolution, recorder size, cable length, the type of building and extras such as an electric fence, a smart lock or backup power set the cost. A home with four cameras on a short cable run sits at the lower end of our range. A school, estate or warehouse with many cameras, long cable runs and large storage sits at the upper end.

After installation: keeping the system healthy

Clean the camera lenses, keep the recorder in a ventilated place and check from time to time that it is recording. Change default passwords and keep the phone app updated. If a camera goes dark or the picture turns grainy, message us so we can check the cable, the power supply and the settings.

What changes the price of a CCTV system

The number of cameras, the resolution, the night-vision range, whether the system is IP or analogue, the hard drive size, the cable length and the difficulty of the building all move the price. Ask how many days of footage the hard drive will hold, whether remote viewing is set up on your phone and what happens if a camera fails.

Questions people ask

How many cameras does a house need?

Many houses are covered with four, one for each entrance and approach, but the right number depends on your layout.

Is CCTV footage admissible if something happens?

Clear footage with correct date and time helps police investigations. We set the recorder clock correctly during installation.

Can I add cameras later?

Often yes, if the recorder has free channels and storage. Tell us your plans so we choose a recorder with room to grow.

Is a wireless camera better than a wired one?

Wired cameras are usually steadier because they do not depend on Wi-Fi strength. Wireless cameras suit places where cabling is hard. We advise after seeing the building.
