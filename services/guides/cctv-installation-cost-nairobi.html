CCTV Installation in Nairobi: Costs, Camera Types and Placement

Quick answer: CCTV installation in Nairobi usually costs between KSh 8,500 and KSh 85,000. The number of cameras, storage and cable distance set the price, and extras such as electric fencing or smart locks add to it.

Choosing the cameras

IP cameras send digital video over a network and are easy to view on a phone. Choose higher resolution for gates and number plates, and wider lenses for yards and corridors. Cameras with night vision matter because most incidents happen after dark.

Where to place them

Cover entrances, gates, parking, stores and blind corners. Mount cameras high enough to be out of easy reach and angled to avoid direct sunlight, which washes out the picture.

Storage and remote viewing

The recorder decides how many days of footage you keep. More cameras and higher resolution need bigger storage. Remote viewing needs a stable internet connection at the site.

Adding fences and smart locks

Electric fencing deters entry along walls and gates, and smart locks remove the risk of lost keys. They work best together with cameras that record what happens at the entry points.

Questions people ask

How many cameras does a house need?

Many houses are covered with four, one for each entrance and approach, but the right number depends on your layout.

Is CCTV footage admissible if something happens?

Clear footage with correct date and time helps police investigations. We set the recorder clock correctly during installation.
