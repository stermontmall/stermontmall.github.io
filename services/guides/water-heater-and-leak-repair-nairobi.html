Leaks

A small drip can waste thousands of litres and damage walls. A rising water bill without more usage can point to a hidden leak. Turn off the water at the main and call a plumber instead of waiting for damage.

Tripping breakers

Breakers trip to protect you. Repeated tripping signals an overload, a faulty appliance or damaged wiring. Unplug appliances, reset once and call an electrician if it trips again.

Instant water heaters

Instant heaters draw heavy current and need a suitable cable, a dedicated breaker and proper earthing. A poor installation can overheat wires. Always have the work tested before use.

Signs of a hidden leak

A rising water bill with no change in use, damp patches or peeling paint, the sound of running water when all taps are closed, and a water meter that keeps moving are all signs. Close all taps, note the meter reading, wait a short time and check again. If it moved, there is a leak.

Choosing and installing a water heater

Choose a heater whose power rating your wiring can carry. Instant heaters need a dedicated breaker, a cable of the right size and earthing, and the water connections must be sound. Ask the technician to explain the settings and to show you where the isolating switch is.

Basic safety at home

Know where your main water valve and your main breaker are. Never touch wet sockets or switches, and keep electrical items away from water. If a socket or switch smells of burning, switch off at the main and call an electrician.

Signs of a failing water heater

No hot water, a breaker that trips when the heater switches on, water pooling around the tank, strange noises or rust-coloured water are all signs to call a technician. Switch the power off at the breaker first, and never open the unit while it is live.

How to spot a hidden leak

Signs include a rising water bill, damp patches, mould, peeling paint and the sound of running water when every tap is off. To test, close all taps and check whether the water meter still moves. If it does, there is a leak somewhere.

Questions people ask

Can I install a water heater myself?

We advise against it. Wrong cable size or missing earthing creates a fire and shock risk, so use a qualified technician.

Do you handle emergency calls?

Message us on WhatsApp with the problem and location and we advise on the fastest visit available.

How do I know which breaker is for my heater?

The breaker should be labelled. If it is not, ask the technician to label the board when the job is finished.

Should I fix a dripping tap myself?

Changing a tap washer is a simple fix for some people, but a worn tap body or pipe fitting is better left to a plumber to avoid leaks behind the wall.
