Pest-control inspection and treatment enquiries

For bed bugs, cockroaches, rodents or termites, describe the pest, the type of building and the affected rooms or areas. For a rental property, hotel, restaurant, office or warehouse, include the approximate size and whether the premises will be occupied during treatment. Photographs of visible signs can help with an initial discussion, but they do not replace an on-site inspection where one is needed.

A technician should identify the pest and assess the extent of activity before recommending a treatment. The quotation should explain the proposed scope, preparation steps, safety instructions, re-entry guidance and whether follow-up visits may be needed. Do not assume that every pest problem can be solved with one general spray or that one treatment method suits every building.

For termite enquiries, mention mud tubes, damaged timber, discarded wings or the areas where activity has been noticed. For bed bugs, identify affected bedrooms and recent movement of furniture or luggage. For rodents, describe droppings, gnawing, noises and likely entry points. These details help the team plan a useful inspection.
