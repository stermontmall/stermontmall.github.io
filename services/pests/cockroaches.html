Cockroaches are among the most common pests in Kenyan kitchens, restaurants, flats and hostels. They hide in dark, warm gaps during the day and come out at night to feed on crumbs, grease, pet food and rubbish. Seeing one in daylight often means the hiding places are already crowded.

Not every cockroach is the same, and the treatment that works for one species can be wasted on another. A small German cockroach lives indoors near food and water. A large American or smokybrown cockroach often comes up from drains and gutters. Finding out which one you have decides where to treat.

Types of cockroaches found in Kenya

German cockroach

About 13 mm long, light brown with two dark stripes behind the head. It lives indoors in kitchens, bathrooms and food areas, close to food and water, and breeds fast.

American cockroach

A large reddish-brown cockroach, about 40 to 50 mm. It likes warm, damp places such as drains, sewers and boiler rooms, and can enter ground floors through damaged pipes and gaps.

Oriental cockroach

A dark brown to almost black cockroach, about 25 to 30 mm. It prefers cool, damp places such as drains, garages and basements and cannot climb smooth vertical surfaces well.

Brown-banded cockroach

A small cockroach with light bands across the wings. It is often found away from kitchens, behind pictures, inside appliances, in furniture and in clutter.

Smokybrown cockroach

A dark brown to mahogany cockroach about 30 to 35 mm long that lives mostly outdoors but flies and can enter upper floors and roofs on warm, humid evenings.

Signs of cockroaches

  • Live cockroaches at night, or in the day when the hiding places are full
  • Small dark droppings like pepper or coffee grounds along cupboard edges, hinges and appliance backs
  • Egg cases (oothecae) in drawers, behind appliances and under sinks
  • An oily, musty smell in a room with a big infestation
  • Chew marks on food packaging, soap and cardboard
  • Cockroaches coming up out of floor drains, sinks and bathroom outlets at night

Health risks and why it matters

Cockroaches can contaminate food and surfaces with bacteria they pick up in drains and waste. Their droppings, shed skins and saliva contain allergens that are linked to asthma, especially in children. In a restaurant, hotel or school kitchen, a cockroach sighting can also lead to a failed inspection, bad reviews and lost customers.

What attracts cockroaches

  • Food crumbs, grease and spills left overnight, and uncovered rubbish
  • Dripping taps, wet sink areas and blocked or unsealed floor drains
  • Cardboard boxes, paper bags and stacked clutter, which give them hiding places
  • Gaps around pipes, door frames and appliances that let them move between rooms and flats
  • Cockroaches arriving on deliveries, in boxes and in second-hand appliances

How to prevent cockroaches

  • Wipe surfaces and sweep floors every night, and do not leave dirty dishes overnight
  • Store food, pet food and dry goods in sealed containers
  • Use covered bins and take rubbish out daily
  • Fix leaks and dripping taps, and keep sink areas dry at night
  • Seal gaps around pipes, skirting and appliances, and fit mesh over floor drains
  • Throw away cardboard boxes quickly, since they hide eggs and insects

How we deal with cockroaches

Gel baiting is the main method for indoor cockroaches. We place small amounts of bait in the cracks, hinges and appliance gaps where cockroaches hide, away from food surfaces. Baits work over days, not minutes, because cockroaches carry the bait back to others, so expect activity to drop over a week or more and plan a follow-up check.

Sprays and foggers on their own usually do not clear an infestation, because cockroaches retreat into cracks. For outdoor species such as American and smokybrown cockroaches, we also look at drains, gutters, manholes and the outside walls. Frequently asked questions about cockroaches

Can I keep using my kitchen during treatment?

Gel baiting is placed in cracks and hiding places away from food surfaces, so kitchens can often stay in use. The technician explains what to cover and clean before and after the visit.

Why am I seeing more cockroaches after treatment?

Bait draws cockroaches out of hiding as it takes effect, so you may see more for a few days. Activity should then fall. If it does not, tell us and we check the hiding places again.

Are cockroaches a sign of a dirty house?

No. They are drawn by warmth, water and food, and they travel between flats, shops and drains. Clean homes still get them, although good hygiene makes them easier to control.

How often should a restaurant be treated?

It depends on the size of the kitchen and the risk. We recommend a schedule after inspecting the premises, and we give a dated service report for inspections.
