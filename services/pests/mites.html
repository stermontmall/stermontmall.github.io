Mites are tiny relatives of spiders. Some live on people, some on birds and rodents, some in house dust, and some on crops. Because they are so small, the first sign is often an unexplained itch or a bite rash, or a poultry house with birds in poor condition.

Treatment depends on the mite. Scabies needs medical treatment for the person and everyone in close contact. House dust mites are managed by washing and cleaning. Bird and rodent mites bite people after their normal host has gone, so the nest or the host has to be found. Poultry red mites and crop mites are farm problems.

Types of mites found in Kenya

Scabies mite (Sarcoptes scabiei)

Burrows into human skin and causes intense itching, worse at night, often between the fingers, on the wrists and around the waist. It spreads by close skin contact and needs medical treatment.

House dust mites

Microscopic mites that feed on skin flakes in mattresses, pillows, carpets and upholstered furniture. Their droppings and body parts can trigger allergies and asthma.

Bird and rodent mites

Mites from nests in roofs, eaves, air conditioners and ceilings. They bite people when the birds or rodents leave or die.

Poultry red mite (Dermanyssus gallinae)

A blood-feeding mite that hides in cracks in poultry houses by day and feeds on birds at night. Heavy infestations cause anaemia and reduced egg laying.

Spider mites and other crop mites

Tiny mites on the underside of leaves that cause speckling, bronzing and webbing, and reduce yields in vegetables, flowers and fruit crops.

Signs of mites

  • Intense itching that is worse at night, with a rash between the fingers or on the wrists, which can point to scabies
  • Several people in a household or institution itching at the same time
  • Itchy bites in a room after a bird nest or rodent nest was removed or abandoned
  • Sneezing, wheezing or a blocked nose that is worse in bed or when dusting, which can point to dust mites
  • Poultry that are restless, pale and laying fewer eggs, with tiny red or grey specks in house cracks
  • Speckled, yellowing leaves and fine webbing on crops

Why it matters

Scabies spreads easily in crowded settings and needs prompt medical treatment, together with laundering of clothes and bedding. Dust mites worsen asthma and allergies. In poultry houses, mites reduce production and can cause serious losses. On crops, mites weaken plants and cut yields.

What attracts mites

  • Close and prolonged skin contact with someone who has scabies
  • Rarely washed bedding, pillows and upholstered furniture
  • Bird nests and rodent nests in roofs, eaves and ceilings
  • Cracks and crevices in poultry houses and dirty bedding
  • Hot, dry conditions on crops and plants under stress

How to prevent mites

  • Wash bedding hot every week and dry it in strong sun or on high heat
  • Use mattress and pillow covers and vacuum mattresses and sofas
  • Remove old bird and rodent nests, wearing a mask and gloves, and seal entry points
  • Keep poultry houses clean and dry and fill cracks where mites hide
  • Scout crops weekly and look under leaves
  • Do not share towels or clothes with someone who has scabies

Frequently asked questions about mites

How do I know if it is scabies?

Intense itching that is worse at night, a rash between fingers and on the wrists, and others in the household itching at the same time suggest scabies. See a health worker for a diagnosis and treatment.

Can you treat dust mites?

Dust mites are managed mostly by washing bedding hot, using covers and vacuuming. We can advise and treat upholstery where suitable.

Why am I being bitten after we removed a bird nest?

The mites in the nest look for a new host when the birds are gone. Clean the area, seal entries and we can treat the space.

Can you treat poultry red mites?

Yes, through our agrovet service. Send the size of the house and the number of birds on WhatsApp.
