Pest control works best when you know the pest. Bed bugs, cockroaches, termites and rats each live, breed and spread in their own way, so the right treatment, the right products and the right follow-up differ. These pages explain what we look for and what you can do yourself, in plain language.

Household crawling insects

Bed bugsSmall, flat, night-feeding insects that live in beds, sofas and cracks. Find them early, treat the hiding places, and stop them travelling in your luggage.CockroachesFive cockroach species behave in five different ways. We identify yours, bait the places they hide, and fix what lets them back in.AntsSmall kitchen ants, wood-nesting carpenter ants and swarming safari ants need different treatment. We identify them first.Centipedes and millipedesThey come in when it is wet. Dry out the damp spots, seal the gaps and treat the edges of the house.

Wood and structural pests

TermitesTermites eat timber, paper and plants from the inside. We find where they come from, treat the soil and the wood, and show you how to keep them out.

Rodents and wildlife

Rats and miceRodents chew wiring, spoil food and carry disease. We find the entry points, set safe bait stations and help you close the gaps.SnakesWe do not catch or kill snakes. We help you remove what attracts them, and we explain what to do if one is in your compound or someone is bitten.Bats and birdsWe close the entry points and protect the building. We do not poison or harm bats or birds.

Biting and blood-feeding pests

Fleas and jiggersFleas live in carpets, bedding and soil, not only on pets. Treat the animal, the home and the floor together, or they return.TicksTicks feed on blood and spread diseases to animals and sometimes to people. Treat the animal, the kennel and the surroundings.MosquitoesKilling adult mosquitoes helps for a few days. Removing where they breed is what keeps numbers down.LicePeople are treated with medicated lotions and combing; we treat the dormitory, bedding and shared furniture so lice are not passed back.MitesMites are too small to see easily. The itch and the source tell you which kind you have and who should treat it.

Flying insects

Houseflies and other fliesFlies breed in waste, manure and drains. Find that source first, or no spray will keep them away for long.Bees, wasps and hornetsStay away from the nest, keep children and pets indoors, and call us. Do not try to burn, smoke or spray a nest yourself.

Spiders and scorpions

SpidersMost spiders are harmless and eat other pests. A few need caution. We help you reduce the ones you do not want indoors.ScorpionsScorpions hide by day and hunt at night. Shake out shoes and clothes, seal the gaps, and call us for an inspection of the building and compound.

Fabric and pantry pests

SilverfishSilverfish eat starch in paper, book glue and fabric. Cut the humidity, store paper in sealed boxes and treat the hiding places.Clothes mothsIt is the larvae, not the adult moths, that eat holes in wool, silk, fur and feathers. Clean, store sealed and treat the cupboard.Carpet beetlesThe adults feed on flowers outdoors. The hairy larvae indoors eat wool, fur, feathers, leather and dead insects. Find the source and clean.

Farm and stored-product pests

Stored grain pestsDry grain properly, clean the store, use airtight storage and protect against weevils and borers before they spread.Locusts and grasshoppersReport swarms early. County and national teams run swarm control. We help farms with scouting, protection and spraying equipment.
