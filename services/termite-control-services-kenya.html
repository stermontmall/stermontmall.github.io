Termite control services in Kenya: identify the risk first

Termites can damage structural and decorative timber while remaining concealed in soil, wall cavities, roof spaces and wooden fittings. Mud tubes, hollow-sounding timber, discarded wings, blistered paint and damaged skirting can indicate activity, but these signs need assessment because moisture damage and other insects can look similar.

Stermont Arcade accepts enquiries from homeowners, landlords, property managers, builders, institutions and commercial premises. The first step is to establish whether the building is under construction or already occupied, where activity is visible, how the site is drained and whether timber or soil is accessible.

Pre-construction and post-construction protection

For new buildings, termite protection should be coordinated with the construction programme. The treatment specification, approved product, application rate, treated zones and contractor responsibilities should be documented before work covers the soil or foundation interfaces. Follow the exact product label and applicable local requirements.

For occupied buildings, the approach may involve targeted treatment around foundations, accessible soil, affected timber, monitoring stations or a combination. Drilling, trenching or injection is site-specific and should only be undertaken by trained personnel using equipment and protective measures appropriate to the product.

Inspection, treatment and monitoring

A useful inspection records active and historic signs, likely entry routes, moisture sources, vulnerable timber, landscaping contact and access limitations. The treatment plan should distinguish active infestation control from longer-term monitoring and prevention. It should also explain what areas are included and which areas could not be reached.

After treatment, keep the service report and follow the recommended inspection schedule. Repair leaks, improve drainage, avoid direct soil-to-timber contact where practical and maintain access to inspection points. Monitoring is important because termite pressure and site conditions can change over time.

Choosing termite products and equipment

The catalogue links below include termiticides, soil-rodding and injection tools, bait stations and inspection equipment. They are not interchangeable. Product selection depends on the label, intended use, site conditions, target termite and whether the work is pre-construction, post-construction or monitoring.

Never apply a concentrate by guesswork or use a product in a way not permitted by its label. Keep people, pets, water sources and food protected as directed. For a building treatment, a qualified applicator should determine the method and document the application.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best termite treatment for a building in Kenya?

There is no single best product for every site. The right approach depends on termite species, construction stage, soil conditions, access, moisture and label-approved use. An inspection should determine whether soil treatment, targeted timber treatment, bait monitoring or a combination is appropriate.

Can termites be treated after construction?

Yes. Post-construction options may include targeted soil or foundation treatments, treatment of affected timber, monitoring and moisture or entry-point corrections. Access and building design affect the plan.

Do you offer pre-construction termite protection?

Pre-construction protection can be planned around the building stage and the product label. Coordinate treatment with the contractor and document the treated areas before slabs or finishes conceal access.

How long does termite treatment last?

Protection duration varies by product, application quality, soil and moisture conditions, disturbance and maintenance. Use the product label and the service report rather than assuming a universal guarantee period.

Do you serve outside Nairobi?

We accept enquiries across Kenya. Share the county, town, property type and whether the building is new or occupied so availability, inspection arrangements and travel costs can be confirmed.
