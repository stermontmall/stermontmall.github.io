Power cuts, high token bills and farms far from the grid are the reasons most Kenyans switch to solar. A solar system only pays off when it is sized correctly: too small and the lights go off at night, too big and you have paid for capacity you never use. We start with your appliances and your daily use, then design the system around them.

Our team handles the whole installation: panel mounting on the roof or ground frame, inverter and charge controller setup, battery wiring and earthing, and a final test with your actual loads. We also service existing systems, so if your inverter beeps, your batteries drain fast or your panels are dirty and underperforming, we can inspect and fix it.

What is included

  • Load assessment of your lights, fridge, TV, pumps, computers and other appliances
  • System sizing for panels, inverter and battery storage
  • Roof or ground mounting with secure brackets and cable routing
  • Inverter, charge controller and lithium battery wiring with proper protection and earthing
  • Testing with your real loads and a walkthrough of how to read the system
  • Maintenance visits: panel cleaning, connection checks and battery health checks

How it works

  1. Tell us your appliances, your daily hours of use and whether you are on grid or off grid.
  2. We send a sizing plan and a quote with the equipment and the labour listed.
  3. We install, test and show you how to monitor the system.
  4. We schedule maintenance checks so output stays steady for years.

What decides the price

  • System size: a few lights and a TV need far less than a full house with fridge, pump and water heater
  • Battery capacity: the hours of backup you want drive the biggest part of the cost
  • Roof type and distance between panels, inverter and batteries
  • Whether you need grid-tied, hybrid or fully off-grid operation

Why more Kenyan homes and farms choose solar

Power cuts, rising prepaid token costs and the cost of running a generator push many households and businesses to look for another source. Solar gives you power from your own roof, and a battery keeps lights, a fridge, a router and a TV on when the grid is off.

Farms use solar for borehole pumps, cold storage, electric fences and lighting, where running a power line is expensive. We size each system around the equipment you actually run, so you do not pay for more than you need.

Maintenance after installation

A solar system is not install and forget. Panels collect dust, especially in dry months and near murram roads. Cable joints loosen over time and batteries age faster when they are discharged too deep. Our maintenance visits cover panel cleaning, connection checks, inverter settings and battery health.

Keep a simple note of how long your lights and appliances run. If the battery goes flat earlier than it used to, tell us. It often means the load has grown or a battery needs attention.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a solar system cost in Kenya?

Our solar service ranges from KSh 15,000 for small setups and maintenance work up to KSh 250,000 and more for large homes, farms and commercial systems. The size of the system and the battery capacity decide most of the price.

What size solar system does my house need?

It depends on the watts of your appliances and the hours you use them. Send us a list of what you want to run and we size the panels, inverter and battery for you.

Do you maintain solar systems you did not install?

Yes. We inspect panels, wiring, inverter settings and batteries on existing systems and tell you what needs repair or replacement.

Why choose lithium batteries?

Lithium batteries usually last longer, weigh less and can be discharged deeper than traditional lead-acid batteries. They cost more upfront, so we compare both options for your budget.

How long do solar panels last?

Good panels work for many years, and brands normally come with warranty papers. Ask for the warranty terms for the brand you buy, and keep the panels clean so they keep giving full output.

Can solar run my fridge and TV at night?

Yes, if the battery and inverter are sized for them. A fridge runs all day, so we count it in the sizing together with your lights, TV and router, and tell you how many hours of backup to expect.
