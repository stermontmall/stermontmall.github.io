Spraying the wrong product, at the wrong rate or at the wrong time wastes money and can damage a crop. Our agrovet team identifies what is attacking your plants or animals first, then recommends a product and a programme that fits the crop stage and the label.

We apply the chemicals on your farm with the right protective gear and equipment, and we advise on soil, nutrition and crop protection schedules. Our shop in the Stermont Arcade catalogue stocks herbicides, fungicides, insecticides and plant inputs, so advice and products come from one place.

What is included

  • Farm visit and diagnosis of pests, diseases and nutrient problems
  • Product recommendation matched to the crop and its growth stage
  • On-farm application by trained operators using protective equipment
  • Spray schedule with intervals and safety waiting periods taken from the product label
  • Soil and nutrition advice
  • Livestock health and housing advice where requested

How it works

  1. Tell us the crop, the farm size, the location and the problem you see.
  2. We visit the farm, diagnose the problem and quote the advice and the application.
  3. We apply the treatment and record what was used.
  4. We return or follow up by phone to check the result and plan the next spray.

What decides the price

  • Farm size and crop: greenhouse, vegetables, maize, fruit or livestock
  • Number of sprays in the programme
  • Travel distance outside Nairobi
  • Advice only versus advice plus product supply and application

Who we serve

Greenhouse owners, vegetable, fruit and cereal farmers, flower farms, dairy and poultry farmers.

Where we work

Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, Kajiado, Nakuru and other counties by arrangement.

Frequently asked questions

How much does farm spraying cost in Kenya?

Agrovet spraying and crop consultancy ranges from KSh 4,500 to KSh 35,000 depending on the size of the farm, the crop and the number of sprays.

Do you follow the product label?

Yes. Doses, mixing and waiting periods before harvest come from the manufacturer label. We never guess rates.

Can you diagnose a crop problem from photos?

Photos help us give a first opinion over WhatsApp, but a farm visit gives a reliable diagnosis because many problems look alike.

Do you also advise on livestock?

Yes. We advise on animal health products, disinfection and housing for dairy, poultry and other livestock.
