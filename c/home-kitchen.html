Choosing home and kitchen appliances in Kenya

Start with the space and job the appliance needs to handle. Measure the available width, depth and height, and compare the product's capacity with the needs of your household. For electrical appliances, check the rated power, voltage, plug type and any installation requirements. For cookware and kitchen tools, compare material, dimensions, cleaning instructions and compatibility with your cooker or other equipment.

Compare useful specifications, not just the headline price

Look at capacity, controls, included accessories, energy use where stated, warranty coverage and after-sales support. Check whether a product needs a separate stand, regulator, hose, filter or other accessory, and confirm exactly what is included in the listing. Product photographs help with appearance, but the written specification should guide decisions about size and compatibility.

Before checkout

  • Confirm the model number, dimensions and capacity.
  • Check delivery access for large or heavy appliances.
  • Review the warranty, returns and installation requirements.
  • Keep packaging and the receipt until the item has been checked.
