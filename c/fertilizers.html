Choosing fertilizer for crops in Kenya

The right fertilizer depends on the crop, soil condition, growth stage and nutrients required. Where possible, use a soil test and local agronomic advice to guide your choice. Compare the nutrient analysis on the bag, such as the stated nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium content, as well as any secondary or micronutrients. Do not choose a product from its name or colour alone.

Compare formulation, pack size and application timing

Check whether the product is granular, soluble, foliar or another formulation and confirm that it is suitable for the intended crop and application method. Pack size affects handling and storage, while the label explains the correct rate and timing. Do not assume that products with similar NPK numbers are interchangeable; nutrient sources and additional ingredients can differ.

Storage and buying checks

  • Inspect the seal, batch number, manufacture or expiry details where supplied.
  • Keep fertilizer dry and protected from moisture.
  • Store it separately from food, animal feed and children.
  • Follow the manufacturer's directions and local agronomic advice.
