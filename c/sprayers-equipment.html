Choosing a sprayer and protective wear

A sprayer that suits the job spreads the product evenly, and protective wear keeps it off your skin and out of your lungs. Hand sprayers suit rooms and small areas, while knapsack sprayers suit compounds and farms. Always wear the gloves, mask and clothing the product label recommends.

Which sprayer do I need for pest control?

Hand sprayers suit rooms and small jobs, and knapsack sprayers suit compounds and farms. Check the product label for any other requirements.
