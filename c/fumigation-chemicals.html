Buying fumigation chemicals in Kenya

Fumigation chemicals are hazardous and should be used only as the label directs. Check for a PCPB registration number, confirm the product is meant for your use, such as stores, grain or premises, and use a licensed operator for large jobs or enclosed spaces. Never enter a treated area until it is declared safe.

  • Use trained operators for large jobs.
  • Wear the protective gear the label requires (see protective wear).
  • Keep people, pets and food out until the label says it is safe.
