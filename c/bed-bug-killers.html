How to choose a bed bug killer in Kenya

Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, skirting boards and cracks in walls, so the product has to reach those hiding places. Most bed bug killers sold in Kenya are liquid insecticide concentrates that you dilute and spray on those spots. Pick a product that is registered for bed bugs, buy the pack size that matches the number of rooms or beds, and compare the active ingredient on the label.

  • Check the PCPB registration. Legal products carry a Pest Control Products Board registration number on the label.
  • Read the label for bed bugs. Use only products whose label lists bed bugs and the surfaces you plan to treat.
  • Match the pack size to the job. A single bed needs far less than a hostel, hotel or rental block. Ask us on WhatsApp if you are unsure.
  • Plan a second treatment. Eggs can survive the first spray, so most infestations need a repeat after the interval given on the label.
  • Add safety gear. Gloves, a mask and a sprayer make application safer and more even. See sprayers and protective wear.

Bed bug treatment tips

Wash bedding and clothes hot and dry them in strong sun or on high heat. Vacuum seams and floor edges and throw the dust away outside. Treat cracks, frame joints and furniture edges as the label directs, keep children, pets and food away during treatment, and never mix products.
