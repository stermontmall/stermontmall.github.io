How to choose Bajaj Boxer motorcycle spare parts in Kenya

Start with the exact motorcycle model and version, not engine capacity alone. Boxer 100, 125 and 150 models can use different assemblies, and old-model, KS, ES and later revisions may not share every part. Check the model designation, model year where known, engine or frame reference and the part number stamped on the original component. A similar-looking spare is not proof of compatibility.

Confirm fitment before ordering

Compare the replacement with the part removed from the motorcycle. Check dimensions, mounting holes, connector shape, terminal count, cable length, thread, spline, teeth and left- or right-hand orientation where relevant. For electrical components, match the wiring plug and rated specification. For brake, steering, wheel, engine and transmission components, have a qualified mechanic verify the exact application before installation.

Buy the correct part the first time

  • Share a clear photo of the old part and any readable part number.

How to choose the correct motorcycle spare part

Start with the motorcycle make, model, engine capacity and model year. A part that looks similar may have different dimensions, electrical connections, mounting points or thread sizes. For Bajaj Boxer motorcycles, confirm whether your bike is a BM100, BM125, BM150 or another specific variant. Do not rely on the name “Boxer” alone when checking fitment.

Check the part number and fitting position

Where the old component has an OEM or manufacturer part number, compare it with the number supplied by the seller or printed on the replacement packaging. For parts fitted on one side of the motorcycle, confirm left or right before ordering. Electrical components such as regulators, CDI units and stators need extra care: connector shape, AC or DC system and wiring arrangement must match the motorcycle. Ask a qualified mechanic to verify uncertain electrical parts before installation.

Common parts and compatibility checks

  • Filters and service parts: compare the engine variant and filter dimensions or part reference.
  • Brake and clutch cables: check cable length, end fittings and the correct front or rear application.
  • Lights and indicators: compare mounting points, connector type and the exact assembly.
  • Bearings and seals: match the printed bearing code, dimensions and application.
  • Engine and electrical components: confirm model code, connector layout and manufacturer reference before fitting.

Before you place an order

Send the seller the motorcycle model, engine capacity, year or frame details where appropriate, plus clear photographs of the old part and any visible part number. Confirm whether the listing is for one piece or a set, what is included in the package and whether installation is included. If a part is safety-critical, have a qualified motorcycle mechanic confirm compatibility and installation. Never force a component that does not align correctly.
