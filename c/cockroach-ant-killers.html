How to choose a cockroach and ant killer in Kenya

Cockroaches and ants live in cracks, behind appliances, under sinks and in drains, so the product must reach those places. Sprays treat surfaces and hiding spots, while gels and baits are placed where the insects feed. Choose a product whose label lists cockroaches or ants and the surfaces you plan to treat, and check for a PCPB registration number.

  • Treat hiding places. Focus on cracks, edges, drains, cupboards and behind fridges and cookers.
  • Clean first. Remove food, grease and waste so the insects have less to eat.
  • Repeat if the label says so. New insects hatch from eggs after treatment.
  • Food areas need extra care. Follow the label on what to cover and when you can use the area again.

For restaurants, hotels or large premises, use a licensed pest control operator. See also our bed bug killers and sprayers and protective wear.
