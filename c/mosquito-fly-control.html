How to choose mosquito and fly control products in Kenya

Mosquitoes and flies breed in standing water, waste and animal manure, so control works best when you treat the insects and remove the places they breed. Pick a product whose label lists mosquitoes or flies and the setting you are treating, such as a home, compound or animal house, and check for a PCPB registration number.

  • Remove breeding sites. Empty containers holding water, clear drains and clean animal housing regularly.
  • Match the product to the place. Indoor sprays, compound treatments and farm products are not interchangeable.
  • Protect yourself. Use the gloves and mask the label recommends and see sprayers and protective wear.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best way to control mosquitoes at home?

Remove standing water, keep drains and gutters clear and treat resting places with a PCPB-registered product that lists mosquitoes on its label. Screens and nets add protection.

Can I order fly control products online in Kenya?

Are fly sprays safe around animals?

Only products labelled for animal housing should be used there, and only as directed. Read the label and keep animals, feed and water covered or away during application.
