How to use a disinfectant responsibly

Remove visible dirt and organic matter first because cleaning is an important step before disinfection. Read the container label for the exact dilution, application method and contact time. Do not transfer a dilution rate from one brand to another. Keep animals, children, feed and drinking water away unless the label specifically allows the intended use. How to choose a farm disinfectant in Kenya

Choose a disinfectant for the premises and surface you need to treat. A product intended for an empty poultry house may not be approved for use around animals, on food-contact surfaces or in drinking-water systems. Before ordering, read the label and confirm the target use, active ingredient, concentration, dilution instructions, contact time, application method and precautions. Check the registration details required for the product in Kenya and buy the correct pack size for the job.

Disinfecting a poultry house

For routine poultry-house hygiene, remove birds, feed, water containers, eggs, litter and movable equipment before treatment unless the product label expressly gives different instructions. Remove manure, dust and other organic matter and wash the surfaces first. Apply only to the surfaces and at the dilution stated on the selected product label. Observe its full contact time, drying and ventilation instructions, and do not return birds until the label's re-entry conditions are met. Never spray a disinfectant directly onto birds, eggs, feed or drinking water unless the label specifically authorises that use.

Livestock pens, equipment and farm surroundings

For livestock housing, check whether the label permits use on floors, walls, partitions, drains, tools, footwear or vehicle-wash areas. Move animals and protect feed and water during application unless the label allows the specific use. Clean off mud, manure and bedding before disinfecting; disinfectant applied over heavy dirt may not perform as intended. Follow the stated contact time and allow surfaces to dry as directed before animals return. Use a separate, clearly identified product and procedure for any foot-dip or wheel-dip application, and replace solutions at the interval on the label.

General sanitation at home or work

For toilets, washable floors, drains and other household or workplace surfaces, use only products whose labels list those settings. Confirm whether the surface must be rinsed after treatment, especially where people prepare food or touch the surface frequently. Do not assume that a farm disinfectant is suitable for every household use. Keep the original container and label available so the product can be identified and handled correctly.

Safety checks before use

  • Wear the gloves, eye protection, protective clothing and other PPE specified on the label.
  • Never mix disinfectants with other cleaners or chemicals unless the label expressly permits it.
  • Keep children, pets, livestock, feed and drinking water away during treatment as directed.
  • Provide the ventilation required by the label and avoid breathing spray or mist.
  • Store the closed original container in the conditions stated on the label and dispose of leftover product safely.

Frequently asked questions

How do I dilute a farm disinfectant?

Use the dilution printed on the exact product label for the intended application. Concentrations and contact times differ between brands and uses; do not copy a rate from another disinfectant.

Can I use a poultry-house disinfectant while birds are inside?

Only if the product label expressly allows that application around live birds. Otherwise remove birds, feed and water, then follow the label for contact time, drying, ventilation and re-entry.

Can the same disinfectant be used for livestock pens and household surfaces?

Not automatically. Check that the label lists each intended setting and surface. Use a product only for its approved applications.

What is the difference between Kerol and Zagro Ultraxide?

They are different formulations. Compare the active ingredients, concentration, approved applications and label dilution for each product. Their rates are not interchangeable.

What safety equipment should I use?

Wear the protective equipment stated on the product label, keep people and animals away as directed, ventilate the area and never mix products unless the label permits it.
