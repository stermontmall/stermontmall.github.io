How to choose a rat and mouse killer in Kenya

Rats and mice follow food and shelter, so the best results come from blocking entry points, removing food and using a registered rodent control product where they travel, such as along walls and near burrows. Choose a PCPB-registered product and place baits where children, pets and livestock cannot reach them.

  • Seal gaps. Close holes around pipes, doors and roofs.
  • Store food and feed in sealed containers.
  • Place baits safely. Use covered bait stations or hidden spots as the label directs.
  • Remove dead rodents carefully with gloves and dispose of them safely.
