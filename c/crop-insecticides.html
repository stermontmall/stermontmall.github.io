How to choose crop insecticides in Kenya

Start by identifying the crop and the pest causing damage. Similar symptoms can have different causes, so check the crop, pest stage and affected plant parts before selecting a product. Compare the active ingredient, formulation, pack size and the crops and pests named on the label. A product listed for one crop or pest is not automatically suitable for another.

Check the label before application

Use only products whose current Kenyan registration and label directions match the intended crop and pest. Follow the stated rate, mixing order, application interval, pre-harvest interval and re-entry instructions. Wear the specified protective equipment, avoid drift onto people, animals, water and neighbouring crops, and do not spray in unsuitable weather. Never guess a dose or combine products unless the label or a qualified agronomist permits it.

Plan the complete crop-protection programme

Consider monitoring, field sanitation and non-chemical controls alongside pesticide use. Rotate modes of action where the label and agronomic advice support it, rather than repeatedly relying on the same active ingredient. Keep records of the product, batch, date, crop and application details.
