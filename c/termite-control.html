How to choose a termite control product in Kenya

Termites damage timber, fences and buildings from the soil or from inside the structure. Treatment depends on where they are, so read the label to see whether a product is meant for soil, timber or existing buildings, and check for a PCPB registration number. Large or hidden infestations in buildings are best handled by a licensed pest control operator.

  • Find the source. Look for mud tubes, hollow timber and soil mounds.
  • Reduce moisture and wood-to-soil contact.
  • Apply as the label directs, using protective wear.

Protective gear: sprayers, masks and gloves.
