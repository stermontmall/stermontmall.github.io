How to choose termite treatment products in Kenya

Termites can damage structural timber, doors, fence posts, stored wood and buildings. The right product depends on the target site and the use printed on its label. Start by identifying whether you need a product for pre-construction soil treatment, an existing building, timber or a smaller outdoor area. Do not choose a chemical based on its name alone: read the full label and confirm the intended pest, application site and method.

Match the product to the job

  • Before construction: check whether the label specifically permits pre-construction soil treatment and whether the project requires a qualified applicator.
  • Existing buildings: look for a label that covers the relevant structure or treatment site. Hidden or widespread activity may need inspection by a licensed pest control operator.
  • Timber, posts and fences: confirm that the product is labelled for the particular timber use and whether it is intended for surface or soil application.
  • Small outdoor areas: check the label's target pest, permitted location and application method before buying.

What to check before you order

  • Registration and label: check the PCPB registration details where applicable, the product name, target pest and permitted use.
  • Pack size: compare the listed pack size with the job and the label. A larger pack is not automatically the right choice.
  • Application equipment: confirm whether the label allows the sprayer or equipment you plan to use. Browse sprayers and protective equipment.
  • Safety instructions: check the required protective wear, site preparation, re-entry instructions, storage and disposal directions.

Reduce conditions that attract termites

Repair leaks, improve drainage, reduce persistent dampness and avoid leaving untreated wood in direct contact with soil where practical. Keep timber off the ground during storage and inspect vulnerable areas regularly. These steps support a treatment plan but do not replace a correctly selected and labelled product.

When to involve a professional

For structural damage, active mud tubes inside a building, a large infestation or any treatment involving enclosed or occupied spaces, seek an assessment from a licensed pest control operator. Do not improvise chemical mixtures, increase a dose or apply a product to a site that is not listed on its label.

Frequently asked questions

Which termite treatment product should I choose in Kenya?

Choose by the exact application site and intended use on the product label, such as soil, an existing structure or timber. Check registration details where applicable and ask a qualified professional if the infestation affects a building.

Can I use a soil termiticide on timber?

Not unless the product label specifically permits that use. Soil, timber and structural treatments are not automatically interchangeable.

What should I check before buying termite control products?

Check the target pest, permitted application site, registration details where applicable, pack size, equipment requirements and safety instructions on the label.
