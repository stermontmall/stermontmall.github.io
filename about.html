What we do

Stermont Arcade makes it easy to buy what you need online in Kenya: home and household items, electronics, appliances and pest control products. You choose your items, we confirm the details, you pay by M-Pesa, and we deliver.

Why shop with us

  • A real address. You can visit us at The Sarit Centre (Westlands), Karuna Road, Nairobi.
  • Clear prices. We tell you the delivery fee and the total before you pay.
  • Real people. Talk to us on WhatsApp or by phone, every day from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
  • Delivery across Kenya. All 47 counties, usually in 1 to 3 days.
  • Help if something is wrong. See our returns and refunds page.
