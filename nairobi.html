Buying pest control products in Nairobi

Check that the label shows a PCPB (Pest Control Products Board) registration number, read the directions before use, and wear the protective gear the label recommends. If you are unsure which product or pack size fits your home, hostel or rental block, message us on WhatsApp. For tips, read our guides on bed bugs, cockroaches and rats and mice.
