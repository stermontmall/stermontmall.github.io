Browse the Stermont Arcade catalogue for pest-control products, agrovet supplies, home essentials, electronics and appliances. Areas in and around Mombasa

Customers enquiring from Nyali, Bamburi, Kisauni, Likoni, Changamwe and Mtwapa can share their exact delivery location and a reachable phone number. These area names help us understand the destination; they do not mean we operate a walk-in branch in each neighbourhood.

How to order

  1. Browse the catalogue and choose the product and pack size you need.
  2. Send us the product link and your town or delivery area on WhatsApp.
  3. We confirm stock, the total price, delivery fee and expected delivery timing.
  4. Pay using the M-Pesa details confirmed for your order, then keep your order confirmation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order from Mombasa?

Yes. You can place an online order and contact us to confirm delivery arrangements for your location.

How do I pay?

We accept M-Pesa. Confirm the total and use only the payment details provided in your order conversation. We will never ask for your M-Pesa PIN.

How much is delivery?

The delivery fee depends on the destination and the size or weight of the order. We confirm the exact fee before payment.

Do you have a physical shop in Mombasa?

Our coordination and pickup point is The Sarit Centre, Westlands, Nairobi. This page is for online shopping and delivery enquiries, not a claim of a local branch in Mombasa.
