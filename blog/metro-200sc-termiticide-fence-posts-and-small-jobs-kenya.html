Metro 200SC termiticide for fence posts and small jobs

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Metro 200SC is a non-repellent, odourless and colourless suspension concentrate termiticide with imidacloprid at 200 grams per litre. It comes in a 25 ml pack for soil treatment, timber, posts and wooden fences. Dilute it as the label directs and mix only what the job needs.

Subterranean termites live in the soil and travel through tunnels to reach fence posts, roof members and other wooden structures. A small, easy-to-store pack is often all a homeowner needs for a post, a fence line or a small treatment.

Why does a non-repellent termiticide matter?

A soil treatment places a protective zone between the termites and the structure you want to protect. Because Metro 200SC is non-repellent, termites do not easily detect and avoid the treated area.

It is also odourless and colourless, so nothing smells strongly in your home or compound.

What is the 25 ml pack good for?

The pack suits homeowners and small construction jobs. Typical uses include:

  • Soil treatment around small structures
  • The base of fence posts and timber before they go into the ground
  • Wooden fences and posts

How do I treat posts and soil?

Find the termite activity first: look for mud tubes on walls, hollow-sounding timber and soil mounds nearby. Then treat the soil thoroughly as directed on the label, and treat the base of fence posts and timber before they are placed in the ground.

The label gives the dilution. A 25 ml pack is small, so measure carefully and mix only what the job needs.

What should I do first, and how do I stay safe?

Remove rotting wood and tree stumps close to your building, repair leaks that keep the ground damp, and keep firewood and stored timber off the ground.

Check the PCPB registration number on the label, and mix and apply exactly as directed. Wear protective gear, keep Metro 200SC away from children, pets, food and water, and never contaminate water sources when you mix or spray.

Frequently asked questions

Will it leave a smell or a stain?

Metro 200SC is described as odourless and colourless, so it leaves no strong smell in your home or compound.

Can I treat a whole building with the 25 ml pack?

The pack suits homeowners, small construction jobs, posts and fences. For a whole building or a large infestation, consider a licensed pest control operator.

How should I store the pack?

Keep it tightly closed in its original container, in a cool, dry place out of reach of children and away from food.
