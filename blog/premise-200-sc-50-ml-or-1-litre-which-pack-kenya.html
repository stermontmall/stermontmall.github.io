Premise 200 SC: choosing the 50 ml or 1 litre pack

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Bayer Premise 200 SC is a non-repellent, odourless termiticide with imidacloprid at 200 grams per litre. Termites cross the treated soil without detecting it and spread the active ingredient through the colony. The 50 ml pack suits a small job, and the 1 litre bottle suits a house or a building site.

Termites (mchwa) rarely announce themselves. They eat door frames, roof timbers and cupboards from the inside until the damage is expensive. Premise 200 SC is made for soil treatment, and it comes in two pack sizes.

How does Premise 200 SC reach the whole colony?

It is non-repellent, so termites walk through the treated soil without detecting it. They carry the active ingredient back and spread it through the colony, which is how it reaches termites you cannot see.

It is suitable for pre-construction and post-construction treatment, and it is also listed for controlling ants.

Which pack should I choose?

The label rates make the choice simple. The 50 ml pack makes one 20 litre knapsack, which treats about 4 square metres of soil or 4 linear metres of trench. That suits a small job.

The 1 litre bottle makes twenty knapsacks, which suits a house or a building site. Check the rates against the label on your pack before mixing.

Where can it be applied?

The catalog lists these applications:

  • Foundations and slab preparation, before the slab is cast
  • Trenches along the inside and outside of foundation lines
  • Termite mounds and wall voids
  • Existing buildings, along foundations and slab joints

What safety steps matter most?

The product is harmful. Wear gloves, overalls, boots and a mask when mixing and spraying. It is toxic to bees and aquatic life, so never let spray or wash water reach streams, ponds, wells or drains.

Do not apply in rain or when rain is expected, and wash all equipment after use. For a large building, consider a licensed pest control operator.

Frequently asked questions

Is Premise 200 SC repellent to termites?

No. It is non-repellent, so termites cross the treated soil without detecting it and carry the active ingredient back to the colony.

Does it have a strong smell?

It is described as odourless, and practically odourless at the recommended dilution.

How should I store leftover product?

Store the unused product in its original, closed pack in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food, feed and children.
