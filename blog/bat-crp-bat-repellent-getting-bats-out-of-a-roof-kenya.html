Bat CRP: getting bats out of a roof or hall in Kenya

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Bat CRP is a heavy-duty bat repellent chemical for roofs, ceilings, halls and other places where bats roost. You dilute it with water as the label directs and apply it where the bats settle. It comes in 250 ml, 500 ml and 1 litre packs.

Bats in a roof leave droppings and urine that stain ceilings, cause strong smells and attract insects, and their noise at night can disturb families, guests and worshippers. Halls, churches, schools, hotels, offices and homes often need a way to move bats out.

What does Bat CRP do?

Bat CRP is a repellent, so its job is to make the roosting spots unwelcome. You dilute it with water as directed on the label and apply the mixture where bats settle, such as the underside of roofs, ceiling spaces, beams and corners.

Treat the roosting surfaces thoroughly and repeat if the label advises. The label also gives the dilution for roofing and hall areas, so confirm it on your pack before mixing.

What should you do before treating?

Prepare the area first:

  • Clear old droppings and wear protective gear, because dried droppings can create dust
  • Check local rules on handling bats if you are unsure
  • Plan how you will reach the roof, and use a ladder or platform safely

Why do the entry points matter?

Once the bats have left, close the entry points so they cannot return. Check the eaves, the gaps around the roof, vents and holes in walls, and seal them. Then clean up droppings safely and keep the roof space dry and clean. Regular inspections help you catch a new problem early.

Bats are useful animals outdoors because they eat insects, so the aim is to move them out of buildings.

What safety steps matter most?

Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when you mix and apply, and wash your hands afterwards. Keep the product away from food, water, pets and livestock, and out of reach of children. Do not let it reach streams or ponds. Store it in the original closed container in a cool, dry, locked place.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bat CRP a repellent or a poison?

It is sold as a heavy-duty bat repellent chemical. The aim is to move bats out of buildings, and the label has the directions.

How do I dilute it?

Dilute it with water as directed on the label. The label gives the dilution for roofing and hall areas.

What do I do after the bats have gone?

Close the entry points, clean up droppings safely and keep the roof space dry.
