Delivery to all 47 counties in Kenya: how it works

Updated 15 October 2026 · 3 min read

You do not need to live in Nairobi to shop online. Stermont Arcade delivers to all 47 counties. Here is what to expect and how to make your delivery go smoothly.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery is usually 1 to 3 days. The time depends on your county and how far you are from the main towns. We confirm the timing for your location before you pay, so you are not left guessing.

What does delivery cost?

The delivery fee, if there is one, depends on your location and the size of your order. We confirm it with you on WhatsApp before you pay, so there are no surprises at checkout.

Give a delivery address that works

Write your county, town and a clear landmark, such as a stage, shop, school or church near you. In many areas a landmark helps a rider find you faster than a street name. Use a phone number that is reachable, and keep it on while your parcel is on the way.

Be ready to receive the parcel

Choose someone who can receive it if you will be away. Rain, poor roads and busy days can add delays, so keep in touch with us on WhatsApp if you do not hear anything.

Check your item on arrival

Open the parcel soon after it arrives. Check that the item is what you ordered, that it is not damaged and that all the accessories are there. If something is wrong, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos, and we will arrange a replacement or refund. Read our returns policy and shipping details.

Pay safely

Pay only through the M-Pesa Paybill details shown at checkout, and keep your M-Pesa SMS until the order arrives. See how to order and pay.
