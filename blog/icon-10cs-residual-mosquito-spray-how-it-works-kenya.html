Icon 10CS: how a long-lasting residual spray works at home

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Icon 10CS is a long-lasting residual insecticide. Dilute it as the label directs, spray the surfaces where mosquitoes, flies and cockroaches rest or walk, and it keeps releasing its active ingredient after the spray has dried. Treat the whole house in one session and clear standing water first.

Mbu (mosquitoes) bite at dusk and through the night, and they can carry diseases such as malaria. A spray that works only while it is wet means spraying again and again, which is why a residual insecticide is worth understanding before you buy one.

What does capsule suspension mean?

Syngenta Icon 10CS contains lambda-cyhalothrin in a capsule suspension (CS) formulation. The active ingredient is held in tiny capsules in a liquid. After spraying, the capsules stay on the treated surface and release the active ingredient slowly.

This is designed to give a longer-lasting residual effect than a spray that acts all at once, so insects that land on or walk across the surface keep picking up the insecticide.

Where should I spray it?

Think about where insects rest or walk, not only where you see them. Icon 10CS is sold for mosquitoes, flies and cockroaches in homes and small businesses, so focus on these places:

  • Walls, and door and window frames
  • Eaves and shaded places
  • Drains and damp corners
  • Cracks and corners where cockroaches hide

What should I do before and after spraying?

Treat the whole house in one session so insects do not simply move to an untreated room. Remove or cover food, dishes and water containers, and keep people and pets out of the treated rooms until the spray has dried.

Clearing stagnant water, rubbish and blocked drains removes breeding sites, and mosquito nets and window screens add protection while you sleep.

Keep any leftover concentrate in the original closed bottle, in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.

Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when you mix and spray, and wash well afterwards. Lambda-cyhalothrin is very toxic to fish and bees, so never rinse sprayers or containers into drains, streams, ponds or fish tanks.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 62.5 ml pack enough for my house?

It is a convenient size for a house, a few rooms or a small business. A large building may need more than one pack, or a licensed pest control operator.

Do I need to dilute Icon 10CS?

Yes. Shake the bottle well, measure carefully and mix it thoroughly in clean water in the sprayer, as the label directs.

Does a residual spray replace nets and clean-up?

No. Use it together with mosquito nets, window screens and the removal of stagnant water and rubbish.
