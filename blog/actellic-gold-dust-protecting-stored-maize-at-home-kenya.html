Actellic Gold Dust: protecting stored maize at home

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Actellic Gold Dust is a ready-to-use dustable powder for protecting stored grain from insect damage. It combines two active ingredients, pirimiphos-methyl and thiamethoxam, and needs no mixing in water. You mix it evenly through the grain as it goes into storage.

Stored-grain pests can ruin a harvest in a few months, hollowing out kernels, leaving dust and spoiling grain for food, seed and sale. A dust that goes in as the grain is stored keeps working inside the bag.

What does Actellic Gold Dust protect against?

The catalog lists the larger grain borer, grain weevil, red flour beetle, lesser grain borer and other stored-grain beetles and moths.

It is registered in Kenya by the Pest Control Products Board, and the registration number is printed on the pack. Check it there.

How do I use it?

Mix the dust evenly through the grain as it goes into storage. Even mixing matters, because pests survive in untreated pockets, so turn the grain until the dust is spread through every bag.

The pack prints a rate for a standard bag of grain. At that rate, a 100 g pack treats about two bags, a 500 g pack about ten and a 1 kg pack about twenty. Follow the label exactly and do not use more than directed.

How should I prepare the store and the grain?

Treatment works well in a clean, dry store:

  • Clean and dry the store, and use dry, clean bags
  • Remove old grain, sweep out dust and fix cracks where insects can hide
  • Make sure the grain is properly dry before storing, because damp grain spoils even when treated

Which pack size should I pick?

There are three sizes: 100 g, 500 g and 1 kg. They suit households and small farms storing maize and other cereals.

Avoid breathing the dust, and wear a mask and gloves when mixing. Keep it away from children, pets and food, and follow the label's waiting period before using treated grain.

Frequently asked questions

Do I mix it with water?

No. It is a ready-to-use dust that is mixed evenly through the grain as it goes into storage.

How should I store the pack?

Keep the pack closed in a cool, dry place out of reach of children.

Can I rinse containers into a stream or pond?

No. Do not rinse containers into water sources.
