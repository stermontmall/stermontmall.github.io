ULV Cold Fogger in Kenya: How to Choose a Cordless Fogging Machine

Pest-control equipment buying guide · 6 November 2026

Quick answer: A cordless ULV cold fogger is a battery-powered machine that mechanically turns a compatible liquid into fine aerosol droplets. Before buying one in Kenya, compare tank capacity, battery runtime, charging time, droplet range, service support and—most importantly—the chemical label's approval for ULV application.

Professional pest management sometimes requires equipment that can distribute a compatible treatment beyond the reach of a hand sprayer. A cordless ultra-low-volume (ULV) cold fogger is one option for trained operators working in suitable environments. Its compact format can be useful where access to a power socket is limited, but the right machine depends on the job, the formulation and the treatment plan.

What does a cordless ULV cold fogger do?

A ULV cold fogger uses a motor and nozzle system to break a liquid into small airborne droplets. “Cold” means that the machine does not rely on a heated pulse-jet system to create the fog. The VectorFog DC20, product code VF-DC20, is listed by a Kenyan pest-control supplier with a 2-litre tank, 12-volt power, a stated spray distance of 2–7 metres and a droplet range of 5–50 microns. The same listing gives a weight of 3.2 kg and dimensions of 480 × 250 × 200 mm.

These are model-specific published details, not guarantees of performance in every setting. Ask the seller to confirm the exact version, included accessories, battery condition and current availability before paying.

Which specifications should you check before buying?

  • Tank capacity: A 2-litre tank is compact. Ask how the machine is intended to be refilled and cleaned, and whether the tank material is compatible with the intended formulation.
  • Battery runtime: The DC20 supplier listing states up to 40 minutes of continuous operation on a charge. Ask whether the battery is new, how it is charged and whether a replacement is available.
  • Charging time: The published DC20 listing states about three hours. Confirm the charger supplied matches the machine's electrical requirements.
  • Droplet range and flow control: Check the actual model manual rather than relying on generic fogger specifications. Different sources may quote different ranges for different versions.
  • Weight and handling: A listed empty weight of 3.2 kg may be manageable, but the filled machine weighs more. Check the handle, shoulder strap and balance.
  • Spare parts and support: Ask about nozzle parts, seals, battery replacement, servicing and warranty support available in Kenya.

Is a cordless ULV fogger suitable for every pest-control job?

No. A fogger is an application tool, not a complete pest-control programme. It does not replace inspection, sanitation, exclusion, monitoring or targeted treatment. It may be unsuitable where the target pest is concealed in protected harbourages, where aerosol cannot reach the target, or where people, animals, food or sensitive equipment cannot be safely excluded and protected.

Do not assume that every insecticide, disinfectant or household liquid can be fogged. Use only products whose label explicitly permits the intended application method and environment. Never put flammable or combustible liquids into an electric ULV machine unless the equipment manufacturer explicitly approves that exact use. Follow the machine manual and product label, and use the protective equipment specified for the task.

What should a Kenyan buyer ask the supplier?

Before ordering, request a written quotation in Kenya shillings that identifies the exact make and model. Ask whether the item is in stock locally or imported on order, what is included in the box, the warranty period, delivery arrangements and who handles repairs. Request a current photo of the exact unit being supplied—not a generic image of a similar fogger.

Also ask the supplier to confirm the machine's compatibility with the formulation you intend to use. If you are a pest-control business, make sure staff are trained and that your operating procedures meet applicable Kenyan requirements.

Frequently asked questions

What is a cordless ULV fogger?

It is a battery-powered machine that mechanically produces a fine aerosol from a compatible liquid. It does not use the heat-based fog generation of a thermal fogger.

How long does a DC20 cordless fogger run?

The supplier listing for the VectorFog DC20 states up to 40 minutes of continuous operation and about three hours of charging. Actual runtime depends on battery condition and operating settings.

Can I put any pesticide in a ULV fogger?

No. Use only formulations whose labels and equipment instructions specifically permit the intended ULV application. Never assume that a product suitable for a hand sprayer is suitable for fogging.

Is a cold fogger the same as a thermal fogger?

No. A cold ULV fogger uses mechanical atomisation, while a thermal fogger uses heat to generate fog. They have different operating requirements and chemical compatibility rules.
