Actellic 50EC for stores, bagged grain and livestock houses

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Actellic 50EC is an organophosphate emulsifiable concentrate with pirimiphos-methyl at 500 grams per litre. It covers three jobs: surface spraying of stores and livestock houses, treating bagged grain, and mixing into stored grain as an admixture. Do not use it with thermal foggers.

Weevils and moths can turn a full store of maize into dust within months. Insects arrive with the harvest and hide in cracks, on walls and on bag surfaces, so the building needs treating as well as the grain.

What can Actellic 50EC do?

It acts by contact, ingestion and fumigant action, and it keeps working on walls, floors and bag surfaces after you spray. The catalog lists weevils, beetles, moths, flies and wasps. It is sold in four sizes: 100 ml, 250 ml, 500 ml and 1 litre.

Its three uses are:

  • Surface spray for walls, floors, empty stores and livestock houses
  • Treatment of bagged grain and bag stacks
  • Admixture mixed into stored grain

How do I use it?

Clean and sweep the store first, removing old grain and debris, then spray to wet all surfaces without run-off. For admixture, dilute the measured amount in the water volume shown on the label and apply it to the grain, not in the knapsack.

The catalog publishes mixing rates and coverage for each use, so check them against the label on your pack before you mix. Measure the area first, so that you mix only the volume you need.

Can I use it in a thermal fogger?

No. The catalog advice for this product is not to use it with thermal foggers. If you own a fogging machine, choose a product whose label allows thermal fogging.

What safety steps matter most?

Actellic 50EC is an organophosphate, so wear gloves, a mask and overalls when mixing and spraying, and keep children, pets, food, feed and water away. Remove food, feed and animals before spraying, and ventilate the store afterwards.

Label grain treated by admixture as chemically treated, and follow any waiting period on the pack. Store the bottle closed, in its original container, in a cool, dry, locked place.

Frequently asked questions

Which pests does Actellic 50EC control?

The catalog lists weevils, beetles, moths, flies and wasps.

Does it keep working after I spray?

Yes. It keeps working on treated walls, floors and bag surfaces after you spray.

Do porous surfaces need more spray liquid?

They can. Surfaces such as concrete, brick and unpainted wood may need more spray liquid than normal surfaces, so check the label.
