Smart TV buying guide for Kenya: size, resolution and tips

Updated 15 October 2026 · 4 min read

A good TV should fit your room, your budget and the way you watch. Use this guide to compare models before you buy, whether you shop in a store or online.

1. Choose the right screen size

As a rough guide, a 32 inch TV suits a bedroom or a small room, 43 inch suits an average living room viewed from about 2 to 3 metres, and 50 inch or larger suits bigger rooms where you sit further back. Measure your wall or TV stand first, and remember the TV is wider than the number suggests, because the size is the diagonal of the screen.

2. Pick the resolution

HD and Full HD are fine for small screens. On 43 inch and above, 4K (Ultra HD) gives a sharper picture, especially for streaming and sports. A 4K TV costs more, so compare the price difference for the size you want.

3. Check the smart features

Smart TVs connect to Wi-Fi so you can use apps like YouTube and streaming services. Look at which system the TV runs (such as Google TV or Android TV, or the maker's own system) and confirm the apps you use are supported. Check the TV has built-in Wi-Fi if you do not want to plug in a cable.

4. Look at the ports

Count the HDMI ports you need for a decoder, a game console and a soundbar. Check for USB ports for playing files and for an audio output if you plan to add a soundbar or home theatre.

5. Digital tuner and decoders

Kenya's free-to-air TV uses digital broadcasting. Check whether the TV has a built-in digital (DVB-T2) tuner, or plan to use a decoder. If you use a pay TV decoder, make sure the TV has the right input for it.

6. Mounting and power

If you will hang the TV on a wall, check the mount type and bracket you need. Power dips and surges can happen, so a surge protector is a cheap way to protect an expensive TV.

7. What to check when it arrives

Open the box in front of the delivery person if you can. Check the screen for cracks or dead pixels, make sure the remote and accessories are included, and switch it on.
