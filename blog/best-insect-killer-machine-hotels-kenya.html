Best Insect Killer Machine for Hotels in Kenya

Updated 17 November 2026 · Kenya buying guide

Buying rule: For food-handling environments, assess a glueboard capture design first because it retains insects on a board rather than using an exposed electric killing grid. Confirm the exact unit's food-safety suitability, documented coverage and maintenance requirements before purchase.

Hotels, restaurants, bakeries, catering kitchens and food stores need a flying-insect monitoring and control plan that fits their premises. An insect light trap is one part of integrated pest management, not a replacement for waste control, screens, door management, cleaning and professional inspection.

Glueboard versus electric grid

FeatureGlueboard captureElectric grid
Capture methodInsects are retained on an adhesive board.Insects contact an electrified grid and are killed.
Food-area considerationCan reduce the risk of insect fragments being projected from the unit; still needs careful placement and board inspection.May scatter insect fragments. Usually needs more careful separation from exposed food and preparation surfaces.
Monitoring recordBoard can be inspected and retained as evidence, subject to site procedures.Catch count and debris handling require a separate inspection routine.
ConsumablesReplacement glueboards and, depending on model, lamps or LEDs.Grid cleaning and lamp replacement; some units also have trays.

Neither design is automatically HACCP certified. HACCP is a site-specific food-safety management approach. Hotels should document trap location, target pests, inspection frequency, corrective action, cleaning and records in the pest-management programme. Obtain written model-specific food-safety or compliance documentation from the manufacturer where required by the auditor or client.

Coverage: don't buy by wattage alone

Wattage is not a reliable substitute for a manufacturer's coverage figure. Effective attraction depends on the lamp spectrum and output, unit design, mounting height, line of sight, competing light, airflow, room partitions and the flying-insect species. Stermont's catalogue does not currently provide verified square-metre coverage for every listed machine. Before specifying quantities, ask for the manufacturer's coverage data for the exact model and compare it with the hotel's room plan.

Calculate the real maintenance cost

Compare the purchase price with the annual cost of replacement boards, tubes or LED modules, cleaning labour, inspection time, power consumption and disposal. For a glueboard model, record the board price and how many boards are used per year under the hotel's actual insect pressure. For a grid model, include safe isolation, cleaning and disposal of insect debris. Ask suppliers for replacement-part numbers and availability in Kenya before selecting a model.

Hotel installation checklist

  • Map kitchens, food stores, waste rooms, loading bays and guest-facing spaces separately.
  • Position traps away from exposed food, clean equipment and food-contact surfaces.
  • Do not place units where they compete with external light or attract insects through open doors.
  • Record unit ID, location, model, inspection date, catch observations and maintenance.
  • Use door seals, insect screens, waste management and structural proofing alongside traps.
  • Check the manufacturer's instructions and the hotel's food-safety plan before installation.

Frequently asked questions

Are glueboard insect traps better for hotel kitchens?

A glueboard unit can retain insects on an adhesive board instead of breaking them on an exposed electric grid. It may be easier to manage around food-handling areas, but suitability depends on the hotel's food-safety risk assessment, placement, maintenance and local requirements.

How many square metres can one insect light trap cover?

Coverage varies by model, installation height, room layout, light competition, insect pressure and manufacturer test method. Stermont's current listings do not provide verified square-metre coverage for every model, so request the exact manufacturer's specification before sizing.

Does an insect killer machine have HACCP certification?

Do not assume that a UV trap is HACCP-certified. HACCP is a food-safety management system; ask the supplier for the exact model's documented compliance or suitability evidence and include it in the hotel's hazard analysis.

How often should glueboards and UV lamps be changed?

Inspect glueboards on a documented schedule and replace sooner when dusty, full, wet or no longer tacky. Replace lamps according to the manufacturer's service interval and verify the exact compatible part.
