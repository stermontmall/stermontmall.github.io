Kenya Online Shopping Checklist: 9 Checks Before You Pay

Updated 1 November 2026 · Practical consumer guide

Buying online can save a trip across town, but a good deal is more than the price shown on a product card. Before sending money, make sure you know who is selling, exactly what is being supplied, what delivery will cost and what to do if the parcel is not right. This checklist is written for shoppers in Kenya, whether you are ordering electronics, household goods, farm supplies or everyday essentials.

The quick rule: do not pay until the seller, exact product, final amount, delivery arrangement and proof of payment are clear in writing.

1. Confirm the seller is real and reachable

Look for a consistent business name, working contact details, a clear returns process and a website address that matches the business. Check that the contact channel you are using is linked from the seller's own website. Be cautious if someone pressures you to move to a personal number or changes payment details at the last minute.

2. Match the exact product before ordering

Read the full product title, brand, model or variant, size, colour and pack quantity. Similar names can describe different specifications. For electronics, compare model numbers and key features; for appliances, check dimensions and capacity; for farm or household supplies, confirm the exact pack size and intended use from the manufacturer's label.

3. Ask for the complete price

Confirm the item price plus delivery, installation or handling charges, if any. Ask whether the quoted amount is the final amount payable and whether any optional add-ons are included. Do not assume delivery is free simply because the product page does not show a fee.

4. Check stock and delivery to your location

Ask whether the exact variant is available now, where it will dispatch from, the estimated delivery window and how you will receive tracking or dispatch updates. Delivery timing can differ between Nairobi, county towns and remote destinations, so get an estimate for your actual destination rather than relying on a general promise.

5. Use a payment method that leaves a record

Follow the payment instructions shown on the seller's official website or confirmed through its published contact channel. For M-Pesa, verify the recipient or Paybill details on your own phone before confirming. Never share your M-Pesa PIN or one-time password with a seller or caller. Keep the transaction message until the order is complete.

6. Save the order details

Keep a screenshot or copy of the product page, the seller's written confirmation, the amount paid, the transaction reference and the delivery estimate. These records make it easier to resolve a missing item, wrong variant or payment query.

7. Understand returns and warranty before payment

Read the returns policy and check the time limit, condition requirements, exclusions and who pays return transport. For products with a warranty, ask who handles the claim, what proof is needed and whether the warranty is supplied by the manufacturer, distributor or seller. A warranty statement should be specific enough to understand before you buy.

8. Inspect the parcel when it arrives

Check the outer packaging for visible damage and compare the delivered item with your order confirmation. Confirm the model, size, quantity and accessories. If something is wrong, take clear photos, keep the packaging and contact the seller promptly using the official channel. Do not discard labels or receipts until you are satisfied.

9. Be alert to common warning signs

  • A price that is dramatically lower than comparable offers with no credible explanation.
  • Pressure to pay immediately or secrecy around the seller's identity.
  • Requests for your PIN, password, one-time code or remote access to your phone.
  • Payment details that change unexpectedly or belong to an unrelated person.
  • No written order confirmation, delivery estimate or returns information.

Frequently asked questions

Should I pay before confirming stock?

It is safer to confirm that the exact product variant is available and agree on delivery details before making payment.

What should I do if an M-Pesa payment goes to the wrong number?

Keep the transaction message and contact your mobile-money provider promptly through its official support channels. Do not share your PIN or verification codes with anyone claiming they can reverse it.

What proof should I keep after ordering online?

Keep the product details, written order confirmation, payment reference, delivery estimate, receipt and any messages about warranty or returns.

Information is general shopping guidance, not legal advice. Always check the seller's current terms and the manufacturer's product information.
