Fly bait for farms and kitchens: choosing an Agita 1GB pack

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Choose the 20 g pack for a small home or shop, and the 100 g or 400 g pack for larger kitchens, animal sheds and farms with several fly areas. Place Agita 1GB where flies gather, as the label directs, and keep it away from food, children, pets and livestock.

Flies gather in the same few places: waste points, drains, animal sheds and food preparation areas. A fly bait lets you act where they gather, instead of chasing them around a room.

Which Agita 1GB pack size should I choose?

Agita 1GB is sold for farms, homes and commercial kitchens in 20 g, 100 g and 400 g packs. A household or a small shop can start with the 20 g pack. A restaurant kitchen, a dairy or a farm with several sheds is likely to want the 100 g or 400 g pack, so you do not run short in the middle of a season.

Where should I place fly bait?

Read the label first and place the bait where flies gather, such as near bins, drains, waste points and animal sheds, in the way the label directs. Keep it out of reach of children, pets and livestock, and away from food and food preparation surfaces. Check the bait regularly and replace it as the label directs.

Several small placements in the places flies actually use usually do more than one pile in a single spot, so spread the bait across the problem areas as the label allows.

What else keeps flies down?

Bait works better alongside hygiene, because flies stay where food and moisture are easy to find.

  • Cover bins and empty them daily
  • Clean up spills and food waste quickly
  • Keep floors and drains dry
  • Use screens on doors and windows

Frequently asked questions

Is fly bait the same as a spray?

No. A bait attracts flies to a placed product, while a spray is applied over surfaces. Many sites use a bait together with good hygiene.

Can I use Agita 1GB in a kitchen?

It is sold for commercial kitchens, but keep it away from food and food preparation surfaces, and follow the label for placement.

How do I store leftover bait?

Keep it in the original, tightly closed pack in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, food, animal feed and children.

Does the pack size change how I use it?

No. Directions always come from the manufacturer's label. The pack size only decides how much bait you have for the number of areas you need to cover.
