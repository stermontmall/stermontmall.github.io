Darvin 50EW 28 ml: a small pack for a bedsitter or rental

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Darvin 50EW is an insecticide for bed bugs, cockroaches and ants in homes and rentals. The small 28 ml pack is sized for a house, a bedsitter or a rental unit. It is an EW formulation, an emulsion in water, which you mix with water before spraying.

A landlord or tenant treating one unit does not always want a large bottle. Darvin 50EW comes in a small 28 ml pack, which is easy to buy, store and use for a single treatment, and it covers three of the most common household pests.

What does EW mean?

EW stands for emulsion in water. It is a liquid concentrate that you mix with water before spraying, using the dilution printed on the label.

The pack is sized for a house, a bedsitter or a rental unit. How far it goes depends on that dilution, so check the label before you buy for a larger space.

Which pests does it treat?

Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards and cracks in walls, and they bite at night. Cockroaches shelter in kitchens, drains, cupboards and behind appliances, and they spread germs on food and surfaces. Ants follow food into kitchens, stores and bedrooms and nest in walls, floors and gardens.

How do you treat a rental unit?

Dilute exactly as directed on the label. Spray cracks, crevices, bed frames, skirting boards, door frames and other hiding places, and spray ant trails and entry points. Keep people, pets and food away until the spray has dried, and repeat the treatment if the label advises it.

  • Wash bedding at a high temperature and vacuum mattresses, floors and corners first
  • Treat all the affected rooms at the same time so pests do not move to rooms you have not sprayed
  • Check second-hand furniture and luggage for bed bugs before bringing them indoors

How do you stop the pests returning?

Store food in closed containers, wipe up crumbs and spills, keep rubbish bins covered, and seal cracks and gaps around pipes and doors.

Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, keep the product away from children, pets, food and water, and never pour leftovers into drains, streams or ponds.
