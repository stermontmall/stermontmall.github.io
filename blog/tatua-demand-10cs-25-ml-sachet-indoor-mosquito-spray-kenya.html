Tatua Demand 10CS: an indoor residual spray for mosquitoes

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Tatua Demand 10CS is a capsule suspension public health insecticide, in Group 3A, used as a residual indoor spray against adult mosquitoes. It comes in a 25 ml sachet that suits homes, hostels, small hotels and offices. Dilute and apply it as the label directs.

Mosquitoes rest on walls and ceilings after feeding. A residual spray leaves a protective layer on those surfaces, so mosquitoes that land there are killed over a period of time. Tatua Demand 10CS is made for that indoor job.

What does capsule suspension mean?

CS stands for capsule suspension, a formulation that holds the active ingredient in tiny capsules. This helps it stay on surfaces longer. It is a practical tool for homes in areas where mosquitoes are a problem, particularly alongside other protection such as treated nets and screens.

How do you apply it?

Spray the walls and ceilings as directed by the label, using the dilution printed there. Remove or cover food, utensils and fish tanks before treating, and keep people and pets out of the room until the spray is dry. The 25 ml sachet is easy to store and handle, and it suits homes, hostels, small hotels and offices.

How can you reduce mosquito breeding?

Fewer mosquitoes arriving means less work for the spray. Take these steps around the home:

  • Empty standing water from buckets, tins, tyres and flower pots
  • Clean gutters and cover water tanks
  • Use treated bed nets and window screens for extra protection

What safety steps matter?

Keep the product away from children, food and pets and read the label before use. Check the PCPB registration number on the label. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and do not contaminate water sources. Keep the sachet in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food.
