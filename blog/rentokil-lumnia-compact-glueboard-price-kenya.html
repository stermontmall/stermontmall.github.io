Genuine versus compatible replacement boards

A genuine board is supplied under the manufacturer's identity and part number. A compatible board is made by another supplier and should be described as compatible, not branded or represented as genuine Rentokil. Compatibility should be established by dimensions, fit, adhesive performance, backing and equipment requirements. A board that physically fits is not automatically approved by the equipment manufacturer. Stermont's two listings above are named as Rentokil Lumnia Compact replacement boards; ask us to confirm the physical pack and provenance if you require a manufacturer-sealed carton or procurement documentation.

When should a glueboard be replaced?

Use a scheduled inspection routine and replace the board when it becomes dusty, wet, full of insects, damaged or loses tack. The correct interval varies with insect pressure, room conditions, unit location and the monitoring programme. Do not promise a fixed four-week interval for every site. In hotels and food businesses, record the date of installation, inspection findings, replacement date and any corrective action. Follow the equipment manual and the site's pest-management plan.

What else should you check?

  • Confirm the machine is specifically the Lumnia Compact.
  • Match the 304858 part number and 385 × 365 mm board size.
  • Check the quantity and whether the pack is manufacturer sealed.
  • Inspect the board for contamination or damage before installation.
  • Follow the unit's isolation and replacement instructions.
  • Keep used boards contained and dispose of them under the site's waste procedure.
