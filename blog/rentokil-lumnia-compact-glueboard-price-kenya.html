The Rentokil Lumnia Compact is a UV-LED insect monitoring device that attracts flying insects and captures them on a replaceable glueboard. The board is a consumable: it needs inspection and replacement as part of the site's pest-monitoring routine. It is not the same as a UV electric-grid zapper, and the glueboard should not be treated as a general-purpose sticky sheet for unrelated machines.

Genuine Rentokil board versus compatible replacement

A genuine board should be identifiable by the Rentokil product reference and the correct Lumnia Compact fit. Rentokil's manual lists 304858 for the black Compact glueboard. A compatible replacement may be made by another supplier and may fit a particular device, but it should not be described as a genuine Rentokil part unless the manufacturer identity and SKU are verified. Compatibility is not determined by colour alone.

Before ordering a compatible board, compare the board dimensions, outline, adhesive coverage, backing and the way it seats in the device. The Rentokil safety data sheet identifies the Lumnia Compact black board as 385 × 365 mm. Check the exact model: Rentokil uses different board sizes and part numbers for other Lumnia models. A board intended for Lumnia Mini, Slim, Standard or Ultimate is not automatically interchangeable with Compact.

When should a glueboard be replaced?

Inspect the board regularly as part of the monitoring programme. Replace it when the adhesive area is substantially covered with insects, dust or debris, when it becomes wet or damaged, or when the manufacturer's maintenance schedule requires it. In a busy hotel kitchen, loading bay or food store, checks may need to be more frequent than in a low-activity room. Record the inspection date and replacement date so changes in insect activity can be tracked over time.

Do not treat a calendar interval as universal. The Rentokil Compact manual describes the board as replaceable and identifies the correct spare part, but site activity and maintenance instructions determine when it needs changing. If your service contract specifies a fixed inspection cycle, follow that documented programme and the current manufacturer's guidance.
