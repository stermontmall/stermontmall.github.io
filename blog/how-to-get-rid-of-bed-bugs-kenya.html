How to get rid of bed bugs in Kenya: step by step

Updated 25 October 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: To get rid of bed bugs, remove clutter, hot-wash and dry bedding, vacuum seams and cracks, treat hiding places with a bed bug insecticide used exactly as the label says, and repeat the treatment, because eggs survive one round. For a large or repeated infestation, call a licensed operator.

Bed bugs are small insects that hide in beds and furniture and feed on blood at night. They are a problem in homes, hostels and hotels. They are hard to remove, but a careful routine works.

How do I know I have bed bugs?

Look for itchy bites in lines or clusters, small dark or rusty spots on sheets and mattresses, shed skins and a musty smell. Check mattress seams, the bed frame, headboard, nearby furniture and cracks in the wall.

Step 1: How do I clean and reduce hiding places?

Remove clutter around the bed. Wash bedding, curtains and clothes on a hot wash if the fabric allows, and dry them on high heat or in strong sun. Vacuum the mattress seams, frame and floor edges, then seal the dust and throw it away outside.

Step 2: How do I treat the hiding places?

Use an insecticide registered for bed bugs and apply it exactly as the label says, focusing on cracks, bed frame joints, skirting boards and furniture edges. Do not soak mattresses or spray bedding unless the label says it is safe to do so.

Step 3: Why must I repeat the treatment?

Eggs can survive and hatch later, so one treatment is rarely enough. Check the label for how long to wait before treating again, and keep checking for new bugs for several weeks.

Step 4: How do I protect my bed afterwards?

A mattress cover made for bed bugs traps any bugs inside and makes inspection easier. Pull the bed slightly away from walls, and avoid bringing second-hand furniture into the room without checking it.

Safety first. Read the whole label. Look for the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB) registration on the pack. Keep children, pets and food away during treatment, wear gloves and a mask as advised, and never mix products. For a large or repeated infestation, or in a hotel, hostel or rental, call a licensed pest control operator.

Frequently asked questions

What are the first signs of bed bugs?

Itchy bites in lines or clusters, small dark or rusty spots on sheets and mattresses, shed skins and a musty smell. Check mattress seams, the bed frame, headboard, nearby furniture and cracks in the wall.

Can I get rid of bed bugs with one treatment?

Rarely. Eggs can survive and hatch later, so check the product label for when to treat again and keep inspecting for several weeks.

Does washing clothes and bedding kill bed bugs?

A hot wash followed by drying on high heat or in strong sun helps remove bugs from fabric that allows it. It does not deal with bugs hiding in the bed frame, furniture or walls.
