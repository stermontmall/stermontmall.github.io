Single-feed rat bait: how Bayer Baraki pellets are used

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Bayer Baraki is a ready-to-use single-feed bait: rats and mice need to eat it only once. Place it in covered bait stations along walls, runs and burrows, check every 2 to 3 days and top up until feeding stops. Choose the 10 g, 100 g or 1 kg pack to match your number of stations.

A rodent problem rarely stays small. Rats gnaw wiring, foul food stores and spread disease, and a few quickly become many. Baraki is made for single-feed control, which means rats and mice need to eat the bait only once.

What does single-feed mean?

Baraki pellets contain difethialone, an anticoagulant, in a ready-to-use bait that needs no mixing. Single-feed means a rodent needs to eat the bait only once, so you are not relying on it returning to the same spot many times.

The pellets resist mould and moisture, which keeps them attractive in damp stores, and Baraki is sold as effective against resistant strains.

Where should the bait stations go?

Three species behave differently. Roof rats climb into ceilings and stores, Norway rats burrow along walls and drains, and house mice nest in small hidden spaces. Inspect for droppings, gnaw marks, runs and burrows first, so you know where they travel.

Put the bait in covered bait stations or tamper-resistant boxes, never loose on the floor. Baraki's placement amounts are:

  • Rats: 50 to 75 g per station, one station every 5 to 10 m along walls, rat runs, burrows and sheltered areas
  • Mice: 15 to 20 g per station, one station every 2 to 4 m along walls, runways and nests

Which pack size should I buy?

The 10 g sachet suits one room or a first trial. The 100 g pack, about ten sachets, suits a shop, a store or a home with several bait points. The 1 kg box, about a hundred sachets, suits farms, markets and industrial sites. Count your stations first, because the amounts above show how much bait each one holds.

Bait does more when it is paired with proofing. Block holes and gaps around pipes, doors and roof edges, keep grain and food in closed containers off the floor, and remove the rubbish and clutter that give rodents shelter.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I check the stations?

Every 2 to 3 days, topping up until feeding stops. Read the label on your pack as well.

Does Baraki need mixing?

No. The pellets are ready to use, so you simply place them in covered bait stations.

What do I do with dead rodents?

Wearing gloves, collect them and dispose of them safely, then wash your hands. Keep the bait away from children, pets and poultry.
