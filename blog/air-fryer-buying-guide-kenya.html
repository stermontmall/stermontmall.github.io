Air fryer buying guide for Kenya

Updated 29 October 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: buy 5 to 6 litres for a family of 3 to 5, or 3 to 4 litres for one or two people. Most use 1,400 to 1,800 watts, so check that your wiring and token budget can handle it.

An air fryer cooks chips, chicken and snacks with far less oil, and heats up quickly. Here is how to choose one that suits a Kenyan kitchen.

Watts and your KPLC bill

Most air fryers draw 1,400 to 1,800 watts. They cook quickly, so a 20-minute session uses roughly 0.5 kWh, which is a small cost on your prepaid KPLC tokens at typical home rates (check your own tariff). Do not plug an air fryer into the same extension as other heavy appliances like a kettle or an iron, and use a good-quality socket with a proper plug.

What to cook

Chips (fries), potato wedges, chicken wings and drumsticks, fish, sausages, samosas, spring rolls and roast vegetables all work well. You can also reheat leftover chapati, mandazi or pizza and bring back the crunch. Preheat for 3 to 5 minutes, do not overcrowd the basket, and shake it halfway through.

Features worth paying for

A digital display with presets and an accurate temperature control help you cook the same way every time. Look for a non-stick, dishwasher-safe basket that is easy to wash, an auto shut-off for safety, and a clear window if you want to watch the food. Some models double as mini ovens for baking.

Before you pay

Check the warranty, the wattage and the plug type, and look for a model with spare parts available. Keep your receipt and M-Pesa confirmation. We accept returns of faulty items within 48 hours, see our returns policy.

Frequently asked questions

What size air fryer should I buy in Kenya?

3 to 4 litres for 1 to 2 people, 5 to 6 litres for a family of 3 to 5, and 7 litres or more for big families.

Does an air fryer use a lot of electricity?

Most use 1,400 to 1,800 watts but cook fast, so a short session often costs less than running an oven. Check your own KPLC tariff.

What Kenyan foods can I cook in an air fryer?

Chips, chicken, fish, samosas, sausages and roast vegetables all work well. You can also reheat chapati and mandazi.
