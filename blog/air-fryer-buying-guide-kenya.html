Air fryer buying guide for Kenya: size, power and tips

Updated 25 October 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: Choose an air fryer by capacity first: about 3 to 4 litres for one or two people, 5 to 7 litres for a family of three to five, and 10 litres or more for big households. Then check the watts, the controls and whether the parts are easy to clean.

An air fryer cooks food with hot moving air, so you can get a crisp finish with much less oil. It heats up fast, which suits busy kitchens. Here is how to choose one.

1. What size air fryer should I choose?

Air fryer size is given in litres. As a rough guide, 3 to 4 litres suits one or two people, 5 to 7 litres suits a family of three to five, and 10 litres or more, including oven-style air fryers, suits big families or people who cook large batches. A bigger basket also lets food spread out, which helps it crisp evenly.

2. Should I pick a basket or an oven-style air fryer?

Basket models are compact and simple to use. Oven-style models have trays and sometimes a rotisserie, so you can cook more at once, but they take more counter space.

3. How many watts does an air fryer need?

Power is shown in watts. More watts usually means faster heating. Check the plug fits your socket and use a socket that can carry the load.

4. Which controls and features are worth having?

Digital controls with preset programmes are convenient, while dials are simple and tough. Useful extras include a timer, an adjustable temperature, a window to watch the food and dishwasher-safe parts.

5. What can I cook in an air fryer?

Chips, chicken pieces, samosas, fish, vegetables and reheating leftovers all work well. Cut food to similar sizes, shake or turn it halfway and do not overfill the basket.

6. How do I clean and look after an air fryer?

Let the fryer cool, then wash the basket and tray with warm soapy water and a soft sponge. Avoid harsh scouring pads that can scratch the coating.

Frequently asked questions

What size air fryer do I need for a family?

As a rough guide, 3 to 4 litres suits one or two people, 5 to 7 litres suits a family of three to five, and 10 litres or more suits big families or large batches. Choose a bit bigger if you often cook for guests.

Is an air fryer cheaper to run than an oven?

Air fryers heat up quickly and are small, so they often use less electricity than a full oven for small meals. For very large batches an oven can still make more sense. Check the watts on the product page.

Can I cook without oil in an air fryer?

Many foods cook with little or no added oil, and a light brush of oil helps some foods crisp. Follow the instruction booklet for your model and do not overfill the basket.

How do I clean an air fryer?

Unplug it and let it cool. Wash the basket and tray with warm soapy water and a soft sponge, or in the dishwasher if the manual says the parts are dishwasher-safe. Avoid harsh scouring pads.
