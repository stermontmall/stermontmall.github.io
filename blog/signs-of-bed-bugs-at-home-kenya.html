Signs of bed bugs at home in Kenya and where to check first

Updated 28 October 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: The clearest signs of bed bugs are small rusty or dark spots on bedding, tiny pale shed skins, and itchy bites in lines or clusters that appear after sleeping. Check mattress seams, the bed frame, headboard and nearby furniture first, because bed bugs hide close to where people sleep.

Bed bugs are small, flat insects that feed on human blood at night and hide in tight gaps during the day. Because they hide so well, most people notice the signs before they ever see a bug. If you wake up with itchy marks or find strange spots on your sheets, this guide shows what to look for, where to check first and what to do next.

What are the first signs of bed bugs in a home?

The most common early sign is a set of itchy red bites that appear after a night's sleep, often on the arms, neck, face or legs. The bites may be in a line or a small cluster. Bites alone cannot confirm bed bugs, because other insects cause similar marks and some people barely react to bed bug bites at all.

Look for physical evidence instead. Bed bugs leave small dark or rusty spots on sheets and mattresses, which are droppings or squashed bugs. You may also see tiny pale yellow shed skins, very small whitish eggs stuck in seams, and sometimes a sweet, musty smell in a heavily affected room.

An adult bed bug is about the size of an apple seed, flat, oval and reddish brown. Young bugs are smaller and paler, so look closely in good light.

  • Itchy bites in lines or clusters after sleeping
  • Rusty or dark spots on bedding and the mattress
  • Pale shed skins near seams and folds
  • Tiny white eggs in cracks and stitching
  • Live bugs in seams, tags or frame joints

Where should I check first?

Start with the bed, because bed bugs stay close to their food source. Strip the bedding and inspect the mattress seams, piping, tags and the underside. Then check the bed frame joints, slats, headboard and any gap where the headboard meets the wall.

If you find nothing, widen the search to bedside tables, the backs of picture frames, curtain folds, skirting boards, wall sockets, sofas and any luggage or bags kept near the bed. In a shared house, check every sleeping room, because bed bugs travel between rooms on clothes, bags and furniture.

How do I check a room step by step?

A careful check takes about 15 to 20 minutes. Use a bright torch and a stiff card, such as an old bank card, to run along seams and push into crevices.

  • Remove all bedding and put it in a bag for washing.
  • Inspect the mattress seams, tags and corners on all sides.
  • Lift the mattress and check the base, slats and frame joints.
  • Look behind the headboard and along the nearest wall edge.
  • Check bedside furniture, curtains and sofa seams.
  • Note where you found signs so you can check those spots again later.

How are bed bug bites different from other insect bites?

Mosquito bites tend to be single and appear on exposed skin. Flea bites are usually around the ankles and lower legs. Bed bug bites can appear on any skin left uncovered in bed and often show up in groups. Still, the pattern varies from person to person, so it cannot be used as proof.

Rely on finding bugs, skins or droppings, not on the marks on your skin. If you have a strong reaction, a lot of swelling or signs of infection from scratching, speak to a doctor or pharmacist.

What should I do once I find signs?

Do not move furniture or bedding into other rooms, because this spreads the problem. Wash bedding and clothes on a hot cycle if the fabric allows it and dry them well. Vacuum seams and cracks, then empty the vacuum into a sealed bag and throw it away outside.

Frequently asked questions

Can you have bed bugs without bites?

Yes. Some people do not react to bed bug bites, so you can have bugs in the room and not notice marks on your skin. Checking for spots, shed skins and live bugs in the mattress seams is more reliable than relying on bites alone.

Do bed bugs only live in beds?

No. They hide in beds most often, but they also live in sofas, bedside furniture, behind headboards, in wall cracks and in luggage. Any place close to where people sleep or rest for long periods can become a hiding place.

Are bed bugs a sign of a dirty house?

No. Bed bugs travel on clothes, bags and second-hand furniture, so they can appear in clean homes, hotels and hostels. Cleanliness does not cause them, although less clutter makes them easier to find and treat.

When should I call a professional?

Call a licensed operator if the signs are in several rooms, if you have treated and they keep returning, or if you live in a building with shared walls, such as a hostel or apartment block. A professional can inspect and treat the whole area.

How long do bed bug signs take to appear?

Signs can take a few weeks to show after the first bugs arrive, because the numbers start small. Bites may appear within days for some people, while others notice spots on bedding only after the population has grown.
