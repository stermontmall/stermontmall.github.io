Cockroach gel, spray or powder: which suits your home in Kenya?

Updated 28 October 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: Gel baits suit kitchens and cracks where you want to avoid spraying, sprays treat surfaces and hiding places where roaches walk, and powders suit dry, hidden gaps. Many homes use two types together, along with cleaning and sealing gaps. Always follow the product label.

In most Kenyan shops you will find cockroach products in three main forms: gels, sprays and powders. They are not interchangeable, and choosing the wrong form is one reason roaches seem to keep returning. This guide compares the three in plain words. For the wider picture, including cleaning routines, see our cockroach control guide.

What is the difference between a gel, a spray and a powder?

A gel bait is a small amount of attractive paste placed in cracks, hinges and corners where roaches travel. They eat it and carry it back toward the nest. A residual spray is applied to surfaces and hiding places such as skirting boards, drains and the backs of cupboards. A powder is a dry product applied thinly in dry, hidden gaps.

Each form has a job. Mixing them up, for example spraying right over a gel bait, may make the bait less effective, so read the label to see which products can be combined.

Which one is easiest to use in a kitchen?

Gel baits are popular for kitchens because they go in as small dots in cracks rather than being sprayed over a wide area. They can be placed away from food preparation surfaces and out of reach. Even so, cover food and keep children and pets away from treated spots, as the label directs.

Sprays cover more surface but need more care: remove or cover food, utensils and pet bowls first, and ventilate the room. Powders should be kept dry and placed only where children and pets cannot reach.

Which one works fastest?

Sprays often affect roaches that touch them quickly, and you may notice a difference within a day. They can also push roaches deeper into walls if you only spray what you can see. Gels act more slowly because roaches have to find and eat the bait, but they can reach hidden nests. Powders can last a long time while dry, but they become less effective when wet.

Speed is not the only goal. The aim is to reach the roaches you cannot see, so many households use a gel for nests and a spray for surfaces, as long as the label allows both.

Why do cockroaches keep coming back?

Products work better when you also remove what attracts roaches. Leftover food, dirty dishes overnight, open bins, leaking taps and piles of cardboard all help them survive, and they move in through gaps around pipes, drains and doors.

  • Wash dishes and wipe surfaces before bed
  • Keep bins covered and empty them daily
  • Fix leaks and dry the sink at night
  • Seal gaps around pipes and cupboards
  • Remove stacks of cardboard and paper

When should I call a licensed operator?

A large kitchen, a restaurant, a hostel or a building where neighbours share walls and drains usually needs a licensed operator, because roaches move between units. Ask a professional if you are unsure what is safe around children, pregnant women or pets.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better for a small kitchen: gel or spray?

Many people start with a gel bait in a small kitchen because it needs only small placements and little spraying. Add a surface spray only if the label allows it, and keep cleaning and sealing gaps as part of the plan.

Is cockroach powder safe around children and pets?

No pest control product should be treated as harmless. Powders should be applied only where children and pets cannot reach, kept dry and used exactly as the label directs. Ask a professional if you are unsure.

How long until I see fewer cockroaches?

Sprays may show results within a day, while gels often take several days as roaches feed and carry the bait back. Keep checking for a few weeks and repeat a treatment only as the label allows.

Can I use gel and spray together?

Sometimes, but not always. Spray can repel roaches away from a bait, so read the label and ask an adviser. A common approach is gel in cracks and nests, with spray only on surfaces away from the bait.

Why do I still see roaches after treatment?

Roaches hidden in walls, drains and appliances take time to come out and feed, and new ones may hatch. Keep the area clean, seal entry points and check again after a couple of weeks.

The right product form depends on where the roaches are hiding and how careful you need to be around food and children. Start with cleaning and sealing, pick the form that fits, and follow the label every time.
