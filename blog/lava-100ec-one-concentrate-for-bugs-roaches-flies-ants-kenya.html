Lava 100EC: one concentrate for bugs, roaches, flies and ants

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Lava 100EC is a public health insecticide concentrate sold for bedbugs, cockroaches, houseflies, ants, fleas and other crawling insects. Dilute it as the label directs, spray the places insects hide or walk, and treat a whole building within a short time.

Most buildings do not have just one pest. A hotel kitchen has cockroaches and flies, a bedroom has bedbugs, and the store has ants. A single multi-pest concentrate in a 100 ml pack keeps the buying simple.

Which pests is Lava 100EC sold for?

The product is sold for bedbugs, cockroaches, houseflies, ants, fleas and other crawling insects in homes, hotels and offices.

Each pest has a habit you can use: bedbugs hide in mattress seams, cockroaches in kitchens, drains and cupboards, houseflies around food and waste, ants along food trails, and fleas in bedding, carpets and pet areas.

A concentrate is also easy to store. Because only the amount you need is diluted at one time, the rest of the 100 ml pack stays sealed in its original bottle for the next treatment, which suits offices and small hotels that spray a little at a time.

Where do I spray in a home, hotel or office?

Dilute with water using the amount stated on the label, then focus on the places insects hide or walk.

  • Cracks, crevices and skirting boards
  • Bed frames and mattress seams
  • The backs of appliances and under sinks
  • Door and window frames
  • Bins and drain edges

Why should I treat the whole building at once?

Insects move from treated rooms to untreated ones. In a building with many rooms, plan the treatment so it is finished in a short time, and prioritise kitchens, stores, bathrooms, bedrooms, staff areas and drains.

Work from the cleanest areas towards the dirtiest, so you do not carry pests or residue from one room into another, and spray lightly, wetting the hiding places rather than soaking every surface.

Keep a short record of the dates and areas treated, and consider a licensed pest control operator for large or ongoing problems.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lava 100EC ready to spray?

No. It is a concentrate, so it must be diluted with water in the amount stated on the label before spraying.

What should I do with food and pets?

Cover or remove food, dishes and cooking surfaces, and cover or remove fish tanks and pet bowls. Keep people and pets out until the spray has dried.

Does hygiene still matter?

Yes. Clean first, seal cracks, store food in sealed containers and take out rubbish daily, so insects have less reason to stay.
