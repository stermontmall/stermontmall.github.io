Bedlam 200SL pack sizes: 15 ml, 50 ml or 100 ml?

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Pick the 15 ml pack for one bedroom or a single treatment, and move up to the 50 ml or 100 ml pack when you look after several rooms, a rental or a small block. Bedlam 200SL is sold for bed bugs, cockroaches and fleas, and every pack is mixed with water as its label directs.

Bedlam 200SL is a soluble liquid insecticide, so the pack you buy decides how many rooms you can treat without a second trip to order. Here is how to match the size to your home, and what to do before you spray.

Which Bedlam 200SL size suits a single room?

A tenant with one infested bed, one kitchen corner or a place where a pet sleeps usually needs only the 15 ml pack. It is a small, low-commitment way to start, and it is easy to store afterwards.

Treat every affected room in the same session. If the pests have already reached the next room, one small pack may not stretch far enough, so count your rooms before you choose.

When should I buy the 50 ml or 100 ml pack?

Choose a larger pack when you look after several rooms: a family home with bedrooms and a kitchen, a rented flat between tenants, or a small block where neighbours have the same problem. A bigger bottle also means you are not short if the label allows a repeat treatment.

The 100 ml pack suits landlords and caretakers who treat more than one unit, because the same bottle covers every unit you manage.

What should I do before I spray?

Preparation decides how well any spray works. Then mix exactly as the label says, wear gloves and a mask, and keep people, pets and food out until the spray has dried.

  • Wash bedding at a high temperature and dry it well
  • Vacuum mattresses, carpets and corners, and empty the vacuum outside
  • Move beds and furniture away from the walls
  • Cover food, dishes, fish tanks and pet bowls

Frequently asked questions

Does the pack size change how I mix it?

No. The dilution always comes from the manufacturer's label, whatever pack you buy. The size only decides how much product you have.

What do I do with leftover concentrate?

Keep it in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and children. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds.

Is Bedlam only for bed bugs?

No. It is sold for bed bugs, cockroaches and fleas in Kenyan homes. For fleas, treat your pets with a vet-approved product at the same time.
