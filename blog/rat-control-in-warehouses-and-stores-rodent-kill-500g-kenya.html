Rat control in a warehouse or store: planning with Rodent-Kill

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: In a large building, walk the whole site first and mark droppings and gnaw marks. Then place covered bait stations along walls, behind stacked goods, near holes and on rat runs. Rodent-Kill is a ready-to-use rat pellet bait, and its 500 g pack is made for premises that need many bait points.

A big building gives rats plenty of places to hide, breed and feed. Baiting one corner of a godown does little when the nest is behind the next stack, so the work starts with a plan rather than a pack.

Why does a large building need a plan?

Infestations in large buildings are brought under control by baiting many points and keeping them topped up as the rodents feed. The 500 g size gives you the quantity to do that without running short halfway, which suits property managers, farmers, shopkeepers and stores that treat regularly.

Grain stores, feed stores, godowns, hotels and poultry houses are the usual places. Pellets need no mixing, so a caretaker can fill stations quickly on a round of the building.

How do I lay out the bait stations?

Use covered bait stations so that only rodents can reach the bait. Wear gloves when you handle it, and never place bait on food surfaces or near food for people or livestock. Put stations in these places:

  • Along walls and behind stacked goods
  • Near holes and on rat runs
  • Wherever you marked droppings and gnaw marks on your first walk-round

What should I do besides baiting?

Baiting is strongest when it is paired with good sanitation and proofing. Inspect the stations often, replace bait that has been eaten and collect dead rodents carefully so that pets and wildlife do not feed on them.

  • Store grain, feed and produce on raised pallets away from walls
  • Seal holes and gaps, and keep drains covered
  • Remove rubbish and clutter
  • Cut grass and bush near the buildings

Frequently asked questions

Who is the 500 g pack for?

It is for warehouses, stores and farm buildings where many bait points are needed, such as grain stores, feed stores, godowns, hotels and poultry houses.

Where should the bait stations go?

Along walls, behind stacked goods, near holes and on rat runs, in covered stations that only rodents can reach.

How should I store the rest of the pack?

Reseal the pack after filling the stations and keep the rest in a locked, dry, cool store, apart from feed and food. Read the label and check the PCPB registration number before use.
