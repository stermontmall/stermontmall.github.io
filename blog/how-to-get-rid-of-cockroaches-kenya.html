How to get rid of cockroaches in Kenya: a home and kitchen guide

Updated 15 October 2026 · 4 min read

Cockroaches spread germs, contaminate food and can trigger allergies. They breed fast, so the best results come from cleaning, blocking their way in and treating the places they hide.

Where cockroaches hide

They like warm, dark and damp places: behind the fridge and cooker, under sinks, in cupboards, around drains and in cracks along walls. You may see them mostly at night.

Step 1: Remove food, water and shelter

Clean up crumbs and spills, store food in sealed containers, empty bins daily and do not leave dirty dishes overnight. Fix leaking taps and pipes, because cockroaches need water. Clear boxes and cardboard they can hide in.

Step 2: Seal the entry points

Close gaps around pipes, cracks in walls and gaps under doors where you can.

Step 3: Use a treatment as directed

Gels and baits are placed in small dots in cracks and hiding places, where cockroaches feed and carry the poison back to the nest. Sprays can treat edges, cracks and crevices. Choose a product registered for cockroaches and follow the label exactly.

Step 4: Keep going and check

It can take days or weeks to bring a population down. Check the label for when to repeat, and keep monitoring. Do not clean away gel baits or spray right after treatment.

Safety first. Read the whole label. Look for the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB) registration on the pack. Keep treatments away from food, children and pets, wear gloves, and never mix products. For a restaurant, hotel or heavy infestation, use a licensed pest control operator.
