K-Othrine WG 250: how a residual wall spray works

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: K-Othrine WG 250 is a water-dispersible granule containing deltamethrin at 250 grams per kilogram, sold as a 20 g sachet. It is a residual contact insecticide for walls and surfaces where insects rest, and the maker lists a residual life of 3 to 6 months on suitable surfaces.

Spraying the air does little once the insects have settled. A residual treatment works differently: it leaves a film on the surfaces where insects land, so the wall itself keeps working after you have finished. K-Othrine WG 250, from Envu (formerly Bayer), is made for that.

How does a residual treatment work?

After you spray and the water dries, a thin film of insecticide stays on the surface. When insects land on it or walk across it, they pick up the active ingredient. Mosquitoes that rest on walls after feeding, including malaria-carrying species, are exposed when they land on a treated wall.

Which insects does it control?

It is used against mosquitoes, sand flies, houseflies, fleas, bed bugs and cockroaches in homes, hostels, hotels and public buildings.

Where and how do you spray?

Mix the granules into water in a compression sprayer and spray the surfaces evenly. Treat the places where insects rest, such as inner walls, eaves, ceilings and door and window frames. The amount per sachet differs for porous surfaces such as mud or concrete block and for non-porous surfaces, so follow the instructions on your pack.

  • Spray evenly, with a steady pace and a little overlap between passes
  • Do not wash or repaint treated surfaces soon after spraying
  • Keep a record of the dates and areas treated

What safety steps matter most?

Do not spray people, pets, food or cooking surfaces. Keep children and animals out until surfaces are dry, and wear gloves and a mask. It is very toxic to aquatic life, so never rinse sprayers or sachets into drains, streams or ponds. Keep the sachet closed in a cool, dry, locked place away from food.

Frequently asked questions

What is the active ingredient in K-Othrine WG 250?

Deltamethrin at 250 grams per kilogram, in a water-dispersible granule. It is made by Envu, formerly Bayer.

How long does the treatment last?

The maker lists a residual life of 3 to 6 months on suitable surfaces. Do not wash or repaint treated surfaces soon after spraying.

Why choose granules?

The small sachet is easy to carry and store, and the granules mix into water more easily than wettable powders.
