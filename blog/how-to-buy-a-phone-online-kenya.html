How to buy a phone online in Kenya

Updated 29 October 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: check the IMEI by dialling *#06# and matching it to the box and receipt, confirm the warranty in writing, make sure the phone supports Kenyan 4G bands, and pay only to a registered business name by M-Pesa.

Buying a smartphone online in Kenya is easy and often cheaper, but fakes and grey imports are real. This checklist keeps you safe.

Specs that matter

For everyday use, aim for at least 4 GB RAM and 64 GB storage, though 6 to 8 GB RAM and 128 GB is more comfortable for years of apps. A battery of 5,000 mAh lasts a full day for most people, and fast charging helps during short power cuts. Make sure the phone is dual SIM if you use more than one line, and that it runs M-Pesa, the Safaricom app and your banking apps without problems.

When the phone arrives

Open the box in front of the rider if you can. Dial *#06# to see the IMEI and match it with the sticker on the box and your receipt. Check the screen, charging port, cameras, speaker and SIM slots. Check that the phone is sealed (if new) and that it comes with a charger, cable and warranty card. Do not accept a phone with a mismatched IMEI.

Pay safely with M-Pesa

Pay through the shop's Paybill or Till number shown on the checkout page, and confirm that the business name appears on the M-Pesa confirmation message. Be careful with sellers who ask you to send money to a personal number. Keep the M-Pesa message and your receipt until the warranty ends. Read how to order and pay by M-Pesa for the steps.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check a phone's IMEI in Kenya?

Dial *#06# on the phone and compare the IMEI with the number on the box and the receipt. They should all match.

Is it safe to buy a phone online in Kenya?

Yes, if the store has a clear returns policy, real contact details and a written warranty, and you pay by M-Pesa to a registered business name.

Should I buy a new or refurbished phone?

New phones come with a full warranty. Refurbished phones cost less but you should check battery health and ask for a written warranty.
