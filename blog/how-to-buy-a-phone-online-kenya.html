How to buy a phone online in Kenya: what to check

Updated 25 October 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: Before buying a phone online in Kenya, compare two or three phones in your budget, check storage (128 GB or more is comfortable), RAM, battery and network support, then confirm the IMEI by dialling *#06# when it arrives. Keep your receipt and report any fault within 48 hours.

Phones are one of the most searched items online, and also one of the easiest to get wrong. Use this checklist before you pay and when your phone arrives.

1. How do I set a budget and choose priorities?

Decide what matters most: battery life, camera, storage, screen or a famous brand. Compare two or three phones in the same price range rather than only the cheapest.

2. How much storage and RAM do I need?

64 GB fills up quickly with photos, videos and apps. 128 GB or more is more comfortable. For RAM, 4 GB works for basic use, and 6 to 8 GB or more feels smoother if you use many apps or play games.

3. What should I know about battery and charging?

Look at the battery size (mAh) and charging speed. A bigger battery helps through power cuts and long days away from a socket. Use the charger that comes in the box, or a good quality one.

4. Do more megapixels mean a better camera?

More megapixels does not always mean better photos. Look at sample photos and reviews if you can. For the screen, check the size and whether it is bright enough to read outside.

5. Does the phone support my network and dual SIM?

Check the phone supports 4G, or 5G if you want it, and has dual SIM if you use two lines. Check the phone is suitable for the networks you use.

6. How do I check the IMEI?

Every phone has an IMEI number. Dial *#06# on the phone to see it, then compare it with the number printed on the box and the phone. They should match. Keep a photo of the IMEI and of your receipt.

7. What should I do when the phone arrives?

Check the box is sealed and the phone is the model you ordered. Switch it on, test the screen, camera, speakers, charging and SIM slot, and confirm the specifications. If anything is wrong, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos. See our returns policy.
