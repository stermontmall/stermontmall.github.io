Navigator 100 SC: cockroaches, bed bugs and mosquitoes

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Navigator 100 SC is a public health insecticide with alpha-cypermethrin at 100 grams per litre. It controls cockroaches and bed bugs and works as a residual indoor spray against mosquitoes. The dilution rate differs by pest and surface, so use the rate on the product label.

Homes, hotels, hostels and schools often face more than one pest at a time. Navigator 100 SC comes in a 1 litre pack for repeat jobs, and a treated surface keeps working after it dries, so the result lasts beyond the day of spraying.

What is Navigator 100 SC?

It is a suspension concentrate (SC) containing alpha-cypermethrin, a pyrethroid that works on contact. It leaves a residual film, so insects that walk across or rest on treated surfaces are still affected. It is used for crack, crevice and surface treatments.

Where do you treat for each pest?

Cockroaches hide behind cookers and fridges, under sinks and along drains. Bed bugs live in mattress seams, bed frames and cracks near the bed. Mosquitoes rest on indoor walls after feeding, so for mosquitoes you treat the indoor resting surfaces as the label directs.

How do you prepare and apply it?

Read the label and measure the dilution for your target pest, because the rate differs by pest and surface. Do not guess it. Mix in clean water in a sprayer, shaking or stirring well, because this is a suspension concentrate. Keep people and pets out until the surfaces are dry, then ventilate the room.

  • Clean and declutter first, remove food, and cover utensils and fish tanks
  • Wash bedding and vacuum mattresses, floors and corners before treating bed bugs
  • Repeat treatment if the label advises it, because bed bugs often need a second visit

What safety steps matter most?

Check the PCPB registration number on the label and read the label before use. Keep the product locked out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask and protective clothing when mixing and spraying. Do not contaminate water sources, and do not pour leftover mixture into drains. For heavy infestations or large buildings, use a licensed pest control operator.
