How to buy agrovet products online in Kenya: a safe buyer's checklist

Updated 17 October 2026 · 4 min read

Agrovet products help farmers grow more and keep animals healthy, but a wrong or fake product can cost you a whole season. This checklist covers what to check before you order seeds, crop chemicals, fertilizers or animal supplies online, and what to do when the parcel arrives.

What counts as agrovet?

Agrovet shops usually sell certified seeds, crop protection products such as insecticides, fungicides and herbicides, fertilizers and foliar feeds, animal feeds and mineral supplements, animal health products, and farm hygiene products like disinfectants and milking salves. Each group has its own things to check.

1. Buy from a seller who shows clear product details

A good product page shows the exact product name, pack size and price, and a real photo of the pack. If a listing is vague about the size or the brand, ask before you pay. You can ask us on WhatsApp and we will confirm the details.

2. Check the label on crop chemicals

In Kenya, pesticides sold to the public should be registered with the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB). Look for a registration number on the label and compare the product name and active ingredient with what you ordered. If the pack has no label, a torn label or a label in a language you cannot follow, do not use it.

3. Check the expiry date and packaging

When your parcel arrives, check that the seal is intact, the pack is not leaking, and the batch number and expiry date are printed clearly. Seeds that are old or stored in heat germinate poorly, so check the packing date on seed packs. Report anything damaged to the seller on the day of delivery.

4. Choose the right pack size

Buy only what you can use within the product's shelf life. A small pack is often the better choice for a kitchen garden or a first trial, while a larger pack costs less per unit on a bigger farm. Many products come in several sizes, so compare the price per millilitre, gram or kilogram, not just the pack price.

5. Follow the label for use and safety

Always read the label before you open the pack. Use the dose, timing and waiting period (the days you must wait before harvest) given on the label, wear gloves, a mask and protective clothing when handling chemicals, and never mix products unless the label says you can. Do not reuse empty chemical containers for food or water.

6. Store products safely

Keep chemicals in their original containers, tightly closed, in a cool, dry, locked place away from food, animal feed, children and pets. Keep fertilizers and seeds dry. Store animal feeds off the floor to keep out moisture and rodents.

7. Ask an expert when the problem is not clear

If you are not sure what is attacking your crop or what is wrong with an animal, do not guess. Show photos or samples to your county agricultural extension officer or a veterinarian before you buy treatments. This is especially important for animal health products, where the right choice and dose depend on the animal and the illness.
