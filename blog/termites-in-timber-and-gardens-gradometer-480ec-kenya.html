Termites in timber and gardens: using Gradometer 480EC

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Gradometer 480EC is an emulsifiable concentrate termiticide with chlorpyrifos, used on timber in buildings, trees, fencing poles, wooden structures and termite nests in lawns and gardens. Dilute it with water exactly as the label directs, treat cut ends, joints and wood touching the soil, and wear full protective gear.

Mchwa (termites) feed on wood, paper and other plant material, and they can weaken roof timbers, door frames, fence posts and furniture before the damage is clearly visible. Early treatment limits the damage and the cost of repairs.

What can Gradometer 480EC treat?

Gradometer 480EC contains 480 grams of chlorpyrifos per litre and comes in a 1 litre bottle. It is used where termites attack:

  • Timber in buildings and construction
  • Trees and fencing poles
  • Wooden structures such as sheds, posts and frames
  • Termite nests in lawns and gardens

How should I prepare and apply it?

It is an emulsifiable concentrate, so it is diluted with water as directed on the label. Use the label dilution for timber and for nests, and never mix it stronger than recommended.

Treat timber thoroughly, paying attention to cut ends, joints and the places where wood touches the soil. For a nest, follow the label instructions for reaching it and treating the soil around it.

What safety steps matter most?

Check the PCPB registration number on the label, and mix and apply exactly as directed. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing, and keep away from children, pets, food and water.

Chlorpyrifos is harmful to fish and aquatic life, so keep it away from streams, ponds, wells and drains. For buildings, consider a licensed pest control operator.

Store the product in its original closed bottle, in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.

How do I stop termites coming back?

Treatment does not replace good housekeeping. Give termites fewer reasons to return:

  • Remove old stumps, dead wood and rotting timber from around buildings
  • Keep timber off the ground where possible, and keep it dry
  • Fix leaking pipes and drains that keep the soil damp
  • Inspect timber regularly for mud tubes, soft wood and flying termites

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it on a termite mound in my garden?

It is used for treating termite nests in lawns and gardens. Follow the label instructions for reaching the nest and treating the soil around it.

Is Gradometer 480EC ready to spray?

No. It is a concentrate, so you dilute it with water as directed on the label before use.

What are the signs of termites in timber?

Look for mud tubes, soft wood and flying termites, especially where timber touches the soil.
