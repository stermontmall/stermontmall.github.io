Bugnil 20EC for landlords: treating a rental between tenants

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Treat the unit in the gap between tenants: clear and clean it, dilute Bugnil 20EC as the label directs, spray every bed bug hiding place, then inspect again. The 25 ml pack is sized for a single treatment, so plan one pack for each unit.

A bed bug complaint after a tenant moves in costs a landlord goodwill and rent. The empty days between tenants are the ideal time to deal with the problem, because the room is clear and nobody needs to sleep there while the spray dries.

Why treat between tenants instead of after a complaint?

Bed bugs travel on luggage, clothes and second-hand furniture, so a previous tenant may leave a few behind without knowing. They can survive a long time without feeding, which means an empty room is not always a clean room.

Treating before the next tenant arrives stops a small problem from becoming a shared one, and it saves you an awkward call in the first week.

What is the routine for an empty unit?

Work in this order and keep the whole job in one visit if you can.

  • Remove or inspect any furniture left behind, especially second-hand items
  • Wash curtains and washable items at a high temperature
  • Vacuum the mattress, frame, floor and corners, and empty the vacuum outside
  • Dilute Bugnil exactly as the label directs and spray mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, skirting boards and cracks
  • Keep people, pets and food out until the room has dried, then ventilate it

How many packs do I need for several units?

The 25 ml pack is sized for a single treatment, such as one bedroom or bedsitter, so buy one for each unit you plan to treat. Keep a short record of the unit, the date and the product used.

For large buildings, hostels or repeated infestations, consider a licensed pest control operator as well.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bugnil 20EC suitable for rentals?

Yes. It is an emulsifiable concentrate insecticide intended for bed bug control in homes and rentals, and it is mixed with water as the label directs.

Why do bed bugs return after a treatment?

Eggs and hidden survivors can stay in cracks, frames and furniture. Repeat the treatment only if the label advises it, and check second-hand items before they enter the unit.

What should I tell new tenants?

Tell them the early signs: itchy bites, small blood spots on sheets and dark droppings near the bed. Early reports are much easier to treat than a long-running problem.
