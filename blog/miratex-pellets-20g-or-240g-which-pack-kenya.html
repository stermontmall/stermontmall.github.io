Miratex rat and mice bait: choosing the 20 g or 240 g pack

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Choose the 20 g pack for a single room, a small store or a first treatment, and the 240 g pack for warehouses, hotels, farms and other larger premises with many bait points. Miratex pellets are ready to use, so count your bait points first, then place them near droppings, along walls and near nesting holes.

Buying rat bait by pack size alone is a guess. A single room and a godown need very different quantities, so it helps to decide how many bait points you need before you choose between the two Miratex packs.

Which Miratex pack fits my premises?

The 20 g pack suits a single room, a kitchen, a small store or a first treatment, so you can start small and see what the activity tells you. The 240 g pack is made for warehouses, hotels, farms and other larger premises that need many bait points.

Larger buildings need bait in many places and need it topped up over time. Think of grain stores, feed stores, poultry houses, workshops and godowns.

How do I count the bait points I need?

Walk around first and look for droppings, gnaw marks, greasy rub marks along walls and burrows. Rodents usually move along walls and through dark, quiet corners, so bait close to those signs works better than bait in the middle of a room.

Count the points before you choose a size, because a pack that runs out halfway through a building leaves gaps. Wear gloves, and remove dead rodents safely. Store the pellets in their closed pack, somewhere dry and secure, away from food and out of reach of children.

What should I do before placing the pellets?

Pellets need no mixing, but they do more alongside good hygiene, because rodents contaminate food, chew wires and damage sacks and furniture wherever food is easy to find.

  • Keep grain and animal feed in sealed containers, on raised pallets in stores
  • Block holes and gaps, and cut tall grass close to buildings
  • Keep rubbish and old materials away from the walls

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to mix the pellets?

No. They are ready to use, so you place them directly at the bait points.

Where do rats usually travel?

Along walls, through dark corners and between their nest and their food. Bait close to droppings, gnaw marks and runways works better than bait in the middle of a room.

Where must I keep the bait away from?

Place it where only rodents can reach it, never near food for people or animals and never inside food containers. Keep it away from children, pets and livestock.
