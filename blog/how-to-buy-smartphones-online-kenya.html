1. Check Key Specifications (RAM, Storage & Battery)

Before purchasing your next phone, consider how you intend to use it:

  • Storage & RAM: For basic daily tasks, social media, and messaging, 4GB to 6GB of RAM with 64GB or 128GB of storage works smoothly. For heavy multitasking, gaming, and extensive photography, look for 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage or higher.
  • Battery Capacity: A 5,000mAh battery or larger is standard for dependable all-day use without constantly needing a charger. Fast charging support is also a great bonus.
  • Camera Quality: Check the multi-lens camera setup, resolution, and low-light capabilities if you enjoy capturing crisp photos and recording videos.
