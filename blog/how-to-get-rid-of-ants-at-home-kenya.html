How to get rid of ants at home in Kenya: a practical guide

Updated 28 October 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer: Follow the ant trail to find where they enter, remove the food and water that draw them in, and seal the gap. Then use a bait along the trail or a spray at entry points, as the label directs. Keep checking until the trail disappears.

Ants in the kitchen are one of the most common household complaints in Kenya, especially in warm weather and after rain. Spraying the ants you see rarely solves it, because the nest and the trail keep supplying new workers. This guide gives a practical order of steps. If you also have roaches, read our cockroach control guide.

How do I find where the ants are coming from?

Ants walk in lines between food and the nest. Watch the trail, ideally in the evening, and follow it back to the crack, window frame, pipe or door gap where it enters. Knowing the entry point lets you seal it and place a bait where it will be found.

Resist wiping the trail away straight away. Observe first, then clean, so you do not lose the route.

What attracts ants into my kitchen?

Sugar, crumbs, honey, fruit, pet food and wet sponges are all easy meals. Ants also find water around leaking taps and sinks. Remove these and the trail often fades without any product at all.

  • Store sugar, flour and snacks in sealed containers
  • Wipe counters and sweep floors every day
  • Wash pet bowls after feeding
  • Take out rubbish before it overflows
  • Fix dripping taps and dry the sink at night

Should I use bait or spray for ants?

Baits are often preferred for kitchen trails because workers carry the bait back to the nest, where it can affect the colony. Sprays treat what they touch and are most useful at entry points, along skirting boards and outdoors. Avoid spraying right over a bait trail, as it may stop ants taking the bait.

Whichever you choose, follow the label: where it may be used, what to cover first and how long to keep children and pets away. Never mix or dilute a product in a way the label does not describe.

How do I stop ants coming back?

Seal cracks and gaps around pipes, windows and doors, fix leaks, and keep food covered. Trim plants and branches that touch the walls, because they give ants a bridge into the house. Keep the area around the home clear of piles of wood and rubbish.

After treatment, keep a close eye on the area for a couple of weeks. New trails mean there is still a way in or a source of food.

When do I need a professional?

Call a licensed operator if ants are nesting inside walls or roofs, if you see damage to timber, or if large outdoor nests keep appearing near the building. A professional can identify the type of ant and recommend a safe approach.

Frequently asked questions

Why do ants suddenly appear after rain?

Wet weather can flood nests and push ants indoors to look for dry shelter and food. Seal entry points, keep food covered and clear damp areas around doors and windows so there is less reason for them to stay.

Does wiping the trail stop ants?

Wiping removes the scent trail and may confuse them for a while, but they usually find a new route. It works best together with sealing the entry point and removing the food that attracted them.

Is ant killer safe to use in the kitchen?

Use only products whose label allows kitchen use, and follow the directions exactly. Keep products away from food, preparation surfaces, children and pets. If you are unsure, ask a pest control professional.

How long does it take to get rid of ants?

Surface ants may disappear within days once food and entry points are dealt with, but nests can take longer to be affected. Keep checking for a couple of weeks and repeat treatment only as the label allows.

Can ants damage my house?

Most household ants are a nuisance and a hygiene concern rather than a structural threat, but some species nest in timber or walls. If you see damaged wood or ants coming from inside walls, ask a licensed operator.

Ants follow food and water, so the most lasting fix is to take both away and close the way in. Use bait or spray carefully as a second step, and ask a professional if the nest is inside the building.
