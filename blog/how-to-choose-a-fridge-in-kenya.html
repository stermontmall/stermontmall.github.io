How to choose a fridge in Kenya

Updated 29 October 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: 100 to 150 litres for one or two people, 200 to 300 litres for a family, and always plug it in through a voltage guard. Choose direct-cool to save on your KPLC tokens, or frost-free if you want less hassle.

A fridge runs 24 hours a day, so the right choice affects your food bills and your electricity bills for years. In Kenya you also need to think about blackouts and voltage swings, which are the top reasons compressors fail.

Direct-cool or frost-free

Direct-cool fridges cost less up front and usually use less power, but you must defrost them by hand when ice builds up. Frost-free fridges defrost themselves and keep an even temperature, but cost more. If you buy in bulk or store a lot of meat and milk, frost-free is worth it.

Built for Kenyan power

Power cuts and sudden surges when electricity returns are hard on compressors. Look for an inverter compressor if your budget allows, since it handles voltage changes better and uses less energy. Whatever you buy, plug it in through a voltage guard or stabiliser, and avoid opening the door during a blackout, because a closed fridge stays cold for several hours. Keep a few inches of space behind and beside it so heat can escape.

Check these before you pay

Read the energy rating, look at the compressor warranty (often longer than the product warranty), and confirm the fridge fits through your door and in the space you have. Ask whether a local service centre can repair it. Keep your receipt and M-Pesa confirmation, and inspect the fridge when it arrives. Frequently asked questions

What size fridge do I need in Kenya?

100 to 150 litres for one or two people, 200 to 300 litres for a family of 3 to 5, and 350 litres and above for large households or small shops.

Direct-cool or frost-free fridge?

Direct-cool costs less and uses less power but needs manual defrosting. Frost-free defrosts itself and cools more evenly but costs more.

Will a fridge survive power cuts?

A closed fridge keeps food cold for a few hours. Use a voltage guard to protect the compressor when power returns.
