Actellic 25EC: preparing a store before harvest

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Actellic 25EC is an emulsifiable concentrate insecticide containing pirimiphos-methyl, sold in a 1 litre bottle for pest control in stores and premises. It is diluted with water as the label directs and sprayed on the surfaces of empty stores and warehouses before stock goes in.

Insects in a store do their damage quietly, and by the time you notice them, stored produce can already be spoiled. Treating the building before the harvest arrives is one of the simplest ways to protect it.

What is Actellic 25EC used for?

It is used for pest control in stores and premises, such as empty grain stores and warehouses before stock goes in. It suits farmers preparing a store before harvest, traders and aggregators who hold produce, estate managers and institutions that keep food and feed.

Pest control operators who want a larger refill pack can also use the 1 litre bottle.

How do I prepare the store?

Cleaning comes before spraying. Residues and gaps give insects somewhere to survive between seasons:

  • Sweep out old grain and dust
  • Repair cracks and holes where insects hide
  • Remove spilled produce before spraying
  • Clean sacks, pallets and containers, because old residues carry pests from one season to the next
  • Use a sprayer that is kept for pesticides only

How is it applied?

As an emulsifiable concentrate, it is diluted with water as directed on the label and applied as a spray. Follow the label for the correct dilution, the surfaces to treat and any waiting period before food, feed or people return to the area.

Allow surfaces to dry as the label directs before storing grain or other produce. Stack new stock off the floor and away from the walls so that air can move and you can inspect it easily.

Is Actellic 25EC the same as Actellic 50EC?

No. They are separate products with their own labels and dilution rates, so never use the rate for one on the other.

Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and keep the product away from children, pets, food and water. Never reuse the empty bottle.

Frequently asked questions

How do I mix it?

Dilute it with water exactly as the label directs. Do not guess the rate.

Where should I keep the bottle?

Keep it in the original, tightly closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food, feed and direct sunlight.

Can I spray it near streams or ponds?

No. Do not contaminate food, feed, streams or ponds when you mix or spray.
