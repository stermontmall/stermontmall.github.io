Bromarat 0.005 RB: setting up bait stations for rats and mice

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Bromarat 0.005 RB is a ready-to-use rodent bait made for bait stations. Fill the stations, place them along walls, near holes and on runs where you see droppings, check them regularly and keep baiting until no more is taken. Choose 25 g for a home or small store, 100 g for larger premises.

Loose bait on the floor is easy to scatter and easy for a child or a pet to find. A bait station solves that, and Bromarat 0.005 RB is sold for use inside one. There is nothing to mix, so the real work is in the placing.

Why use a bait station at all?

A bait station is a small covered box or tube that holds the bait. It keeps the bait dry and clean, makes it harder for children, pets and livestock to reach it, and gives rodents a dark, sheltered place where they feel safe to feed.

Stations also show you how much has been eaten, so you learn where the rodents are most active.

Where do the stations go?

Walk around the building first and look for droppings, gnaw marks and runs. Then place stations along walls, near holes and burrows, behind cupboards and stacked goods, and on the runs themselves. A home or small shop usually needs a few. A store or farm needs more, spaced as the label advises.

Prepare the site at the same time:

  • Keep food and grain in closed containers and stack bags away from the walls
  • Block holes around pipes and doors
  • Clear rubbish and long grass near the building

How long should I keep baiting?

Check the stations regularly, top up bait that has been eaten and replace bait that is wet or mouldy. Carry on until no more bait is taken, then remove the stations and the leftover bait as the label directs.

Keep the stations and the pack away from food, feed and drinking water, wear gloves, and wash your hands afterwards. Check the PCPB registration number on the label, and store the pack tightly closed in a cool, dry, locked place out of reach of children and pets.

Frequently asked questions

Which Bromarat pack suits my building?

The 25 g pack suits a home or a small shop with a few stations. The 100 g pack suits stores, farms and larger premises with many.

Does Bromarat need mixing?

No. It is ready to use, so you fill the stations and place them where the rodents travel.

What if an animal eats the bait?

If an animal eats it, call a vet. If anyone swallows the bait, get medical help at once and show the label.
