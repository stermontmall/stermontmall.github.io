Air Chopper 7.5SC: which pack size should you buy?

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Air Chopper 7.5SC is a suspension concentrate insecticide used against bed bugs, cockroaches and ants in homes, hostels and small premises. It comes in a pack of three 15 ml units, 100 ml, 250 ml and 1 litre. Dilute it in water exactly as the label directs.

Bed bugs bite at night, cockroaches work the kitchen after dark and ants follow any food left out. One product that treats all three saves you buying a different spray for each. Air Chopper 7.5SC is sold in four sizes, so the first decision is how much you need.

What does Air Chopper 7.5SC treat?

It is a suspension concentrate (SC) insecticide for bed bugs, cockroaches and ants. These pests thrive where there are cracks, crevices and warm, dark hiding places. Bed bugs shelter in mattress seams, bed frames and wall joints. Cockroaches settle in kitchens, drains and behind cupboards. Ants follow food and trails into rooms and stores.

Which pack size suits which job?

The range lets you match the size to the job: a smaller pack for a single home, a larger bottle for a hostel block. The pack of three 15 ml units is the smallest and the 1 litre bottle is the largest, with 100 ml and 250 ml in between.

How far each pack goes depends on the dilution printed on the label, so check it before you buy for a large space.

How do you use it?

Read the label first and prepare the dilution exactly as it directs. Spray cracks, crevices, bed frames, skirting boards and the other places where the pests hide or walk. Keep people, pets and food away from treated areas until the label says it is safe to return, and repeat the treatment if the label advises it. Before you start:

  • Wash bedding and vacuum mattresses, floors and corners
  • Remove food, and cover utensils and fish tanks
  • Treat all the affected rooms in the same visit so the pests do not move next door

What safety steps matter?

Wear gloves and a mask while mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Keep the product away from children, pets, food and water. Never pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle. For a large or persistent infestation, call a licensed pest control operator. Store the bottle closed in a cool, dry, locked place.

Frequently asked questions

Which pests is Air Chopper 7.5SC used against?

Bed bugs, cockroaches and ants in homes, hostels and small premises.

How many pack sizes are there?

Four: a pack of three 15 ml units, 100 ml, 250 ml and 1 litre.

How much should I mix?

Follow the dilution and spraying instructions on the label of your pack. The rate is not worked out from the pack size.
