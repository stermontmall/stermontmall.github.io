PCPB Registered Pesticides in Kenya: A Buyer's Guide

Updated 17 November 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer: PCPB registration means a product has been tested and approved by the Pest Control Products Board in Kenya. Buying registered pesticides ensures the product meets safety and quality standards, protecting your health and the environment. Always check the label for the official PCPB registration number before purchasing.

When you are shopping for pest control solutions in Kenya, you might notice that some products have official registration details while others do not. As a shopper at Stermont Arcade, it is important to understand that these details are not just random numbers. They represent the work of the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB) Kenya, the body responsible for regulating the sale and use of these chemicals.

What is the Pest Control Products Board Kenya?

The Pest Control Products Board is a government agency that oversees the industry. Their main job is to make sure that any chemical sold for killing pests is safe when used exactly as instructed on the label. They review the science behind each product to see how it affects people, pets, and the soil.

When you shop in our Pest Control & Fumigation category, you are looking at items that have undergone this rigorous process. By only dealing with products that have cleared these checks, you are taking a responsible step toward keeping your home or place of work safe.

Why is a registered pesticide safer for my home?

A registered pesticide comes with a label that provides critical instructions. This label is not just a suggestion; it is a legal requirement that tells you how to use the product safely. It includes information on how to handle the chemical, what protective gear you might need, and how to store it away from children or food.

Unregistered products often lack these clear instructions or might contain ingredients that have not been vetted for local conditions. Using unknown substances can lead to health risks or damage to your property. Always prioritize your safety by confirming the product has a valid registration number printed on the package.

How do I tell if a product is officially registered?

The easiest way to check is to look at the product container. A legitimate, registered pesticide will always have a clear label displaying the PCPB registration number. This number is your proof that the product has been through the regulatory process.

If you are ever in doubt while browsing, you can look for the official board's stamp or reference on the packaging. Never assume a product is safe just because it is sold in a shop. Take a moment to read the label carefully. If the information seems missing, incomplete, or is written in a language you cannot understand, it is better to choose a different, verified product.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if I cannot find a registration number?

If you cannot find a registration number on the packaging, do not use the product. It is safer to choose an alternative that clearly displays the PCPB registration details. Using unknown chemicals can pose unnecessary risks to your family and environment.

Does registration mean the product is safe to use anywhere?

No. Registration means the product has been approved for specific uses when handled according to the label. You must always read the label to see if it is meant for indoor use, outdoor use, or specific types of pests. Always follow the label instructions exactly.

Should I mix different pesticides to make them more effective?

You should never mix pesticides unless the labels specifically state that it is safe and provides instructions on how to do so. Mixing chemicals can be dangerous and may create harmful reactions. Always consult with a qualified professional or extension officer if you have questions.
