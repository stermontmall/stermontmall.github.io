Odourless bed bug spray in Kenya: what Promax 20 EC means

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Odourless means the spray has little smell, which suits bedrooms. It does not mean harmless. Promax 20 EC is a propoxur-based insecticide concentrate, so follow the label for mixing, keep people and pets out until surfaces are dry, and inspect again after about two weeks.

Many Kenyan households avoid sprays because of the smell, especially in bedrooms. An odourless formulation removes that problem, but it is still a pesticide. Here is how to use Promax 20 EC sensibly.

What does odourless actually mean?

It means the product is formulated so that the spray does not leave a strong smell in the room. It does not change how the product works or how carefully you must handle it. Promax 20 EC contains propoxur, a carbamate insecticide, so the same protective steps apply as for any concentrate.

If someone in the home is sensitive to strong smells, an odourless formulation is easier to live with, but it should never be a reason to skip the label, the gloves or the drying time.

Which pests and places is Promax for?

It is sold for bed bugs, called kunguni, and also for cockroaches, fleas, ants and houseflies. It leaves a residual film on treated surfaces, so pests that crawl across later are still affected.

Spray mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, skirting boards, cracks, the backs of sockets and furniture joints. Wet the surface lightly and do not soak it.

Bedrooms, hostel rooms, hotel rooms and offices are the usual places, because bed bugs and cockroaches settle wherever people sleep, eat and leave food.

What safety steps should I keep?

Keep children, pets and fish tanks out of the room until the surfaces are dry and the room is ventilated. Wear gloves and wash your hands afterwards. Do not spray food, utensils or bedding you will use before it dries.

  • Read the label and confirm the mixing rate on your pack
  • Strip the bed, wash bedding at a high temperature and vacuum first
  • Store leftover concentrate sealed, cool, dry and locked away

Frequently asked questions

Can I sleep in the room straight after spraying?

Not until the surfaces are completely dry and the room is ventilated. Follow any waiting time printed on the label.

Does one treatment remove bed bugs?

Eggs can hatch later, so inspect after about two weeks and treat again if you still see bugs.

Which sprayer should I use?

It works with any hand-pump or knapsack sprayer. Choose the size that matches the area, and always confirm the rate on your pack.
