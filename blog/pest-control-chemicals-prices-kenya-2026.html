About dilution rates and pack size

This guide deliberately does not publish dilution rates. A rate is not determined by pack size alone: it depends on the exact formulation, target pest, treatment site, application method and current PCPB-approved label. A 100 ml bottle of one formulation cannot be dosed using instructions for another 100 ml product. Read the label on the actual container, or ask a qualified professional to confirm the approved use.

How to compare total cost

Compare the price per labelled pack, the amount required for the job according to the label, delivery cost and any professional application fee. For rodent control, include secure bait stations and inspection labour where required. For bed bugs, budget for preparation, monitoring and follow-up rather than the spray bottle alone. For termites, inspection and correct treatment planning are often more important than choosing the lowest-priced container.

Frequently asked questions

Are the prices on this page guaranteed?

No. They are the active Stermont catalogue prices checked on 17 November 2026. Prices and stock can change; confirm the live product page before payment.

Why are dilution rates not shown?

Dilution depends on the exact registered product, formulation, target pest, site and label revision. The approved Kenyan label must be followed; never transfer a rate between brands or concentrations.

Are all listed products PCPB registered for every use shown in their product names?

No. A product name or category does not prove approval for a particular site or pest. Check the exact registration number and current label in the PCPB register.

Can I get help choosing a product?

Yes. Share the target pest, treatment site, property type and town so the team can help identify the correct product listing and whether a professional service is more appropriate.
