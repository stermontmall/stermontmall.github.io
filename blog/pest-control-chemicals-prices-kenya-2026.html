Why this guide does not publish generic dilution rates

A dilution rate depends on the product's exact formulation, concentration, target pest, treatment site and label version. The same active ingredient can be sold in different concentrations and formulations. A rate from one product must not be transferred to another. Before use, check the current label for approved sites, target pests, dilution, application volume, personal protective equipment, re-entry, food and water precautions, storage and disposal. If the label is missing or unclear, do not guess; ask the supplier or a qualified operator.

How to use this price guide

Prices are a dated snapshot of the active Stermont Arcade catalogue, not a guarantee that every item will remain in stock at the same price. The linked product pages are the source to check before checkout. Confirm the pack size, product identity, delivery destination and fee before paying.
