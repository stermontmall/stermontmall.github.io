Dudu-Stop 25EC in hostels and hotels: a room-by-room plan

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Treat every affected room on the same day, spray cracks, bed frames and corners after diluting Dudu-Stop 25EC as the label directs, and keep a record of rooms and dates. The 100 ml pack gives enough product to handle several rooms.

In a hostel or hotel, one room with bedbugs becomes five rooms very quickly. A planned routine works better than spraying whichever room complains first, and Dudu-Stop 25EC is sold for bedbugs and cockroaches in homes, hostels and hotels.

Why do infestations spread between rooms?

Bedbugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards and wall cracks, and they move along pipes, walls and luggage. Cockroaches travel through kitchens, bathrooms and drains. If you treat one room and ignore its neighbours, the survivors simply move next door.

How do I run a room-by-room treatment?

Plan the work so affected rooms are treated close together, ideally in one day.

  • Strip the beds and wash bedding, curtains and clothing at a high temperature
  • Vacuum mattresses, floors and corners, and empty the vacuum outside
  • Move furniture away from the walls so you can reach the hiding places
  • Cover or remove food, dishes, fish tanks and pet bowls
  • Dilute the concentrate as the label directs and spray cracks, bed frames, mattress seams, skirting boards and door and window frames
  • Keep people, pets and food out of the room until it has dried

What records and follow-up should I keep?

Write down each room, the date and the product used, and inspect beds regularly. Act at the first sign of bites or droppings. Repeat the treatment if the label advises it.

For large or repeated infestations, consider a licensed pest control operator alongside your own routine.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 100 ml pack enough for several rooms?

It is intended to give enough product for several rooms, but the number depends on room size and the dilution on the label. Count your rooms before you order.

Can guests stay in a room soon after spraying?

Not until the spray has dried completely and the room has been ventilated. Follow any waiting time printed on the label.

What about cockroaches in the kitchen?

Dudu-Stop is also sold for cockroaches. Spray cracks and corners behind cupboards, but cover or remove food, dishes and cooking surfaces first.
