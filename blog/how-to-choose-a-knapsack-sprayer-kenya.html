How to choose a knapsack sprayer for your farm in Kenya

Updated 1 November 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: Choose by matching listed tank capacity, operating type, weight and maintenance needs to the job. Check the liquid product's label: not every product is approved for every sprayer or application method.

A knapsack sprayer is carried on the back and is commonly used to apply liquids over crops, gardens and other areas. The right choice is not simply the largest tank or the cheapest model. It should be comfortable to carry when filled, suitable for the task and practical to clean and maintain.

Manual, battery or motorised?

Manual pump sprayers do not depend on a battery or fuel. The operator supplies the pumping action, so consider the effort involved and whether you can maintain steady pressure during a long job.

Battery-powered sprayers reduce repeated manual pumping but introduce charging and battery-care requirements. Confirm battery type, charging method, operating time and replacement availability for the exact model. Do not assume these details from a product photograph.

Motorised sprayers may suit particular larger or specialist jobs, but can be heavier and have more maintenance requirements. Confirm actual motor specifications, fuel requirements and service support with the manufacturer or seller.

Choose tank capacity around the work

A larger tank can reduce refills, but a full tank adds weight to the machine you carry. Think about the area to be treated, water access, walking distance, terrain and the person who will operate it. Compare the capacity printed in the listing with the manufacturer's manual; do not rely on a generic photo or similar model name.

For a small garden or spot treatment, ease of handling may matter more than maximum capacity. For repeated work across a larger area, refill time and carrying comfort become more important. If possible, try the empty unit on your back and check how the straps sit before filling it.

Check comfort and construction

  • Harness: look for adjustable straps and back support that fits the operator.
  • Tank: check the material, cap, seals and whether the tank is easy to inspect and clean.
  • Hose and lance: check that connections are secure and the lance is comfortable to hold.
  • Nozzles: ask which types are supplied and whether compatible replacements are available.
  • Spare parts: confirm access to seals, filters, hoses, nozzles and service parts before buying.

Match the sprayer to the product label

Do not assume every pesticide, herbicide, disinfectant or foliar product can be used in every sprayer. Read the product label and confirm that it permits the equipment and application method. Follow the stated protective equipment, mixing instructions, weather restrictions, re-entry precautions and disposal directions.

Never estimate a dilution rate, combine products because they appear similar, or use a fogging-only product in a hand sprayer. If the label is unclear, ask the manufacturer, supplier or a qualified agricultural professional before use.

Cleaning and storage matter

Follow the sprayer manual and product label for cleaning. Use the specified method and dispose of rinsate as directed; never pour leftover chemical into drains, rivers, wells or fish ponds. Keep the sprayer identified and separate from containers used for drinking water or food. Store it securely as the manual instructs, away from children, animals, food and feed.

Frequently asked questions

What size knapsack sprayer should I buy?

Choose by the area you treat, refill frequency and the weight you can comfortably carry. Compare the actual tank capacity on the listing; there is no single size for every farm.

Is a battery sprayer better than a manual sprayer?

It depends on workload, access to charging and maintenance. Compare operating type, weight, battery or pump details and spare-part availability.

Can I use any pesticide in a knapsack sprayer?

No. Use a product only if its label permits the equipment and application method. Follow the label and never guess dilution rates or mix products.
