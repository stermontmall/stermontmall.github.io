Soil treatment for termites: Mchwatox 350 SC explained

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Mchwatox 350 SC is a suspension concentrate termiticide containing imidacloprid at 350 grams per litre, sold in a 1 litre bottle. It is used for soil treatment against termites, before a building goes up or around one that already stands. Dilute it as the label directs and wear protective gear.

Termites work out of sight. They tunnel through the soil, reach a building at the foundation and can weaken doors, frames and roof timbers before anyone sees a sign. Treating the soil is one way to put a barrier between the colony and the structure.

What is Mchwatox 350 SC used for?

It is a termiticide for soil treatment in buildings and construction. It is used in two situations:

  • Pre-construction: before the slab or foundation is laid
  • Post-construction: on a building that already stands, around foundations, trenches and the perimeter, as the label directs

How is it applied?

Mchwatox 350 SC is a suspension concentrate (SC), so it is diluted with water exactly as the label directs. Apply the diluted liquid thoroughly and evenly to the soil, using the dilution and the volume of spray liquid printed on the label.

Start with an inspection to find where termites enter and where they nest. That tells you where the treatment matters most.

What should I do before treating the soil?

Give termites fewer reasons to stay near the building:

  • Fix leaking pipes and damp areas that keep the soil wet
  • Remove old tree stumps and buried timber near the building
  • Keep timber and firewood out of direct contact with the soil

What safety steps matter most?

Check the PCPB registration number on the label, and mix and apply exactly as directed. Wear gloves, a mask, overalls and boots, and avoid contact with skin and eyes. Keep the product away from children, pets, food, water sources and fish ponds.

Do not contaminate wells, boreholes or drains when you mix, spray or wash equipment. For a large building, a licensed pest control operator is a sensible choice. Keep the bottle closed, in a cool, dry, locked place.

Frequently asked questions

What is the active ingredient in Mchwatox 350 SC?

Imidacloprid at 350 grams per litre, formulated as a suspension concentrate. The exact strength and registration details are printed on the label.

Can I use it on a building that is already finished?

Yes. It is used for post-construction treatment of existing buildings, applied around the foundations, trenches and perimeter as the label describes.

Should I hire a professional to apply it?

For a large building, a licensed pest control operator is a sensible choice. Whoever applies it must follow the label for dilution, spray volume and safety.
