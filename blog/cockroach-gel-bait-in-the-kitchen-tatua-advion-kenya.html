Cockroach gel bait in your kitchen: how Tatua Advion works

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Clean first, then place small dots of Tatua Advion gel in the cracks, crevices and corners where cockroaches hide. Do not spray insecticide on top of the bait, and check after a few days to re-apply where it has been eaten or has dried out.

A gel bait looks very different from a spray, and it works differently too. Tatua Advion is a ready-to-use cockroach gel in a 30 g applicator tube, suited to homes, restaurants, hotels and offices where sprays are awkward.

How does a gel bait differ from a spray?

Cockroaches feed on the gel and return to their hiding places, which helps reach insects that never come out into the open. Because you use only small dots, a gel is neat and precise, and it suits kitchens and food areas.

That is why a gel is popular in restaurants, hotels and offices, where a strong spray smell or wet surfaces would disrupt service. It is also easier to place around hinges and behind equipment, where a sprayer nozzle cannot reach.

Where do I place the gel?

Clean the area first and remove food and crumbs, so the bait is the most attractive thing on offer. Then apply small dots, not long lines.

Look for the signs first: dark droppings in corners, a musty smell behind cupboards and insects that appear when you switch on the light at night. Place gel closest to those areas.

  • Near cupboard hinges and along cupboard edges
  • Behind cookers and other appliances
  • Under sinks and around drains
  • Where you see droppings or cockroach activity

What mistakes should I avoid?

Do not spray insecticide on top of the bait, because it can put cockroaches off. Do not leave food out, since it competes with the gel. Check after a few days and re-apply where the bait has been eaten or has dried out.

Place gel only where children and pets cannot reach, and wash your hands afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Which pack should I choose?

The 30 g tube suits a home kitchen or a small shop. The 4 x 30 g pack suits restaurants, hotels and offices with many areas to treat.

How soon will I see fewer cockroaches?

It depends on the size of the problem and the sources of food, so check regularly, re-apply where needed and keep up good hygiene.

What else should I do?

Seal cracks, fix leaks, store food in closed containers, empty bins daily and wash dishes before night.
