Best Bed Bug Treatment in Nairobi: Spray, Steam or Professional Service?

Updated 17 November 2026 · Stermont Arcade buying guide

Quick answer: The best bed bug treatment depends on the size of the infestation, room construction, previous treatment and the people using the space. Cleaning, heat or steam, approved residual products, monitoring and professional follow-up often work best as a coordinated plan rather than a single spray.

Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, furniture joints, skirting boards and cracks near sleeping areas. In Nairobi, they can affect apartments, hostels, hotels, furnished rentals and family homes. Bites alone do not confirm bed bugs; inspect for live insects, shed skins, eggs and dark spotting before choosing a treatment.

Option 1: DIY residual sprays

A residual insecticide may be useful on surfaces and harbourage sites listed on its label. The label must specifically cover bed bugs and the intended indoor application. Pack size, active ingredient and formulation vary, so never copy a dilution rate from a different product. Do not spray mattresses, bedding, clothing, children's items or food-contact surfaces unless the exact label expressly permits it.

Examples currently listed in Stermont Arcade's Bed Bug Killers category include Bedlam 200SL 15 ml at KSh 250, Bedlam 200SL 50 ml at KSh 350, Bedlam 200SL 100 ml at KSh 1,099, Bugnil 20EC 25 ml at KSh 750 and PROMAX Bedbug Spray 100 ml at KSh 1,150. These are catalogue listings, not a statement that every product is suitable for every surface or property. Check current PCPB registration and the supplied pack label.

Option 2: Steam and heat treatment

Steam can kill bed bugs and eggs that receive sufficient direct heat. A trained operator moves slowly over suitable seams, joints and fabric surfaces, taking care not to damage materials or drive insects into inaccessible areas. Steam does not leave a chemical residual and cannot reach every concealed harbourage point. A steam pass is therefore one part of a plan, not proof that an entire room or building is clear.

Stermont lists the Polti Cimex Eradicator Professional Bed Bug Steam Cleaner at KSh 65,000. This is specialist equipment, and its correct operation depends on the manufacturer's instructions, suitable surfaces and trained handling.

Option 3: Professional two-stage treatment and follow-up

A professional programme should begin with an inspection and a room-by-room treatment plan. The first visit may combine preparation advice, vacuuming or steam where suitable, targeted label-approved treatment and monitoring. A follow-up visit checks for surviving activity and newly emerged insects, then determines whether another treatment is needed. The timing and method depend on the product label and inspection findings.

“Two-stage” here means an inspection-led initial treatment followed by a scheduled review or repeat treatment where required. It does not mean that a gas fumigant is automatically used. Ask the provider to explain the method, products, label-approved sites, preparation, occupant precautions, re-entry instructions and follow-up scope in writing.

Preparing a Nairobi property for treatment

  • Reduce clutter around beds and furniture so hiding places can be inspected.
  • Bag washable bedding and clothing before moving it through the property.
  • Vacuum seams and edges carefully and dispose of collected material securely.
  • Do not move infested furniture to another room or property without advice.
  • Keep children, pets and food away from treated areas and follow the exact re-entry instructions.
  • Tell the technician about previous sprays, steam treatments and any sensitive occupants.

Frequently asked questions

Is steam better than bed bug spray?

Steam can kill bed bugs it directly reaches, while a correctly labelled residual product may provide control on approved surfaces. Neither method alone guarantees that a whole infestation is eliminated.

Can I get rid of bed bugs in one visit?

Not always. Eggs may hatch after the first visit, so monitoring and a follow-up treatment may be needed depending on inspection findings and the product label.

Can I spray a mattress with insecticide?

Only if the exact product label explicitly permits application to that mattress surface. Never assume that an indoor-use label authorises spraying bedding or sleeping surfaces.
