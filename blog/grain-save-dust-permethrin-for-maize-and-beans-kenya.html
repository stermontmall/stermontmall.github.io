Grain-Save Dust: permethrin for stored maize and beans

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Grain-Save Dust is a permethrin-based dust that protects stored maize and beans against weevils and other storage pests. It is mixed into dry grain at the rate printed on the label and comes in a 500 g pack suited to farmers and households storing grain.

After harvest, weevils can hollow out kernels, leave dust and holes, lower germination and make grain unfit for food or sale. Losses can be heavy within a few months, especially in warm, humid stores.

What does Grain-Save Dust protect?

It protects stored maize and beans against weevils and other storage pests. Treating grain as it goes into storage protects the work you put into the crop and keeps seed and food grain in good condition for longer.

How do I mix it into grain?

Make sure the grain is properly dry before storage, because damp grain spoils even when treated. Mix the dust into the dry grain at the label rate, turning the grain thoroughly so the dust is spread evenly through every bag or heap.

Even mixing is important, because untreated pockets let pests survive. Put the treated grain into clean, dry bags or containers, and store it off the ground and away from walls.

Can I add it to grain that was already treated?

No. Using two protectants on the same grain can leave too much residue. If you are not sure whether your grain has been treated, do not add more.

Before you start, sweep out old grain and dust, repair cracks and holes, and keep the store dry and well ventilated. Check stored grain regularly for live insects, holes and powdery dust.

What safety steps matter most?

Read the label before use. Wear a mask, gloves and protective clothing when mixing, and avoid breathing the dust. Follow the label's waiting period before using treated grain.

Do not feed treated grain to animals unless the label allows it. Permethrin is very toxic to fish, so never rinse containers or equipment into streams, ponds or drains.

Frequently asked questions

What is Grain-Save Dust used for?

It protects stored maize and beans against weevils and other storage pests.

Can I feed treated grain to animals?

Not unless the label allows it. Read the label for the waiting period and for any limits on feed use.

Where should I keep the pack?

Keep the pack closed in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
