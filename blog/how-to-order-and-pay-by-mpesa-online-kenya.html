How to order and pay by M-Pesa online in Kenya

Updated 25 October 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer: Add items to your cart, enter your name, phone and delivery details, pay the total by M-Pesa Paybill (Lipa na M-Pesa), then enter the M-Pesa transaction code from the SMS at checkout. We confirm on WhatsApp and deliver to all 47 counties, usually in 1 to 3 days.

Shopping online in Kenya is simple when you know the steps. Here is exactly how an order works at Stermont Arcade, from choosing your item to receiving it.

Ordering steps at a glance
StepWhat you do
1Add items to your cart
2Enter your name, phone number and delivery details
3Pay the total by M-Pesa Paybill
4Enter the M-Pesa code from the SMS at checkout
5We confirm on WhatsApp and deliver

Step 1: How do I add items to my cart?

Browse the full product list or a category, open the item you want and add it to your cart. You can change quantities in the cart before you check out.

Step 2: What details do I enter?

At checkout, enter your name, phone number and delivery details (your county and address). Check the phone number carefully, because we use it to confirm your order on WhatsApp.

Step 3: How do I pay by M-Pesa Paybill?

The checkout shows our M-Pesa Paybill details and the total to pay. Open M-Pesa on your phone, choose Lipa na M-Pesa, then Paybill, and enter the business number and the amount shown. Always read the amount on the screen against your cart total before you send.

Step 4: Where do I enter my M-Pesa code?

When the payment goes through, you receive an SMS from M-Pesa with a transaction code. Enter that code at checkout to place your order. Keep the SMS until your item arrives.

Step 5: How do you confirm and deliver my order?

We check that your payment arrived, confirm stock and the delivery fee on WhatsApp, and process your order. We deliver to all 47 counties, usually in 1 to 3 days. The delivery fee, if there is one, is confirmed before you pay.

What if something is wrong with my order?

If an item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange a replacement or refund. Read the full returns policy and shipping details.

What are some tips for a smooth order?

Double-check your phone number and delivery location. Keep your M-Pesa SMS. Open the parcel and check the item soon after delivery. If you cannot find something, ask us on WhatsApp and we will try to source it.
