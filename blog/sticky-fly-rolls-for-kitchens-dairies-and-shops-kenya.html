Sticky fly rolls for kitchens, dairies and shops in Kenya

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Hang a Green Killer sticky roll near windows, doors, bins and counters where flies gather, but not directly above open food. Replace it when it is full of flies or dust. The rolls catch flies with no mess, no vapours and no spraying.

Where food is prepared, stored or sold, spraying insecticide can be risky or may not be allowed. Sticky rolls give you another way to control flies without chemicals in the air.

Where do sticky fly rolls work well?

They suit places where food is handled and people are close by: kitchens and food preparation areas, shops, butcheries, dairies and milk collection points, hotels, restaurants and canteens, market stalls, schools and offices. They also work in stables, poultry houses and animal sheds.

A shop owner or caterer who cannot spray near customers can combine rolls with covered bins, clean counters and door screens for steady control.

How do I hang and replace them?

Open a pack, take out a roll and hang it where flies gather, such as near windows, doors, bins, counters and light fittings. Do not hang it directly above open food, and keep it where people will not brush against it.

Replace the roll when it is full of flies or has collected dust. In a bigger room, hang several rolls for stronger coverage.

Check the rolls once a week. A roll that is dusty or full stops catching, so swapping it promptly keeps the trap working.

Why buy a bundle of 20 rolls?

The Green Killer bundle has 5 blister packs of 4 rolls each, 20 rolls in total. That is enough to keep fresh traps in several rooms, or across a whole business, for a long period, and each blister pack keeps the remaining rolls clean and sealed until you need them.

Frequently asked questions

Do the rolls use chemicals or spray?

No. They catch flies on a sticky surface, with no mess, no vapours and no spraying.

Are they safe around children and pets?

Keep them out of reach. The surface is sticky and can stick to skin, hair and clothing, so do not hang a roll where people will touch it.

What else reduces flies?

Keep rubbish bins covered and empty them daily, clean up spills and food waste quickly, fix drains, keep floors dry and use screens on doors and windows.
