Washing machine buying guide for Kenya: front load vs top load

Updated 25 October 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: Choose fully automatic if you want to load and leave it, or semi-automatic for a lower cost and less water. Front load is gentler and uses less water, top load is easier on the back. Pick capacity by household: 5 to 7 kg for one to three people, 7 to 9 kg for four to five.

A washing machine saves hours every week, but the wrong one wastes water, power and money. Use these points to choose the right one for your home.

1. Should I choose semi-automatic or fully automatic?

Semi-automatic machines have a wash tub and a separate spin tub, and you move clothes between them by hand. They are cheaper and use less water, but need more effort. Fully automatic machines wash, rinse and spin in one cycle, so you can load them and leave them. They cost more and suit busy households.

2. Is front load or top load better?

Front load machines open at the front. They are usually gentler on clothes, tend to use less water and spin faster, which leaves clothes drier. You can often place a counter or shelf on top. Top load machines open from the top, are easier on the back since you do not bend as much, and often wash a cycle faster. They usually use more water.

3. What capacity in kg do I need?

As a rough guide, 5 to 7 kg suits one to three people, 7 to 9 kg suits a family of four to five, and 10 kg or more suits large families or people who wash bedding often. Check the capacity on the product page, since the kg figure refers to dry clothes.

4. What do I need to install it?

Fully automatic machines need a water inlet, a drain and a steady water supply. If your water pressure is low, check the machine can work with it, or plan to use a tank placed higher up. Leave space around the machine for the hoses and for ventilation.

5. How do I protect it from power problems?

Power dips and surges can happen. A surge protector or stabiliser helps protect the motor and the control board.
