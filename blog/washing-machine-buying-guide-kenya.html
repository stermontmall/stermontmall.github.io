Washing machine buying guide for Kenya

Updated 29 October 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: a fully automatic top loader of 7 to 10 kg suits most Kenyan families. A semi-automatic twin tub costs less, works with bucket water, and is a smart choice if water or power is unreliable.

A washing machine saves hours of hand washing, but the right type depends on your water supply, your power and your family size.

How many kg do you need?

Choose 6 to 7 kg for 2 to 3 people, 8 to 10 kg for a family of 4 to 6, and 10 kg or more for big households or a small laundry business. A bigger drum costs more to buy, but you wash fewer loads.

Water and power in Kenya

If you have water rationing, a tank or a reliable bucket supply is important. Twin tubs are forgiving because you fill them by hand. For automatics, check the minimum water pressure in the spec sheet, and consider a small pump if your tank is low. Check the machine's wattage and use a surge-protected socket or a stabiliser, because voltage swings damage control boards. Wash during daylight if you use solar.

Before you pay

Read the warranty, especially for the motor and the control board. Ask whether a service centre is available in your area, check that the machine fits your space and door, and look for dents when it arrives. Keep your receipt and M-Pesa confirmation. Frequently asked questions

Which washing machine is best for Kenyan homes?

A fully automatic top loader suits most families. A semi-automatic twin tub is a good low-cost choice where water is irregular.

What kg washing machine do I need?

6 to 7 kg for 2 to 3 people, 8 to 10 kg for a family of 4 to 6, and larger for big households.

Can a washing machine work with water rationing?

Yes. Twin tubs can be filled from a bucket or tank, and many automatics work with a water tank. Check the minimum water pressure.
