Promax 20EC: one concentrate for bed bugs, roaches and fleas

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Promax 20EC is a propoxur-based, odourless insecticide concentrate for bed bugs (kunguni), cockroaches (mende), fleas, ants and houseflies. One 1 litre bottle makes 40 litres of ready-to-spray liquid, and it works with any hand-pump or knapsack sprayer.

Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames and skirting boards, then bite while you sleep. A concentrate you dilute yourself means one bottle can cover a house, a hostel, a hotel room or an office, and you only mix what you need for the day.

What is Promax 20EC?

It is an emulsifiable concentrate (EC) with propoxur at 20% w/v, a carbamate insecticide that blocks nerve signal transmission and causes paralysis. It is odourless, which suits bedrooms and other indoor spaces, and it leaves residual action on treated surfaces so pests that crawl across later are still affected.

How much spray does one bottle make?

One 1 litre bottle makes 40 litres of ready-to-spray liquid. Most homes need only a small amount, so a concentrate goes a long way. The label gives the mixing amount for a hand sprayer, a garden sprayer and a knapsack, so confirm it on your pack before you mix. The knapsack amount is meant for hotels, hostels and large premises, while most homes need only a small volume of spray.

How do you treat bed bugs?

Half-fill the sprayer with clean water, add Promax, top up and shake well. Spray mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, skirting boards, cracks, behind sockets and furniture joints. Wet surfaces lightly; do not soak them.

Bed bug eggs can hatch later, so inspect after about two weeks and treat again if needed.

What safety steps matter?

Keep children, pets and fish tanks out of the room until surfaces are dry and the room is ventilated. Do not spray food, utensils or bedding you will use before it dries. Wear gloves, wash your hands after use and keep the bottle closed in a cool, dry, locked place away from food.

Bed bugs, cockroaches, fleas, ants and houseflies in homes, hostels, hotels and offices.
