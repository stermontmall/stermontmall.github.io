Insecticide dust or spray? When Kelvin Dudu Dust fits

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Use a dry dust where liquids cannot go or would soak things: wall cracks, ant trails, nest boxes and kennels. Kelvin Dudu Dust is a ready-to-use carbaryl dust sold for ants, fleas, bed bugs, lice, mites and ticks, applied as a thin, even film.

Sprays are the usual choice, but they are not always the right one. A powder can reach a dry wall crack or a poultry nest box that a wet spray would soak or miss. Here is how to decide.

When does a dust work better than a spray?

Dusts stay dry, so they can be placed exactly where pests travel. Ants nest in walls and under paving, fleas hide in floor edges and bedding areas, and poultry houses and kennels have perches, nest boxes and corners where moisture is unwelcome.

Kelvin Dudu Dust needs no mixing, no sprayer and no water, which makes it handy in a small 100 g pack.

A spray can still be the better tool for open surfaces such as skirting boards or door frames. The two methods often work together: dust the cracks and nests, and spray the surfaces pests cross.

How do I apply Kelvin Dudu Dust?

Apply it dry, with no per-litre rate. Use a thin, even film and do not heap it up.

  • Ants: along trails, entry points and around the nest, repeating when ants return
  • Fleas and bed bugs: floor edges, cracks, bed-frame joints, pet bedding areas and carpets
  • Poultry houses and kennels: litter edges, perches, nest boxes and corners

What safety steps does a dust need?

Avoid breathing the dust, so wear a mask and gloves. Do not dust food, utensils or drinking water, and keep the pack out of reach of children and pets. Wash your hands after use, and read the label before you start.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need water or a sprayer?

No. The dust is applied dry, straight from the pack.

How often should I reapply it?

Reapply when the pests reappear. A thin film is enough, because heaps of powder do not work better.

Can I use it instead of a spray everywhere?

No. Use it where dry placement helps, such as cracks and nest areas, and use a spray or bait where those suit the pest better.

Can I use it in a poultry house?

Kelvin Dudu Dust is handy for poultry houses, kennels and livestock sheds. Dust litter edges, perches, nest boxes and corners lightly, and keep drinking water and feed covered while you work.
