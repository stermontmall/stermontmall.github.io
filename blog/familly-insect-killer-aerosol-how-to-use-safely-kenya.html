Aerosol insect killer at home: using Familly spray safely

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Familly Insect Killer is a ready-to-use super strength aerosol for flying and crawling insects such as cockroaches, mosquitoes and flies. Cover food, spray corners, cracks and the air as the label directs, keep it away from flames, and ventilate the room afterwards.

An aerosol is the quickest answer when you see a cockroach in the kitchen or mosquitoes in the bedroom. There is no mixing and no sprayer to wash, but a can under pressure needs more care than most people give it.

When is an aerosol the right tool?

An aerosol suits quick treatment as soon as you see the pest, and places where mixing a concentrate is not practical, such as a flat, a bedroom or a kitchen. It is sold as 1 can and works on flying and crawling insects, including cockroaches, mosquitoes and flies.

For a large or long-running infestation, a concentrate or bait is usually a better main treatment, with the aerosol as a back-up.

How do I spray flying and crawling insects?

For flying insects, spray into the air in the room and then close the door and window for a while, as the label directs. For cockroaches, spray along skirting boards, behind appliances and in cracks, taking care not to spray food or cooking surfaces.

Keep food and people away while spraying, and open windows and doors afterwards to let fresh air in.

What are the safety rules for the can?

Aerosol cans are flammable and under pressure.

  • Do not spray near flames, cookers, heaters or sparks
  • Do not puncture or burn the can, even when empty
  • Do not leave it in direct sunlight or a hot car
  • Cover or remove food, dishes and baby items, and move pets and birds out
  • Store upright in a cool, well-ventilated place, away from children

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to mix or dilute it?

No. It is a ready-to-use aerosol, so you spray straight from the can as the label directs.

How can I keep mosquitoes away for longer?

Use nets and window screens, clear stagnant water around the compound, take out rubbish daily and fix leaking taps so insects have fewer places to breed.

Can I spray it on food or cooking surfaces?

No. Cover or remove food and utensils first, and do not spray cooking surfaces.
