Fendona 60SC: a residual spray for hostels and hotels

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Fendona 60SC is a BASF insecticide containing alpha-cypermethrin at 60 grams per litre, supplied as a water-based suspension concentrate. It gives fast knockdown and a lasting residual film on treated surfaces, and it is used for public health and household pest control.

A hostel or hotel cannot treat one room and hope the pests stay put. Cockroaches, bed bugs, ants, houseflies and mosquitoes move between rooms, so the treatment needs to be planned across the whole building. Fendona 60SC is a 1 litre pack aimed at that kind of job.

How does a residual spray work?

A residual insecticide leaves a thin film on the surface after the water dries. Insects that walk across it pick up the active ingredient, so the treatment keeps working for a period after spraying. Because Fendona 60SC is water-based, it can be used on many surfaces in and around buildings.

Which pests does it control?

It is used against cockroaches, bed bugs, ants, houseflies, mosquitoes and other crawling and flying insects in homes, hostels, hotels, offices and food premises.

How do you treat a whole building?

Mix with clean water and apply as a coarse spray to cracks, crevices, skirting edges, behind furniture and other places where insects hide or walk. Shake the bottle well before measuring, because it is a suspension, and mix thoroughly in the sprayer. Rates can differ between markets, so always follow the rate printed on your pack.

Plan the treatment of the whole building in one go so that insects do not move between rooms.

  • Pay special attention to kitchens, store rooms, drains, door and window frames and bed frames
  • Keep a simple record of dates, areas treated and rates used
  • For large buildings or persistent infestations, consider a licensed pest control operator

What safety steps matter most?

Do not spray people, pets, food or cooking surfaces. Remove or cover food and utensils, keep children and animals out until surfaces are dry, and wear gloves and a mask. Fendona is very toxic to fish and aquatic life, so never rinse sprayers or empty containers into drains, streams or ponds. Store it in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food.

Frequently asked questions

What is the active ingredient in Fendona 60SC?

Alpha-cypermethrin at 60 grams per litre, in a water-based suspension concentrate.

Is it safe near fish ponds and drains?

No. It is very toxic to fish and aquatic life, so never rinse equipment into drains, streams or ponds.

Why do I shake the bottle?

Because it is a suspension, shake the bottle well before measuring and mix thoroughly in the sprayer.
