Moles (fuko) and rats on a small farm: using Fuko-Kil

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Fuko-Kil is a rodenticide sold for rats and moles in homes, stores and farms. Follow the label for placement and quantity, put bait in protected spots or active runs rather than on the soil surface, and keep it away from children, pets and poultry. The 25 g pack suits a limited area.

Moles (fuko) rarely show themselves. What you see is the mounds of soil and the tunnels through a lawn, garden or field. Rats are easier to spot but just as costly in a store, which is why Fuko-Kil is sold for both.

How do moles and rats cause damage?

Moles push up mounds of soil and cut tunnels through lawns, gardens and fields. They damage the roots of young plants, which matters most on small farms and in kitchen gardens where young plants have shallow roots.

Rats eat and spoil stored food, gnaw wires, packaging and furniture, and spread germs through droppings and urine. They breed fast, so a small problem soon becomes a large one.

How is Fuko-Kil placed for each pest?

The label decides placement and quantity, and the method differs for each animal. Look for fresh mounds and runs first, so you know where the moles are active.

  • Rats: small amounts of bait in protected spots near nesting holes, along walls and on runs where you see droppings and gnaw marks, in a covered tray or bait station where possible
  • Moles: place the product in active runs as the label directs, and never spread it on the soil surface where other animals could find it

What should I do around the bait?

Check the PCPB registration number on the label and read it before use. Keep Fuko-Kil away from children, pets, poultry and other animals, and never place it where food, feed or water can be contaminated.

Replace bait that has been eaten or has got wet, and stop when the activity ends. Collect dead rodents regularly, wearing gloves, and remove leftover bait at the end of the treatment as the label directs.

Frequently asked questions

Can Fuko-Kil be used for both rats and moles?

It is sold for rats and moles. Placement differs for each, so follow the label for rats in buildings and for moles in active runs.

Is the 25 g pack enough?

It is a small pack for a home or a small farm treating a limited area. A large store or farm will need more bait than one pack.

What if a pet or another animal eats the bait?

Contact a vet immediately. If a person swallows it, get medical help at once and show the pack.
