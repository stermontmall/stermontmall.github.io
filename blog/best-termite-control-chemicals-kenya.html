Quick answer: Start with the termite species and treatment site, then check the product's current PCPB registration and label. Termite control in Kenya may involve an existing building, a new construction site, timber, a fence post or a forestry setting. The right product depends on that use case. A trade name or active ingredient alone does not establish that a particular treatment is approved for your site. The Pest Control Products Board (PCPB) register and the label supplied with the exact container are the controlling references.

How to choose by job

Pre-construction or building work

Ask the project designer or qualified pest-management professional to specify the treatment stage and product whose current label explicitly permits that use. Confirm substrate, site preparation, application method and documentation requirements.

Existing buildings and active termite evidence

Begin with inspection. Mud tubes, damaged timber, moisture and concealed entry points can mean that surface spraying will not address the colony or the route of entry. A professional may recommend monitoring, targeted treatment, proofing and follow-up checks.

Timber and wood protection

Use only products labelled for the specific timber use. A product registered as a wood preservative should not automatically be treated as a general soil barrier or indoor residual spray.

PCPB and safe-use checks before buying

  1. Match the full trade name, formulation and registration number on the container to the current PCPB record.
  2. Read the label for target termite, site, application method, restrictions and protective equipment.
  3. Confirm pack size, seal, batch and expiry details on delivery.
  4. Do not calculate or guess dilution rates from the active ingredient percentage.
  5. Use a qualified or licensed pest-control professional where the label, property or treatment requires it.

Official references: PCPB public-health registered products, PCPB crop register and PCPB registered-product lists.

Frequently asked questions

Which termite chemical is best in Kenya?

There is no single best active ingredient for every property. Choose only a product whose current Kenyan PCPB registration and label cover the target termite, site and application method.

Is imidacloprid the same as fipronil?

No. They are different active ingredients with different modes of action and product labels. Do not substitute one for another or transfer application rates between products.

Can chlorpyrifos be used around any house?

No. The registered use and restrictions must be checked for the exact product. Some chlorpyrifos products are restricted to specified building, construction or wood-treatment uses.

How long does a termite treatment last in soil?

There is no reliable universal duration. Persistence depends on the formulation, soil, moisture, construction details, application quality and approved label. Ask the registered product holder or a qualified operator.
