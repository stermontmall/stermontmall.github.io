Thermal fogging machine: what it does and what goes in

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: The Thermal Fogging Machine Pulsed Power Sprayer is a professional pulse thermal fogger for mosquito, fly and pest control and fumigation, with dual tanks and stainless steel heat pipes. It is equipment, not an insecticide, so pair it with a product whose label allows thermal fogging.

Mosquitoes, flies and other pests hide in corners and crevices that a hand sprayer reaches slowly. A fog spreads through a space, which is why fogging is used where pests hide in cracks and corners.

What is the machine used for?

It is sold as a single unit for farms, greenhouses, warehouses and residential areas. Its features include:

  • A pulse thermal fogger for mosquito, fly and pest control and fumigation
  • Durable dual tanks
  • Stainless steel heat pipes
  • Fast, efficient fog distribution that reaches crevices and corners

Which insecticide goes in the tanks?

The catalog does not name one. Choose a product whose label allows thermal fogging, and follow that label for the dilution and the time the space must stay closed.

For example, the catalog advice for Actellic 50EC says it must not be used with thermal foggers. Read every insecticide label before you fill a tank.

How do I start and stop it?

The catalog does not list the fuel type, tank capacity, output or settings, so follow the manufacturer's manual for filling, start-up and shut-down.

Treat only the space you have prepared, and let the machine cool before you handle or move it.

How do I stay safe around a thermal fogger?

The machine gets hot, so never touch the heat pipes while it is running or cooling. Remove people, pets, food, feed and animals from the space, and keep flammable materials away from the machine and its hot parts.

Wear the protective equipment named on the insecticide label, including a respirator if the label requires one, and do not enter a fogged space until the label says it is safe. Keep the machine away from children.

Frequently asked questions

Does the machine come with insecticide?

No. It is the equipment only, sold as 1 unit. You choose a suitable insecticide separately.

Where can I use it?

The catalog lists farms, greenhouses, warehouses and residential areas.

How should I store it?

Let the machine cool completely, empty the tanks as the manual directs, and store it dry and out of reach of children, away from food and feed.
