Rat control in a poultry house: using Tatua bait blocks safely

Updated 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer: Use Syngenta Tatua bait blocks only inside bait stations. Find where rodents travel from droppings, gnaw marks and runways, put stations along walls and near holes, and keep the blocks away from feed, drinkers and the birds. A solid block stays where you place it better than loose bait.

In a farm store or a poultry house, rodents eat and spoil feed and disturb the birds. Controlling them takes bait that stays where you put it, and that is the idea behind a bait block fixed inside a station.

Why use a block instead of loose bait?

A block is heavy and solid, so it resists damp and handling better than loose bait. Rodents can carry or gnaw a block, but one held inside a station stays where you put it.

In a poultry house the birds, the feed and the drinkers are all close to the places rodents travel, which is why the blocks belong inside stations and nowhere else.

That also makes it easier to see which stations are being visited, and to replace only the bait that has been eaten.

How do I place the stations?

Look for droppings, gnaw marks, runways and burrows to find where rodents travel. Place bait stations along walls, near holes, behind stacked goods and in dark corners, and put the blocks inside so that children, pets and livestock cannot reach them.

Check the stations regularly, replace blocks that have been eaten, and move a station if there is no activity at that spot.

What should I prepare and avoid?

Use the blocks only inside bait stations, never on food or in food areas. Wear gloves, collect dead rodents, dispose of them safely and wash your hands afterwards. Check the PCPB registration number on the label and read the label before use. Prepare the building first:

  • Store grain, feed and produce on raised pallets, away from walls, in rodent-proof containers
  • Seal holes and gaps, and keep drains and doors closed or screened
  • Remove rubbish and clutter, and cut tall grass and bush near buildings

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the blocks in a poultry house?

They are sold for poultry houses as well as stores and farms. Use them only inside bait stations, away from feed, drinkers and the birds.

Why are bait blocks used in stations?

A block fixed inside a station is not scattered or carried off, and children, pets and livestock cannot reach it.

What size is the pack?

The product is sold as one pack. Check the pack for its size, and keep the blocks in their original pack, dry and locked away from children, pets and food.
