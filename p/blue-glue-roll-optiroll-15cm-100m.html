About Russell IPM Optiroll Blue Sticky Trap Roll – 15cm x 100m

Russell IPM Optiroll Blue is a colour-optimised adhesive roll designed primarily to attract and monitor thrips in suitable greenhouse and horticultural crops. This listing identifies one roll measuring 15cm wide by 100m long. The blue colour is selected for its attraction to thrips, including western flower thrips, and the adhesive surface retains insects that land on it. The roll can support early detection, catch monitoring and integrated pest-management programmes when it is installed and inspected consistently.

Why Blue Sticky rolls are used

Thrips are small insects that can damage leaves, flowers and developing fruit. Because early infestations may be difficult to see, growers use monitoring tools to identify activity before symptoms become widespread. Russell IPM describes the Optiroll Blue range as designed to maximise attraction of thrips and some capsids. The standard Optiroll Blue, Super Blue and Super Plus Blue variants are not identical: some versions include additional visual patterns or a pheromone component. This product listing should be treated as the 15cm x 100m blue roll named here; confirm the exact variant and any pheromone feature from the packaging before relying on it.

Crop and growing environments

Blue sticky rolls may be useful in protected horticulture, vegetable crops, soft-fruit production, ornamental crops and nursery environments where thrips monitoring is relevant. The exact position and number of rolls depend on crop layout, pest pressure, greenhouse structure and the monitoring plan. Russell IPM advises positioning rolls throughout the crop and giving attention to entrances; growers should follow the current manufacturer guidance for height and spacing. Do not assume one fixed roll length is sufficient for every greenhouse or crop area.

Installation and handling

Plan the route before unrolling the adhesive tape. Use suitable brackets or supports recommended for the roll so it can be suspended securely and positioned in relation to the crop. Avoid allowing the adhesive to touch leaves, irrigation lines, workers or equipment. Keep the roll away from dust and dirt during installation, and handle it carefully to prevent accidental sticking to clothing or skin. The adhesive surface should remain exposed to the intended monitoring area without obstructing routine crop work.

Monitoring catches

Record the date, crop, location and height for each monitoring section. Inspect the sticky surface regularly and note the number and type of insects caught. Compare results from consistent monitoring points over time. A rise in thrips catches can signal a need for closer crop scouting, but trap counts alone do not establish the level of crop damage or the best control measure. Examine leaves, flowers and growing points, and seek identification support if the insect species is uncertain. Replace the roll or affected section when it is covered with insects, dust or debris, or when the manufacturer's guidance indicates replacement.

Integrated pest management

Optiroll Blue can be used alongside biological crop-protection programmes and other IPM practices. Russell IPM notes compatibility with beneficial pollinators and natural enemies for the product range, but growers should confirm compatibility for the exact roll variant and their own programme. Use monitoring information to guide crop inspection, sanitation, plant-entry checks and decisions made with an agronomist. Do not rely on sticky rolls alone to manage a severe infestation. Where pesticides are considered, check registration and crop labels, and follow application and pre-harvest requirements.

Storage and product details

Confirm the exact variant, material, packaging and any special lure feature from the product label. Store unused rolls in a cool, dry place in their original packaging and protect them from dust and direct sunlight as directed by the supplier.
