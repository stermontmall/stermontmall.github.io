Product overview

Lecatech WP is a microbial insecticide supplied in a 250g wettable powder pack. The Kenya Pest Control Products Board biopesticide register lists LECATECH WP under registration number PCPB(CR)1144. Its active biological ingredient is Lecanicillium lecanii strain J27, with a concentration of approximately 1 × 10^10 colony-forming units per gram.

The registered uses include control of thrips and whiteflies on French beans and whiteflies on roses. Always check the current Kenyan label supplied with the pack for the approved crop, target pest and application directions. Lecanicillium lecanii is an entomopathogenic fungus used in biological crop protection.

Its spores attach to susceptible insects, germinate and penetrate the insect cuticle. The fungus then develops within the insect and causes infection and death. Because it acts through a biological infection process, Lecatech WP should be used with attention to humidity, timing, spray coverage and storage conditions.

It is not a conventional synthetic contact insecticide, a public-health room spray, a rodenticide, herbicide or fungicide. The 250g pack is suitable for commercial growers, nurseries, flower farms and crop-protection professionals who need a practical pack size for planned biological control programmes. The Kenyan registration specifically identifies thrips and whiteflies on French beans and whiteflies on roses.

Do not assume that registration for these uses automatically authorises treatment of other crops or pests. If a grower needs to manage another pest or crop, check the current Kenyan label and consult a qualified agronomist before choosing a product. Whiteflies and thrips can multiply quickly in protected crops.

Whiteflies often occur on the undersides of leaves and can produce honeydew, while thrips may damage flowers, leaves and growing points. Regular scouting helps identify infestations before they become severe. Inspect representative plants, examine the underside of leaves and growing points, and use monitoring tools where appropriate.

Record pest levels and treatment dates to evaluate the programme. Correct pest identification is important because leaf damage, silvering, yellowing and poor growth can also result from disease, nutrient imbalance or water stress. Lecatech WP should form part of an integrated pest-management programme.

Combine monitoring with crop hygiene, removal of heavily infested plant material where appropriate, weed management, screening, biological control and conservation of beneficial organisms. Avoid unnecessary broad-spectrum insecticide applications that may disrupt natural enemies. Biological control is often most useful when it is applied early and consistently as part of a planned programme rather than after an infestation has become overwhelming.

Agropests product information describes a reference foliar application of 20g in 100 litres of water and recommends a high-volume spray to achieve even coverage on leaf surfaces. It also advises late-afternoon application to reduce exposure to strong ultraviolet light and low humidity, which can affect spore germination. Use these details only if they match the current approved Kenyan label for the supplied pack.

The label determines the exact rate, water volume, spray method, interval, number of applications, re-entry conditions and any restrictions on tank mixing. Do not increase the concentration or apply to an unlisted crop. Because Lecatech contains living fungal spores, storage and handling are important.

Keep the product in its original, tightly closed packaging and follow the manufacturer’s storage temperature and shelf-life instructions. Protect it from direct sunlight, excessive heat and moisture. Do not store a prepared spray mixture longer than the label permits.

Use clean application equipment and avoid residues of fungicides or other materials that may affect the viability of the biological agent. Do not mix Lecatech with another pesticide, fertiliser or adjuvant unless compatibility is confirmed by the label or manufacturer.
