How a Twin Tub works

A twin tub has two tubs side by side: one for washing and one for spinning. You load the clothes in the wash tub and run the wash cycle, then move the clothes by hand to the spin tub to remove most of the water. Because the tubs are separate, you can spin one load while the next is washing, which saves time on laundry day.

Why families choose Twin tubs

  • Semi-automatic machines are simple to use and easy to understand
  • You control the process and can move and rinse clothes as you like
  • Washing and spinning at the same time speeds up large loads
  • Clothes come out well spun and ready to hang up

Tips for better Washing

  • Sort whites, colours and heavy fabrics
  • Do not overload the wash or spin tub

  • Clean the lint filter often
  • Spread clothes evenly in the spin tub so the machine stays balanced
  • Wipe the tubs dry after use and leave the lid open to air
