Plan loads around the separate wash and spin tubs

The WSQB753W is a semi-automatic twin-tub machine with a stated 7.5 kg wash capacity. Unlike a fully automatic washer, it requires you to move clothes from the wash tub to the spin tub yourself. This gives you direct control over sorting, washing and rinsing, but it also means you should allow time to transfer each load and balance garments in the spin tub. Follow the manual for the appropriate wash and spin load limits; the stated wash capacity should not be assumed to be the spin capacity.

For more consistent results, separate whites, colours and heavily soiled items, and avoid mixing bulky bedding with small everyday garments if the load becomes difficult to balance. Use detergent suitable for the fabric and follow the dosage on its packaging. The clear lid makes it easier to observe the wash, while the dial controls keep operation straightforward.

Installation, drainage and routine care

Choose a level, stable floor with access to the water supply and a suitable drain. Arrange inlet and drain hoses as described in the installation guide, and keep the electrical outlet and plug dry. Do not use an extension arrangement that is not approved for the appliance, and avoid operating the machine if the cable, plug or housing is damaged. Leave enough space to reach the controls and move clothes safely between tubs.

After laundry, remove lint as directed, wipe away detergent residue and let the tubs air-dry with the lids open when the machine is not in use. Check that the spin load is distributed evenly before starting; an uneven load can cause excessive movement.
