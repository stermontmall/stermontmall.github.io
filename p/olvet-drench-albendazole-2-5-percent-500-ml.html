Why Deworming Is Important Worms drain animals of blood and nutrients. Infected cattle, goats and sheep may lose weight, produce less milk, grow slowly, develop diarrhoea and become weak and more open to other diseases. Young animals are especially at risk. Regular, well-planned deworming protects growth, milk yield, fertility and the value of your animals.
