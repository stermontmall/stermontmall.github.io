Grain-Save Dust Permethrin Grain Protectant (500 g) Grain-Save Dust is a permethrin-based protectant for stored maize and beans against weevils and other storage pests. It is mixed into dry grain at the label rate and comes in a 500 g pack suited to farmers and households storing grain. KEY FEATURES • Permethrin-based dust • Protects stored maize and beans • Works against weevils and other storage pests • Mixed into dry grain at the label rate • 500 g pack • Delivered to all 47 counties WHY GRAIN PROTECTION MATTERS After harvest, insects such as weevils can hollow out kernels, leave dust and holes, lower germination and make grain unfit for food or sale. Losses can be heavy within a few months, especially in warm, humid stores. Treating grain as it goes into storage protects the work you put into the crop and keeps seed and food grain in good condition for longer. HOW TO USE IT Make sure the grain is properly dry before storage, because damp grain spoils even when treated. Mix the dust into the dry grain at the rate on the label, turning the grain thoroughly so the dust is spread evenly through every bag or heap. Even mixing is important, because untreated pockets let pests survive. Put treated grain into clean, dry bags or containers and store it off the ground and away from walls. IMPORTANT: DO NOT DOUBLE TREAT Do not use on grain already treated. Using two protectants on the same grain can leave too much residue and is not safe. If you are not sure whether your grain has been treated, do not add more. PREPARE THE STORE • Sweep out old grain and dust • Repair cracks and holes where insects hide • Use clean, dry bags • Keep the store dry and well ventilated • Check stored grain regularly for live insects, holes and powdery dust SAFETY Read the label before use. Wear a mask, gloves and protective clothing when mixing, and avoid breathing the dust. Keep away from children, pets, food and water, and wash your hands and face afterwards. Follow the label's waiting period before using treated grain, and do not use treated grain for animal feed unless the label allows it. Permethrin is very toxic to fish, so never rinse containers or equipment into streams, ponds or drains. STORAGE Keep the pack closed in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
