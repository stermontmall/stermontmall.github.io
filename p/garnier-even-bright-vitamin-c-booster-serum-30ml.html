How to use

Start with clean, dry skin. Apply a small, pea-sized amount or the quantity directed on the packaging, spreading it evenly over the face while avoiding direct contact with the eyes and lips. Allow the serum to absorb, then follow with a moisturiser suited to your skin type.

In the morning, finish with a broad-spectrum sunscreen and reapply sunscreen as directed on its packaging, especially when outdoors. If using the serum in the evening, keep the rest of the routine gentle and avoid introducing several new active skincare products at the same time. If you are new to vitamin C, niacinamide or exfoliating acids, introduce the serum gradually and observe how your skin responds.

A patch test on a small area is a sensible precaution before applying a new skincare product to the whole face. Do not apply to broken or irritated skin. If persistent irritation occurs, stop using the product and seek advice from a qualified skincare professional.

Always check the ingredient list on the actual pack if you have known sensitivities, allergies or specific ingredient restrictions.
