RESPONSIBLE TRAP PLACEMENT

A live-capture cage is only appropriate when the target species, location and capture method are lawful and the operator can inspect it at suitable intervals. Choose a cage dimension suited to the intended animal; a trap that is too small can injure an animal, while an unsuitable opening may fail to capture it. Place the unit on stable ground away from public access, pets and avoidable weather exposure. Follow local wildlife requirements and obtain specialist advice where protected species may be present. Plan the response before setting the trap, including safe transfer, release or authorised professional handling. Never leave a live animal unattended for extended periods.
