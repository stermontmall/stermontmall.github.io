Care, cleaning & storage

Stainless steel is widely used for dairy utensils because a sound, smooth, non-porous surface can be washed and sanitised effectively when handled correctly. The bucket’s simple open-top shape gives the farmer access for washing, visual inspection and transfer of milk into a suitable storage can. Prepare a clean, dry place for the bucket, wash hands, follow the farm’s udder-cleaning routine and keep the opening away from dust, feed particles, manure and splash water. Use only clean water and dairy-appropriate detergents for washing. After each milking, rinse away milk residues promptly, wash all interior surfaces, rinse thoroughly and allow the bucket to dry in a protected position. Do not store milk in a bucket that is still wet from washing or that has retained soap or chemical odours.
