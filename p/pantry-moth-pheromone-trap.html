About Pantry Moth Pheromone Sticky Trap

Product overview

PANTRY MOTH PHEROMONE STICKY TRAP IN KENYA

The Pantry Moth Pheromone Sticky Trap is a monitoring tool for kitchens, food cupboards, dry-goods stores and other places where packaged or stored food is kept. Pantry moths can become noticeable when small moths fly around cupboards, larvae appear near dry foods, or fine webbing is found inside food packaging. A pheromone trap helps you check whether adult moth activity is present and whether it changes after you have cleaned and inspected the storage area. It is a monitoring aid, not a complete treatment for an established infestation.

HOW THE PHEROMONE TRAP WORKS

The lure is intended to attract adult moths of the target pantry-moth group to the sticky adhesive surface. When an attracted moth lands on the board, it becomes caught and can be counted during routine inspection. This gives a visible indication of activity and can help a household, shop, restaurant or food-store operator decide whether a closer inspection is needed. The trap does not spray insecticide onto food and should not be treated as a substitute for removing infested food or cleaning storage shelves.

WHERE TO USE IT

Consider monitoring dry-food storage areas where flour, cereals, rice, maize meal, nuts, dried fruit, seeds, pet food or similar products are stored. In a home, suitable monitoring points may include a pantry cupboard, a dry-food cabinet or a shelf near previously affected packages. In a shop or commercial store, place monitoring devices in suitable non-food-contact positions around dry-goods storage zones. Do not place the adhesive surface where it can touch unpackaged food, food-contact equipment or preparation surfaces.

INSPECTION AND PREVENTION

Before setting a trap, inspect stored foods and packaging for signs of infestation. Check seams, folds, corners and the underside of shelves. Discard food that is clearly infested in a sealed bag and clean the affected storage area thoroughly. Vacuum cracks and shelf joints where crumbs or residues may collect, then dispose of the vacuum contents safely. Store susceptible dry foods in clean, tightly closed containers and avoid keeping old, opened packets for long periods. These housekeeping steps address sources that a monitoring trap cannot remove.

POSITIONING AND MAINTENANCE

Follow the supplier's instructions for assembling, activating and positioning the trap. Keep it dry and away from direct contact with food, water, dust and excessive heat. Place it where it can be checked easily but cannot be handled by children or pets. Record the date it is installed and inspect it at regular intervals. Replace the trap when the adhesive becomes dusty, full or no longer effective, and follow the pack guidance for lure life and disposal. Do not assume a trap remains active indefinitely.

WHO MAY FIND IT USEFUL

The trap may be useful for households, mini-markets, supermarkets, restaurants, bakeries, hotels, food handlers and managers responsible for dry-goods storage. It can also support a professional pest-management inspection by helping identify where adult moth activity is being detected. In a business, use a simple monitoring log to note trap location, inspection date and catch observations. A rising catch or repeated activity should prompt inspection of nearby stock and storage practices rather than simply adding more traps.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT INFORMATION

This listing is for a pheromone-based sticky monitoring trap. The exact lure composition, target species, dimensions, replacement interval and number of traps supplied should be confirmed from the product pack or supplier before use. Do not infer that the product controls larvae inside food or eliminates every life stage. Keep the trap away from children, pets and non-target insects, and dispose of used adhesive material responsibly.
