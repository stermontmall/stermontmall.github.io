How to Use

Fill the kettle with clean water between the minimum and maximum marks, close the lid, place it on its power base and switch on. Lift it off the base to pour. Never switch it on empty or overfill it, because boiling water can splash out of the spout.

Care and Cleaning

Unplug and let the kettle cool before cleaning. Wipe the outside with a soft damp cloth and never put the base in water. If you see limescale inside, boil a mixture of water and white vinegar, leave it for a short while, then rinse well and boil clean water a couple of times before drinking. Empty the kettle after use so that water does not stay inside for days.

Safety Tips

Keep the kettle on a flat, dry surface away from the edge of the counter and out of reach of children. Hot steam comes out of the spout, so keep your hands and face away when pouring. Use the kettle only with its own base, and follow the user manual.
