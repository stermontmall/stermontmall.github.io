Descaling, cleaning and everyday safety

Mineral deposits can build up inside a kettle, especially where water is hard. If limescale appears, descale using the method and products recommended in the manual. If the manual permits a mild vinegar solution, rinse the kettle thoroughly afterwards and boil and discard clean water before using it for drinks again. Do not use abrasive tools that could damage the interior, and never immerse the electrical base, plug or cord in water.
