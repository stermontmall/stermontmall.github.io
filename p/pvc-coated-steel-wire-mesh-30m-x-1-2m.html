Product Overview & Key Benefits

PVC Coated Wire Mesh 30m × 1.2m is a roll of steel wire mesh with a PVC outer coating, listed by Afeef Maimun under supplier SKU AM-AGR-0025. The supplier specifies a 30-metre length and 1.2-metre height, with high-grade steel wire and PVC coating as the stated material. It is intended for fencing and enclosure projects where a coated wire screen is preferred to bare metal.

Agricultural uses listed by the supplier include poultry and livestock enclosures, garden protection and aquaculture perimeter screening. The roll is sold as one unit. The product combines a steel-wire core with an external PVC coating.

The supplier describes the coating as protection against corrosion and weather exposure. Where & How to Use in Kenya

On a farm, the mesh can be used to create or repair boundaries around poultry houses, livestock pens, gardens and utility areas. For poultry housing, the mesh opening must be appropriate for the bird size and the risks from predators. For livestock, the wire diameter, post spacing and tensioning method must be matched to the animals being contained.

In a garden, it can provide a perimeter barrier or a support screen, provided the frame is strong enough for the span and local weather. The supplier also lists aquaculture applications, including protective screening around fish ponds or tank structures. In these settings, the mesh is a perimeter or barrier material; it is not a substitute for purpose-built fish cage netting or a water-retaining pond liner.

Avoid installing it where exposed wire ends could injure fish, workers or animals. Use suitable framing and cover cut edges. PLANNING AND INSTALLATION Measure the full fence line before opening the roll.

Decide the position of corner posts, intermediate supports, gates and ground-level fixing points. The supplier recommends support posts at approximately 2m to 2.5m intervals for a typical installation; the required spacing can vary with terrain, wind exposure, frame strength and tension. Unroll the mesh to avoid kinks.

Attach one end securely, pull the mesh taut without overstressing it, and fasten it along each support. Suitable ties, staples or clips should be selected for the post material and wire coating. Keep fasteners snug but avoid crushing or cutting through the PVC layer.

BEFORE YOU BUY Confirm the mesh aperture, wire gauge and coating thickness with the supplier. The published product page does not provide these measurements, and they determine whether the roll is suitable for a particular animal, crop or security application. Check whether the 1.2m height is sufficient for the intended barrier.

If the installation is exposed to coastal air, frequent wetting or chemical contact, ask about the coating's suitability for those conditions. Do not assume that the mesh is a complete fence system: posts, gates, fasteners and tools are not specified as included. CARE AND MAINTENANCE Inspect the installed mesh periodically for loose ties, damaged coating, bent wires, sagging spans and sharp cut ends.

Repair loose sections before they become larger gaps. If the PVC coating is cut or scraped, ask the supplier about an appropriate repair method and monitor the exposed wire. Store unused roll material dry and away from sharp tools that could damage the coating.
