  • Delivered to all 47 counties WHAT THE NUTRIENTS DO
  • Nitrogen (N) drives leaf and shoot growth and gives plants a healthy green colour
  • Phosphorus (P) supports strong roots, early growth, flowering and seed formation
  • Potassium (K) helps fruit fill, quality, water balance and resistance to stress
  • Magnesium (MgO) is part of chlorophyll and supports photosynthesis
  • Iron, manganese, boron, copper, zinc and molybdenum are needed in small amounts for enzymes, flowering, fruit set and general plant health WHY A BALANCED FEED HELPS Crops often run short of nutrients at key moments, such as after transplanting, at flowering and during fruit or grain fill. A fertilizer with the main nutrients and a full set of trace elements helps cover those gaps in one product. Bio-stimulants are added to support plant vigour and help crops cope with stress, though they work best on a well-managed crop with enough water and good soil. WHERE IT FITS Because it is made for all crops at all stages, it can be used in vegetables, flowers, fruit, cereals and other crops, from young seedlings to fruiting plants. The 250 ml pack suits small plots, nurseries, kitchen gardens and growers who want to test the product first.

How to use

Follow the dose, water volume, method and interval given on the label. Shake the container well before use, because it is a suspension, and mix with clean water. Apply evenly, and do not exceed the label rate. Spraying in the cool of the morning or late afternoon helps avoid leaf scorch. If you are mixing with other products, test a small amount first.

Safety information

Read the label before use. Wear gloves and avoid contact with eyes and skin. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Do not let rinse water reach streams or ponds.

Storage & handling

Store in the original closed container in a cool, dry place away from direct sun.
