Why Choose Touchdown

Weeds compete with crops for water, light and nutrients, and perennial weeds such as couch grass and kikuyu grass return again and again after slashing or hand weeding. A systemic glyphosate herbicide is taken in through the green leaves and moves down to the roots and growing points, so the whole plant is killed and not only the top growth. This reduces repeat labour and leaves clean land ready for planting.

Weed Control Tips

Spray on actively growing weeds with plenty of green leaf, and do not slash or mow for several days before and after spraying so the product can move into the roots. Avoid spraying just before rain and use a clean, calibrated sprayer with a flat fan nozzle. Because glyphosate harms any green plant it touches, use a shield or directed spray near crops, keep drift away from vegetables, flowers and neighbouring farms, and never spray in windy conditions. Allow the waiting period on the label before planting. Always follow the dose rate on the label for the weeds you are treating.
