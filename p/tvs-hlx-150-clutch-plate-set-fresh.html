About TVS HLX 150 Clutch Plate Separator Set

Product overview

The TVS HLX 150 clutch plate separator set is a group of steel plates used within a compatible multi-plate motorcycle clutch pack. Separator plates work alongside friction plates: the assembled clutch pack transmits engine torque when engaged and separates to interrupt drive when the rider operates the clutch. Worn, warped, burnt or incorrectly matched plates can contribute to clutch drag, slipping, difficult gear selection or inconsistent engagement.
