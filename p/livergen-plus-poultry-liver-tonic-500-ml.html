Why the Liver matters in Poultry

The liver does a lot of work in a bird. It processes nutrients, builds proteins, handles fats and helps remove toxins and drug residues from the body. In busy flocks, the liver can come under pressure from stress, heavy feeding, moulds and toxins in feed, medications and disease challenges. A healthy liver supports growth, egg production, feed conversion and overall resistance.

When farmers use a Liver Tonic

  • During and after treatment with medicines, when the liver is working hard
  • After feed problems such as mouldy or poor-quality feed
  • In periods of stress, such as heat, vaccination, handling and transport
  • In the recovery period after disease
  • As part of a routine health programme for layers and broilers

How to use it

Livergen Plus is added to the birds' drinking water. Follow the label for the dose per litre of water or per number of birds and for how many days to give it. Mix the right amount in clean water, make sure all birds can reach the water, and use up the medicated water within the time recommended on the label. Remove other water sources while the medicated water is in use, so birds drink the right amount. Keep drinkers clean.

Good flock management

A liver tonic works best as part of good management. Provide clean water and quality feed, store feed in a dry place to avoid mould, keep houses clean and ventilated, follow your vaccination programme and watch birds for signs of illness.

When to call the vet

If birds are dying, look sick, stop eating or show a sudden drop in egg production, contact your vet. A tonic supports the flock but is not a treatment for disease.

Safety

Read the label before use. Keep out of reach of children. Wash your hands after handling. Follow any withdrawal advice on the label.

Storage

Keep the bottle tightly closed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
