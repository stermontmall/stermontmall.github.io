Product overview

Norbrook Milking Salve (100 g) Dry, cracked teats hurt the animal, make milking harder and give dirt and bacteria a place to settle. Norbrook Milking Salve is a soothing udder and teat ointment containing 0.55% w/w Dichlorophen BP, with lanolin as an emollient. It helps prevent chapping and cracking caused by milking and harsh weather, keeping teats soft and comfortable for dairy cows, goats and other milking animals. It comes in a handy 100 g pack. For animal use only.

  • Delivered to all 47 counties WHY TEAT CARE MATTERS Cracked teats are painful, and the animal may become restless at milking. Cracks can also trap dirt and bacteria, which may increase the risk of udder infections such as mastitis. Regular use of a good teat and udder ointment keeps the skin supple, supports comfortable milking and helps protect milk quality and yield on dairy farms of any size, from one cow in a backyard shed to a larger herd.

How to use

Apply the salve after milking as directed on the label. Clean, dry teats take it best, so wipe and dry the teats first. Use a clean hand or applicator each time, and do not share a dirty tub between animals during an outbreak of udder problems. DAIRY HYGIENE TIPS

  • Wash and dry the udder and teats before milking
  • Keep milking equipment and milkers' hands clean
  • Keep bedding dry and the milking area free of mud and manure
  • Check teats daily for cracks, sores or swelling
  • If you notice swelling, hot or hard quarters, or blood or clots in the milk, contact a veterinarian promptly WHO IT SUITS
  • Smallholder dairy farmers with cows or dairy goats
  • Larger dairy farms that need a daily teat care routine
  • Farmers whose animals suffer from windy, dusty or wet conditions
