Why Teat Care Matters

Dry, cracked teats are painful for the animal and can make milking difficult. Cracks can also trap dirt and bacteria, which may increase the risk of udder infections such as mastitis. Regular use of a good teat and udder ointment keeps the skin supple, supports comfortable milking and helps protect milk quality and yield on dairy farms of any size.

Dairy Hygiene Tips

Wash and dry the udder and teats before milking, keep milking equipment and milkers hands clean, and keep bedding dry. Apply the salve after milking as directed on the label. If you notice swelling, hot or hard quarters, blood or clots in the milk, contact a veterinarian promptly.
