Product overview

Actara 25 WG Insecticide 8g is a small pack of a water-dispersible granule formulation containing thiamethoxam at 250 g/kg. The product is listed by Kenya's Pest Control Products Board under registration number PCPB(CR)0320. The 8g pack is intended for buyers who specifically need this pack size and have confirmed that Actara 25 WG is registered for the crop and pest combination on their farm. Actara belongs to the neonicotinoid class. The PCPB register identifies a range of crop uses, including selected sucking and chewing pests on crops such as cabbage, tomatoes, snow peas, roses, hypericum, coffee and tobacco. The exact approved use depends on the crop, pest, production system and current label; the product must not be treated as a universal insecticide for every crop or insect.

Key features & benefits

AND FORMULATION The name 25 WG identifies a wettable granule formulation with thiamethoxam at 250 grams per kilogram. The granules are designed to be dispersed in water in accordance with the label, rather than used as a ready-to-apply liquid. The active ingredient and formulation strength are important when comparing Actara with products that have a similar brand name, a different concentration or another formulation. Confirm that the container says Actara 25 WG and that the net content is 8g. Do not assume that a product with a similar name or another pack size has identical directions. The formulation should only be prepared and applied using the instructions printed on the approved Kenyan label. REGISTERED CROP AND PEST CONTEXT The PCPB crop register lists Actara 25 WG for control of aphids and diamondback moth in cabbages; whiteflies in tomatoes and snow peas; whiteflies and aphids in roses and hypericum; leafminer in coffee; and aphids, weevils, whiteflies and leaf beetles on tobacco. The register also includes crop-specific pre-harvest intervals and other conditions, and indicates that certain uses are restricted to protected cultivation rather than open-field production. These distinctions matter for growers, agronomists and commercial buyers. A product's registration for one crop does not authorize its use on a different crop. Safety information

AND RESPONSIBLE USE Actara 25 WG is a pesticide and must be handled responsibly. Keep it away from children, food, animal feed, medicines and drinking water. Avoid contact with skin and eyes and avoid inhaling dust or spray mist while handling or preparing the product. Wear the personal protective equipment stated on the label. Do not eat, drink or smoke during handling. Wash hands and exposed skin after work, and clean equipment only as directed. Observe all label directions for re-entry and pre-harvest intervals. The PCPB register notes crop-specific pre-harvest intervals, including seven days for certain listed crops; the exact interval must be checked against the crop and current label. Do not apply to open-field crops where the label or registration restricts the use to protected cultivation.

Storage & handling

Keep the 8g pack in its original sealed packaging in a secure, dry and ventilated pesticide store. Protect it from moisture, heat, direct sunlight and physical damage. Store separately from food, feed, seed, household goods and medicines. Never transfer pesticide into a food or drink container. Do not reuse empty packaging. Follow the label and applicable Kenyan requirements for collection and disposal. Do not discard the product or its packaging into rivers, drains, ponds or other water sources.
