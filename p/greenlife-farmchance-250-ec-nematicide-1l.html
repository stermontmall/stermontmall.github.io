Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

This is a pest-control or agricultural chemical product. Use it only for the crop, pest, site and application method expressly authorised on the current Kenya-approved product label. Read the full label before opening or use.

Follow the label exactly for personal protective equipment, dilution, application, ventilation, re-entry period, pre-harvest interval, environmental restrictions and any other precautions. Do not guess a dose, mix it with another product, or use it on food, animals, people or surfaces unless the label specifically permits that use. Product overview

Greenlife Farmchance 250 EC is a nematicide supplied in a one-litre emulsifiable concentrate bottle. The Kenya Pest Control Products Board crop register lists FARMCHANCE 250 EC under registration number PCPB(CR)1403. Its active ingredient is fosthiazate 250 g/L.

The current Kenyan registration identifies it for control of nematodes on roses and lists a 12-hour re-entry interval. It is classified as WHO Class II, moderately hazardous, with a yellow colour band. Always check the current Kenyan label supplied with the bottle for the approved crop, target nematode and directions before use.

Fosthiazate is an organophosphate nematicide used to manage plant-parasitic nematodes in soil and around crop roots. Farmchance is intended for crop-protection use where nematode pressure has been identified and the crop matches the approved label. It is not a public-health room spray, a household aerosol, a rodenticide, a foliar insecticide or a general-purpose soil treatment for every crop.

The one-litre pack is suited to commercial rose growers, flower farms and professional crop-protection users who need a larger pack for planned nematode-management programmes. Plant-parasitic nematodes are microscopic roundworms that live in soil and can damage roots. Root-knot nematodes may cause galls or swellings on roots, stunting, yellowing, wilting during warm periods and reduced plant vigour.

In rose production, root damage can affect nutrient uptake and flower quality. These symptoms may also be caused by root diseases, water stress, nutrient imbalance or poor soil conditions. Correct diagnosis is essential before selecting a nematicide.

Inspect roots and use soil or root testing where necessary to confirm the nematode species and infestation level. The PCPB registration specifically identifies nematode control on roses. Do not assume that the product can be used on tomatoes, French beans, potatoes or other crops merely because the supplier describes it as an agricultural nematicide.

A product registered for one crop is not automatically authorised for another. Use only the crop and target organism stated on the current Kenyan label and seek advice from a qualified agronomist or licensed pesticide professional when the correct treatment is uncertain. Farmchance should be part of an integrated nematode-management programme.

Combine treatment with crop rotation, field sanitation, removal of heavily infested roots where appropriate, management of volunteer host plants, use of clean planting material and improvement of soil structure. Avoid moving infested soil or plant material between production blocks. Where practical, use resistant or tolerant varieties and maintain records of crop history, nematode diagnosis, treatment dates and follow-up observations.

This helps identify recurring problem areas and evaluate the effectiveness of the management plan. Agroduka product information describes Farmchance as having nematicidal and nematostatic activity and lists a reference application rate of 20ml per 20 litres of water or 2 litres per hectare. Use these figures only if they match the current approved Kenyan label for the supplied bottle.

The label is the authority for the exact dose, water volume, soil placement, crop stage, application method, treatment interval, maximum number of applications and worker-protection requirements. Do not increase the concentration, shorten the interval or apply the product to an unlisted crop. Do not mix Farmchance with another pesticide, fertiliser or adjuvant unless the label expressly permits the combination.

Before purchase, check that the bottle is sealed, genuine, correctly labelled and within its expiry date. Confirm PCPB(CR)1403 and fosthiazate 250 g/L on the actual label. The product photograph is for identification; the current Kenyan label supplied with the bottle is the authority for approved use, application, storage, personal protective equipment, first aid and disposal.

If the label is missing, damaged or inconsistent with the expected registration, do not use the product until the supplier or PCPB has clarified the discrepancy. Farmchance is classified by PCPB as WHO Class II, moderately hazardous. Wear the personal protective equipment specified on the label and avoid contact with eyes and skin.

Do not inhale vapour or spray mist. Keep workers, children, pets and livestock away during preparation and application. The register lists a 12-hour re-entry interval; follow this and any additional label instructions before allowing people back into treated areas.

Protect food, drinking water, animal feed and medicines from contamination. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. After application, clean equipment and protective clothing according to the label and wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly.

Store Farmchance in its original container, tightly closed, in a secure, cool, dry and well-ventilated pesticide store. Keep it away from food, animal feed, medicines, children and unauthorised persons. Never transfer the concentrate into an unlabelled bottle or reuse an empty pesticide container.

Dispose of unused product and packaging through approved pesticide-waste arrangements; do not pour residues or wash water into drains, rivers, ponds, wells or irrigation channels. Keep the batch number, purchase record and expiry date for traceability. Regular root inspections and follow-up monitoring help growers assess whether nematode pressure is declining and whether additional soil-management measures are required.

Use Farmchance only as part of a planned programme and in accordance with the approved Kenyan label.
