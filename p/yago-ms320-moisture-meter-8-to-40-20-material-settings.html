For farmers, grain handlers, produce buyers, stores and small processors, moisture checking is an important part of deciding whether a batch needs further drying or whether it is suitable to move into the next stage of handling. Care, cleaning & storage

Why moisture checking matters in Kenya Moisture management affects the quality and storage life of many agricultural commodities. Grain that is stored too wet can develop mould, heat, unpleasant odours and quality losses. A moisture reading can help a farmer decide whether more drying is required before bagging or storage. Traders and aggregators can use repeated readings to compare deliveries and identify batches that need closer inspection. For maize, beans and other crops, follow the recommended safe-storage moisture guidance for that specific commodity and local conditions. The MS320 is a checking aid; it does not dry grain, remove mould or guarantee that a store is pest-free. Care, handling and storage The meter is a precision checking instrument and should be handled carefully. Keep it dry, protect the display from impact and store it in a clean case or dry location when not in use. A meter can support more consistent records when used in the same way each time. It should be combined with clean drying surfaces, suitable ventilation, proper bagging and dry storage rather than treated as a replacement for good post-harvest management.
