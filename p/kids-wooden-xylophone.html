MUSIC PLAY AND EARLY RHYTHM

A wooden xylophone gives children a hands-on introduction to sound, rhythm and simple musical patterns. An adult can demonstrate gentle striking, play a short repeated sequence and invite the child to copy it. The exact number of bars, mallets, tuning and dimensions should be confirmed from the product package because these specifications are not stated in the listing title. Inspect the instrument for loose parts, splinters and damaged mallets before use. Encourage controlled play on a stable surface and store the mallets safely after each session. Supervise young children and follow the manufacturer's age recommendations.
