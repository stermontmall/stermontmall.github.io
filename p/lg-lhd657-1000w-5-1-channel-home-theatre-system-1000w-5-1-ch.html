What 5.1 Channel means

The 5.1 in 5.1 channel stands for five main speakers and one subwoofer. In this system, the four tall floor-standing speakers and the centre speaker are the five main channels, and the powered subwoofer is the .1 channel that handles deep bass. This arrangement lets sound come from different directions around you, so a helicopter can seem to fly across the room and a crowd can sound as if it surrounds you.

The speakers

  • Four tall floor-standing speakers: placed at the front and rear of the room, they carry music and movie effects across the room and give a wide, immersive soundstage
  • Centre speaker: handles dialogue and voices so that speech stays clear
  • Powered subwoofer: delivers deep bass for explosions, action scenes, music beats and football crowd noise

1000W OF POWER

With 1000W of power, the system is made to fill a living room or lounge with sound. Higher power gives you more headroom, which means the system can play loud without distortion when you want a big sound.

Great for

  • Movie nights at home
  • Music at parties and family gatherings
  • Football and other live sport
  • Gaming and TV shows
  • Church, small events and gatherings in large rooms

Setup tips

  • Place the centre speaker below or above the TV, facing the seating area
  • Put the front tower speakers on either side of the TV
  • Place the rear speakers behind or beside the seating area
  • Put the subwoofer on the floor near the front, and try different spots for the best bass
  • Keep speakers away from walls and corners that muddy the sound
  • Use proper speaker cables and follow the manual for connections

Care

Keep the system in a ventilated place away from water, heat and direct sun. Turn the volume down before switching on. Use a surge protector to guard against power spikes.
