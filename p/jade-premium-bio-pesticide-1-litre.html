Integrated Pest Management Tips

Biological products usually work best when applied early, while pest numbers are still low, so scout your crop every week. Spray in the cool of the morning or evening, cover both sides of the leaves and repeat as directed on the label. Avoid mixing with strong chemical products unless the label allows it, and keep spray equipment clean. Encourage ladybirds, lacewings and other helpful insects by avoiding unnecessary broad-spectrum sprays, remove weeds that host pests and follow the interval before harvest stated on the label.
