About Absolute 375SC Systemic Fungicide (Greenlife)

Absolute 375SC Systemic Fungicide by Greenlife Crop Protection Africa

Absolute 375SC is a systemic fungicide that protects crops before infection and treats diseases already on the plant. It combines three active ingredients in one product: Azoxystrobin 200 g/L, Difenoconazole 125 g/L and Hexaconazole 50 g/L. Together they give broad disease control on vegetables, fruit trees, cereals and ornamentals.

Why farmers choose Absolute 375SC

  • Three active ingredients in one product for broad disease control
  • Preventive and curative action
  • Absorbed by the plant and moves quickly through it, so protection reaches new growth
  • Suitable for vegetables, fruit trees, cereals and ornamentals
  • Genuine Greenlife product

How it protects your crop

Fungal diseases can spread fast in warm, humid weather. Because Absolute 375SC is systemic, the active ingredients are taken into the leaf and carried through the plant rather than sitting only on the surface. This means the product can stop an infection that has already started and also protect leaves from new infection. Using three different active ingredients also supports resistance management compared with relying on a single one.

When to use it

Start your spray programme early, before disease is widespread, and keep scouting your crop. Preventive sprays work best when disease pressure is rising, for example after prolonged leaf wetness or heavy dews. If you already see spots, mildew or rust, spray promptly, because curative action works best on early infections.

Pack sizes

50 ml | 100 ml | 250 ml | 1 L

How to use

Always follow the dosage and pre-harvest interval on the product label. Wear protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and keep out of reach of children. Spray in calm weather, early morning or late afternoon, and do not spray if rain is expected soon. Do not let spray or rinse water reach streams, ponds or fish.

Storage

Keep in the original closed container in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.

Safe use of Original Absolute 375SC Systemic Fungicide (Greenlife)

  • Read the whole label before you buy or mix. The label is the legal instruction for crops, pests, rates and safety.
  • Look for the PCPB registration number on the pack. Do not buy chemicals that carry none.
  • Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI) printed on the label, the number of days that must pass between the last spray and harvest. Use the product only on the crops the label lists.
  • Wear gloves, a mask or respirator, eye protection and long protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards.
  • Keep chemicals in their original container, out of reach of children, pets and livestock. Never reuse empty containers for food or water.
