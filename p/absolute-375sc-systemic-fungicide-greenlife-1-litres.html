Absolute 375SC Systemic Fungicide by Greenlife Crop Protection Africa Absolute 375SC is a systemic fungicide that protects crops before infection and treats diseases already on the plant. It combines three active ingredients in one product: Azoxystrobin 200 g/L, Difenoconazole 125 g/L and Hexaconazole 50 g/L. Together they give broad disease control on vegetables, fruit trees, cereals and ornamentals. WHY FARMERS CHOOSE ABSOLUTE 375SC • Three active ingredients in one product for broad disease control • Preventive and curative action • Absorbed by the plant and moves quickly through it, so protection reaches new growth • Suitable for vegetables, fruit trees, cereals and ornamentals • Genuine Greenlife product HOW IT PROTECTS YOUR CROP Fungal diseases can spread fast in warm, humid weather. Because Absolute 375SC is systemic, the active ingredients are taken into the leaf and carried through the plant rather than sitting only on the surface. This means the product can stop an infection that has already started and also protect leaves from new infection. Using three different active ingredients also supports resistance management compared with relying on a single one. WHEN TO USE IT Start your spray programme early, before disease is widespread, and keep scouting your crop. Preventive sprays work best when disease pressure is rising, for example after prolonged leaf wetness or heavy dews. If you already see spots, mildew or rust, spray promptly, because curative action works best on early infections. PACK SIZES 50 ml | 100 ml | 250 ml | 1 L Choose the size that matches your acreage. Small packs suit kitchen gardens and trial plots, while the 1 litre pack suits commercial growers and larger farms. HOW TO USE Always follow the dosage and pre-harvest interval on the product label. Wear protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and keep out of reach of children. Spray in calm weather, early morning or late afternoon, and do not spray if rain is expected soon. Do not let spray or rinse water reach streams, ponds or fish. STORAGE Keep in the original closed container in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
