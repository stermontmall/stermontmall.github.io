Crop Nutrition and Product Selection

A foliar fertilizer or biostimulant is one part of a complete crop-management programme. It does not replace soil testing, basal or top-dressing fertilizer, irrigation, drainage or pest management. Identify the crop, growth stage and problem before choosing a product.

Weak growth, pale leaves, poor flowering or uneven fruit development can have several causes, including nutrient imbalance, water stress, root problems, soil pH, pests or disease. If the cause is uncertain, seek advice from a qualified agronomist or agricultural extension officer.

Practical Crop Management

Plan nutrient applications alongside regular field scouting and good agronomy. Keep records of the crop, planting date, soil or leaf test results where available, products used and observed response. Apply only when the crop need and label instructions support it.

Avoid spraying in intense heat or when wind could carry spray away; follow the label for weather restrictions. Use clean equipment and measure with suitable tools. Maintain equipment as directed and prevent wash water from reaching drains, streams, ponds or irrigation sources.

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Use the protective clothing and gloves specified on the label. Avoid contact with eyes and skin and avoid breathing mist or vapour. Keep products away from children, pets, livestock, food, animal feed and drinking water.

Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling; wash hands after use. Store tightly closed in the original labelled container, under the conditions stated by the manufacturer. Never transfer it into an unmarked bottle or reuse an empty container.

Manage spills, leftover product and packaging according to the label and local guidance. If accidental exposure or irritation occurs, follow the label's first-aid instructions and seek medical advice where needed.
