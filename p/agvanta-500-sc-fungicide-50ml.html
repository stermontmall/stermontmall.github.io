Product Overview & Key Benefits

Agvanta 500 SC Fungicide 50ml is a dual-active fungicide containing azoxystrobin 250 g/L and flutriafol 250 g/L in a suspension concentrate formulation. Kenya's Pest Control Products Board crop register lists AGVANTA 500 SC under PCPB(CR)1741. Its registered uses cover several important crop diseases: Northern and Southern leaf blight, grey leaf spot and leaf rust on maize; powdery mildew and Botrytis in roses; rust in carnations; early and late blight in tomatoes; powdery mildew and leaf spots in peppers; rust in soybeans; yellow rust and stem rust in wheat; and rice blast in rice.

This page is specifically for one 50ml pack. The selected pack should be matched to the farm's label-based requirement and not confused with other sizes or fungicide formulations. ACTIVE INGREDIENTS AND FORMULATION Agvanta 500 SC combines azoxystrobin 250 g/L with flutriafol 250 g/L.

Azoxystrobin belongs to the QoI fungicide group, while flutriafol is a triazole fungicide. The combination gives the product two different modes of action, but it does not remove the need for resistance management. The SC designation means suspension concentrate.

Confirm both active ingredients and their concentrations on the original container. Do not confuse Agvanta 500 SC with products that contain only azoxystrobin, only flutriafol or a different concentration. It is a concentrate, not a ready-to-use spray; preparation and application must follow the current Kenyan label.

KENYAN REGISTRATION AND APPROVED USES The PCPB crop register lists Agvanta 500 SC as PCPB(CR)1741 and specifies the registered crop and disease uses. The maize uses include Northern and Southern leaf blight, grey leaf spot and leaf rust. The flower uses include powdery mildew and Botrytis in roses and rust in carnations.

The vegetable uses include early and late blight in tomatoes and powdery mildew and leaf spots in peppers. The register also lists rust in soybeans, yellow rust and stem rust in wheat, and rice blast in rice. These uses are crop-specific.

A registration for one crop does not automatically authorize treatment of another crop or disease. Check the crop, disease, growth stage and current label before purchase and before use. The PCPB register states a 12-hour restricted-entry interval with work wear and chemical-resistant gloves.

It also gives crop-specific pre-harvest intervals: three days for tomatoes and peppers, 21 days for soybeans, 35 days for wheat, seven days for maize and 28 days for rice. Always verify the current label for the exact crop, any updated restrictions and additional personal protective equipment. Do not transfer a pre-harvest interval from one crop to another.

50ML PACK SIZE AND BUYING FIT This listing is for one 50ml container. It is not another pack size. Buyers should check the net content printed on the delivered bottle and compare it with the order before opening.

The right quantity depends on the approved label rate, crop area, disease pressure, crop stage, water volume and the permitted treatment programme. Bottle size alone does not determine the area covered. A small pack can be useful for a limited treatment requirement, while a larger pack may suit a farm with a broader approved programme.

Select only the quantity required by the label and avoid storing excess concentrate unnecessarily. DISEASE IDENTIFICATION AND RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT Accurate disease identification is important before selecting a fungicide. Leaf spots, blights, rusts and powdery mildew can have different causes and may require different management.

Inspect the crop and confirm the disease with a qualified agronomist where symptoms are uncertain. Agvanta's two active ingredients do not mean that it should be applied repeatedly without a plan. Follow resistance-management guidance, rotate with fungicides from different FRAC groups when recommended, and avoid unnecessary repeat applications.

Good field hygiene, removal of infected crop residues, adequate crop spacing, balanced nutrition and monitoring can support an integrated disease-management programme. Use Agvanta only for the crop and disease combinations listed on the current Kenyan label.
