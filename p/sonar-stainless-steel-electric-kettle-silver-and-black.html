How to Use

Fill the kettle with clean water, staying between the minimum and maximum marks, close the lid firmly and place the kettle on its base. Switch on and watch the red light. When the water boils, lift the kettle off the base to pour. Never switch it on empty or overfill it.

Care and Cleaning

Unplug and let the kettle cool before cleaning. Wipe the steel with a soft damp cloth and dry it to keep it shiny. Never put the base in water. To remove limescale, boil water with white vinegar, leave it for a short while, then rinse well and boil clean water twice before drinking. Empty the kettle after use.

Safety Tips

Keep the kettle on a flat, dry surface away from the edge of the counter and out of reach of children. The steel body and steam become very hot, so use the handle only. Use the kettle only with its own base and follow the user manual.
