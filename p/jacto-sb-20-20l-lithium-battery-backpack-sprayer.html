The Jacto SB-20 is a battery-powered backpack sprayer designed to reduce the physical effort associated with manual lever pumping. Its 20-litre format makes it suitable for planned crop-protection work in farms, orchards, nurseries and other agricultural settings where a backpack sprayer is the appropriate application tool.

Battery operation can make a difference during longer spraying sessions. Keep spare seals and approved replacement parts available where the manufacturer recommends them. A battery-powered pump can be useful for maintaining a more consistent spray pattern, but the nozzle remains critical. Select a nozzle that matches the product label and target. Care, cleaning & storage

The battery system must still be charged, inspected and maintained correctly. Read the supplied manual for charging instructions, battery care, operating limits and storage recommendations. Check for a clean, even spray pattern using water before introducing chemicals. A worn, damaged or blocked nozzle can cause uneven distribution, excessive drift or incorrect application rates. Always protect nearby people, pollinators, livestock, fish, water sources and sensitive crops from spray drift. Before each session, inspect the tank, cap, filter, hose, lance, trigger valve, straps and battery compartment. Confirm that all seals are correctly seated and that there are no leaks. Charge the battery in a dry, ventilated location away from direct heat. Clean the filter and nozzle carefully, avoid damaging small openings, and allow components to dry before storage. Keep the battery stored as directed by the manufacturer, and store the sprayer away from children, food and animal feed.

Safety information
