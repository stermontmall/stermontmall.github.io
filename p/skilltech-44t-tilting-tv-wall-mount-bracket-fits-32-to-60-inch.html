Why Use a Tilting TV Wall Mount

Mounting your TV on the wall frees up floor and furniture space, gives a clean modern look and helps keep the screen out of reach of small children. A tilting bracket is especially useful when the TV is mounted higher than eye level, in a bedroom, restaurant or lounge, because you can angle the screen downward for comfortable viewing and to reduce reflections from windows and lights.

Installation Tips

Check that your TV size and weight suit the bracket, and confirm the hole pattern on the back of your TV before buying. Choose a strong wall, such as solid concrete or brick, and use suitable plugs and screws for your wall type. Mark the position with a pencil, use the spirit level to keep the bracket straight, and drill carefully, avoiding hidden pipes and electrical wires. Ask a second person to help you lift the TV onto the bracket, because TVs can be heavy and fragile.
