IMPACT DERMI ROLL YELLOW STICKY TRAP – 15CM X 100M IN KENYA

The Impact Dermi Roll Yellow Sticky Trap is a long adhesive monitoring roll measuring 15cm wide by 100m long. It is designed for use in suitable crop-growing environments such as greenhouses, nurseries and protected horticulture, where monitoring flying insect pests can help growers understand pest pressure over time. Yellow sticky surfaces are commonly used to monitor insects such as whiteflies and aphids, and may also catch other flying pests depending on the crop, environment and insect species present.

WHY USE A YELLOW STICKY ROLL

Yellow is visually attractive to several groups of small flying crop pests. When insects land on the adhesive surface, they remain attached and can be counted during inspections. A roll format allows the user to cut or position a length that suits the monitoring layout, subject to the manufacturer's instructions. This can be useful in larger growing areas where multiple monitoring points are needed. Sticky rolls are monitoring and trapping tools; they do not replace crop scouting, identification, sanitation or a broader integrated pest-management plan.

SUITABLE GROWING ENVIRONMENTS

Yellow sticky monitoring rolls may be used in vegetable production, nurseries, ornamental crops and other protected growing areas where target flying insects are present. The exact placement should be adjusted to crop height, plant growth stage, airflow and the pest being monitored. In greenhouses, growers may place monitoring points near crop rows, vents or entry areas where pest movement is expected. Avoid allowing the roll to become entangled with leaves, irrigation equipment or workers. The roll should be positioned so that it can be inspected and replaced safely.

HOW TO PLAN MONITORING

Before installation, map the growing area and identify zones that need monitoring. Use a consistent placement approach so that catch counts can be compared from one inspection to the next. Record the date, location, crop, trap position and number or type of insects observed. Check traps regularly and replace sections when the adhesive is covered with insects, dust or debris, or when the supplier's service guidance says replacement is due. Do not compare counts from traps with very different exposure or placement without noting the difference.

INTERPRETING CATCHES

A sticky roll can help indicate whether flying pest activity is increasing, decreasing or concentrated in a particular part of the growing area. It cannot always identify insects to species, and a high catch does not by itself determine the best treatment. Inspect affected plants for feeding damage, eggs, larvae or other symptoms and seek identification support when necessary. Use catch records alongside crop scouting and local agronomic advice. Avoid making pesticide decisions from a single trap count.

INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT

Monitoring is most useful when it informs prevention and timely action. Inspect incoming plants and planting materials, remove heavily infested plant debris where appropriate, manage weeds that may host pests and maintain good greenhouse hygiene. Review ventilation and crop layout where these affect pest movement. Where a crop-protection product is considered, confirm that it is registered and labelled for the crop and target pest, then follow all directions and pre-harvest intervals. Sticky traps should not be described as a replacement for every control method.

HANDLING AND

Storage & handling

Keep the adhesive roll protected from dust, dirt and accidental contact before use. Handle carefully to avoid sticking the adhesive to clothing, tools or skin. Store the roll in its original packaging in a clean, dry place and follow any temperature or storage guidance from the manufacturer. Dispose of used adhesive material according to local waste procedures and the supplier's instructions. Keep it away from children, pets and non-target wildlife.
