Easygro Vegetative for foliage-stage nutrition

Easygro Vegetative is a soluble crystalline fertiliser listed with an NPK 27:10:16 formulation, trace elements and biostimulants. Its higher nitrogen proportion makes it relevant to crop stages when the nutrition plan calls for additional support for vegetative growth. The 1kg pack suits measured foliar or fertigation use.

Choose the Vegetative formulation only where it matches the crop's nutrient requirement. Excess nitrogen can encourage unwanted vegetative growth or disturb the balance with other nutrients. Use soil tests, crop stage and label directions to determine whether this blend is suitable for the field.

  • Check that the pack says Easygro Vegetative 27:10:16.
  • Measure the recommended quantity rather than estimating by eye.

How to compare Easygro formulations

Easygro Starter and Easygro Vegetative are different formulations for different crop nutrition stages. The Starter grade carries a different NPK balance, while Vegetative is higher in nitrogen. They should not be treated as interchangeable products simply because both dissolve in water and come in 1kg packs.

Before ordering, compare the formulation printed on the pack with the programme recommended for your crop. Keep the powder dry, reseal the bag after use and follow the label for mixing, timing and repeat intervals.

  • Use the formulation that matches the current crop stage.
  • Do not mix with other products unless compatibility is confirmed.
