What are UV-A fly-killer replacement tubes used for?

UV-A fluorescent lamps are used in compatible electric insect light traps. The lamp provides the ultraviolet light source used by the trap to attract flying insects towards its capture area. These replacement tubes are intended only for equipment designed for the same lamp type, wattage, length and base. They may be used in compatible fly-control equipment in shops, restaurants, food preparation areas, warehouses and other premises where an electric insect trap is installed. Follow the equipment manufacturer's placement and maintenance instructions.

Safe replacement and handling

  1. Switch off and unplug the insect killer.
  2. Allow the old lamp to cool completely.
  3. Handle fluorescent glass carefully and do not force the pins.
  4. Install only a lamp matching the appliance's specified wattage, length and base.
  5. Refit protective covers before reconnecting power.

Follow the appliance manufacturer's instructions and dispose of used fluorescent lamps through an appropriate local collection route.
