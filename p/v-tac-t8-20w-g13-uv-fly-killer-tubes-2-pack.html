UV fly-killer replacement tubes for commercial and home insect traps

UV-A blacklight tubes are replacement lamps used in compatible electric insect light traps. They attract flying insects towards the trap's capture mechanism and are used in suitable kitchens, food-service premises, shops, warehouses and homes.

Check compatibility before ordering

This is a 20W T8 tube measuring 605mm with a G13 base. It is much longer than 10W tubes measuring approximately 330–345mm. Check the existing lamp's wattage, total length, diameter, pin spacing and the insect-killer manufacturer's instructions. Do not assume a 10W fitting accepts a 20W tube.

Safe installation

Switch off and unplug the insect killer, allow the lamp to cool, handle fluorescent glass carefully and follow the equipment manufacturer's replacement instructions. Refit protective covers before reconnecting power.
