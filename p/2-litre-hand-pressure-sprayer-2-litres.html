KEY FEATURES • 2 litre tank with clear sides, so you can see the level at a glance • Measuring marks from 500 ml to 1750 ml help you mix small, accurate volumes • Steel pump rod built for repeated pumping • Adjustable brass nozzle to change the spray pattern for the job • Compact and light enough to carry in one hand • Made in Kenya HOW TO USE IT Fill the tank to the level you need, using the marks on the side, and add your product as directed on its label. Do not fill above the top mark, because the tank needs air space to build pressure. Close the cap firmly, pump the handle until you feel resistance, then spray evenly, moving steadily so every surface is covered. Pump again when the spray weakens. WHAT IT IS GOOD FOR The sprayer suits garden pests, foliar feeds, disinfectants and household pest control. Its small size makes it handy for kitchen gardens, flower beds, nurseries, greenhouse corners, small poultry or livestock houses and spot treatments where a large knapsack sprayer would be too much.
