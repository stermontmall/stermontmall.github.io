How to use it

Fill the tank to the level you need, using the marks on the side, and add your product as directed on its label. Do not fill above the top mark, because the tank needs air space to build pressure. Close the cap firmly, pump the handle until you feel resistance, then spray evenly, moving steadily so that every surface is covered. Pump again when the spray weakens.

Before you start

  • Read the label of the product you plan to spray, and mix only the volume you need
  • Check that the cap closes firmly and the nozzle is clear
  • Where possible, use a separate sprayer for herbicides to avoid contaminating other crops

Safety

Wear gloves, a mask and eye protection when spraying chemicals, and release the pressure before opening the cap. Never eat, drink or smoke while spraying. Keep the sprayer and any chemical away from children.

Storage

Rinse the tank and nozzle with clean water after every use so that residue does not block the nozzle, then store the sprayer clean and dry, out of direct sun and away from children.
