About Lab Coat

A lab coat is a practical workwear layer for laboratory, inspection, cleaning and light-duty handling environments. This coat provides a simple outer garment that can help keep everyday clothing cleaner during routine tasks. It is suitable for workplaces that specify a coat as part of their dress or hygiene procedure, but it should not be treated as chemical splash protection unless the garment’s material and rating are confirmed for the substance involved. Select the correct size, follow the care label, and replace it when fabric, seams or fasteners are damaged.
