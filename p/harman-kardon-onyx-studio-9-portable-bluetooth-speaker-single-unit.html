Pair the speaker and position it for everyday listening

The Onyx Studio 9 is listed as a single portable stereo Bluetooth speaker. Pair it with a phone or tablet using the Bluetooth menu and follow the speaker's prompts in the supplied instructions. If it does not appear in the device list, check that it is in pairing mode, move the phone closer and disconnect any device that may already be connected. Bluetooth performance can be affected by distance, walls and nearby wireless equipment, so keep the source device within a practical range.

For a fuller listening experience, place the speaker on a stable, level surface with open space around it. A shelf, sideboard or table can work well; avoid placing it inside a closed cabinet or where fabric can cover the speaker. Adjust the volume on both the source device and speaker, and start at a moderate level when reconnecting so playback does not begin unexpectedly loud.

Understand the single-unit listing and look after the speaker

This product page describes one speaker, not a pair. If you want sound in multiple rooms or a two-speaker arrangement, check the manufacturer's instructions for supported pairing options before buying a second unit; do not assume that every speaker can be linked wirelessly. Confirm the supplied accessories and charging method with the seller if a particular cable or adapter is essential to your setup.

The listing does not state a water-resistance rating, so treat the speaker as an indoor electronic device unless the manual confirms otherwise. Keep it away from rain, splashes, steam, direct sunlight and excessive heat. Clean the exterior with a soft, dry cloth and follow the manufacturer's guidance for charging, storage and battery care. If sound becomes distorted, check the source volume and Bluetooth connection before assuming the speaker is faulty.
