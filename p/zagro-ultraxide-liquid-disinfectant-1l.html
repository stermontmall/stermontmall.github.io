Product overview

Zagro Ultraxide 1L Liquid Disinfectant for Farm Hygiene Zagro Ultraxide is a concentrated liquid disinfectant designed for farm biosecurity and hygiene programmes. The 1-litre pack is suitable for poultry keepers, livestock farmers, hatcheries, animal housing operators and agricultural facilities that need a disinfectant for routine cleaning and disease-prevention procedures. It is formulated with glutaraldehyde and a quaternary ammonium compound.

The exact formulation and label directions should always be checked on the container supplied.

Where Ultraxide is used

Ultraxide is intended for hard-surface disinfection in poultry and livestock environments. Typical areas include poultry sheds, hatchery rooms, animal pens, wash rooms, farm equipment, egg-storage areas, transport vehicles and other washable surfaces associated with animal production. It can form part of a farm's hygiene programme between production cycles and during routine cleaning schedules.

Remove visible dirt, litter, manure and other organic material before disinfection because heavy contamination can reduce the effectiveness of disinfectants.

Concentrated formula

Zagro product information describes Ultraxide as a broad-spectrum disinfectant based on glutaraldehyde and quaternary ammonium chemistry. The Kenyan-market product label reference identifies glutaraldehyde 150 g/L and benzalkonium chloride 100 g/L. Confirm the ingredients and concentrations printed on your own pack before use, because product variants and regional labels can differ.

Do not confuse Ultraxide with similarly named disinfectant products or with Ultraxide Plus.

Dilution and application

The Zagro product information lists different dilution guidance for different farm situations. Published directions include 1:150 for hatchery rooms, poultry and livestock buildings, wash rooms and farm equipment; 1:320 for egg-storage areas; and 1:400 for vehicles. General disinfection is listed in a range of 1:150 to 1:400.

These are reference figures from manufacturer product information, not a substitute for the instructions on the exact Kenyan pack. Always follow the supplied label for dilution, contact time, application method, protective equipment, ventilation and re-entry requirements. Never mix Ultraxide with other chemicals unless the label expressly permits it.

Safe handling

Read the full label and safety directions before opening or mixing. Wear the protective equipment specified by the manufacturer, avoid contact with skin and eyes, and do not inhale spray mist. Keep the concentrate in its original, closed container, away from children, animal feed and food.

Store in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place protected from direct heat and sunlight, and follow the label's storage temperature guidance. Do not apply to feed or drinking water unless the product label specifically authorises that use. Remove animals and birds from treatment areas where required, and ventilate treated buildings before allowing people or animals to re-enter.

Choosing the 1-litre pack

The 1L container is a practical pack for smaller farms, poultry units, animal-care facilities and customers who want to trial or replenish a farm-hygiene programme without purchasing a large drum. For larger operations, Zagro also lists 5L, 20L and larger pack formats in some markets. Availability of those sizes in Kenya should be confirmed separately; this listing is specifically for the 1-litre pack.
