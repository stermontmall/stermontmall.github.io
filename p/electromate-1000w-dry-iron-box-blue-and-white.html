How to Iron Well

Check the care label on each garment first. Start with delicate fabrics and synthetics at a low setting, then move to cotton and linen as the iron gets hotter. If you are ironing thick fabrics such as denim, give the iron a little extra time to reach the right heat. Iron school uniforms while they are slightly damp for sharper creases, and keep the iron moving so it does not stay too long on one spot.

Care and Safety

Always unplug the iron and let it cool fully before you store it. Wipe the soleplate with a soft damp cloth once it is cool, and avoid scratching it with zips, buttons or metal. Never leave a hot iron unattended, keep it out of reach of children and rest it on its heel when not in use. Do not immerse the iron in water, and follow the user manual.
