How to use

Wet your face with clean water. Dispense a small amount of cleansing foam into your palm and work it with water to create a lather. Gently massage the foam over the face using circular movements, taking care around the eyes and lips.

Rinse thoroughly with clean water and pat the face dry with a clean towel. Follow with skincare products suited to your skin, such as a moisturiser. In the morning, use sunscreen as the final step of your daytime skincare routine.

Use the cleanser as directed on the packaging. Avoid vigorous scrubbing, as rubbing too hard can irritate the skin. If you wear heavy or water-resistant makeup or sunscreen, you may need a suitable first cleansing step before using a water-based face wash.

Do not leave the foam on the face as a mask unless the manufacturer specifically instructs you to do so. WHO MAY CONSIDER THIS CLEANSER Garnier Even & Bright Cleansing Foam may suit adults looking for a straightforward daily facial wash and who prefer a foaming cleanser. It can be included in routines focused on cleansing away excess oil and improving the appearance of dull skin.

Skin types and sensitivities differ, so no cleanser is guaranteed to suit everyone. If your skin is very dry, sensitive, reactive or currently irritated, introduce any new cleanser cautiously and monitor how your skin responds. A BALANCED ROUTINE For a simple routine, cleanse gently, apply a serum if required, moisturise and protect the skin from the sun during the day.

Cleansing supports the rest of the routine by removing surface residue before leave-on products are applied. Avoid combining too many exfoliating products at once, especially if your routine already includes acids, retinoids or acne treatments. If you have persistent acne, eczema, pigmentation concerns or another skin condition, a qualified dermatologist or healthcare professional can help you choose appropriate products.

WHAT TO EXPECT A facial cleanser can help the skin feel fresh and clean after washing, but it should not be expected to erase dark marks, cure acne or change natural skin colour. The appearance of skin tone is influenced by many factors, including sun exposure, hormones, genetics and skincare habits. Consistent gentle cleansing and daily sun protection can support a healthy-looking routine, while visible changes vary between individuals.
