Product overview

Agita 10 WG Fly Bait 80g is a water-dispersible granule public-health insecticide containing thiamethoxam 100 g/kg and the fly attractant cis-9-tricosene 0.50 g/kg. Kenya's Pest Control Products Board public-health register lists AGITA 10 WG under PCPB(CR)0369. The registered uses include control of houseflies and cockroaches in homes, hotels, butcheries, abattoirs, stables, poultry houses, pigsties, dairies and industrial buildings. This listing is for one 80g pack. It is intended for buyers who need a registered fly-bait product for a suitable site and who can follow the exact directions and restrictions on the approved Kenyan label. ACTIVE INGREDIENTS AND FORMULATION Agita 10 WG combines thiamethoxam, an insecticide active ingredient, with cis-9-tricosene, a fly attractant. The product is formulated as water-dispersible granules. The WG designation is important: it is not a ready-to-use aerosol, dust or liquid concentrate. The product must be prepared or placed only as directed on the current label. Check the product name, active ingredients and net weight on the original packaging before purchase. Do not confuse Agita 10 WG with Agita 1 GB granular bait. Agita 1 GB has a different formulation and concentration and is registered for different use conditions. The product name and formulation must match the intended treatment. KENYAN REGISTRATION AND APPROVED USE The PCPB public-health register identifies AGITA 10 WG as PCPB(CR)0369, containing thiamethoxam 100 g/kg plus cis-9-tricosene 0.50 g/kg. The register lists control of houseflies and cockroaches in homes, hotels, butcheries, abattoirs, stables, poultry houses, pigsties, dairies and industrial buildings. This registration is site-specific. It does not mean the product may be applied directly to livestock, poultry, food, animal feed, milk, meat, food-contact surfaces or water. WHERE AGITA 10 WG FITS Houseflies can be a nuisance and a hygiene concern in food premises, animal housing and other buildings. Agita 10 WG should be used as one part of integrated pest management, not as a substitute for sanitation or exclusion. Identify the pest and inspect the premises before selecting a control method. Safety information

Read and follow the complete, current product label and safety data sheet before opening or using this pesticide. Confirm that the product, formulation, intended crop or site, target pest or disease and application method are approved for the intended use. Do not guess a dose, dilution, tank mix, application interval or treatment method, and never use the product for an unlisted purpose.

Wear all personal protective equipment specified on the label and safety data sheet during handling, measuring, mixing, loading, application and equipment cleaning. Avoid contact with skin and eyes and avoid inhaling dust, vapour or spray mist. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. Wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly after work, and remove and launder contaminated clothing separately.

Keep children, animals and unprotected people away from the product and treated areas. Prevent spray drift, spills and run-off. Do not contaminate food, animal feed, medicines, drinking water, drains, ponds, rivers or neighbouring crops. Observe the exact restricted-entry interval and pre-harvest interval stated on the approved label; do not re-enter or harvest earlier.

If exposure, a spill or suspected poisoning occurs, stop work, follow the first-aid and emergency instructions on the product label and safety data sheet, and seek prompt medical or professional advice. Keep the original container and label available for medical personnel. Do not induce vomiting or administer treatment unless instructed by the label or a qualified medical professional.

Storage & handling

Store the 80g pack in its original sealed packaging in a secure, dry and ventilated pesticide store. Protect it from moisture, heat and direct sunlight. Keep it separate from food, feed, medicines and household goods. Prevent access by children and unauthorized persons. Never transfer the granules into a food or beverage container. Do not reuse empty packaging. Follow the label and applicable Kenyan requirements for collection and disposal. Do not discard unused product or washings into drains, rivers, ponds or other water sources.
