About Bayer Baraki Pellets Rat and Mice Poison

Bayer Baraki Pellets Rat and Mice Poison (10 g sachet, 100 g, 1 kg box)

Rats gnaw wiring, foul food stores and spread disease, and a few quickly become many. Bayer Baraki Pellets are a ready-to-use rodenticide bait made for single-feed control: rats and mice need to eat the bait only once. The pellets contain difethialone, an anticoagulant, and need no mixing. Baraki comes as a 10 g sachet, a 100 g pack and a 1 kg box.

  • Single-feed anticoagulant bait for roof rats, Norway rats and house mice
  • Mould and moisture resistant, so it stays attractive in damp stores

Where it helps

Rodents chew wiring, sacks and packaging, eat and spoil stored food, and leave droppings and urine that spread germs. Roof rats climb into ceilings and stores, Norway rats burrow along walls and drains, and house mice nest in small hidden spaces, so the places to bait differ. Pellets that resist mould and moisture help in damp stores and farm buildings, where loose bait goes off quickly.

Placement amounts (no mixing)

  • Rats: 50 to 75 g per station (about 5 to 7 sachets of 10 g), one station every 5 to 10 m along walls, rat runs, burrows and sheltered areas
  • Mice: 15 to 20 g per station (about 2 sachets of 10 g), one station every 2 to 4 m along walls, runways and nests
  • A 100 g pack is about 10 sachets of 10 g, and a 1 kg box is about 100 sachets

How to use it

Inspect first for droppings, gnaw marks, runs and burrows, so you know where the rodents travel. Place the bait in covered bait stations or tamper-resistant boxes, never loose on the floor. Check every 2 to 3 days and top up until feeding stops. Wearing gloves, collect dead rodents and dispose of them safely.

Before you start

  • Block holes and gaps around pipes, doors and roof edges
  • Store grain, feed and food in closed containers, off the floor
  • Remove rubbish and clutter that give rodents shelter

Safety

This is a poison. Read the label before use and keep the bait out of reach of children, pets and poultry. Wear gloves and wash your hands after handling it. If a person or an animal swallows any, get medical or veterinary help immediately and show the pack.

Storage

Keep sachets and packs closed in a dry, locked place, away from food, animal feed and children.
