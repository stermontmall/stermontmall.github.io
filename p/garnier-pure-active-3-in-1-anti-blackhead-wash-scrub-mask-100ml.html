How to use

AS A DAILY WASH Wet your face with clean water. Squeeze a small amount of product into your palm and gently work it over the face, avoiding the delicate eye and lip areas. Rinse thoroughly with water and pat the skin dry.

Do not scrub aggressively or use more product than necessary. If your skin feels dry or tight after cleansing, follow with a suitable moisturiser. AS A SCRUB OR MASK For scrub use, gently massage the product over damp skin using light circular movements, then rinse thoroughly.

Avoid applying pressure or repeatedly scrubbing the same area. For mask use, follow the exact frequency and duration printed on the tube or carton; rinse completely after the recommended time. Do not assume that using all three methods every day will produce faster results.

Exfoliating products can irritate skin when used too often, so choose the method and frequency that suit your skin and the manufacturer's guidance. WHO MAY CONSIDER THIS PRODUCT Garnier Pure Active 3-in-1 may be considered by adults who want a multi-purpose cleanser for oily or blemish-prone skin and who prefer a wash, scrub and mask format in one product.
