Selecting and maintaining sprayer equipment

Before filling the tank, inspect the unit, hose, nozzle, straps and connections for damage or leaks. Confirm that the materials are compatible with the liquid you intend to use. Never fill it with fuel, solvents, corrosive liquids or any product not approved by the sprayer manufacturer.

When applying pesticides, use only products approved for the intended site and method.
