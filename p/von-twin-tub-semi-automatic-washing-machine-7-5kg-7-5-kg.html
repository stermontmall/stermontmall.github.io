Understand the 7.5 kg twin-tub laundry arrangement

The VON listing describes a 7.5 kg semi-automatic washing machine with two separate tubs: one for washing and another for spinning. It also lists two wash programmes, a wash-mode selector, a pulsator and a rust-free plastic body. Unlike a fully automatic washer, a twin-tub design requires the user to move clothes from the wash tub to the spin tub after the wash stage.

Consider the size of your usual laundry loads and the space available near a water point and drainage area. The catalogue does not state the machine's external dimensions, spin capacity or water consumption. Ask for the manufacturer's specifications if you need to fit it into a particular laundry area or plan to wash large bedding.

  • Measure the floor space and check access to water, drainage and a suitable power outlet.
  • Confirm the dimensions, included accessories and warranty paperwork before purchase.
  • Plan for manual transfer between the wash tub and spin tub.

Prepare the laundry area and follow the model instructions

Place the washer on a stable, level surface where splashes can be managed and the drain hose can be positioned as instructed. Follow the manual for water filling, detergent amounts, load limits and electrical connection. Separate garments by colour and fabric care requirements, and avoid overloading either tub. When using the spin tub, distribute the laundry as the instructions describe and close the lid before starting the spin cycle.

After each wash, remove lint where the manual indicates, rinse away detergent residue and leave the tubs open to dry if the manufacturer recommends it. The listing states a 12-month warranty; check which provider honours it, what proof of purchase is required and where service is available.
