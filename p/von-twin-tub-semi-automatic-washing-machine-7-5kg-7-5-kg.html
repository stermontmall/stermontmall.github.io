Why Choose a Twin Tub Washing Machine

Twin tub machines are a favourite in Kenyan homes because they are affordable, simple to use and use less water and electricity than many fully automatic machines. With one tub for washing and a separate tub for spinning, you can wash one load while spinning the previous one, which saves time on laundry day. Simple and Practical Laundry

Load your clothes into the wash tub, add water and detergent, choose the wash mode and program, and let the pulsator do the work. When the wash is done, move the clothes to the spin tub to remove water, which shortens drying time on the line. The rust-free plastic body is designed to last in humid conditions and is easy to wipe clean.

Care and Safety Tips

Place the machine on a level, dry floor near a water point and a socket. Do not overload either tub, and balance the clothes in the spin tub for smooth spinning. Close the lid before spinning and keep children away while the machine is working. Clean the lint filter after use, rinse the tubs and leave the lids open to dry to prevent bad smells. Use the right amount of detergent as advised on its pack.
