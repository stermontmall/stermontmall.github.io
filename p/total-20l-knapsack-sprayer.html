Product overview

Total 20L Knapsack Sprayer 4.5Bar (20 Litre) A good chemical is wasted if your sprayer drips, clogs or gives uneven coverage. The Total 20L Knapsack Sprayer (THSPP42002) carries a full 20 litre tank on your back, builds pressure up to 4.5 bar with the hand lever, and lets you switch the nozzle from a tight jet to a fine mist. It suits crop spraying, weed control, livestock-house disinfection and pest control around the home. KEY BENEFITS

  • 20 litre tank covers more ground per fill, with fewer refill trips
  • Adjustable nozzle: jet for reach, fine mist for even leaf coverage
  • Y-shaped trigger with a fibreglass lance that is light and corrosion-resistant
  • Light at about 2.6 kg, so it is comfortable to carry
  • Compatible with the insecticides, herbicides and disinfectants in our store WHERE YOU CAN USE IT
  • Crop protection sprays on vegetables, fruits and field crops
  • Weed control in fields and around buildings
  • Disinfecting poultry houses, livestock sheds and farm premises
  • Pest control sprays around the home and compound The larger tank is especially useful on bigger plots, where stopping often to refill wastes time and breaks your rhythm. MIXING AND APPLICATION GUIDE (20 L TANK) 1. Fill the tank about half full with clean water. 2. Add the measured chemical, then top up to the 20 L mark and shake well. 3. Pump to a steady working pressure. Do not exceed 4.5 bar. 4. Calibrate once: spray 20 L over a measured plot so you know how much ground one tank covers. Always follow the label of the chemical you are using for the dose, the timing and the re-entry period. CARE After each use, empty the tank, rinse it and spray clean water through the hose and lance. Keep a separate sprayer for herbicides if you can, since glyphosate residue can damage crops later. Check the nozzle and seals regularly, and store the sprayer in a shaded, dry place.

Safety information

Wear gloves, a mask, overalls and boots whenever you spray, and never use the tank for drinking water. Keep chemicals and the sprayer away from children. TECHNICAL SPECS
