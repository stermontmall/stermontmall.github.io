MIXING AND APPLICATION GUIDE (20 L tank)

1. Fill the tank about half full with clean water.

2. Add the measured chemical, then top up to the 20 L mark and shake well.

3. Pump to a steady working pressure. Do not exceed 4.5 bar.

4. Calibrate once: spray 20 L over a measured plot so you know how much ground one tank covers.

Care

After each use, empty the tank, rinse it and spray clean water through the hose and lance. Keep a separate sprayer for herbicides if you can, since glyphosate residue can damage crops later.

Safety

Wear gloves, a mask, overalls and boots whenever you spray, and never use the tank for drinking water.
