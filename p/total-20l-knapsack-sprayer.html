Brand: Total

The Total 20L Knapsack Sprayer is a manual backpack sprayer built for farmers, gardeners and pest control operators who need to cover large areas without constant refilling. With a generous 20 litre tank and a working pressure of up to 4.5 bar, it applies pesticides, herbicides, foliar fertilizers and disinfectants evenly across crops, lawns, animal housing and compounds. The sturdy teal tank with a grey screw cap is made to handle daily use on Kenyan farms, from vegetable plots in Kirinyaga to flower farms in Naivasha.

Operating the sprayer is simple. Fill the tank through the wide opening, close the cap, and work the side pump lever up and down to build pressure. Squeeze the trigger on the lance and a fine, even mist leaves the nozzle, so the chemical reaches both sides of the leaf with less waste. The long lance lets you spray tall crops and hard to reach corners while keeping your distance from the product. The flexible hose moves with you, and the moulded carry handle makes it easy to lift, fill and store.

Comfort matters when you spray for hours, so the set comes with two padded shoulder straps. They spread the weight of a full tank across your shoulders and back, and the flat profile of the tank sits close to your body. This reduces strain and helps you finish more rows in a day. The 20 litre capacity means fewer trips to the mixing point, saving time and chemical.

Care tips: Rinse the tank, hose and nozzle with clean water after every use, especially after herbicides, and store the sprayer upside down in a dry, shaded place. Never leave chemicals in the tank for long periods. Always wear gloves, boots, overalls and a face mask while spraying, and keep chemicals and equipment away from children.
