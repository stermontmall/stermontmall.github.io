How to Use

Place the subwoofer on the floor or a stable table and the two speakers on each side, then connect the cables and plug in the power. Switch on, press the mode button to choose Bluetooth, USB or SD, and pair your phone. Turn the volume knob up slowly, and use the bass knob to set the low sound you like.

Care and Cleaning

Unplug before cleaning. Wipe the cabinets with a soft dry or slightly damp cloth and never spray liquid on them. Keep the system away from water, direct sun and heat. Store the remote in a dry place and replace its battery when buttons stop responding.

Safety Tips

Do not play at very loud volume for long periods, because it can damage your hearing and the speakers. Keep the system on a stable surface, away from children and pets, and do not open the cabinets. Use a properly rated socket and follow the user manual.
