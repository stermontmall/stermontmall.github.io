Monitoring and handling

Place cards near plants or other monitoring points without allowing them to contact leaves unnecessarily. Replace cards when the adhesive surface is covered or no longer effective. Keep sticky cards away from children, pets and non-target wildlife. These are monitoring aids and should be used as part of an integrated pest-management programme.
