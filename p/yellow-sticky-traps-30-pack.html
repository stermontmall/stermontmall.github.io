YELLOW STICKY INSECT MONITORING TRAPS – 30 PACK IN KENYA

Yellow Sticky Insect Monitoring Traps – 30 Pack are adhesive cards designed to help detect and monitor selected flying crop pests in greenhouses, nurseries, gardens and other suitable growing areas. The bright yellow surface attracts insects that respond to the colour and holds them on the sticky adhesive. This listing identifies a 30-card pack with a double-sided adhesive surface. Sticky cards are monitoring tools and should be used alongside crop scouting and an integrated pest-management plan.

WHICH PESTS CAN YELLOW CARDS MONITOR

Yellow sticky cards are commonly used to monitor whiteflies, aphids, fungus gnats and leaf-miner flies. They may also catch other flying insects depending on the crop and local pest population. The exact target species and catch rate depend on card dimensions, colour, adhesive and placement. These cards do not identify insects automatically; captured specimens may need to be examined by someone familiar with crop pests. Do not assume that every insect caught is harmful to the crop.

WHY EARLY MONITORING HELPS

Flying pests can become established before visible crop damage is widespread. Sticky cards provide a simple way to observe activity over time and identify areas where closer inspection may be needed. A regular record of trap catches can help growers compare pest pressure between crop rows, greenhouse sections or inspection dates. The cards do not determine treatment thresholds on their own. Use them alongside leaf inspections, plant symptoms, weather observations and advice from an agronomist.

WHERE TO PLACE THE CARDS

Place cards in suitable monitoring locations near the crop canopy or in areas where flying pests are likely to enter. Keep them visible and accessible for inspection without allowing the adhesive surface to touch leaves, irrigation lines or equipment. Adjust the height as plants grow so that the cards remain positioned appropriately for the target insects. Avoid placing them where beneficial insects are likely to be trapped unnecessarily. Follow the supplier's instructions for placement and monitoring density.

INSPECT AND REPLACE

Check cards regularly and record the date, location and type or number of insects observed. Replace a card when the adhesive is covered with insects, dust or debris, or when it no longer holds insects effectively. A sudden increase in catches should prompt a closer crop inspection and review of nearby entry points or host plants. Compare results from cards installed in similar positions and over similar periods. Do not make pesticide decisions from a single card count.

USE WITH INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT

Sticky cards are one part of a broader crop-protection strategy. Combine them with inspection of incoming seedlings, sanitation, weed management and removal of infested plant material where appropriate. Where beneficial insects are used, select card colour and placement carefully to avoid unnecessary capture of natural enemies. If a pesticide is considered, confirm it is registered and labelled for the crop and target pest, and follow all application directions and pre-harvest intervals.

HANDLING AND

Storage & handling

The adhesive surface can stick to skin, clothing and other materials. Handle cards by their edges and keep them away from children, pets and non-target wildlife. Store unused cards flat in their packaging in a clean, dry place away from dust and direct sunlight. Dispose of used cards responsibly according to the supplier and local waste procedures. Do not wash or reuse cards once the adhesive is full or contaminated.
