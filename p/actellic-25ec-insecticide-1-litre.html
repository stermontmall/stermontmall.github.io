Actellic 25EC Insecticide (1 Litre) Actellic 25EC is an emulsifiable concentrate (EC) insecticide in a 1 litre bottle, supplied for pest control in stores and premises. It is a professional pack for farms, grain stores, warehouses and other premises where insects need to be kept under control. KEY FEATURES • Emulsifiable concentrate insecticide that mixes with water for spraying • 1 litre professional pack for stores and premises • Actellic brand product • Delivered to all 47 counties HOW IT IS USED As an emulsifiable concentrate, Actellic 25EC is diluted with water as directed on the label and applied as a spray. Typical uses are on the surfaces of empty stores, warehouses and other premises before stock goes in, and in places where crawling and flying insects gather. Always follow the label for the correct dilution, the surfaces to treat, and any waiting period before food, feed or people return to the area. PREPARING THE STORE Good hygiene makes any insecticide work better. Sweep out old grain and dust, repair cracks and holes where insects hide, and remove spilled produce before spraying. Allow surfaces to dry as directed on the label before storing grain or other produce. SPECIFICATIONS • Product: Actellic 25EC • Formulation: EC (emulsifiable concentrate) • Pack size: 1 litre SAFETY Read and follow the label before use. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands and face afterwards. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Do not contaminate food, feed, streams or ponds, and never reuse the empty container. STORAGE Keep in the original, tightly closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food, feed and direct sunlight. ORDER Pay by M-Pesa.
