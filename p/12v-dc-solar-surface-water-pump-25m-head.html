Product Overview & Key Benefits

The 12V DC Solar Surface Water Pump is a compact solar-powered surface pump listed with a 250W motor, a maximum head of 25 metres, maximum flow of 2,000 litres per hour and a 1-inch outlet. It is designed for buyers who want to move water using a low-voltage DC supply rather than relying on a conventional mains-powered pump. The supplier lists it for family water supply and irrigation systems and describes a brushless motor with automatic water-shortage protection.

Its surface-pump configuration means it is installed beside the water source, not lowered into the well like a submersible pump. The pump operates on 12V DC and has a listed power rating of 250W. Its 25m maximum head gives an indication of the maximum lifting height under the supplier's stated conditions.

The maximum flow of 2,000L/h is a separate maximum rating; actual flow falls as the required head, pipe length and system resistance increase. The 1-inch outlet is important when choosing the discharge hose and fittings. The brushless motor is listed as a low-consumption design, while the automatic water-shortage protection is intended to stop the pump when water is unavailable.

Where & How to Use in Kenya

This pump may suit a small water-transfer arrangement where the source is accessible from ground level and the required lift is within its rated range. Potential uses include moving water to a raised storage tank, supplying a small irrigation line or transferring water around a garden or homestead. The supplier's stated applications include family water supply and irrigation.

It should not be selected for a deep borehole because it is a surface pump and its suction capability is not specified in the available listing. SOLAR SYSTEM PLANNING Before ordering, establish whether the pump will connect directly to a solar panel, a battery system or a controller. The product listing confirms 12V DC and 250W but does not confirm which solar components are supplied.

Ask the seller for the required panel wattage, controller rating, recommended cable size and whether a battery is necessary for the intended duty. Solar output varies with sunlight, panel orientation, shading, weather and time of day. The system should be sized for the required operating hours and water volume, not just the motor rating.

HEAD, FLOW AND PIPEWORK Measure the vertical distance between the pump and the delivery point. Add the effect of the pipe route, bends, valves and outlet fittings when estimating total head. The listed 25m is the maximum head, not a promise of 2,000L/h at 25m.

Use a compatible 1-inch discharge line and avoid unnecessary restrictions. Ask the supplier for the pump curve if the installation requires a particular flow at a known lift height. Also confirm the maximum suction lift and the priming procedure before connecting the unit.

INSTALLATION AND

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Install the pump on a firm, level surface near the water source and protect it from flooding, rain exposure and physical damage. Use suitable strainers or filters where the water contains debris, subject to the manufacturer's instructions. Keep DC connections dry and secure.

Observe correct polarity and use electrical protection suitable for the system. Do not allow the pump to run without water unless the manufacturer confirms that the protection system covers the specific operating condition. Route cables safely and keep them away from sharp edges and hot surfaces.

MAINTENANCE AND

Buying & Pack-Size Guide

Inspect the inlet, outlet, hose and filter regularly. If flow decreases, check for a low water level, blocked strainer, air entering the suction line, leaks or excessive pipe resistance. Clean the pump only as directed by the manufacturer.
