How to Use

Fill the kettle with clean water between the minimum and maximum marks, close the lid, place it on its base and switch on. Lift it off the base to pour. Never switch it on empty and never overfill it, because boiling water can splash from the spout.

Care and Cleaning

Unplug and let the kettle cool before cleaning. Wipe the outside with a soft damp cloth and never put the base or cord in water. To remove limescale, boil a mixture of water and white vinegar, leave it for a short while, then rinse and boil clean water twice before drinking. Empty the kettle after use so that water does not stay inside for days.

Safety Tips

Keep the kettle on a flat, dry surface away from the edge of the counter and out of reach of children. Steam from the spout is very hot, so keep your hands and face away when pouring. Use the kettle only with its own base and follow the user manual.
