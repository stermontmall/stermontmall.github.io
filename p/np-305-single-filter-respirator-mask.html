Respirator selection and maintenance

The facepiece alone does not determine respiratory protection: the filter type, approval, fit and condition are essential. Identify the contaminant and concentration before selecting a cartridge, and verify that the NP 305 accepts the required filter model. Conduct the fit checks described by the manufacturer every time the respirator is worn. Facial hair or damaged seals can prevent an effective fit. Store the facepiece and cartridges as instructed, keep filters sealed when not in use and replace them according to the manufacturer schedule or sooner if damaged, contaminated or difficult to breathe through. Never use an air-purifying respirator in oxygen-deficient or unknown atmospheres.
