Product overview

The FS-08 Thermal Fogging Machine is a portable agricultural and pest-management fogger designed to disperse compatible liquid formulations as a fine visible fog. Kenyan retailers list the FS-08 model for pest-control, farm, greenhouse and sanitation work. This listing identifies the FS-08 machine itself; confirm the exact accessories, tank configuration, power and operating instructions supplied with the unit before purchase.

Use only formulations whose labels and equipment instructions expressly permit fogging. The FS-08 is described by Kenyan suppliers as a heat-based thermal fogging machine with rechargeable-battery ignition. Thermal foggers use heat to create an aerosol from a compatible formulation.

This differs from a cold ULV fogger, which atomises liquid mechanically without heating it. The two machine types are not interchangeable for every chemical or application. Confirm whether the treatment plan calls for thermal fogging or cold fogging, and check chemical compatibility before ordering.

The machine may be considered by professional pest-control operators, agricultural service providers, greenhouse managers and facilities teams who need portable fogging equipment. It is equipment only; chemical products, fuel, protective equipment and any additional accessories are not included unless specifically stated on the supplier quotation.

Selecting the right fogging equipment

Choose a thermal fogger only when the intended site, formulation and treatment method are suitable for thermal application. For indoor work, confirm that both the machine and the selected product are approved for that environment, and follow professional risk controls, ventilation and re-entry requirements. Petrol-powered or heat-producing equipment can create fire, burn and exhaust hazards.

Never assume that a disinfectant, insecticide or other liquid can safely be heated or aerosolised.

Before buying, compare the exact FS-08 model with the supplier's current specification sheet. Ask whether the machine includes its rechargeable ignition battery and charger, chemical tank, fuel tank, carrying accessories, funnel, spare seals and user manual. Confirm warranty coverage, spare-part availability and after-sales servicing.

Supplier listings can vary, so the included package should be confirmed in writing.

Safe operation and maintenance

Read the manufacturer's manual before assembly or use. Operate the machine only outdoors or in locations expressly permitted by the manual and the chemical label. Keep people, children, pets, food, feed and unprotected workers away from the treatment area.

Use the specified personal protective equipment and observe all label restrictions, exposure controls and re-entry intervals. Do not point hot outlets toward people, animals, combustible materials or flammable liquids.

Allow the machine to cool fully before inspection, cleaning, refuelling or storage. Check hoses, seals, tanks, ignition components and connections for wear or leaks. Use only the fuel and formulation types stated by the manufacturer.

Never mix chemicals or modify the machine to increase output. Clean and store the equipment exactly as instructed, and arrange servicing if it develops leaks, abnormal noise, poor output or ignition problems.
