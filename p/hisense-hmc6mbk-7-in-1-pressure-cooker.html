Seven ways to cook

Instead of keeping several pots and appliances, you can do pressure cooking, slow cooking, steaming, sauteing, rice cooking, warming and soup or stew making in one pot. Tough cuts of meat, beans and stews cook faster under pressure, while slow cooking suits dishes that benefit from a gentle, long simmer.

Save time and energy

Pressure cooking shortens cooking time for many dishes, which can also save gas or electricity. Preset timers help you plan meals, and the warm function keeps food ready to serve.

Safe to use

Integrated pressure control valves, a lid lock and overheat protection give peace of mind during cooking. Always follow the user manual, make sure the valve is clear and the lid is properly closed, and let the pressure release before opening.
