Responsible use and compatibility

Use fertilizer as part of an integrated crop plan that considers soil pH, organic matter, irrigation water, previous nutrient applications and expected crop removal. More fertilizer is not automatically better: excessive nutrients can waste money, injure crops or move into water systems. Check the product label for protective equipment, permitted crops, timing and precautions.

Do not mix products unless the manufacturer permits it.

Storage and handling

Keep the product in its original labelled bag or bottle, closed after use and stored in a dry, secure place away from direct sunlight, rain, food, animal feed, children and animals. Prevent moisture from entering soluble or granular fertilizers. Avoid excessive heat for liquid products.

Clean measuring equipment after use and never reuse fertilizer containers for drinking water or household storage.
