Product overview

Vanguard 525 WG Fungicide 40g is a water-dispersible granule fungicide supplied in a 40-gram pack. Kenyan supplier information identifies the active ingredients as cymoxanil 300 g/kg and famoxadone 225 g/kg. The supplier lists the product for management of early and late blight in potatoes and tomatoes, and downy mildew in crops including roses, snow peas and watermelon. This listing is for the 40g pack only. Always check the actual product label and current PCPB registration before purchase and use, because the approved crop, disease, rate, timing, pre-harvest interval and re-entry instructions on the supplied pack take precedence over online information. PRODUCT IDENTITY AND FORMULATION Vanguard 525 WG is a fungicide formulation in water-dispersible granule form. The WG designation indicates granules intended to disperse in water for application as directed by the label; it does not mean the product can be handled or mixed casually. The 40g pack is a small-format option for growers and crop-protection operators who need this specific formulation and pack size. Keep it in its original labelled packaging until use. Do not split, repackage or combine it with another product unless the label and applicable professional guidance allow it. ACTIVE INGREDIENTS AND MODE OF ACTION Supplier technical information lists cymoxanil at 300 g/kg and famoxadone at 225 g/kg. These are the stated formulation strengths, not a recommendation for a particular crop or spray programme. The supplier describes the combination as providing complementary activity, including protective and locally systemic or therapeutic activity. Farmers should use the product only for the diseases and crops stated on the current label, and should not assume that the formulation controls every fungal or fungal-like disease. DISEASE MANAGEMENT CONTEXT Early and late blight can affect potato and tomato crops, while downy mildew can be important in several horticultural crops. Symptoms, severity and disease pressure vary with crop variety, weather, field history and crop stage. Correct diagnosis matters: nutrient disorders, bacterial diseases, insect injury and environmental stress can resemble disease symptoms. Inspect the crop regularly, check both leaf surfaces and compare symptoms with reliable local agronomic advice before selecting a fungicide. A fungicide should form only one part of an integrated disease-management plan. Use healthy planting material, suitable varieties, crop rotation where practical, field sanitation, good drainage, appropriate plant spacing and irrigation practices that reduce prolonged leaf wetness. Remove infected crop residues where recommended and monitor the crop after treatment. Avoid repeated reliance on one mode of action. Rotate or combine fungicide groups only where the label and a qualified agronomist recommend it, observing resistance-management guidance. MIXING AND APPLICATION A Kenyan supplier page lists a reference dose of 10g per 20 litres of water for Vanguard 525 WG. This is a supplier-published reference, not a universal instruction for every crop or situation. Confirm that the rate applies to the crop, disease and use pattern on the pack you receive. Follow the label for water volume, spray coverage, mixing order, equipment calibration, application interval, maximum number of applications, rainfastness, re-entry period and pre-harvest interval. Do not increase the concentration or shorten the interval to compensate for poor disease diagnosis or inadequate coverage. Use calibrated equipment and clean water as directed. Prepare only the quantity required for the labelled application. Avoid spray drift onto neighbouring crops, people, livestock, beehives, homes, waterways and sensitive habitats. Do not spray in unsuitable weather or near harvest unless the label permits it. Wear the protective clothing and equipment specified on the label throughout mixing, application and equipment cleaning. RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT Fungal pathogens can develop reduced sensitivity when fungicides with similar modes of action are used repeatedly. Observe the label's resistance-management instructions and any FRAC guidance applicable to the active ingredients. Alternate with effective fungicides from different groups when advised, and integrate non-chemical practices. Do not assume that changing a trade name changes the mode of action. Keep records of product, active ingredients, treated field, date, crop stage and disease response. WORKER, CONSUMER AND ENVIRONMENTAL Product: Vanguard 525 WG Fungicide. Pack size: 40g. Safety information

Read the complete label before opening the pack. Keep unprotected people and animals away from mixing and application areas. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling pesticides. Avoid inhalation, skin contact and eye contact. Wash hands and exposed skin after handling, and wash contaminated clothing separately as directed. Observe the label's re-entry and pre-harvest intervals exactly; these intervals protect workers and consumers and must not be shortened. Prevent contamination of wells, rivers, ponds, drains, irrigation channels, food, feed and animal drinking water. Protect pollinators and other non-target organisms by following label restrictions on flowering crops, spray timing and buffer zones. Clean equipment only in a location and manner that prevents runoff into water bodies. Dispose of unused product and empty packaging according to label directions and Kenyan requirements. Never reuse pesticide packaging for food or household storage.

Storage & handling

AND FIRST AID Store the 40g pack in its original, sealed container in a cool, dry, secure pesticide store, away from food, feed, medicines, children, animals, heat and direct sunlight. Keep the label legible and retain batch and expiry information. Do not use product from damaged, unlabelled or expired packaging. In case of suspected exposure or poisoning, follow the label's first-aid instructions and seek prompt medical advice. Take the container or label when seeking assistance.
