Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

This is a pest-control or agricultural chemical product. Use it only for the crop, pest, site and application method expressly authorised on the current Kenya-approved product label. Read the full label before opening or use.

Follow the label exactly for personal protective equipment, dilution, application, ventilation, re-entry period, pre-harvest interval, environmental restrictions and any other precautions. Do not guess a dose, mix it with another product, or use it on food, animals, people or surfaces unless the label specifically permits that use. Keep the original container tightly closed and locked away from children, unauthorised persons and animals, and separate from food, drink and animal feed.

Prevent spills and contamination of drains, wells, streams and other water sources. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling; wash hands and exposed skin after handling. Never reuse an empty pesticide container; follow the label and approved local hazardous-waste collection or disposal instructions.

Product overview

BASF Goliath 0.05% Gel is a ready-to-use professional insecticidal bait for cockroach control. The 35g cartridge contains fipronil 0.05% and is registered in Kenya under PCPB(CR)0667. BASF Kenya identifies Goliath Gel for professional control of German, American and oriental cockroaches, while the Kenya Pest Control Products Board register describes it as a ready-to-use insecticidal gel bait for cockroach control by professional pest-control operators.

The gel format is intended for precise placement in label-approved cracks, crevices and other locations where cockroaches hide or travel. It is used as part of an integrated pest-management programme in homes, apartments, hotels, restaurants, offices, food-service premises, warehouses and other buildings where cockroach activity has been identified. The product is not a general-purpose spray and is not intended for mosquito, termite or rodent control.

BASF describes the product as a fast-acting bait with a cascade effect and low application rates. Actual results depend on pest species, infestation pressure, bait placement, competing food sources, sanitation and compliance with the label. Good cockroach management also involves inspection, cleaning, removal of food and water sources, sealing suitable entry points, monitoring and follow-up.

Gel bait should not be placed on food, food-contact surfaces, utensils, bedding, clothing or any surface where it may be washed or wiped away.

Use only as directed on the current Kenyan product label. The label specifies the approved use, placement rate, precautions and first-aid information. Do not increase the quantity or frequency beyond label instructions.

Avoid contaminating bait placements with cleaning products or insecticide sprays. Keep the cartridge in its original labelled packaging, securely stored away from children, pets, food, animal feed, heat and direct sunlight. Wear the protective equipment stated on the label, wash hands after handling and never transfer the product into an unlabelled container.

Goliath Gel is intended for trained pest-control professionals who require a targeted bait for cockroach management. Before purchase, check that the cartridge is sealed, genuine, correctly labelled and within its expiry date. Kenyan regulations prohibit distribution of unregistered, expired, counterfeit, smuggled or improperly packed pest-control products.

For commercial premises, baiting should be planned around inspection findings. A technician should identify cockroach harbourages, activity routes, moisture sources and food residues before deciding where gel placements are appropriate. Kitchens, food preparation rooms, service ducts, equipment voids, washrooms and storage areas can require different inspection and monitoring approaches.

The gel should be placed only at label-approved points and in a way that avoids contact with food, utensils, packaging and frequently washed surfaces. Where the label requires it, record the treatment location and date for the site service report.

A professional cockroach programme should also include housekeeping recommendations. Store food in closed containers, remove spills and crumbs, empty waste regularly, repair leaking taps and pipes, and reduce clutter that creates hiding places. Seal gaps around service penetrations where practical and safe.

Monitoring traps can help determine whether activity is declining and whether additional inspection is needed. If cockroaches continue to appear, do not simply add more bait; review placement, sanitation, competing food sources, species identification and the possibility of resistance or reinfestation.

The 35g cartridge is a convenient format for controlled placement by an operator who has the appropriate application equipment and training. Keep the nozzle clean and replace the cap after use as directed by the manufacturer. Do not leave the cartridge exposed in treatment areas.

Store unused product in its original container and follow the storage temperature, shelf-life and disposal instructions printed on the pack. Never pour unused bait into drains or watercourses. The product is toxic to aquatic life; prevent any release to the environment and follow the label and safety data sheet.
