Ridomil Gold MZ 68WG 1kg: active ingredients and formulation

Ridomil Gold MZ 68WG is a water-dispersible granule fungicide combining metalaxyl-M at 40 g/kg with mancozeb at 640 g/kg. The two active ingredients provide different roles in the formulation: the supplier describes metalaxyl-M as systemic and mancozeb as a contact protectant that forms a film on plant surfaces. This combination is intended for management of diseases caused by oomycete fungi, including late blight and downy mildew.
