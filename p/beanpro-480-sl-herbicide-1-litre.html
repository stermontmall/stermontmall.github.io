About Beanpro 480SL Herbicide – 1 Litre

Beanpro 480SL Herbicide (1 Litre) Beanpro 480SL is a selective herbicide for control of broadleaf weeds and grasses in bean fields, applied early post-emergence. It is a soluble liquid (SL) supplied in a 1 litre pack. KEY FEATURES • Selective herbicide for bean fields • Controls broadleaf weeds and grasses • Applied early post-emergence • Soluble liquid that mixes into water • 1 litre pack • Delivered to all 47 counties WHY WEED CONTROL IN BEANS MATTERS Beans are slow to cover the ground in the first weeks, which gives weeds a chance to grow and compete for light, water and nutrients. Heavy weed growth lowers pod set and yield, makes harvest harder and can cause quality problems. Controlling both broadleaf weeds and grasses early gives the crop the best start. WHAT EARLY POST-EMERGENCE MEANS Early post-emergence means you spray after the weeds and the crop have come up, while the weeds are still young. Young weeds take up the product more easily and are easier to control than large, established ones. Spraying too late can give poorer control and put more stress on the crop, so follow the crop and weed stages on the label. HOW TO USE IT Use a calibrated knapsack sprayer so that every part of the field gets the same, correct amount of spray. Follow the dose and water volume on the label. Avoid windy or rainy conditions, because wind can carry spray onto nearby crops and rain soon after spraying can wash the product off the leaves. Spray in calm weather, early in the morning or late in the afternoon. Do not spray when the crop is stressed by drought, waterlogging or disease. GOOD PRACTICE • Scout fields first and identify the weeds present • Prepare a clean seedbed and plant on time • Use hand weeding for any weeds that escape • Keep records of dates and doses SAFETY Read the label before use. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Do not let spray or rinse water reach streams, ponds or wells. Wash your hands and face after use, and rinse the sprayer well away from crops and water. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
