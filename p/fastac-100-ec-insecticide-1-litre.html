Product overview

Fastac 100 EC Insecticide 1 Litre is a BASF emulsifiable concentrate containing alpha-cypermethrin at 100 g/L. The Kenyan product label identifies registration number PCPB(CR)0167 and lists specific crop-protection uses: cottony cushion scale and leaf miner control in coffee, bean flower thrips control in French beans, and caterpillar control in roses and carnations. This listing is for one 1-litre bottle. It is suited to commercial growers, flower farms, coffee producers and agricultural buyers who have confirmed that the exact registered crop and pest use matches their operation. It should not be treated as an all-purpose insecticide for every crop or pest. ACTIVE INGREDIENT AND FORMULATION The active ingredient is alpha-cypermethrin 100 g/L. Fastac 100 EC is an emulsifiable concentrate and belongs to the pyrethroid insecticide group. BASF describes its mode of action as a sodium-channel modulator. The concentration and formulation are important when comparing products: Fastac 100 EC must not be confused with another alpha-cypermethrin brand, a different strength or a different formulation. The 1-litre bottle is a concentrate, not a ready-to-use spray. Preparation and application must follow the current Kenyan label, including the labelled rate, water volume, application equipment, maximum treatment number and safety precautions. KENYAN REGISTERED USES The product label and PCPB registration identify the approved Kenyan uses as follows: cottony cushion scale and leaf miner in coffee; bean flower thrips in French beans; and caterpillars in roses and carnations. BASF product information provides crop-specific guidance for these uses. For bean thrips, the label information lists 120ml in 600 litres of water per hectare, or 5ml per 20 litres for small-scale use, with a seven-day pre-harvest interval. For coffee leaf miner, it lists 80ml in 600–1,000 litres of water per hectare, or 3ml in 20 litres to cover 25 coffee bushes, with a three-day pre-harvest interval. For caterpillars in carnations and roses, the label gives a crop-specific range and application notes. These figures are included to help buyers identify the correct product label; always check the physical Kenyan label for the final instructions and do not transfer a rate from one crop to another. 1-LITRE PACK SIZE AND BUYING FIT This listing is for one 1-litre bottle. It is not the 250ml pack or any smaller container. The 1L size can suit a commercial farm or professional crop-protection operation that has calculated its requirement using the approved label and planned treatment area. A larger bottle does not mean the user should apply a higher concentration or treat crops beyond the approved uses. Compare the requested product, pack size and supplier quotation before placing an order. On delivery, confirm that the net content is one litre and that the bottle carries the Fastac 100 EC name, alpha-cypermethrin 100 g/L guarantee and Kenyan registration details. PRODUCT AUTHENTICITY CHECKS Check the BASF product name and the exact formulation on the original packaging. The label should be readable and should identify alpha-cypermethrin 100 g/L. Check the seal, batch number, manufacturing date and expiry date. BASF's Kenyan label states a two-year shelf life from manufacture when the product is kept in a tightly sealed original container under cool and dry storage conditions; the printed expiry information on the actual bottle remains the controlling reference. Do not accept a leaking, damaged, unlabelled or tampered container. If the label or pack size differs from the order, do not open or use the product; contact the supplier for clarification.

Safety information

Read and follow the complete, current product label and safety data sheet before opening or using this pesticide. Confirm that the product, formulation, intended crop or site, target pest or disease and application method are approved for the intended use. Do not guess a dose, dilution, tank mix, application interval or treatment method, and never use the product for an unlisted purpose.

Wear all personal protective equipment specified on the label and safety data sheet during handling, measuring, mixing, loading, application and equipment cleaning. Avoid contact with skin and eyes and avoid inhaling dust, vapour or spray mist. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. Wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly after work, and remove and launder contaminated clothing separately.

Keep children, animals and unprotected people away from the product and treated areas. Prevent spray drift, spills and run-off. Do not contaminate food, animal feed, medicines, drinking water, drains, ponds, rivers or neighbouring crops. Observe the exact restricted-entry interval and pre-harvest interval stated on the approved label; do not re-enter or harvest earlier.

If exposure, a spill or suspected poisoning occurs, stop work, follow the first-aid and emergency instructions on the product label and safety data sheet, and seek prompt medical or professional advice. Keep the original container and label available for medical personnel. Do not induce vomiting or administer treatment unless instructed by the label or a qualified medical professional.

Storage & handling

Store the 1-litre bottle in its original sealed container in a secure, cool, dry and ventilated pesticide store. Keep it away from direct sunlight, excessive heat, food, feed and household items. Protect the bottle from puncture and accidental leakage. Never decant the product into a water or beverage bottle. Do not reuse empty pesticide containers. Follow the product label and Kenyan requirements for container management and disposal. Do not pour leftover concentrate, diluted spray or washings into drains, rivers, ponds or other water sources.
