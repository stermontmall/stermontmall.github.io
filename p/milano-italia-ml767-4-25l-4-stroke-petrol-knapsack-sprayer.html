WHY CHOOSE A 25-LITRE ENGINE-DRIVEN KNAPSACK?

A motorised pump can help when the work covers several crop rows, orchard sections, garden beds or plantation areas. It reduces the need to pump by hand throughout the job, while the backpack design allows the operator to move with the machine. The 25-litre tank can reduce refill interruptions compared with smaller hand sprayers. It also adds weight when filled, so consider the operator's strength, terrain and working duration. Use the harness correctly and plan safe refill points. Do not assume that a larger tank automatically makes the machine suitable for every site or treatment.

FOUR-STROKE PETROL ENGINE

The ML767-4 is listed with a four-stroke petrol engine. A four-stroke engine normally uses petrol in the fuel tank and engine oil separately in the crankcase. This differs from a two-stroke engine that may require a petrol-and-oil mixture. Always check the engine manual for the exact fuel grade, oil grade, oil capacity and maintenance schedule. Do not guess or combine fuels and oils. Check the oil level before starting, inspect the fuel system for leaks and operate outdoors in a well-ventilated area. Switch off the engine and allow hot parts to cool before refuelling. Keep fuel away from flames, sparks and smoking.

BRASS PISTON PUMP

The supplier identifies a heavy-duty brass piston pump. The pump pressurises liquid and delivers it through the hose and lance. Brass pump components are used in many sprayer designs, but service life still depends on compatible liquids, clean filtration, correct pressure and routine care. Follow the manual for priming, operating speed and pressure adjustment. Do not run the pump dry or allow dirt and debris to enter the tank. If the pressure becomes inconsistent, stop and inspect the filter, nozzle, hose and seals. Do not force a blocked nozzle or increase engine speed beyond the manufacturer's operating range to compensate for a fault.

PRESSURE AND FLOW CONTROL

The supplier lists adjustable pressure of 15–25 kg/cm² and flow of up to 8 litres per minute. Actual output varies with nozzle selection, engine speed, pump condition, hose configuration and liquid properties. The highest pressure is not automatically the best setting. Use the application rate and spray pattern required by the chemical label. Calibrate with clean water before treatment and check that the nozzle delivers a consistent pattern. Avoid excessive application, runoff and drift. Do not direct the spray toward people, animals, water sources, neighbouring property or sensitive plants.

APPLICATIONS AND CHEMICAL COMPATIBILITY

The ML767-4 may be considered for compatible agricultural spraying, garden and orchard maintenance, plantation work and selected outdoor pest-management tasks. Use it only with liquid products whose labels approve the target, site and application method. A sprayer is equipment for delivering liquid; it does not make a particular pesticide, herbicide, fertiliser or disinfectant suitable for every use. Check compatibility with the tank, pump seals, hose, lance and nozzle. Follow Kenyan registration and label directions. Do not use agricultural pesticides in homes, food-handling premises or public buildings unless the label specifically permits the treatment. Use a qualified professional where the work requires licensing or specialist controls.

PLANNING THE WORK

Before mixing, identify the treatment area and calculate the solution volume using the product label and a calibrated application method. Prepare only what is needed. Inspect the site for people, animals, food, animal feed, drains, wells, ponds and sensitive crops. Check wind direction and postpone spraying if drift could carry droplets beyond the target. Maintain the distance and walking pace required for even coverage. Divide large properties into manageable sections and keep records of the product, batch, dilution, date, operator and treated area. For commercial sites, coordinate access restrictions and re-entry according to the label.

OPERATOR

Safety information

Read the equipment manual and chemical label before use. Wear all personal protective equipment specified on the label, which may include chemical-resistant gloves, coveralls, boots, eye protection and respiratory protection. Do not eat, drink or smoke while mixing or spraying. Keep people and animals away from the treatment area. Never point a pressurised lance at a person or use your hand to locate a leak. Stop the engine and release pressure as directed before opening the tank, clearing a blockage or disconnecting the hose. Refuel only when the engine is off and cool, away from ignition sources. Keep children away from the machine and chemical containers.

CLEANING AND MAINTENANCE

After application, follow the chemical label and sprayer manual for emptying, rinsing and disposal. Do not discharge leftover product or rinse water into drains, rivers, ponds, wells or storm-water channels. Clean the tank, filter, pump, hose, lance and nozzle using the recommended procedure. This is especially important before changing products, because residue may contaminate a later treatment or damage a crop. Inspect the pump, seals, clamps, tank, harness and hose for wear. Maintain the engine's oil, air filter and spark plug according to the manual. Replace damaged pressure components with compatible rated parts.

Storage & handling

AND TRANSPORT

Store the sprayer empty, clean and dry in a secure, ventilated location away from food, animal feed, children and direct sunlight. Follow the engine manual for fuel handling and longer-term storage. Keep petrol in an approved container and away from flames and heat. Secure the machine during transport so it cannot tip, leak or damage the pump and hose. Do not transport or store chemical mixture in the tank unless the product label and equipment manual explicitly permit it.
