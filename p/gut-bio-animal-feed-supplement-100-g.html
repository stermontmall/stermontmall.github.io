Gut Bio Animal Feed Supplement (100 g) Gut Bio is an animal feed supplement for use in animal rations on livestock and poultry farms in Kenya. The convenient 100 g pack suits small and medium farms that want to add a supplement to the daily ration. KEY FEATURES • Feed supplement for use in animal rations • For livestock and poultry farms • Convenient 100 g pack for small and medium farms • Delivered to all 47 counties SPECIFICATIONS • Product: Gut Bio • Type: Animal feed supplement • Pack size: 100 g WHY FEED SUPPLEMENTS ARE USED Animals do best when their rations are balanced and when they stay healthy and eat well. Feed supplements are added to rations to support the animal's diet, and farmers use them as part of a wider feeding and management programme. A supplement is not a substitute for clean water, good feed, housing and veterinary care, but it can be a useful addition to a well-managed farm. WHERE IT FITS Gut Bio is sold for livestock and poultry farms, so it can be used by farmers who keep cattle, goats, sheep, pigs, chickens and other animals. The 100 g pack is a small, easy size to try on a small flock or herd or to use on a farm that feeds in small batches. HOW TO USE IT Read and follow the label for the correct inclusion rate for each animal type and for how to mix the supplement into feed. Mix thoroughly so that every animal gets an even amount. Where the label recommends it, premix the supplement with a small portion of feed before adding it to the main batch. If you are unsure about the right rate for your animals, ask your vet, animal nutritionist or agrovet. GOOD FEEDING PRACTICE • Provide clean, fresh water at all times • Keep feed dry and free of mould • Change feed gradually rather than suddenly • Watch your animals' appetite, droppings, growth and general health • Keep records of feeds and supplements used • Talk to your vet if animals are sick, as a supplement is not a treatment SAFETY Read and follow the label before use. Keep away from children and pets. Wash your hands after handling. STORAGE Keep the pack tightly closed in a cool, dry place away from moisture, heat and direct sunlight.
