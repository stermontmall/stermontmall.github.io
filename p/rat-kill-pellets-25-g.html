Why Rodent Control Matters

Rats and mice eat and spoil stored food, gnaw on electrical wires, damage furniture, sacks and packaging, and can spread disease through their droppings and urine. They breed quickly, so a few rodents can become a large problem within weeks. Early baiting together with good hygiene is the best way to protect your home, business and stored produce.

How to Get Better Results

Look for droppings, gnaw marks, greasy rub marks along walls and holes where rodents live and travel. Place the bait close to these signs, along walls and near nesting holes, in places children, pets and livestock cannot reach. Keep other food sources covered, store grain and feed in sealed containers, block holes and gaps, and keep the area clean. Check the bait often, replace it when it is eaten, and remove dead rodents safely while wearing gloves.
