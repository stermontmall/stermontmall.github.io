Uses & applications

Rats and mice eat and spoil stored food, gnaw electrical wires, damage furniture, sacks and packaging, and spread disease through their droppings and urine. They breed quickly, so a few rodents can become a large problem within weeks. Early baiting, combined with good hygiene, protects a home, a business and stored produce before the damage grows.

How to use

Look for droppings, gnaw marks, greasy rub marks along walls and holes where rodents live and travel. Place the bait close to those signs, along walls and near nesting holes, in places that children, pets and livestock cannot reach. Check it often, replace bait that has been eaten, and remove dead rodents safely, wearing gloves.

Before you start

  • Cover food and store grain and feed in sealed containers
  • Block holes and gaps around pipes, doors and floors
  • Keep the area clean and free of crumbs and spilled feed
  • Pick your bait spots first, away from children and pets

Safety information

Check the PCPB registration number on the label and read the label before use. Keep the pellets away from children, pets and livestock, and put them only where other animals cannot reach them. Wash your hands after handling.

Storage & handling

Keep the pack closed and out of reach, in a cool, dry cupboard or store that can be locked.
