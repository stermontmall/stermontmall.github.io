Fit and installation considerations

A mosquito screen only works as a barrier when its edges fit closely and the mesh has no tears or gaps. Measure the full frame, including the area needed for the fastening strip. Clean and dry the mounting surface before installation, then follow the supplier's fitting instructions.

Avoid stretching the mesh unevenly or leaving corners loose. Check that the window can still open and close safely after fitting.

Selecting a physical pest-control product

Choose a trap or barrier by identifying the pest and where it is entering or moving. A fly ribbon is intended for flying insects, a glue-book trap for rodent monitoring, and window mesh for mosquito exclusion. These products are not interchangeable.

Check the room layout, likely pest routes, access for inspection and exposure to moisture or dust before deciding where to place the item.

Site assessment and integrated pest management

Inspect the area before installation. For flies, check waste bins, drains, uncovered food, animal areas and damp organic matter. For rodents, inspect gaps around doors and pipes, stored food, clutter and signs of movement along walls.

For mosquitoes, check window openings, damaged screens and standing water nearby. Remove food residues, cover refuse, repair openings and reduce standing water where possible. Use traps to monitor activity and support an integrated pest-management plan rather than relying on one device to solve every source of infestation.

Placement and inspection

Follow the supplied fitting or placement instructions. Keep adhesive surfaces away from food, utensils, children, pets, customer contact and walkways. Avoid wet, dusty or windy positions that can reduce adhesion or make a trap difficult to inspect.

Check products regularly and replace them when full, damaged, dirty, wet or no longer effective. For rodent traps, inspect frequently and handle any captured animal or used adhesive board with care.

Care and responsible disposal

Store unused items in their original packaging in a clean, dry place. Protect adhesive products from heat, direct sunlight and moisture. Do not burn used traps or place them where wildlife can become entangled.

Seal disposable used traps before putting them into suitable waste and wash hands after handling. Clean the surrounding area and continue monitoring after replacement. If pest activity persists or spreads, arrange a site inspection by a qualified pest-management professional.
