Why Choose This Blender

One machine does two jobs. Use the big jug for fruit smoothies, juices, soups and sauces, then switch to the grinder jar for coffee beans, spices and dry chillies. The 1.6 litre capacity suits a family, and the speed dial lets you go gentle for soft fruit or faster for ice and tougher ingredients.

How to Use

Cut fruit and vegetables into small pieces, add them with a little water or milk, fit the lid and lock the jug onto the base. Turn the dial to start on a low speed and increase as needed. For the grinder, add dry beans, spices or chillies, lock the jar on and run in short bursts. Switch off and wait for the blades to stop before opening.

Care and Cleaning

Unplug first. Rinse the jug straight after use, then wash it with warm soapy water and a soft brush, taking care around the blades. Never put the motor base in water; wipe it with a damp cloth. Dry all parts before storing. Grind strongly scented spices last, then wash well so flavours do not mix.

Safety Tips

Always lock the jug properly, because the safety lock design stops the machine from working otherwise. Do not run it empty or overfill it, and do not blend hot liquids above the recommended level. Keep hands and utensils away from the blades and keep the blender away from children. Follow the user manual.
