About Rafiki Fly Catcher Sticky Paper – 8 Piece Pack

The Rafiki Fly Catcher Sticky Paper is an eight-piece pack of adhesive fly-catching strips for use in suitable locations where flying insects are a nuisance. The sticky-paper format provides a simple physical capture surface that can help reduce the number of flies around selected non-food-contact areas and make insect activity easier to observe. It is a supplementary control and monitoring product, not a replacement for sanitation, waste management, screens or professional pest management where fly activity is persistent.

Where to use Sticky Fly Paper

Potential locations include suitable utility rooms, waste-management areas, covered outdoor spaces, stores and other places where flying insects gather and where a hanging adhesive strip can be used safely. Avoid positioning sticky paper above food, food-preparation surfaces, open ingredients, clean utensils or packaging. In food businesses, placement should follow the site's hygiene programme and should not create a contamination risk. Keep strips away from fans and strong airflow that could cause them to swing into people or equipment.

How to position and check it

Open and hang each strip according to the product instructions. Choose a stable location where the paper can hang freely and be inspected without touching walls, shelves or stored goods. Keep it out of reach of children and pets. Inspect the strip regularly and replace it when the adhesive is full, dusty, wet or no longer effective. Do not leave a used strip in place indefinitely. Dispose of it in a way that prevents the adhesive from contacting people, animals or other surfaces.

Reduce the sources that attract flies

Sticky paper catches insects that reach it, but it does not remove the conditions that attract them. Keep rubbish in covered bins and empty waste containers frequently. Clean food and drink spills promptly, wash waste-bin lids and surrounding floors, and remove decaying organic material. Keep drains and grease traps maintained where relevant. Store food in closed containers and repair gaps in screens or doors that allow flies to enter. In commercial kitchens, shops and food stores, follow a written cleaning and waste-management schedule.

Use as part of a Fly-monitoring routine

In a business, place traps at consistent monitoring points and record installation dates, replacement dates and observations. A sudden increase in catches can point to a nearby waste, drainage or entry issue that needs investigation. Compare trap results with visual inspections and staff observations. Avoid moving traps repeatedly if you need to compare results over time. If flies continue to appear in large numbers, identify the species and breeding source before choosing additional control methods.

Safety and hygiene

Keep the sticky surface away from food, utensils, children, pets and non-target animals. Avoid contact with the adhesive and wash hands after handling used strips. Do not use the product in a way that conflicts with food-safety requirements or the supplier's instructions. If a strip falls onto a food-contact surface, follow the site's cleaning and contamination procedure. Do not place used sticky paper where birds or other wildlife may become trapped.
