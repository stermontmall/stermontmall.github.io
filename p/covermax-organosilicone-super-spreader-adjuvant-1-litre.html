Product Overview & Key Benefits

CoverMax Organosilicone Super Spreader Adjuvant 1 Litre is a non-ionic trisiloxane spray adjuvant designed to help pesticide sprays and foliar feeds spread more evenly across plant surfaces. By reducing surface tension, it helps droplets wet waxy or difficult-to-cover leaves rather than forming beads, supporting more uniform spray coverage when used as directed.

For crop spraying, apply CoverMax only with products and crops permitted by the relevant label. Use the specified mixing rate and sequence, and avoid assuming that more adjuvant improves results. Compatibility, weather, crop sensitivity and application method all matter.

Read the pesticide or foliar-feed label and the CoverMax directions before mixing.

The 1-litre bottle is a practical size for farm, horticultural and professional spraying operations. Wear suitable protective equipment during handling and mixing, and prevent spray drift or runoff.
