Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

This product listing is for one 1-litre bottle. Check the seal, label, batch number and expiry date when receiving the product. Keep it in its original container with the label readable.

Store it in a secure, ventilated agrochemical store, away from food, animal feed, medicines, children, livestock, heat and direct sunlight. Do not decant it into beverage bottles or unmarked containers. Keep purchase and application records, including the product name, batch, date, crop, target pest and operator.

Use dedicated pesticide measuring equipment and calibrated application equipment. Do not use kitchen utensils. Prepare only the quantity needed for the label-approved operation, and follow the label directions for mixing, application, equipment cleaning and disposal.

Do not assume that a larger bottle means a higher dose or that the full bottle should be used in one operation. PERSONAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION Read the complete product label before opening the container. Wear the protective clothing and personal protective equipment specified there when handling, measuring, mixing or applying the concentrate.

Avoid contact with skin and eyes and avoid inhaling spray mist. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling pesticides. Keep unprotected people and animals away from mixing and application areas, and observe the re-entry period stated on the current label.

Prevent spray drift and runoff. Do not contaminate rivers, ponds, wells, drains, irrigation channels or drinking-water sources. Deltamethrin can be hazardous to aquatic organisms and non-target insects, so take particular care around water and pollinators.

Avoid spraying flowering crops while bees are actively foraging unless the approved label specifically provides safe conditions. Dispose of leftover mixture and empty packaging according to the label and applicable Kenyan requirements. Never reuse pesticide containers.

RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT Because deltamethrin is an IRAC Group 3A pyrethroid, avoid repeated consecutive applications of products from the same group. Where permitted by the crop label and advised by a qualified agronomist, rotate with an effective registered product from a different mode-of-action group. Rotation should be based on the actual active ingredient and pest biology, not brand names alone.

Product labels and registrations can change, and the physical Kenyan label takes priority over general online descriptions. PRODUCT AND FORMULATION Atom 2.5EC is an emulsifiable concentrate. EC formulations are liquid concentrates intended to be diluted only as directed by the approved label.

The 1-litre pack is suited to farms, horticultural operations and agricultural input retailers that require a larger pack than the small-volume retail sizes. The pack size does not determine the application rate: the correct quantity depends on the crop, target pest, label-approved dose, spray volume and treated area. Never estimate a dose by using a capful or by copying an instruction intended for a different product concentration.

Deltamethrin is a synthetic pyrethroid that affects insect voltage-gated sodium channels. It has contact and stomach action, and belongs to IRAC Group 3A. This classification is important for resistance management.

Products that have different brand names can still share the same mode of action, so check the active ingredient and IRAC group before planning rotations. Repeated reliance on the same group can select for resistant pest populations and reduce future control options.
