Why Termite Protection Matters in Kenya

Termites quietly damage timber, roofing, doors, frames, fences, furniture and even stored paper. By the time the damage shows, repairs can be costly. Treating the soil around and under a building is one of the most effective ways to create a protective barrier, and it is best done at construction stage and repeated or topped up where termites appear later. Mchwatox 350 SC is made for exactly this job.

How to Get the Best Results

Good termite control starts with a proper inspection to find where termites enter and nest. Treatment should cover the soil thoroughly and evenly, following the label for the correct dilution and volume of spray liquid. Fix leaking pipes and damp areas, remove old tree stumps and buried timber near the building, and keep wood away from direct contact with soil. For large buildings, consider hiring a licensed pest control operator.
