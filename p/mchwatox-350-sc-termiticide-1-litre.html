About Mchwatox 350 SC Termiticide – 1 Litre

Mchwatox 350 SC Termiticide (1 litre)

Termites work out of sight. They tunnel through the soil, reach a building at the foundation and can weaken doors, frames and roof timbers before anyone sees a sign. Mchwatox 350 SC is a suspension concentrate (SC) termiticide containing imidacloprid 350 g/L, supplied in a 1 litre bottle. It is used for soil treatment against termites in buildings and construction, both before a building goes up (pre-construction) and on buildings that already stand (post-construction).

Where it helps

Termite damage to timber, roofing, doors, frames, fences and furniture is costly to repair once it shows. Treating the soil around and under a building is one way to build a protective barrier, applied at construction stage and repeated or topped up where termites appear later. Mchwatox 350 SC is made for this soil-treatment job, so it suits builders, contractors, pest control operators and property owners.

How to use it

Start with an inspection to find where termites enter and where they nest. Dilute Mchwatox 350 SC with water exactly as the label directs, and apply it thoroughly and evenly to the soil, using the dilution and the volume of spray liquid printed on the label. For a new building the treatment goes on before the slab or foundation is laid. For an existing building it is applied around the foundations, trenches and perimeter as the label describes.

Before you start

  • Fix leaking pipes and damp areas that keep the soil wet
  • Remove old tree stumps and buried timber near the building
  • Keep timber and firewood from direct contact with the soil
  • For a large building, consider a licensed pest control operator

Safety

Check the PCPB registration number on the label, and mix and apply exactly as directed. Wear gloves, a mask, overalls and boots, avoid contact with skin and eyes, and keep the product away from children, pets, food, water sources and fish ponds. Do not contaminate wells, boreholes or drains when you mix, spray or wash equipment.

Storage

Keep Mchwatox 350 SC in its original, tightly closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food, feed and direct sunlight.
