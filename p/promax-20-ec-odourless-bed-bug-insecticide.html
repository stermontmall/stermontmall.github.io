Why Bed Bugs Are Hard to Fight Bed bugs are small, flat insects that hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, cracks in walls, behind skirting boards and in furniture during the day, and come out at night to feed on people. Their bites cause itching and sleepless nights, and they spread easily between rooms, houses and hostels, in luggage, clothing and second-hand furniture. Because they hide so well, treatment needs to reach the cracks and joints where they live.
