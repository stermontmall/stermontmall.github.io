Why Bed Bugs Are Hard to Fight

Bed bugs are small, flat insects that hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, cracks in walls, behind skirting boards and in furniture during the day, and come out at night to feed on people. Their bites cause itching and sleepless nights, and they spread easily between rooms, houses and hostels, in luggage, clothing and second-hand furniture. Because they hide so well, treatment needs to reach the cracks and joints where they live.

Tips for Better Bed Bug Control

Strip the bed and wash bedding in hot water, then dry on high heat or in strong sun. Vacuum mattresses, bed frames and cracks, and dispose of the dust bag safely. Apply the product to seams, joints, frame edges and cracks as directed on the label, and allow the surfaces to dry before putting clean bedding back. Repeat treatment may be needed to catch newly hatched bed bugs. Seal cracks, reduce clutter and check second-hand furniture before bringing it into your home.
