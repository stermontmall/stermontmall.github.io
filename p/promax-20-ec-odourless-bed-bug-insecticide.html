About Promax 20 EC Odourless Bed Bug Insecticide

Promax 20 EC Odourless Bed Bug Insecticide (50 ml sachet)

Bed bugs, called kunguni in Swahili, hide in mattress seams, bed frames and skirting boards and bite while you sleep. Promax 20 EC is a propoxur-based, odourless insecticide concentrate that you dilute and spray straight into those hiding places. It also deals with cockroaches (mende), fleas, ants and houseflies, so one sachet protects a house, hostel room, hotel room or office.

Key features

  • Odourless formulation suited to bedrooms and other indoor spaces
  • Residual action on treated surfaces, so pests that crawl across later are still affected
  • Multi-pest: bed bugs, cockroaches, fleas, ants and houseflies
  • Concentrated: 25 ml makes 1 litre of ready-to-spray liquid
  • Works with any hand-pump or knapsack sprayer

Specifications

  • Active ingredient: Propoxur 20% w/v (carbamate insecticide)
  • Formulation: Emulsifiable concentrate (EC)
  • Use: household and public-health crawling-insect control
  • Pack: 50 ml sachet, which makes 2 litres of spray
  • Mode of action: blocks nerve signal transmission, causing paralysis

Where it helps

Bed bugs bite at night and hide by day in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, skirting boards and cracks. Cockroaches, fleas, ants and houseflies use the same cracks and corners to travel and breed. Promax stays active on treated surfaces, so pests that crawl across them later are still affected, which helps in bedrooms, hostel rooms and offices where you cannot spray every day.

Mixing rates (from the product label)

  • 1 litre hand sprayer: 25 ml Promax 20 EC, topped up with water to 1 litre
  • 5 litre garden sprayer: 125 ml, topped up to 5 litres
  • 20 litre knapsack: 500 ml, topped up to 20 litres
  • Most homes need only 1 to 5 litres; the knapsack rate is for hotels, hostels and large premises
  • Always confirm the rate on your pack's label before mixing

How to use it

Half-fill the sprayer with clean water, add Promax, top up with water and shake well. Spray mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, skirting boards, cracks, the backs of sockets and furniture joints. Wet the surfaces lightly and do not soak them. Bed bug eggs can hatch later, so inspect after about two weeks and treat again if needed.

Before you start

  • Strip the bed and wash bedding at a high temperature
  • Vacuum the mattress, frame and floor
  • Do not spray food, utensils or bedding you will use before it dries

Safety

Keep children, pets and fish tanks out of the room until surfaces are dry and the room is ventilated. Wear gloves and wash your hands after use. Odourless does not mean harmless: it is still an insecticide, so keep it away from food, water and children.

Storage

Keep the sachet or any leftover concentrate sealed in its original pack, in a cool, dry, locked place.
