What it is listed for

This product is associated with cockroaches. Correct pest identification matters because cockroaches, bedbugs, ants and other household pests have different harbourages and control needs. Look for live insects, droppings, shed skins, egg cases, trails and repeated activity.

In kitchens, inspect cupboard joints, appliance spaces and plumbing gaps. For suspected bedbugs, inspect mattress seams, bed frames and nearby cracks. Treat only pests and sites authorised by the product label.

The 35g size is the exact pack represented here. Pack size tells you the quantity supplied; it does not establish a treatment area, number of applications or dilution rate. Compare variants only after confirming that formulation and registration match.

A larger pack is not permission to increase a dose or apply more often. This is a targeted bait format, not a spray concentrate. Do not dilute it or put it in a sprayer.

Place it only as its own label permits, away from children, pets and food.

Pest prevention and preparation

Inspection and prevention should accompany any correctly selected treatment. Store food in sealed containers, clean crumbs and grease, empty bins, repair leaks and reduce clutter. Seal practical entry gaps around pipes and skirting boards.

Trace ant trails and remove accessible food sources; for cockroaches, check warm, dark and damp harbourages. These steps support an integrated approach but do not replace label directions or professional assessment.

Safe use and label compliance

Read the complete label before opening the pack. Confirm the target pest, permitted site, preparation method, dose, protective equipment, ventilation, evacuation and re-entry instructions. Never guess a dilution, combine pesticides or reuse food utensils.

Do not use an agricultural product indoors or a public-health product on crops unless the current label expressly allows it. If the label is missing, damaged or inconsistent with this listing, do not apply the product until its identity is confirmed.

Storage and personal safety

Keep the product in its original labelled container, locked away from children, pets, food, animal feed and drinking water. Wear the protective equipment specified on the label and avoid skin and eye contact or inhalation of vapour and spray mist. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling it.

Never transfer pesticide into an unlabelled container. Follow first-aid instructions; seek prompt medical help after suspected exposure and take the label with you.
