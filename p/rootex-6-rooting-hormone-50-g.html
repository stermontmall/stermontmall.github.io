About Rootex 6 Rooting Hormone – 50 g

Rootex 6 Rooting Hormone (50 g)

Rootex 6 is a rooting hormone powder that helps cuttings of roses, carnations and other ornamentals form strong, healthy roots. Nurseries, flower farms and home gardeners use it for faster, more even rooting and a higher success rate.

Key specifications & features

  • Composition: See the product label
  • Target: Rooting of cuttings: roses, carnations and other ornamentals
  • Formulation: Powder
  • Application settings: Nurseries, flower farms and home gardeners

Mixing & application instructions

1. Dilution rate: Used as a powder dip: no mixing with water is needed. Pour a small amount into a separate container for dipping, not into the main tub, to avoid contaminating the rest.

2. Application method: Take a healthy cutting with a clean, sharp knife or secateurs. Dip the cut end into the powder, tap off any extra and plant it straight away into a clean, well-drained rooting medium.

3. Coverage: The number of cuttings one pack treats depends on how many you dip; the label gives the quantity. Available in 50 g.

4. Water lightly and keep the cuttings in a warm, shaded and humid place until roots form.

5. Use clean tools and trays, choose healthy cuttings, keep the medium moist but not waterlogged and protect cuttings from direct hot sun until they have rooted.

Safety, handling & storage

  • Wear gloves and avoid contact with skin and eyes; wash your hands after handling.
  • Keep out of reach of children, pets and livestock, and away from food and feed.
  • Store tightly closed in the original pack, in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
