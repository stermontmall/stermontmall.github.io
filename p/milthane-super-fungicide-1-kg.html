About Mancozeb Fungicides

Mancozeb is a contact, protective fungicide. It stays on the surface of the leaf and protects the plant against spores that cause diseases such as early and late blight and many leaf spots. Because it protects rather than cures, it is most effective when sprayed before disease arrives or at the very first signs, and when the spray covers the leaves evenly. Rain can wash the product off, so follow the label on spray intervals and re-application after heavy rain.

Farm Tips for Blight Control

Blight spreads fast in cool, wet and humid weather. Plant certified seed potatoes or healthy seedlings, avoid overcrowding, keep fields weed-free and remove infected plants. Rotating with fungicides that have a different mode of action helps protect their long-term effectiveness. Spray in the early morning or late afternoon and cover both sides of the leaves.

Safe use of Original Milthane Super Fungicide – 1 kg

  • Read the whole label before you buy or mix. The label is the legal instruction for crops, pests, rates and safety.
  • Look for the PCPB registration number on the pack. Do not buy chemicals that carry none.
  • Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI) printed on the label, the number of days that must pass between the last spray and harvest. Use the product only on the crops the label lists.
  • Wear gloves, a mask or respirator, eye protection and long protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards.
  • Keep chemicals in their original container, out of reach of children, pets and livestock. Never reuse empty containers for food or water.
