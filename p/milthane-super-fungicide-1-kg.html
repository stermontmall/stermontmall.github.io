About Mancozeb Fungicides Mancozeb is a contact, protective fungicide. It stays on the surface of the leaf and protects the plant against spores that cause diseases such as early and late blight and many leaf spots. Because it protects rather than cures, it is most effective when sprayed before disease arrives or at the very first signs, and when the spray covers the leaves evenly. Rain can wash the product off, so follow the label on spray intervals and re-application after heavy rain.
