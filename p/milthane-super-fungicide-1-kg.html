About Milthane Super Fungicide – 1 kg

Milthane Super Fungicide (1 kg)

Milthane Super is a mancozeb wettable powder fungicide for protective control of blight and leaf spots on potatoes, tomatoes and vegetables. The 1 kg pack is easy to measure and well suited to regular preventive spraying.

Key specifications & features

  • Active ingredient: Mancozeb
  • Target: Early and late blight and leaf spots on potatoes, tomatoes and vegetables (protective, contact action)
  • Formulation: Wettable powder (WP)
  • Application settings: Agricultural: potato and tomato farms and vegetable plots

Mixing & application instructions

1. Dilution rate: use the dose and water volume printed on the manufacturer's label for your crop and growth stage.

2. Application method: Mix into clean water as the label directs and spray in the early morning or late afternoon, covering both sides of the leaves evenly.

3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Available in 1 kg.

4. Spray before disease arrives or at the very first signs: mancozeb protects rather than cures.

5. Rain can wash the product off, so follow the label on spray intervals and re-application after heavy rain, and avoid breathing the dust.

Safety & re-entry precautions

  • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.
