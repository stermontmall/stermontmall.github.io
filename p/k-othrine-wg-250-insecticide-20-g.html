About K-Othrine WG 250 Insecticide – 20 g

K-Othrine WG 250 Insecticide (20 g Sachet) K-Othrine WG 250 (Envu, formerly Bayer) is a water-dispersible granule containing deltamethrin at 250 g/kg, sold here as a 20 g sachet. It is a residual contact insecticide for the walls and surfaces where insects walk and rest. The maker lists a residual life of 3 to 6 months on suitable surfaces. KEY FEATURES • Deltamethrin 250 g/kg • Water-dispersible granule (WG) • Residual contact insecticide • Maker lists a residual life of 3 to 6 months on suitable surfaces • 20 g sachet that is easy to carry and store • Envu, formerly Bayer • Delivered to all 47 counties WHAT IT CONTROLS Mosquitoes (including malaria-carrying species), sand flies, houseflies, fleas, bed bugs and cockroaches in homes, hostels, hotels and public buildings. HOW A RESIDUAL TREATMENT WORKS After you spray and the water dries, a thin film of insecticide stays on the surface. When insects land on or walk across it, they pick up the active ingredient, so the surface keeps working long after you have sprayed. Mosquitoes that rest on walls after feeding, for example, are exposed when they land on a treated wall. HOW TO USE IT Mix the granules into water in a compression sprayer and spray surfaces evenly. Envu's guidance for Africa is one 20 g sachet in 8 to 10 litres of water to cover about 200 to 250 m2 of porous surfaces such as mud or concrete block, and two sachets in the same volume for 400 to 500 m2 of non-porous surfaces. Follow the instructions on your pack. TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS • Spray evenly, with a steady pace and overlap • Treat the surfaces where insects rest, such as inner walls, eaves, ceilings and door and window frames • Do not wash or repaint treated surfaces soon after spraying • Keep a record of the dates and areas treated WHY BUYERS CHOOSE IT The small sachet is easy to carry and store, and the granules mix into water more easily than wettable powders. SAFETY Do not spray people, pets, food or cooking surfaces. Keep children and animals out until surfaces are dry and wear gloves and a mask. It is very toxic to aquatic life, so never rinse sprayers or sachets into drains, streams or ponds.
