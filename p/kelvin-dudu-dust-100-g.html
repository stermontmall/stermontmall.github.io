About Kelvin Dudu Dust – 100 g

Kelvin Dudu Dust (100 g) Kelvin Dudu Dust is a broad-spectrum insecticide dust for control of domestic pests, including safari ants, fleas, bedbugs, lice, mites and ticks. It comes in a 100 g pack and is applied by dusting. KEY FEATURES • Broad-spectrum insecticide dust • Controls safari ants, fleas, bedbugs, lice, mites and ticks • For domestic pests • Applied by dusting, with no mixing in water • 100 g pack • Delivered to all 47 counties PESTS IT CONTROLS • Safari ants: marching columns of ants that can enter houses, stores and animal shelters in large numbers • Fleas: bite people and animals and live in bedding, carpets and cracks • Bedbugs: hide in mattress seams and bed frames and bite at night • Lice: live on people and animals and spread between them • Mites: tiny pests that live in bedding, animal housing and on animals • Ticks: attach to animals and can spread disease WHY CHOOSE A DUST A dust is ready to use and does not need water, which makes it handy where there is no water supply or where you do not want to wet a surface. It can be puffed into cracks, crevices, nesting places and along ant trails and stays on the surface where insects walk. HOW TO USE IT Apply by dusting, following the label. Apply a light, even layer along ant trails and nests, in cracks and crevices, around bed frames, and in places where fleas, mites and ticks live. Keep dust away from food, water and cooking areas, and do not apply on surfaces where food is prepared. A thin layer works better than a thick heap, since insects need to walk through it. BEFORE YOU START • Clear the area and remove food and utensils • Clean floors, bedding and pet resting places • Keep children and pets away from treated areas • Wear gloves and a mask to avoid breathing dust FOR ANIMALS AND PEOPLE Follow the label strictly if the product is used near animal housing. Do not apply to people or directly to animals unless the label says it is allowed, and ask a vet about treating pets and livestock. SAFETY Read the label before use. Avoid breathing dust and contact with eyes and skin. Keep out of reach of children, pets and food, and wash your hands after use. Do not let dust reach streams, ponds or fish tanks. STORAGE Keep the pack closed in a cool, dry place out of reach of children.
