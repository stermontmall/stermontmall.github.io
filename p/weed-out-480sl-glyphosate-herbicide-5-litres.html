Why Choose Weed-Out 480SL Weeds compete with crops for water, light and nutrients, and they can shelter pests and diseases. Glyphosate is absorbed through the leaves and moves through the whole plant, including the roots, which makes it effective on tough perennial weeds such as couch grass and other weeds that regrow after slashing or hoeing. Clearing fields before planting gives your crop a clean start and reduces early weed pressure. Application Tips Spray when weeds are actively growing and have enough leaf area to absorb the product. Use clean water, a clean sprayer and a flat-fan nozzle, and follow the label rate. Avoid spraying just before rain, and do not slash or cut weeds immediately after spraying, because the product needs time to move through the plant. Avoid spray drift onto crops, trees, gardens and neighbouring farms by spraying in calm weather and using a shielded sprayer where possible.
