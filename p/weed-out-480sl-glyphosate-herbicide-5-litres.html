Product overview

Weed-Out 480SL Glyphosate Herbicide (5 litres) Clearing a plot, fence line, compound or farm before planting by hand takes days and costs a lot in labour. Weed-Out 480SL is a non-selective, systemic glyphosate herbicide that is absorbed through the leaves and travels down to the roots, so grasses and broadleaf weeds die instead of regrowing. The 5-litre pack suits farmers, estate caretakers and contractors who clear land often. KEY BENEFITS

  • Systemic action kills the roots, which means less regrowth than slashing or hoeing
  • Controls annual and perennial grasses and broadleaf weeds, including tough couch grass
  • Saves labour on land preparation, zero-tillage planting, fence lines, paths and compounds
  • Economical: 5 L makes 16 to 25 knapsack loads depending on the weed
  • Rain-free period is short, so you can spray on the next dry morning TECHNICAL SPECS
  • Active ingredient: Glyphosate 480 g/L
  • Formulation: Soluble liquid (SL)
  • Type: Non-selective, post-emergence, systemic herbicide
  • Pack: 5 litres MIXING AND APPLICATION DOSAGE (per 20 L knapsack)
  • Young broadleaf weeds and annual grasses: 200 ml (5 L pack makes 25 knapsacks)
  • Perennial grasses such as couch grass: 300 ml (5 L pack makes about 16 knapsacks) At about 4 L per hectare in 200 L of water (200 ml per 20 L), one 5 L pack treats roughly 1 hectare. Always confirm the rate on your pack's label. 1. Use clean water. Half-fill the knapsack, add Weed-Out, then top up to 20 L and shake. 2. Spray actively growing weeds until the leaves are wet but not dripping. 3. Expect yellowing within a few days and root die-off soon after. 4. Avoid spraying if rain is expected within 4 to 6 hours.

Safety information

It kills any green plant it touches, so shield crops and avoid drift in wind. Keep away from water sources. Wear gloves, overalls and a face mask. Rinse the sprayer very thoroughly afterwards, or keep a separate sprayer for herbicides. WHAT'S IN THE BOX 1 × Weed-Out 480SL Glyphosate Herbicide (5 litres)
