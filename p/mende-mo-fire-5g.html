Buying & Pack-Size Guide

A 5g sachet is a small quantity that may be appropriate for a limited requirement where the approved label allows it. A 250g pack is a different purchase quantity and should not be treated as a larger version of the same retail SKU without checking the label, product identity and pack details. Buyers should choose the quantity based on the site, the infestation assessment and the approved label—not simply because a larger pack looks cheaper per gram.

Bait products are distinct from residual sprays, wettable powders and liquid concentrates. They are not interchangeable in application method. A bait should only be placed in locations permitted by the label and in a way that prevents access by children, pets, food or non-target animals.

Do not place bait on food-contact surfaces or where it can contaminate food, utensils or water. Do not open, split or repackage the sachet unless the label specifically instructs it.
