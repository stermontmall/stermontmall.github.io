Uses & applications

ULV stands for ultra-low volume. A ULV machine applies a small amount of liquid as a fine mist, which is why this kind of equipment is used for rooms, corners and crop areas. This machine is the equipment, not the chemical, so you pair it with a product whose label allows ULV or fogging use and follow that label for the amount to use.

How to use

The catalog does not give the power supply, output or settings, so follow the manufacturer's manual for filling, start-up and shut-down. Before you fill the tank, read the label of the product you plan to use and confirm that it may be used in a fogger or ULV machine, then follow its dilution and the time the space must stay closed. Treat only the space you have prepared, and switch the machine off before you refill or clean it.

Before you start

  • Read the machine manual and the product label before the first use
  • Remove people, pets, food, feed and animals from the space you will fog
  • Keep the electrical parts dry and away from spilled liquid

Safety information

Electric equipment and liquid do not mix, so keep the machine's electrical parts dry and do not use it if a part is damaged. Wear the protective equipment named on the product label, including a respirator if the label requires one, and do not enter a fogged space until the label says it is safe. Keep the machine and any chemical away from children, pets, food and water sources.

Storage & handling

Empty and clean the tank as the manual directs, let the machine dry, and store it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, food, animal feed and children.
