Product Overview & Key Benefits

Catchmaster 100i adhesive insect monitors are pesticide-free glue traps used to detect and monitor crawling insects such as cockroaches in suitable indoor locations. The folded station provides an adhesive capture surface, while the pre-baited formulation is intended to encourage insects to investigate the trap. Monitoring results can help a pest technician identify activity points and review whether sanitation, proofing and treatment measures are working.

This listing is for a 9-pack, not a chemical insecticide or a complete pest-control service. Catchmaster's published information describes the 100i monitor as pre-baited with food-grade molasses and fitted with an adhesive backing. Keep the trap accessible for inspection and avoid placing it where routine cleaning, water or heavy dust will quickly damage the adhesive. The openings should face likely insect movement and the station should not be blocked by stored goods or furniture. To prepare the monitor, follow the Catchmaster instructions for separating perforated sections, folding the card and removing the protective liner from the adhesive.

Inspect the monitors on a consistent schedule, noting the presence and approximate number of insects. Replace traps that are full, dusty, wet, damaged or no longer sticky. Comparing results across rooms can help distinguish a localised issue from activity that may be spreading through service routes or adjoining spaces.

The monitor is a detection and capture aid, not a guaranteed eradication method. A low catch does not prove that a room is free of cockroaches; placement, species, food availability and insect movement all affect results. If activity is found, investigate food residues, moisture, waste, drains, clutter and access points.

A control plan may require cleaning, exclusion, targeted baiting or other approved methods. Do not place pesticide bait or spray onto the monitor unless the product instructions specifically permit that combination. Coordinate placement with the facility's hygiene procedure and keep traps away from children, pets and routine handling where possible. Although the monitor is not a pesticide product, captured insects and used adhesive boards should be handled hygienically.
