Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

This is a pest-control or agricultural chemical product. Use it only for the crop, pest, site and application method expressly authorised on the current Kenya-approved product label. Read the full label before opening or use.

Follow the label exactly for personal protective equipment, dilution, application, ventilation, re-entry period, pre-harvest interval, environmental restrictions and any other precautions. Do not guess a dose, mix it with another product, or use it on food, animals, people or surfaces unless the label specifically permits that use. Keep the original container tightly closed and locked away from children, unauthorised persons and animals, and separate from food, drink and animal feed.

Prevent spills and contamination of drains, wells, streams and other water sources. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling; wash hands and exposed skin after handling. Never reuse an empty pesticide container; follow the label and approved local hazardous-waste collection or disposal instructions.

In case of suspected poisoning or significant exposure, seek urgent medical attention and show the product label. Kenya PCPB poison information hotlines listed in its labelling guidance: 0800 720 021 or 0800 730 030.

Product overview

Hafeton 100 SC is a public-health insecticide supplied in a one-litre bottle for authorised pest-management work in domestic environments. The formulation contains alpha-cypermethrin 100 g/L in a suspension concentrate and is registered in Kenya under PCPB(CR)1932. The Kenya Pest Control Products Board public-health register lists Hafeton 100 SC for control of cockroaches, houseflies, fleas and bedbugs in domestic environments and classifies it as WHO Class II, moderately hazardous, with a yellow colour band.

The current product label supplied with the bottle must always be checked before use. Hafeton 100 SC is designed for residual surface treatment as part of a planned public-health pest-control programme. It is not a ready-to-use aerosol, a crop fungicide, a rodenticide or a product for direct application to people or animals.

The one-litre pack is suited to pest-control contractors, institutional maintenance teams, hotels, schools, warehouses and other customers who need a larger pack for authorised treatment work. Select the product only after identifying the pest and confirming that the intended premises and treatment method are permitted by the label. The product information from Osho Chemical Industries identifies cockroaches, houseflies, fleas and bedbugs as target pests in domestic environments.

It also notes use in poultry houses against poultry red mite and litter beetle. These uses must be confirmed on the current Kenyan label for the exact premises and pest before application. Do not assume that approval for domestic environments automatically permits use in every food-handling area, animal house or commercial facility.

Follow all label restrictions on surfaces, animal occupancy, food protection and re-entry. Alpha-cypermethrin is a pyrethroid insecticide that acts on insect nerve signalling. It affects sodium-channel function, leading to uncontrolled nerve activity in susceptible insects.

Product performance depends on pest species, treatment surface, infestation pressure, spray coverage, environmental conditions and compliance with the label. A residual treatment may be one part of a wider integrated pest-management plan, but it does not replace inspection, sanitation, exclusion, monitoring or follow-up. Persistent activity may indicate hidden harbourages, untreated routes, reinfestation or the need to reassess the control strategy.

Before treatment, inspect the premises and identify likely harbourages and movement routes. Cockroaches often shelter around sinks, cupboards, appliances, waste areas and service penetrations. Bedbugs require a careful inspection of bed frames, furniture joints and other hiding locations.

Fleas require assessment of their source and the environment. Housefly control should include waste management, sanitation and removal of breeding sources. Use monitoring and service records to identify where pest activity is occurring and to evaluate whether a treatment programme is working.

Osho product information gives a reference dilution of 50ml in 10 litres of water to cover approximately 200 square metres for a single spray. Use this only if it matches the current approved Kenyan label on the supplied pack. The label is the authority for the exact dilution, spray volume, target surface, application interval, maximum number of treatments, re-entry interval, protective equipment, first aid and environmental precautions.

Do not increase the concentration or apply more frequently than directed. Do not mix Hafeton with another pesticide, detergent or adjuvant unless the label expressly permits the combination. Hafeton 100 SC is classified by PCPB as WHO Class II, moderately hazardous.

Wear the protective equipment stated on the label, avoid inhaling spray mist and prevent contact with eyes and skin. Keep unprotected people and animals away during application and until the label's re-entry conditions are met. Protect food, food-contact surfaces, utensils, drinking-water containers, aquariums and animal feed from contamination.

Prevent spray drift onto neighbouring homes, crops, waterways, livestock and non-target organisms. Do not apply directly to people, pets or livestock unless the label specifically authorises that use. After treatment, clean equipment and protective clothing according to the label and workplace procedures.

Wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly. Store the bottle tightly closed in its original packaging in a secure, cool, dry and well-ventilated pesticide store. Keep it away from children, food, animal feed, medicines and unauthorised persons.

Never transfer the product into an unlabelled container or reuse an empty pesticide bottle. Dispose of leftover product and packaging through approved pesticide-waste arrangements; never pour residues or wash water into drains, rivers, ponds, wells or storm-water channels.
