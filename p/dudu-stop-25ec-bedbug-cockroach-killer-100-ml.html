Dudu-Stop 25EC Bedbug and Cockroach Killer (100 ml) Dudu-Stop 25EC is a fast-acting liquid concentrate for bedbugs and cockroaches in homes, hostels and hotels. It is supplied in a 100 ml pack and is diluted with water before spraying on cracks, bed frames and corners. KEY FEATURES • Fast-acting liquid concentrate • For bedbugs and cockroaches • For homes, hostels and hotels • Dilute with water and spray cracks, bed frames and corners • 100 ml pack • Delivered to all 47 counties WHERE IT HELPS Bedbugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, cracks in walls and furniture, and they feed on people at night, causing itchy bites and sleepless nights. Cockroaches live in kitchens, bathrooms, drains and behind cupboards, and they spread germs on food and surfaces. Hostels, hotels and rentals need to deal with these pests quickly, because infestations spread between rooms and affect guest and tenant satisfaction. HOW TO USE IT Dilute the concentrate with water as directed on the label. Spray cracks, bed frames, mattress seams, skirting boards, door and window frames, corners and other places where the insects hide. Treat every affected room at the same time. Keep people, pets and food out of the room until the spray has dried, and repeat the treatment if the label advises it. BEFORE YOU SPRAY • Strip beds and wash bedding, curtains and clothing at a high temperature • Vacuum mattresses, floors and corners, and empty the vacuum outside • Move furniture away from walls so you can reach the hiding places • Cover or remove food, dishes, fish tanks and pet bowls FOR HOSTELS AND HOTELS A 100 ml pack gives enough product for several rooms. Keep a simple record of rooms treated and the dates, inspect beds regularly and act at the first sign of bites or droppings. For large or repeated infestations, consider a licensed pest control operator. SAFETY Keep away from children, food and pets. Read the label before use. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle. STORAGE Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place.
