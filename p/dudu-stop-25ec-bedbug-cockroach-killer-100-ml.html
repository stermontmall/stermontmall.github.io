Uses & applications

Bedbugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards and wall cracks, and feed on people at night. Cockroaches live in kitchens, bathrooms, drains and behind cupboards. In hostels, hotels and rentals an infestation spreads quickly between rooms and affects guest and tenant satisfaction, so a quick, coordinated treatment matters more than treating one room at a time.

How to use

Dilute the concentrate with water as the label directs. Spray cracks, bed frames, mattress seams, skirting boards, door and window frames, corners and the other places where insects hide. Treat every affected room at the same time. Keep people, pets and food out of the room until the spray has dried, and repeat the treatment if the label advises it.

Before you start

  • Strip beds and wash bedding, curtains and clothing at a high temperature
  • Vacuum mattresses, floors and corners, and empty the vacuum outside
  • Move furniture away from the walls
  • Cover or remove food, dishes, fish tanks and pet bowls

Safety information

Keep away from children, food and pets. Read the label before use. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle.

Storage & handling

Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place.
