It contains copper oxychloride 700 g/L, which is 400 g/L metallic copper equivalent, and it corrects and prevents copper deficiency in vegetables, fruit, cereals and ornamentals. It is supplied in a 1 litre pack. KEY FEATURES • Liquid chelated copper fertilizer in suspension concentrate (SC) form • Copper oxychloride 700 g/L (400 g/L metallic copper equivalent) • Corrects and prevents copper deficiency • For vegetables, fruit, cereals and ornamentals • 1 litre pack • Greenlife brand WHY COPPER MATTERS Copper is a trace element that plants need in small amounts. It plays a part in enzyme activity, in the formation of chlorophyll and in flowering, pollen formation and grain filling. When copper is short, plants can show stunted growth, pale or twisted young leaves, poor flowering and lower yields. Cereals are especially sensitive, and deficiencies are more common in sandy, peaty and very high-organic soils. HOW TO USE IT Apply as directed on the label, normally as a foliar spray, using the dose and water volume recommended for your crop. Mix by half-filling the tank with water, adding the measured product, topping up and agitating well. Spray evenly, covering the leaves. Apply when the crop is actively growing and at the stage recommended on the label, and spray early in the morning or late in the afternoon when it is cooler. If you are unsure whether copper is really short, consider a soil or leaf test to confirm. GOOD PRACTICE • Do not exceed the label rate, because too much copper can harm plants and soil • Avoid spraying in hot midday sun • Test a small area first if you plan to mix with other products • Keep records of dates and doses SAFETY Read the label before use. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Copper compounds can be harmful to fish and aquatic life, so keep spray and rinse water out of streams, ponds and drains. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry place away from food and feed. Shake well before use.
