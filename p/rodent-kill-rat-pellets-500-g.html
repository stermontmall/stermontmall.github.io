Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the 500 g pack for?
It is for warehouses, stores and farm buildings where many bait points are needed, such as grain stores, feed stores, godowns, hotels and poultry houses.

Where should the bait stations go?
Along walls, behind stacked goods, near holes and on rat runs, in covered stations that only rodents can reach.

Is baiting enough on its own?
Baiting is strongest when it is paired with good sanitation and proofing: raised pallets, sealed gaps, covered drains and cleared rubbish and bush.

How to use

Use covered bait stations so that only rodents can reach the bait. Place them along walls, behind stacked goods, near holes and on rat runs. Wear gloves when handling the bait and wash your hands afterwards, and never place bait on food surfaces or near food for people or livestock.

Inspect the stations often, replace bait that has been eaten and collect dead rodents carefully so that pets and wildlife do not feed on them.

Before you start

  • Store grain, feed and produce on raised pallets away from walls
  • Seal holes and gaps and keep drains covered
  • Remove rubbish and clutter, and cut grass and bush near the buildings
  • Walk the whole building first and mark where the droppings and gnaw marks are
