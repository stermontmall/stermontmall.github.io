Uses & applications

Infestations in large buildings are brought under control by baiting many points and keeping them topped up as the rodents feed. The 500 g size gives you the quantity to do that without running short halfway, which suits property managers, farmers, shopkeepers and stores that treat regularly. Pellets need no mixing, so a caretaker can fill stations quickly on a round of the building.

How to use

Use covered bait stations so that only rodents can reach the bait. Place them along walls, behind stacked goods, near holes and on rat runs. Wear gloves when handling the bait and wash your hands afterwards, and never place bait on food surfaces or near food for people or livestock. Inspect the stations often, replace bait that has been eaten and collect dead rodents carefully so that pets and wildlife do not feed on them.

Before you start

  • Store grain, feed and produce on raised pallets away from walls
  • Seal holes and gaps and keep drains covered
  • Remove rubbish and clutter, and cut grass and bush near the buildings
  • Walk the whole building first and mark where the droppings and gnaw marks are

Safety information

Read the label before use and check the PCPB registration number. Keep Rodent-Kill away from children, pets and livestock, and wear gloves whenever you handle it.

Storage & handling

Reseal the pack after filling the stations and keep the rest in a locked, dry, cool store, apart from feed and food.
