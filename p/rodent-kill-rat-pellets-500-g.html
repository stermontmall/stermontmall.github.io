Safe and Effective Baiting

Use covered bait stations so that only rodents can reach the bait, and place them along walls, behind stacked goods, near holes and on rat runs. Wear gloves when handling the bait, wash your hands afterwards, and never place bait on food surfaces or near food for people or livestock. Inspect the stations often, replace bait that has been eaten and collect dead rodents carefully so that pets and wildlife do not feed on them.

Rodent Prevention Tips

Store grain, feed and produce on raised pallets away from walls, seal holes and gaps, keep drains covered and remove rubbish and clutter. Cut grass and bush near buildings to reduce shelter. Combining baiting with strong sanitation and proofing gives lasting results.
