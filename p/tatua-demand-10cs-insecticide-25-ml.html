Where it helps

Mosquitoes rest on walls and ceilings after feeding. A residual spray leaves a protective layer on treated surfaces, so mosquitoes that land there are killed over a period of time. This is a practical tool for homes in areas where mosquitoes are a problem, particularly alongside other protection such as treated nets and screens. The capsule suspension formulation holds the active ingredient in tiny capsules, which helps it stay on surfaces longer.

How to use it

Spray the walls and ceilings as directed by the label. Remove or cover food, utensils and fish tanks before treating, and keep people and pets out of the room until the spray is dry. Follow the dilution on the label.

Before you start

  • Empty standing water from buckets, tins, tyres and flower pots
  • Clean gutters and cover water tanks to reduce mosquito breeding
  • Use treated bed nets and window screens for extra protection
  • Read the label before you mix

Safety

Keep away from children, food and pets and read the label before use. Check the PCPB registration number on the label. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and do not contaminate water sources.

Storage

Keep the sachet in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food and out of reach of children.
