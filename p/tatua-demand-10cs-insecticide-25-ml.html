Why Choose a Residual Mosquito Spray

Mosquitoes rest on walls and ceilings after feeding. A residual spray leaves a protective layer on treated surfaces, so mosquitoes that land there are killed over a period of time. This is a practical tool for homes in areas where mosquitoes are a problem, particularly alongside other protection measures such as treated nets and screens. The capsule suspension (CS) formulation holds the active ingredient in tiny capsules, which helps it stay on surfaces longer.

Tips for Mosquito Control

Spray walls and ceilings as directed by the label, and remove or cover food, utensils and fish tanks before treating. Keep people and pets out of the room until the spray is dry. Reduce mosquito breeding by emptying standing water from buckets, tins, tyres and flower pots, cleaning gutters and covering water tanks. Use treated bed nets and window screens for extra protection.
