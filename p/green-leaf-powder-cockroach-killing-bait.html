Using Green Leaf powder responsibly in Kenyan homes and businesses

Before use, read the complete label and follow the stated application amount, placement instructions and precautions. Apply only to the sites approved by the manufacturer. Avoid placing powder where it can be spread by sweeping, airflow, water or routine contact. Keep the original packaging for reference and store any unused sachets sealed in a cool, dry, secure location. Wash hands after handling and do not transfer the powder into unlabelled containers.

In kitchens, shops, restaurants, apartments and stores, combine any correctly labelled bait treatment with practical prevention: keep food sealed, clean crumbs and spills, empty refuse regularly, repair leaks and reduce clutter around cupboards and appliances. These measures help remove food, water and shelter that support cockroach activity. Results depend on the infestation and correct use; no product should be presented as a sure or permanent solution. If activity continues, seek advice from a qualified pest-management professional.
