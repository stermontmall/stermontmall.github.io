Using an electric mosquito vapouriser carefully

Place the appliance only where the manufacturer permits and use it with the specified refill. Do not cover the heater or position it where it can be knocked over, become wet or contact combustible materials. Follow the stated ventilation and operating-time instructions; odour or an LED indicator is not a substitute for reading the label. Disconnect or switch off the appliance as directed before replacing the refill, and allow hot components to cool if the instructions require it.

Reduce mosquito exposure alongside any vapouriser use by fitting intact window and door screens, removing containers that collect rainwater, emptying water-holding items regularly and keeping outdoor drains clear. Use nets where appropriate and follow public-health guidance on mosquito prevention. A vapouriser does not remove breeding sites or guarantee protection in every room or outdoor setting. If mosquito activity remains high, inspect for nearby standing water and consider professional advice for the property.
