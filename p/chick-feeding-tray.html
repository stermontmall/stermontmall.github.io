Product overview

Chick feeding tray for poultry farmers in Kenya raising kienyeji, broiler and layer chicks. The open tray format makes starter feed accessible during brooding and early chick development, making it a practical accessory for backyard poultry keepers, small farms and commercial poultry units. Place the tray on a stable, dry surface where chicks can reach the feed without crowding. Use enough feeding space for the flock, refill with fresh feed as needed and remove spilled or wet feed. Wash and dry the tray regularly to maintain a cleaner brooding area and support everyday chick management.
