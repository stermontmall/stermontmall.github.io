Product overview

BASF TRELONA ATBS ANNUAL TERMITE BAIT STATIONS – 16 PACK BASF Trelona ATBS is an in-ground termite bait station system designed for planned monitoring and bait-based management of subterranean termites around buildings. The low-profile stations are installed in the soil around a property and provide access points where foraging termites can encounter the station's compatible bait system. This 16-pack is intended for pest-management programmes at residential and commercial properties. It is not a general-purpose insecticide spray. WHY CHOOSE BASF TRELONA? The main reason to choose Trelona is that it is designed for more than simply checking whether termites are present. When used with the correct Trelona bait cartridges and according to the product label, the system supports bait-based termite colony management. BASF's Trelona bait uses novaluron, an insect growth regulator that interferes with normal termite development. Foraging termites feed on the bait and can share it within the colony. This makes the system different from a monitoring-only station, which is primarily used to detect termite activity and does not, by itself, provide an active colony-control bait. HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS Stations are placed in suitable soil locations around the building perimeter according to the site assessment and manufacturer's instructions. Termites foraging through the soil may enter the station and encounter the compatible monitoring material or bait. Where termite activity is identified, the appropriate Trelona bait configuration is used in accordance with its label and the pest-management plan. Stations are then inspected and maintained at the intervals specified by the manufacturer and product label. Results depend on correct installation, station placement, bait configuration, inspection and site conditions; no termite system should be presented as a guaranteed solution for every infestation. WHERE IT CAN BE USED

Trelona ATBS stations may form part of a planned termite management programme around homes, offices, warehouses, commercial premises and other buildings where subterranean termite risk is being assessed. A qualified pest-management professional should determine the station layout and spacing after considering the building perimeter, soil conditions, termite pressure and the manufacturer's instructions.

TRELONA BAIT STATIONS VS MONITORING-ONLY STATIONS

A monitoring-only station is mainly an inspection point: it helps identify termite activity, but a separate suitable treatment may be needed if termites are found. Trelona ATBS is designed to work as part of a compatible baiting system, so it can support a treatment programme as well as monitoring when the correct bait is used. Do not assume that all in-ground stations, bait cartridges or attractants are interchangeable. Confirm compatibility before combining products from different manufacturers.
