Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Read the label and safety instructions before opening the container. Wear all protective clothing and personal protective equipment specified by the manufacturer. Avoid skin and eye contact and avoid breathing spray mist.

Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling crop-protection products. Keep the product in its original labelled container, tightly closed and secured away from children, pets, livestock, food, animal feed and water sources. Store in the conditions specified on the label.
