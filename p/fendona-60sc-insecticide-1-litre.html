About Fendona 60SC Insecticide – 1 Litre

Fendona 60SC Insecticide (1 Litre) Fendona 60SC is a BASF insecticide containing alpha-cypermethrin at 60 g/L, supplied as a water-based suspension concentrate. It is used for public health and household pest control, giving fast knockdown and a lasting residual film on treated surfaces. This 1 litre pack suits homes, hostels, hotels, offices and food premises that need a reliable, professional-grade treatment. KEY FEATURES • Alpha-cypermethrin 60 g/L • Water-based suspension concentrate • Fast knockdown of insects • Lasting residual film on treated surfaces • BASF brand • 1 litre pack • Delivered to all 47 counties WHAT IT CONTROLS Cockroaches, bed bugs, ants, houseflies, mosquitoes and other crawling and flying insects in homes, hostels, hotels, offices and food premises. WHY A RESIDUAL TREATMENT HELPS A residual insecticide leaves a thin film on the surface after the water dries. Insects that walk across the surface pick up the active ingredient, so the treatment keeps working for a period after spraying. Because Fendona 60SC is water-based, it can be used on many surfaces in and around buildings. HOW TO USE IT Mix with clean water and apply as a coarse spray to cracks, crevices, skirting edges, behind furniture and other places where insects hide or walk. BASF's published guidance for Fendona 6 SC is 25 ml in 5 litres of water for about 100 m2 for routine treatment, and 50 ml in 5 litres for heavy infestations. Rates can differ between markets, so always follow the rate printed on your pack. Shake the bottle well before measuring, because it is a suspension, and mix thoroughly in the sprayer. FOR HOSTELS, HOTELS AND OFFICES Plan treatment of the whole building in one go so insects do not move between rooms. Pay special attention to kitchens, store rooms, drains, door and window frames, and bed frames. Keep a simple record of dates, areas treated and rates used. For large buildings or persistent infestations, consider a licensed pest control operator. SAFETY Do not spray people, pets, food or cooking surfaces. Remove or cover food and utensils, keep children and animals out until surfaces are dry, and wear gloves and a mask. It is very toxic to fish and aquatic life, so never rinse sprayers or empty containers into drains, streams or ponds. STORAGE Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food.
