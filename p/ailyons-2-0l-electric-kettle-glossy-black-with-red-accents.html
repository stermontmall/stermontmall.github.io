Everyday Uses

Make tea and coffee for the family, fill a thermos flask before work, prepare instant oats or noodles, warm water for washing baby bottles, or boil water for hot water bottles on cold nights. Because it heats only the water you need, it can be cheaper to use than boiling on a stove.

Cleaning and Care

Switch off and unplug the kettle before cleaning, and let it cool down. Wipe the body with a soft damp cloth. To remove limescale from the inside, boil a mix of water and white vinegar, leave it for a short time, rinse thoroughly and boil fresh water once or twice before use. Never put the base or the plug in water.

Safety Tips

Do not switch the kettle on with no water inside, and do not fill beyond the maximum mark. Place it on a flat, dry surface away from the edge of the counter and keep it out of reach of children. Steam and hot water can cause burns, so keep your face away from the spout when pouring. Follow the user manual.
