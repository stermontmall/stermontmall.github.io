KOPPERT HORIVER YELLOW ROLLERTRAP ORGANIC – 100 METRES IN KENYA

Koppert Horiver Yellow Rollertrap Organic is a long adhesive ribbon for monitoring and trapping selected flying crop pests in suitable greenhouse, nursery and horticultural environments. The listing identifies a 100-metre yellow roll. The yellow colour attracts insects that respond to this wavelength, and the wet sticky adhesive retains insects that land on the surface. It is intended for crop monitoring and hotspot trapping as part of an integrated pest-management programme, not as a chemical spray or a complete replacement for crop scouting.

TARGET PESTS AND HOW THE ROLL WORKS

Koppert identifies Horiver Rollertrap for trapping whiteflies, thrips, leaf miners and fungus gnats in areas where pest pressure is expected to be high. Insects are attracted to the coloured ribbon and become caught in the adhesive. The yellow roll is useful where growers want a long continuous monitoring surface rather than individual cards. The product is a polyethylene ribbon coated with wet sticky glue. It is a physical trapping tool and does not rely on a pesticide active ingredient in the adhesive.

WHERE TO INSTALL THE ROLLERTRAP

Koppert recommends placing Rollertraps in locations where high pest pressure is expected, such as along greenhouse gables, near ventilation windows and other areas with airflow. The roll should be attached securely to crop wires, greenhouse poles or other suitable support structures. The correct height depends on the crop and the width of the roll. Koppert's directions specify a different positioning height for 30cm-wide ribbon than for narrower rolls, so follow the guidance for the actual 100m product width supplied. This listing does not specify the width; confirm it before installation.

PLAN THE MONITORING LAYOUT

A 100-metre roll can be used to create long monitoring runs, but the quantity required depends on the greenhouse dimensions, crop rows, entrances and pest pressure. Map the growing area and decide which sections need monitoring before cutting the ribbon. Use additional monitoring points around known hotspots or ventilation openings where justified by crop observations. Do not assume one roll will cover a fixed number of square metres. Record the location and length of each installed section so that future inspections can compare results consistently.

INSTALLATION AND HANDLING

Koppert advises bringing the Rollertrap to room temperature before installation so the adhesive softens and the ribbon unrolls more easily. Use a suitable rod through the centre opening to allow the roll to rotate freely while unrolling. Attach the beginning and end of each run securely to the support structure and provide intermediate support where necessary. Keep the adhesive away from workers, clothing, leaves and equipment. Avoid placing the ribbon where beneficial insects may be caught unnecessarily; follow crop-specific biological-control guidance.

INSPECTION AND REPLACEMENT

Inspect the ribbon regularly and record the number and type of insects observed. Replace the section when the adhesive surface is largely covered with insects or debris, or according to the manufacturer's replacement guidance. A high catch may indicate a hotspot that needs closer crop scouting. It does not identify the insect species automatically or determine the appropriate treatment. Check leaves, flowers and growing points and consult an agronomist when pest identification or action thresholds are uncertain.

INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT

Horiver Rollertrap can support early detection and mass trapping in a broader IPM programme. Combine sticky monitoring with incoming-plant inspection, greenhouse hygiene, weed management, removal of infested plant material where appropriate and biological-control practices. If pesticides are considered, confirm that the product is registered and labelled for the crop and pest, and follow application directions and pre-harvest intervals. Do not rely on adhesive ribbon alone to control a severe infestation.
