Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

This is a pest-control or agricultural chemical product. Use it only for the crop, pest, site and application method expressly authorised on the current Kenya-approved product label. Read the full label before opening or use.

Follow the label exactly for personal protective equipment, dilution, application, ventilation, re-entry period, pre-harvest interval, environmental restrictions and any other precautions. Do not guess a dose, mix it with another product, or use it on food, animals, people or surfaces unless the label specifically permits that use. Keep the original container tightly closed and locked away from children, unauthorised persons and animals, and separate from food, drink and animal feed.

Prevent spills and contamination of drains, wells, streams and other water sources. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling; wash hands and exposed skin after handling. Never reuse an empty pesticide container; follow the label and approved local hazardous-waste collection or disposal instructions.

In case of suspected poisoning or significant exposure, seek urgent medical attention and show the product label. Kenya PCPB poison information hotlines listed in its labelling guidance: 0800 720 021 or 0800 730 030.

Product overview

Osefin 7.5 WP Dudu Dust is a public-health insecticide formulated for domestic control of cockroaches, ants and fleas. The 200g pack contains carbaryl 7.5 g/kg and is registered in Kenya under PCPB(CR)1394. The Kenya Pest Control Products Board public-health register lists Osefin 7.5 WP for control of cockroaches, ants and fleas in domestic environments and classifies it as WHO Class U, unlikely to present acute hazard under normal use.

This classification does not mean that the product is harmless: follow the label, keep it away from children and pets, and use it only as directed. Osefin is a dust formulation intended for targeted application in places where crawling pests hide or travel. Cockroaches, ants and fleas can shelter in cracks, crevices, floor edges and other protected spaces.

A dust product is different from an aerosol spray or liquid residual treatment: it is placed as a dry formulation at approved locations, rather than diluted in water. The product should only be used in the manner and locations permitted by the current Kenyan label. Do not apply it to food, food-contact surfaces, kitchen utensils, bedding, clothing, toys or areas where children or pets can contact the dust.

The active ingredient carbaryl belongs to the carbamate insecticide group. Osefin is intended for the domestic pests and uses stated on its registration and label. It is not a crop spray, mosquito larvicide, termite treatment or rodenticide.

Correct pest identification is important because ants, cockroaches and fleas require different inspection and sanitation approaches. If the pest is not identified correctly, the chosen treatment may be unsuitable and the infestation may continue. A practical household pest-management programme begins with inspection and housekeeping.

Remove food crumbs and spills, store food in sealed containers, clean grease and food residues, repair leaks, empty waste regularly and reduce clutter that creates hiding places. For ants, identify trails and entry points and remove food sources that attract them. For cockroaches, inspect around sinks, cupboards, appliances and service penetrations.

For fleas, identify the source of infestation and address affected pets and their resting areas with products specifically approved for that use. Osefin is one possible part of a wider integrated pest-management programme, not a substitute for sanitation or source control. Osho Chemical Industries describes Osefin Dust 7.5% as a domestic insecticide with contact and stomach action.

The manufacturer's product information directs users to apply the dust to infested areas and repeat only as specified on the product label or when pests reappear. The manufacturer identifies cracks, floors and crevices as examples of cockroach treatment locations, and floor cracks and crevices for flea control. For ants, it describes placement around the house.

Always follow the exact instructions on the pack supplied, including any restrictions on placement, quantity, frequency and re-entry. Before buying, check that the container is sealed, correctly labelled, within its expiry date and displays PCPB(CR)1394. The product photo is for identification; the actual pack label is the controlling source for use and safety directions.

Do not use a product if its label is missing, damaged, inconsistent with the expected registration or appears counterfeit. Keep the batch number, purchase record and expiry date available for traceability. If the pest problem is severe, recurring or in a food-handling facility, consult a qualified pest-control professional for an inspection and treatment plan.

During handling, avoid breathing dust and prevent it from contacting eyes or skin. Use the personal protective equipment specified on the label and wash hands after use. Keep children, pets and bystanders away from treated areas until the label permits access.

Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. Do not apply near aquariums, ponds, drains or water sources unless the label specifically permits it. Never sweep or wash pesticide dust into a drain or watercourse.

Follow the label and local requirements for storage and disposal. Store Osefin 7.5 WP in its original container, tightly closed, in a cool, dry and secure place away from food, animal feed, medicines, children and unauthorised persons. Never transfer it into an unlabelled container or reuse the packaging for another purpose.

Dispose of unused product and empty packaging through approved local pesticide-waste arrangements.
