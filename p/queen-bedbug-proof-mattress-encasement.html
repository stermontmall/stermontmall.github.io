About Zippered Bed Bug Proof Mattress Encasement – Queen Size

Product overview

A full-zip mattress encasement designed to create a protective physical barrier around a mattress during a bed bug prevention or management programme. The fitted cover encloses the mattress and helps reduce hiding places and simplify inspection of the sleeping surface. It is a monitoring and exclusion aid, not a stand-alone cure for an active infestation; bed frames, headboards, bedside furniture and surrounding rooms still require inspection and appropriate treatment. Check mattress dimensions and zipper closure before ordering. Keep the cover intact, inspect seams regularly and follow the care instructions so the barrier remains functional. Measure the mattress before selecting the queen size. Mattress dimensions are not identical across every manufacturer or market. Measure the width, length and depth, including any integrated pillow-top layer, before ordering. A cover that is too tight can put strain on the zipper and stitching; an oversized cover can bunch, shift or feel uncomfortable beneath bedding. Confirm the exact dimensions stated on the supplied product packaging rather than relying only on the word queen. If the mattress is unusually deep or has a topper, obtain a fit confirmation before purchase. Unlike a fitted sheet or thin mattress protector, a full encasement is intended to surround the mattress on all sides and close with a zipper. This creates a continuous outer surface that can make visual inspection easier and reduce the number of original mattress seams and folds available as hiding places. The zipper and its end closure are critical parts of the barrier. This listing does not claim a laboratory certification, a particular fabric rating or a guaranteed insect-proof performance beyond the physical cover design described. Remove bedding and inspect the mattress before fitting. Follow the maker's directions and, if the mattress is heavy, use two people to guide it into the cover without dragging it across sharp edges. Do not pull hard on the zipper or force fabric into its teeth. Check that corners are seated, the cover is not twisted and the closure reaches its intended end point. After installation, inspect the entire perimeter and confirm that no part of the mattress remains exposed. An encasement is a supportive barrier and inspection aid, not a treatment for the whole room. Bed bugs may remain in bed-frame joints, screw holes, headboards, bedside cabinets, upholstered furniture, skirting gaps, luggage and nearby rooms. A complete response includes a careful inspection, monitoring, appropriate treatment and follow-up checks. Where suitable, bed-leg interceptor cups can provide additional monitoring information. Do not tell customers that covering the mattress alone eradicates an infestation. Examine the fabric, seams, corners and zipper regularly for tears, punctures, loose stitching or a closure that no longer seals. Keep sharp tools, pets' claws and rough furniture edges away from the cover. Follow the actual care label for laundering and drying; high heat, bleach or aggressive cleaning can damage some materials. If the cover is torn or the zipper fails, treat the barrier as compromised and replace it or seek a repair method approved by the manufacturer. Record inspection dates in accommodation settings. In Kenyan homes, rental apartments, student hostels, guest houses and hotels, mattress encasements can support consistent housekeeping and bed bug monitoring. Multi-room accommodation should use a room-by-room inspection process instead of relying on covers only where bites are reported. Staff should know how to report sightings and suspicious marks promptly. When moving mattresses, protect them from contact with infested furniture and inspect bed frames and headboards as well. A standard procedure helps reduce the chance of carrying insects between rooms. Do not spray insecticide onto the cover or zipper unless the product label specifically permits that use. The encasement is a physical accessory, not a chemical treatment. If pesticide treatment is needed, follow the pesticide label, Kenyan registration requirements, personal protective equipment guidance and re-entry instructions. Keep children and pets away from treatment products and equipment. Never use an encasement to conceal a known infestation from a tenant or guest; report findings and arrange an appropriate inspection and treatment plan. This listing is for a queen-size zippered mattress encasement at KSh 4,500. Confirm mattress width, length and depth, zipper construction, fabric details, colour and pack contents before checkout. Pillows, sheets, mattress, bed frame, interceptor traps and treatment services are not included unless product options explicitly say otherwise. The product is intended for a mattress, not a pillow or duvet. Choose the size by measurement and retain the product care label for future cleaning and inspection. For accommodation managers, the cover can form part of a standard room-turnover checklist. Staff can check that the zipper remains closed, the fabric is intact and the mattress exterior is clean before fresh bedding is applied.
