WHERE IT MAY BE USED The supplier lists agricultural spraying, gardening and outdoor maintenance as application areas, with examples including farms, orchards, gardens and greenhouses. Avoid using the machine in enclosed areas unless the manufacturer expressly permits that use and the risk assessment and product label support it. SAFE SETUP Before use, read the manual for the exact machine and the label for the material being applied. Inspect the tank, cap, straps, engine housing, air outlet, hoses, controls, attachments and fasteners. Confirm that all guards and covers are fitted. Prepare fuel and engine oil exactly as the manufacturer specifies for the 4-stroke engine; do not use a two-stroke premix unless the manual explicitly requires it. Start the engine outdoors in a well-ventilated area, away from sparks, flames and combustible materials. Do not refuel a hot engine. Keep the unit stable while starting and never point the outlet toward people or animals. PERSONAL PROTECTION AND ENVIRONMENT Use the protective clothing, gloves, eye protection, hearing protection and respiratory protection required by the equipment manual and material label. Engine-powered blowers can create noise and move dust, so keep bystanders away and avoid directing airflow toward roads, neighbouring properties, livestock, food, water or sensitive plants. For chemical application, check wind speed and direction and avoid drift. Follow label instructions for re-entry, harvest intervals, exposure control and disposal. Never use an unlabelled powder or liquid. Do not mix formulations or switch between modes without cleaning the machine as instructed. CLEANING AND MAINTENANCE After use, empty and clean the relevant tank and application path according to the material label and machine manual. Dispose of leftover solution and rinse water only as permitted; never discharge residues into drains, streams, ponds or drinking-water sources. Remove powder residue before changing to a liquid mode, and clean filters and outlets using the recommended method. Maintain the four-stroke engine according to the service schedule and use the specified fuel and oil. Inspect straps, seals, hoses and attachments for wear.
