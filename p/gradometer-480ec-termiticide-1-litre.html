Gradometer 480EC Termiticide (1 Litre) Gradometer 480EC is a termiticide containing chlorpyrifos 480 g/L, supplied in a 1 litre bottle. It is used for treating timber, fencing poles and wooden structures, and for treating termite nests in lawns and gardens in Kenya. KEY FEATURES • Emulsifiable concentrate termiticide • Active ingredient: chlorpyrifos 480 g/L • For timber in buildings and construction, trees and fencing poles • For treating termite nests in lawns and gardens • 1 litre bottle • Delivered to all 47 counties SPECIFICATIONS • Brand: Gradometer • Product: 480EC termiticide • Active ingredient: Chlorpyrifos 480 g/L • Pack size: 1 litre THE TERMITE PROBLEM Termites feed on wood, paper and other plant material. They can weaken roof timbers, door frames, fence posts and furniture before the damage is clearly visible. In gardens and lawns they build nests and mounds, and they can attack the roots of young plants and trees. Early treatment limits the damage and the cost of repairs. WHERE IT IS USED • Timber in buildings and construction • Trees and fencing poles • Wooden structures such as sheds, posts and frames • Termite nests in lawns and gardens HOW TO USE IT Gradometer 480EC is an emulsifiable concentrate, so it is diluted with water as directed on the label. Mix and apply exactly as directed. Use the label dilution for timber treatment and for nest treatment, and do not use stronger mixtures than recommended. Treat timber thoroughly, paying attention to cut ends, joints and places where the wood touches the soil. For nests, follow the label instructions for reaching the nest and treating the soil around it. GOOD PRACTICE • Remove old stumps, dead wood and rotting timber from around buildings • Keep timber off the ground where possible and keep it dry • Fix leaking pipes and drains that keep the soil damp • Inspect timber regularly for mud tubes, soft wood and flying termites SAFETY Check the PCPB registration number on the label and mix and apply exactly as directed. Wear protective gear, including gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing, and keep away from children, pets, food and water. Chlorpyrifos is harmful to fish and aquatic life, so keep it away from streams, ponds, wells and drains. For buildings, consider a licensed pest control operator. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed. ORDER Pay by M-Pesa.
