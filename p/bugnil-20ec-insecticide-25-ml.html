Uses & applications

Bed bugs are small, flat insects that spend the day in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, wall cracks, loose wallpaper and furniture. At night they feed and leave itchy bites, small blood spots on sheets and dark droppings. They can go a long time without feeding and travel easily on luggage, clothes and second-hand furniture, which is why a problem in one room often reaches the next.

How to use

Strip the bed and wash bedding, curtains and clothes at a high temperature. Vacuum the mattress, frame, floor and corners and empty the vacuum outside. Dilute Bugnil exactly as the label directs, then spray mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, skirting boards, cracks and other hiding places. Keep people, pets and food out of the room until it has dried, and repeat the treatment if the label advises it, because eggs and survivors can cause a return.

Before you start

  • Move beds away from walls so you can reach the joints
  • Check second-hand furniture before bringing it indoors
  • Cover or remove food, dishes and pet bowls
  • Do not use bedding again until it is dry

Safety information

Read the label before use. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Keep it away from children, pets, food and water. Do not spray bedding that will be used before it has dried. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle.

Storage & handling

Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 25 ml pack enough for a rental?
It is sized for a single treatment, such as one bedroom or bedsitter. For several units, hostels or repeated infestations, consider more than one pack or a licensed pest control operator.

Why do bed bugs come back after spraying?
Eggs and hidden survivors can remain in cracks, frames and furniture. Treat every affected room together, follow the label on repeat treatments and check second-hand items before they enter the home.

Can landlords use Bugnil between tenants?
Yes, it is intended for homes and rentals. Treating promptly between tenants stops a small problem becoming a bigger one, and a record of dates and rooms treated helps you follow up.
