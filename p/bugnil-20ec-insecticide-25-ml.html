Why bed bugs are hard to remove

Bed bugs are small, flat insects that hide during the day in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, cracks in walls, behind loose wallpaper and in furniture. They come out at night to feed and leave itchy bites, small blood spots on sheets and dark droppings. They can survive for long periods without feeding, and they spread easily on luggage, clothes and second-hand furniture.

How to get the best results

  • Strip the bed and wash bedding, curtains and clothes at a high temperature, then dry them well
  • Vacuum mattresses, bed frames, floors and corners, and empty the vacuum outside the house
  • Dilute exactly as directed on the label
  • Spray mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, skirting boards, cracks and other hiding places
  • Keep people, pets and food away from the room until the spray has dried
  • Repeat the treatment if the label advises it, because eggs and survivors can cause a return
  • Move beds away from walls and check second-hand furniture before bringing it indoors

For landlords and tenants

Treating a rental promptly between tenants stops a small problem from becoming a larger one. For large buildings, hostels or repeated infestations, consider a licensed pest control operator.

Safety

Read and follow the label before use. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Do not spray bedding that will be used before it has dried. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle.

Storage

Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place.
