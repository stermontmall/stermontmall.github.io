About Habei Bear 3-Piece Insulated Bottle and Tumbler Set (260ml, 700ml, 1200ml)

The Habei Bear black insulated set of 3 gives you three drinkware sizes in one: a 260 ml pocket flask, a 700 ml sports bottle with a flip lid, and a 1200 ml tumbler with handle, straw and lid. It keeps drinks hot or cold on the go, for the office, gym, school or car. It is sold as 1 set of 3.

  • Insulated to keep drinks hot or cold
  • Sold as 1 set of 3

The three pieces

  • 260 ml pocket flask: a small flask that slips into a bag or pocket. It suits a cup of tea or coffee, a short trip, school snacks, or a small drink for a child.
  • 700 ml sports bottle with flip lid: a medium bottle that is easy to carry to the gym, a walk, a run or a game. The flip lid makes it simple to drink with one hand.
  • 1200 ml tumbler with handle, straw and lid: a large tumbler for long days at the desk, in the car or at home. The handle makes it easy to carry, and the straw lets you sip without lifting the lid.

Why Insulated drinkware

An insulated bottle helps hot drinks stay hot and cold drinks stay cold for longer than an ordinary bottle. This means tea stays warm through a meeting, and water stays cool in the car, at the gym or at school.

Use it anywhere

  • At the office for tea, coffee and water through the day
  • At the gym for cold water or a sports drink
  • At school for a child's drink
  • In the car on long drives and commutes
  • At home, on trips and at outdoor events

Care tips

  • Wash with warm soapy water before first use and after each use
  • Rinse lids and straws thoroughly and let all parts dry
  • Do not put in a microwave or freezer
  • Do not fill carbonated drinks to the top, because gas can build up
  • Check that lids are closed firmly before putting a bottle in a bag
