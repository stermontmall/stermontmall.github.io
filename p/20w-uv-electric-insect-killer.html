About Rebune 20W UV Mosquito and Insect Killer Lamp

The Rebune 20W UV Mosquito and Insect Killer Lamp is an electric flying-insect control unit intended for suitable indoor use in homes, shops and other premises. The product uses ultraviolet light to attract flying insects towards an enclosed electrified grid. The listing identifies a 20W model. It is a plug-in electrical appliance and should be installed and maintained according to the manufacturer's instructions, with particular care around children, water and electrical connections.

How the UV Insect Killer works

The lamp uses UVA tubes to attract certain flying insects towards the unit. The electrified grid is designed to kill insects that contact it. This is different from a glue-board insect light trap, which retains insects on an adhesive board. The product record identifies the Rebune 20W model and supplier information describes a 220–240V, 50/60Hz electrical supply with two T8 10W tubes. Confirm these details from the product label before installation.

Where to use it

The unit may be considered for suitable indoor spaces such as homes, offices, shops and other rooms where flying insects are a nuisance. Place it in a location recommended by the manufacturer where it can attract insects without being exposed to rain, water or excessive moisture. Avoid installing it directly above food, food-preparation surfaces or open ingredients. In commercial food premises, choose an insect-control device and placement method that meet the site's hygiene requirements. Do not assume that one lamp provides effective coverage for every room size or layout.

Installation and electrical safety

Check the voltage and power requirements printed on the appliance before connecting it. Use a suitable outlet and keep the unit away from water and damp areas. Mount or position it securely so that it cannot fall or be knocked over. Ensure the electrified grid is inaccessible to children and pets. Do not touch the grid while the unit is connected to power. If the cable, plug, casing or internal components are damaged, stop using the appliance and have it checked by a competent technician.

Cleaning and maintenance

Switch off and disconnect the unit from power before cleaning or servicing. Allow the appliance to cool and follow the manufacturer's instructions for removing dead insects from the grid. Do not use water on live electrical components and do not insert metal tools into the grid while the unit is connected. Inspect the housing and protective guard for damage. Replace tubes only with compatible parts of the correct length, wattage and base type. The 20W model's supplier information identifies two 10W T8 tubes; verify this against the actual lamp before ordering replacements.

What the Lamp can and cannot do

An electric insect killer can help reduce some adult flying insects in a suitable room, but it does not eliminate breeding sources or prevent new insects from entering. It may not attract every mosquito species equally, and performance depends on placement, competing light sources, airflow and insect behaviour. Combine the device with window screens, closed doors, waste management and removal of standing water where mosquitoes breed. For persistent fly activity, identify the breeding source rather than relying on the lamp alone.
