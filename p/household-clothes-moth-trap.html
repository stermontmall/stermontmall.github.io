Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

IN KENYA

The Moths Pheromone Trap for Wardrobes and Storage is a sticky monitoring device for checking adult moth activity around clothing, wardrobes, closets, linen cupboards and textile-storage areas. Clothes moth activity can be difficult to notice at first because damage may occur in quiet, undisturbed parts of a wardrobe or storage room. A pheromone trap can help reveal the presence of adult moths and indicate whether an inspection of nearby fabrics and stored items is needed.

It is a monitoring aid; it does not repair damaged clothing or remove larvae hidden in fabrics.

HOW A PHEROMONE MONITORING TRAP HELPS

The lure is intended to attract adult moths from the target group to an adhesive surface. Captured moths provide a visible record that can be checked over time. This information may help a household, hotel, laundry, uniform store, clothing retailer or textile manager identify where moth activity is being detected.

A trap should not be interpreted as proof that every garment is infested, nor does an empty trap prove that no moths or larvae are present. Monitoring results need to be considered alongside a careful inspection.

WHERE TO POSITION IT

Potential monitoring points include wardrobes, closets, linen stores, carpet-storage areas and shelves holding natural-fibre textiles. Place the trap where it can be inspected easily but will not touch clothing, bedding or other fabrics. Keep it away from children, pets, direct contact with water and areas where it may be crushed or covered.

Follow the supplier's instructions for placement distance, activation and replacement. The exact target species and lure coverage should be confirmed from the pack.

INSPECT FABRICS AND AREAS

When moth activity is suspected, inspect stored garments, blankets, carpets, upholstery and other susceptible textiles. Pay attention to seams, folds, collars, cuffs, pockets, hems and dark undisturbed corners. Look for irregular holes, larvae, shed skins, cases or fine debris.

Check stored items before moving them to another room because infested textiles can spread the problem. Empty the affected storage area and vacuum shelf joints, skirting edges and cracks. Dispose of vacuum contents in a sealed bag.

CLEANING AND PREVENTION

Clean garments according to their care labels. Where suitable, laundering, professional cleaning or other approved fabric-care methods may help address eggs or larvae. Do not apply pesticides to clothing or bedding unless the product label specifically permits that use.

Store clean seasonal clothing in clean, sealed containers or garment bags. Reduce dust and lint accumulation, and avoid storing worn clothing for long periods without cleaning. Inspect second-hand textiles and items returning from storage before placing them in wardrobes.

MONITORING IN HOMES AND BUSINESSES

For a home, a simple record can include the trap location, installation date and observations at each check. In hotels, guest houses, laundries, clothing shops and uniform stores, assign each trap a reference so that results can be compared across rooms or storage zones. A repeated catch in one area should trigger a closer inspection and review of storage and cleaning routines.

If damage continues or multiple areas show activity, a professional inspection can help distinguish clothes moths from other fabric pests and recommend an appropriate response.

PRODUCT DETAILS AND CARE

This listing describes a pheromone sticky trap for wardrobe and textile-storage monitoring. Confirm the number of traps supplied, dimensions, lure type, target moth species and recommended replacement interval from the supplier's packaging. Keep the adhesive surface clean and dry, and replace the unit when it is dusty, full or past the stated service life.

Dispose of used traps responsibly and keep them out of reach of children and pets.
