Why Choose Pentagon 50EC Thrips and aphids suck sap from leaves, flowers and young shoots, causing curling, scarring, stunted growth and lower yields. Aphids also spread plant viruses and leave sticky honeydew that attracts sooty mould. Lambda-cyhalothrin is a pyrethroid that acts quickly on contact and by ingestion, helping growers bring pest numbers down fast. As an emulsifiable concentrate (EC), it mixes with water to form a milky spray liquid.
