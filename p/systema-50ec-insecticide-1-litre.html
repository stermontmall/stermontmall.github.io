About Emulsifiable Concentrate Insecticides

An emulsifiable concentrate, or EC, is a liquid formulation that mixes with water to form a milky spray liquid. EC products are popular with Kenyan farmers because they are easy to measure, mix well in a knapsack sprayer or tank, and spread evenly over leaves. Always check the label of Systema 50EC for the crops it is approved for, the pests it controls, the recommended dose and the safety period before harvest.

Smart Pest Management Tips

Good pest control starts with scouting. Walk your field often, check the underside of leaves and young shoots, and spray when pests reach damaging levels. Rotate insecticides with different modes of action through the season to slow resistance, use clean seed or seedlings, remove crop residues and keep weeds under control, since weeds can shelter pests. Spray in the early morning or late afternoon, avoid strong wind and do not spray when bees are foraging.

Safe use of Original Systema 50EC Insecticide – 1 Litre

  • Read the whole label before you buy or mix. The label is the legal instruction for crops, pests, rates and safety.
  • Look for the PCPB registration number on the pack. Do not buy chemicals that carry none.
  • Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI) printed on the label, the number of days that must pass between the last spray and harvest. Use the product only on the crops the label lists.
  • Wear gloves, a mask or respirator, eye protection and long protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards.
  • Keep chemicals in their original container, out of reach of children, pets and livestock. Never reuse empty containers for food or water.
