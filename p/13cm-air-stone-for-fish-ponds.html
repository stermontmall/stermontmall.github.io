Product Overview & Key Benefits

The 13cm Air Stone (supplier SKU AM-AQU-0012) is an aquaculture aeration accessory for connecting to an air pump and distributing air into a fish tank or pond. A porous stone releases the supplied air as bubbles into the water. The supplier lists this model at 13 centimetres and describes its material as durable porous stone with HDPE fittings.

It is sold individually. This listing is intended for fish farmers, hatchery operators and aquarium users who need a replaceable aeration point in a freshwater or brackish-water system. The defining specification is the 13cm dimension.

The porous body is designed to diffuse air rather than discharge it from an open tube end. The supplier identifies HDPE fittings as part of the construction. The unit connects to compatible air tubing, which in turn connects to an air pump.

Airflow is supplied by the pump; the stone itself is not an electric device and does not generate oxygen independently. Its role is to distribute the air delivered by the connected equipment.

Where & How to Use in Kenya

In a fish tank, an air stone helps distribute air bubbles through the water. In a pond, several correctly sized aeration points may be distributed to improve the spread of air. The number of stones required depends on pond or tank volume, stocking density, water depth, pump output, tubing layout and the oxygen demand of the fish.

A single 13cm stone should not be treated as a complete aeration system for every pond. Commercial operators should size the pump and aeration layout for their actual production conditions. The supplier identifies recreational aquaculture, professional fish farming, commercial aquaculture, hatcheries and fry-rearing systems among the intended applications.

Buyers can use this product as an aeration component in a larger system, or as a replacement for a compatible existing stone.

How to use

Connect the air stone to suitable air tubing and connect the tubing to an appropriately rated air pump. Check that the tubing connection is secure and that the pump is suitable for the depth and volume of the water system. Submerge the stone fully and start the pump.

Observe the bubble pattern and confirm that air is flowing. If the stone is being installed in a pond or tank with existing aeration, distribute equipment so that circulation is appropriate for the layout rather than placing all outlets in one corner.

Before you start

Check the diameter and type of the tubing connection against the fittings on the stone. Confirm that the air pump can deliver sufficient airflow at the intended operating depth. A 13cm measurement describes the stone's listed size; it does not specify pump capacity, bubble output, maximum depth or pond coverage.

Ask the supplier to confirm any connection dimensions or performance rating that your installation requires. For a large pond, obtain an aeration design based on water volume, fish species and stocking level. CARE AND MAINTENANCE The supplier recommends periodic cleaning to remove algae and mineral deposits, inspection of the tubing connection for leaks, and storage away from direct sunlight when the unit is not in use.

Use a gentle cleaning method appropriate for porous aeration media and rinse thoroughly before returning the stone to the water. Replace a stone if it is cracked, damaged, persistently blocked or no longer produces an even flow after cleaning. The supplier gives an expected service life of two to three years with regular maintenance; actual life will depend on water quality, operating hours and handling.
