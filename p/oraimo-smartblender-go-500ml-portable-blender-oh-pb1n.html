Its compact 500ml bottle format makes it suitable for a work desk, student room, home kitchen or travel bag, while its rechargeable battery allows blending away from a wall socket once charged. For shoppers comparing a portable blender in Kenya, the important distinction is that this is a personal drink-preparation appliance rather than a large countertop blender for family-sized batches. Uses & applications

Use a portable blender when preparing a single serving of a fruit smoothie, mixing soft fruit with liquid, making a simple protein drink or preparing a refreshing drink at the office. It can be convenient in a bedsitter, student accommodation, small kitchen or during a trip where a compact appliance is easier to store. The 500ml capacity is intended for personal portions; it is not a substitute for a large blender when preparing several servings, grinding hard dry ingredients or processing heavy mixtures.

How to use

Charge the appliance using a compatible 5V/2A USB power source and the supplied charging accessories, following the manual. Cut fruit into small pieces and add sufficient liquid. Do not fill beyond the marked maximum capacity. Secure the lid and operate the blender as instructed by Oraimo. If ingredients do not circulate, stop the motor, check the fill level and add liquid rather than forcing the motor to run under strain. Switch off and disconnect the appliance before cleaning the blade assembly.

Before you start

Read the supplied manual and check the cup, lid, seal and blade assembly before first use. Confirm that the product is the OH-PB1N model and that the accessories listed on the package are present. This personal blender is intended for suitable food and drink ingredients; it is not designed for hot liquids, boiling mixtures or industrial food preparation. Do not immerse the motor or charging port in water.

Safety information

AND CARE Keep fingers and utensils away from the blades. Never operate the blender with the lid unsecured, and do not open it while the blades are moving. Allow the motor to stop fully before opening. Do not blend hard objects, large ice blocks or ingredients that the manual excludes. Clean the cup and removable parts promptly after use, taking care around the sharp blade edges. Wipe the motor base with a damp cloth only. Keep the charging port dry and allow the unit to dry completely before storage. Stop using the appliance if the casing, battery or charging cable is damaged.
