KEY FEATURES • Amino acid supplement for animal feed formulation • For balancing poultry and pig rations • 25 kg bag for farms and feed mixers • Delivered to all 47 counties SPECIFICATIONS • Product: Lysine • Type: Feed amino acid • Pack size: 25 kg WHAT IS LYSINE? Lysine is an essential amino acid. Amino acids are the building blocks of protein, and animals cannot make lysine themselves, so it must come from the feed. In poultry and pigs, lysine is the amino acid that is most often short in cereal-based rations such as those made mostly with maize. When lysine is short, growth slows, feed conversion worsens and muscle development suffers. WHY ADD LYSINE TO RATIONS Adding lysine helps farmers and feed mixers balance rations so that animals get the amino acids they need. This can support growth, feed efficiency and meat yield in pigs and broilers, and egg production in layers. It also helps balance protein levels in feed formulations, which can help reduce the amount of protein-rich ingredients needed. WHERE IT IS USED • Poultry rations: chicks, broilers, growers and layers • Pig rations: weaners, growers and finishers The correct amount depends on the species, age, production level and the other ingredients in the ration. HOW TO USE IT Read and follow the label and inclusion rate before use. Add lysine to the ration at the rate recommended on the label or by your nutritionist or vet. Mix very thoroughly so that every animal receives an even amount, using a proper mixer or mixing in stages for small batches. Do not exceed the recommended rate. GOOD PRACTICE • Use a written feed formulation for every batch • Balance lysine with other amino acids, energy and minerals • Provide clean water at all times • Weigh ingredients carefully • Monitor growth, feed intake and animal health • Ask a nutritionist for help with formulations SAFETY Read and follow the label and inclusion rate before use.
