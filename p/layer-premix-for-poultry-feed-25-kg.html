KEY FEATURES • Premix for formulating layer chicken feed • For poultry layer rations • 25 kg bag for farms and feed mixers • Delivered to all 47 counties SPECIFICATIONS • Product: Layer premix • Use: Poultry layer rations • Pack size: 25 kg WHAT IS A PREMIX? A premix is a concentrated blend of nutrients, such as vitamins and trace minerals, that is added in a small amount to a larger batch of feed. Mixing a premix with grains and other ingredients lets farmers prepare their own balanced rations at a lower cost than buying complete feed, while still giving the birds the vitamins and minerals they need. WHY LAYERS NEED A SPECIAL RATION Laying hens use a lot of nutrients to produce eggs every day. Their rations need to support egg production, eggshell quality and the health of the bird. A layer ration is different from chick or broiler feed, which is why a premix made for layers is used when mixing layer feed. HOW TO USE IT Read and follow the label and mixing rate before use. Add the premix to the other feed ingredients at the rate on the label, and mix very thoroughly so that every bird gets an even amount. For small batches, premix with a portion of the feed before adding it to the main batch. Use a proper mixer where possible, since uneven mixing can lead to poor performance. Do not exceed the recommended rate. GOOD PRACTICE • Keep feed formulations written down for each batch • Provide clean, fresh water at all times • Use fresh ingredients and avoid mouldy grain • Store mixed feed in a cool, dry place and use it within a reasonable time • Watch egg production, shell quality and the health of the flock • Ask your vet or a poultry nutritionist for advice on rations WHO IT SUITS The 25 kg bag is suited to commercial layer farms, farmers who mix their own feed and feed mixers. Smaller flocks may find a smaller pack more practical. SAFETY Read and follow the label and mixing rate before use. Keep away from children and pets. Avoid breathing the dust, and wash your hands after handling. STORAGE Store in a cool, dry place off the floor, away from moisture, rodents and direct sun. Close the bag tightly after use.
