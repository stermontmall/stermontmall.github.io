About B&G Versatool 415 Termite Treatment Injection Tool

Product overview

The B&G Versatool 415 is a professional termite treatment delivery tool designed for trained operators who need to place compatible liquid termiticide at defined soil or structural locations. The tool is part of a termite treatment equipment system, not a ready-to-use pesticide. Its valve, pipe and selected tip control delivery to the treatment point. Correct selection and use depend on the building construction, product label, treatment plan and the exact kit configuration supplied. B&G's published information identifies the 415 as a Versatool termite tool kit with a valve featuring tip shut-off. A supplier specification for the 415 kit lists 12-inch and 40-inch 7/16-inch professional termite pipes, a 40-inch 5/8-inch professional rodding pipe, and several tips for soil rodding, void and directional delivery. The exact parts supplied with this listing must be verified before purchase; the model number alone should not be taken as proof that every accessory in a larger kit is included. The tip shut-off feature is intended to help the operator control flow at the delivery point and reduce unwanted discharge when repositioning the tool. B&G describes the Versatool system as compatible with selected pipe sizes and delivery tips for different treatment tasks. The operator must match the tip and pipe to the intended method and the pesticide label. Identify the evidence of termite activity, the building construction, slab edges, expansion joints, service penetrations, soil contact and previous treatment history. Determine whether the planned treatment method is permitted by the pesticide label and suitable for the site. Locate water, gas, electrical and communication services before drilling or inserting a pipe. A tool kit should be selected only after the operator knows which pipes, tips and access points the treatment plan requires. Before use, inspect the valve, pipe, tips, shut-off mechanism, seals and connectors. Confirm that all components are securely fitted and that the chosen tip is correct for the application. Follow B&G's instructions for pressure, flow, handling and maintenance. Do not force a pipe into an obstruction, apply pressure beyond the equipment rating or inject into an unknown void. The 415 kit is designed for professional application work; it should not be improvised as a general-purpose sprayer or used by an untrained person. The treatment product determines the permitted application rate, dilution, site, target pest, PPE and re-entry requirements. Use only pesticide products registered in Kenya for the intended purpose and follow the current label and safety data sheet. Wear the required protective clothing, gloves and eye protection, control access to the treatment area and prevent spills or runoff. Do not apply near drains, wells or water bodies unless the label explicitly permits the location and all environmental precautions are met. After treatment, clean or flush the equipment only as directed by the chemical label and B&G instructions. Manage rinsate and residues in accordance with the product label and local waste requirements; never discharge them into storm drains or surface water. Inspect the valve for leaks, check the tip for blockage and examine pipes for damage. Store the equipment securely, away from unauthorised users, and keep a maintenance record. Replace worn seals and damaged parts before the next job. For Kenyan pest-control businesses, the Versatool 415 may support professional termite work on suitable residential, commercial and institutional properties.
