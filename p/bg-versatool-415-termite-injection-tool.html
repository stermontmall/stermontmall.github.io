The tool is part of a termite treatment equipment system, not a ready-to-use pesticide. Its valve, pipe and selected tip control delivery to the treatment point.

Correct selection and use depend on the building construction, product label, treatment plan and the exact kit configuration supplied. B&G's published information identifies the 415 as a Versatool termite tool kit with a valve featuring tip shut-off. A supplier specification for the 415 kit lists 12-inch and 40-inch 7/16-inch professional termite pipes, a 40-inch 5/8-inch professional rodding pipe, and several tips for soil rodding, void and directional delivery.

The exact parts supplied with this listing must be verified before purchase; the model number alone should not be taken as proof that every accessory in a larger kit is included. Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

This is a pest-control or agricultural chemical product. Use it only for the crop, pest, site and application method expressly authorised on the current Kenya-approved product label. Read the full label before opening or use.

Follow the label exactly for personal protective equipment, dilution, application, ventilation, re-entry period, pre-harvest interval, environmental restrictions and any other precautions. Do not guess a dose, mix it with another product, or use it on food, animals, people or surfaces unless the label specifically permits that use.
