What 4K UHD means

A 4K UHD screen has four times as many pixels as a Full HD screen, which gives sharper pictures with finer detail. On a 55 inch screen, that extra detail is easy to see, making movies, series, sport, nature programmes and games look clear and lifelike.

4K AI UPSCALER

Much of what we watch is not filmed in 4K. The 4K AI Upscaler improves lower-resolution content, such as standard broadcasts and older videos, to look sharper and cleaner on a 4K screen.

Dolby vision

Dolby Vision supports richer contrast and colour on compatible content, so bright and dark scenes look more detailed.

Game mode plus

Game Mode PLUS is designed to make gaming feel smoother and more responsive when you connect a console.

Frameless design
