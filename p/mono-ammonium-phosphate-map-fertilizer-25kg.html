More about Mono Ammonium Phosphate MAP Fertilizer 25kg

MAP for soluble nitrogen and phosphorus

Mono Ammonium Phosphate, or MAP, is a water-soluble fertiliser supplying nitrogen and a concentrated source of phosphorus. It is used in crop nutrition programmes where early root establishment or another growth stage requires readily available phosphorus. The 25kg Cropcare pack is listed for growers who use soluble fertilisers in managed feeding programmes.

MAP should be selected according to the crop's nutrient requirement and the water quality of the irrigation system. Check the exact analysis printed on the bag, since formulations can differ by supplier. Use the product label for dilution and application rates rather than estimating from another MAP product.

  • Confirm the MAP grade and water-soluble formulation before mixing.
  • Use clean water and follow the label's dissolution instructions.

Fertigation compatibility and storage

Phosphate fertilisers can react with calcium-containing products in concentrated stock solutions. Check compatibility guidance before combining MAP with calcium nitrate or other fertilisers, and use separate stock tanks where the technical instructions require them. This helps reduce the risk of precipitate forming in irrigation equipment.

The 25kg pack should be stored in a dry, secure area. Reseal opened packaging and keep it away from moisture. When comparing MAP prices in Kenya, consider the nutrient analysis, pack weight and delivery cost together.

  • Do a small compatibility check before preparing a tank mix.
  • Keep application records for each fertigation cycle.
