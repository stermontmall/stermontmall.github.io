About Mosquito Window Net Kit – Self-Adhesive Mesh Screen

Product overview

MOSQUITO WINDOW NET KIT – SELF-ADHESIVE MESH SCREEN IN KENYA

The Mosquito Window Net Kit – Self-Adhesive Mesh Screen is a cut-to-fit insect-exclusion kit designed to cover suitable window openings while allowing air to pass through the mesh. A properly fitted screen can help reduce the entry of mosquitoes and other flying insects through an open window. It is a physical barrier rather than an insecticide, so it does not release chemicals or eliminate mosquitoes already inside the room. The exact mesh dimensions and accessories should be confirmed from the pack before purchase.

WHY FIT WINDOW MESH

Open windows provide ventilation, but they can also allow mosquitoes, flies and other insects to enter. A mesh screen creates a barrier across the opening while still allowing airflow. This can be useful in bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, offices and other spaces where windows are opened regularly. A screen is most effective when it covers the entire opening and the edges are secured without gaps. Even a small opening at a corner or along the frame can allow insects to pass through.

MEASURE THE WINDOW FIRST

Measure the width and height of the clear window opening or the area that needs to be covered. Check whether the window frame is square and whether the mesh will need to fit around handles, hinges, sliders or other hardware. The kit is described as cut-to-fit, but the actual mesh size, adhesive tape length and number of pieces vary between suppliers. Compare your measurements with the pack dimensions before ordering. If one kit cannot cover the full opening, calculate the additional material required rather than stretching the mesh beyond its intended size.

PREPARE THE FRAME

For a self-adhesive installation, the fixing surface should be clean, dry and free of loose paint, grease and dust. Wipe the frame using a suitable cleaning method and allow it to dry fully before applying the adhesive tape. Follow the supplied instructions for the correct temperature and pressure needed to secure the tape. Do not attach the mesh to a surface that is damp, crumbling or unsuitable for adhesive. If the frame material is delicate, test the adhesive in an inconspicuous area first.

FIT THE MESH

Apply the adhesive fixing strip around the frame as directed by the supplier. Align the mesh carefully and press it into place so that the edges are secured continuously. Trim excess mesh with suitable scissors only after checking the final fit. Avoid leaving loose corners, folds or open gaps. The mesh should not interfere with the window's opening and closing mechanism. If the screen is removable, confirm how it is detached and reattached without damaging the adhesive or mesh.

CARE AND MAINTENANCE

Inspect the screen regularly for tears, loose edges, dust build-up and damage from pets or window movement. Clean the mesh gently using a method compatible with the material and adhesive. Do not scrub or pull the mesh in a way that stretches it away from the frame. Replace damaged sections promptly because small holes can become entry points. If the adhesive loses its grip, replace it using a compatible fixing strip and recheck the entire perimeter.

LIMITATIONS AND MOSQUITO PREVENTION

Window mesh helps reduce insect entry through the screened opening, but it does not prevent mosquitoes from entering through doors, vents or other gaps. It also does not kill insects already inside the room. Combine screens with closing doors, repairing gaps, removing standing water around the property and using other locally recommended mosquito-prevention measures. If insecticide-treated nets or other protective products are needed, choose products designed and labelled for that purpose.
