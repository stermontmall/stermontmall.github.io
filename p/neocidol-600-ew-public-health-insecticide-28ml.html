Uses & applications

Diazinon affects the normal functioning of the insect nervous system. This product is not a household aerosol, a ready-to-use spray or a crop-protection insecticide.

Safety information

It is a concentrated formulation that must only be prepared and applied according to the approved label by people who understand pesticide handling. Never use it on food, food-contact surfaces, kitchen utensils, bedding, clothing, toys, drinking-water containers or any surface where people or animals may be exposed contrary to the label. Bedbugs hide in seams, joints, bed frames, furniture gaps and other protected places. Treatment must follow the product label and should be planned carefully to avoid exposing people, pets, bedding and personal belongings. Keep people and animals away from the treatment area during application and until the label’s re-entry conditions are satisfied. Never apply directly to people, pets, livestock or food. Wear the protective clothing and equipment stated on the label, avoid breathing spray mist and prevent contact with eyes and skin. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. Never transfer the concentrate into an unlabelled bottle or reuse an empty pesticide container. Do not pour unused product or wash water into drains, rivers, ponds, wells or storm-water channels.

Care, cleaning & storage

Cockroaches often hide in cracks, crevices, wall gaps, cupboards, service voids, under sinks and around waste storage areas. Effective management begins with inspection to identify harbourages, food sources, water sources and routes of movement. Remove food residues, store food in sealed containers, repair leaks, clean grease and spills, empty rubbish regularly and seal suitable gaps where practical. Neocidol may be used only where the current product label permits treatment. For recurring infestations, a qualified pest-control professional should inspect the premises and recommend a suitable programme. Before purchase, check that the pack is sealed, genuine, correctly labelled and within its expiry date. After work, clean application equipment and protective clothing in accordance with the label and workplace procedures. Wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly after handling. Store Neocidol 600 EW in its original container, tightly closed, in a secure, cool, dry and well-ventilated pesticide store. Keep it away from children, pets, food, animal feed, medicines and unauthorised persons.
