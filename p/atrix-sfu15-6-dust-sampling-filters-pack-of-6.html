Safety information

This accessory does not identify a pest, diagnose an infestation or determine whether a substance is hazardous. If a sample may contain biological, chemical or other hazardous material, follow the relevant risk assessment, protective equipment requirements and approved handling procedure. Treat a used filter as containing the material collected during the inspection. Wear suitable gloves and any additional personal protective equipment required by the risk assessment. Avoid opening a used filter unnecessarily, shaking it or exposing the contents to other people. Dispose of it in accordance with the nature of the sample and applicable local waste procedures.
