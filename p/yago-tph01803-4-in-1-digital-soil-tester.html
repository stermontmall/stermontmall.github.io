Product overview

The YAGO TPH01803 4-in-1 Digital Soil Tester is a handheld garden and farm instrument for checking soil moisture, soil pH, soil temperature and light level. It gives growers a field indication before deciding whether to water, investigate soil acidity, adjust a growing area or compare conditions between beds. The model is identified by the supplier as TPH01803, with a 200 mm metal probe and a backlit LCD. It is a screening tool for routine crop and garden checks, not a laboratory soil analysis.

Key features & benefits

Four measurement modes are built into one portable device. The tester reports soil moisture through five display levels, pH through twelve levels, temperature in Celsius or Fahrenheit, and light intensity through nine levels. A single switch button selects the operating mode. The LCD has a backlight, and an automatic power-off function switches the unit off after five minutes without use. A low-battery indicator helps the user recognise when the power supply needs attention. The supplier lists a metal probe, plastic housing and a 9V battery power supply.

Uses & applications

For vegetable growers, the moisture mode can help compare beds that appear to dry at different rates. A reading taken in the root zone can prompt a closer inspection before watering, especially where one part of a plot receives more sun or has a different soil texture. The pH mode can help identify areas that may need a more reliable soil test. The temperature mode gives an indication of soil warmth during seedbed preparation, transplanting and seasonal comparisons. The light mode can help compare a shaded nursery corner with a brighter growing position. For nurseries, home gardens and greenhouse operators, the four modes provide a convenient first look at differences between containers, beds and growing areas. A grower can record readings on the same day, at similar depths and under comparable conditions to see whether a pattern is developing. The tool is useful for observation and comparison; it does not prescribe fertiliser, lime, irrigation volume or crop treatment.

How to use

Read the product instructions before first use and fit the specified 9V battery. Select the required mode using the switch button. For soil readings, insert the probe carefully into representative soil rather than forcing it through stones, hard roots or compacted material. Take readings at more than one point in the bed because one spot may not represent the whole field. Keep the probe clean between locations and avoid damaging or bending the metal section. For light checks, position the instrument where the crop canopy receives light and compare readings at similar times of day.

Before you start

Soil moisture and pH can vary with depth, recent irrigation, fertiliser placement, soil texture and sampling position. Avoid comparing a freshly watered point with a dry point and treating the difference as a crop-wide result. If a pH result will determine a major lime or fertiliser purchase, confirm it with a suitable laboratory or professional soil-testing service. The device's displayed levels are useful for screening, not a guarantee of laboratory accuracy. CARE AND

Storage & handling

Wipe soil from the probe after use and store the instrument dry. Do not immerse the LCD housing in water or leave the meter exposed to rain. Remove the battery if the instrument will be stored for a long period and follow the manual for battery replacement. Keep the probe protected from knocks and store the unit away from children and heavy tools.

Buying guide

If you need nutrient concentration, NPK values, electrical conductivity or a laboratory-grade pH result, this four-mode model is not described as measuring those parameters; choose a device specifically rated for that work.
