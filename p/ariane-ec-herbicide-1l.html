Product Overview & Key Benefits

Ariane EC Herbicide 1L is a selective post-emergence herbicide listed by AgriJibu for broad-leaved weed control in wheat and barley. The supplier identifies three active ingredients: fluroxypyr 60 g/L, clopyralid 23.3 g/L and MCPA 266.7 g/L. The current Pest Control Products Board crop register lists Ariane under registration PCPB(CR)0652 and confirms these active ingredients and the registered cereal use.

This listing is for the 1-litre pack.
