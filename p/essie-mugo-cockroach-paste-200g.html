About Essie Mugo Cockroach Paste

Essie Mugo Cockroach Paste 200g is a ready-to-use cockroach bait paste for targeted indoor pest management in homes, apartments, shops, restaurants, offices and other buildings. The 200g pack is a practical option for smaller properties, spot treatments and customers who want to start with a compact pack. It is supplied as a paste in a container; check the actual container label for the full ingredient declaration, manufacturer directions, batch details and any product registration information before use. Place bait only in locations permitted by the label and where cockroach activity has been observed. Focus on cracks, crevices and sheltered harbourage areas rather than open food-preparation surfaces. Keep the product away from food, utensils, animal feed, children and pets. Do not ingest. Avoid contact with eyes and skin, wash hands after handling and store the container closed in its original packaging in a secure, cool, dry location. Do not transfer the paste into an unlabelled container. For better long-term pest management, combine correctly labelled bait placement with good sanitation: seal food, clean crumbs and spills, remove rubbish regularly, repair leaks and reduce clutter around cupboards and appliances. Avoid applying other sprays directly over bait placements unless the product labels specifically permit this, because incompatible treatments may interfere with bait acceptance. Inspect treated areas as directed on the label and replenish only according to its instructions. Product performance depends on infestation pressure, placement, hygiene and following the manufacturer directions; no bait should be represented as a guaranteed or permanent solution. For extensive or recurring infestations, consider a professional inspection to identify nesting areas, entry routes and contributing conditions. This listing is for the 200g size only.
