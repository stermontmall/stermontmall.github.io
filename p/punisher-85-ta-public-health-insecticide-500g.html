Safety information

Punisher is a pesticide product and must be handled only by trained and appropriately authorized personnel in accordance with the current Kenyan-approved label. Read the label and safety data sheet before opening the pack. Use the specified protective equipment, application equipment, dilution or preparation procedure, site restrictions and re-entry interval exactly as directed by the label. Keep people, children, pets, food, animal feed and water sources away from handling and treatment areas as instructed. Never improvise a dose, mix it with another chemical without label authorization, or apply it to food, food-contact surfaces or occupied spaces contrary to label directions. Store it in its original sealed packaging in a secure, dry, ventilated pesticide store. Dispose of waste and empty packaging according to the label and applicable Kenyan requirements.
