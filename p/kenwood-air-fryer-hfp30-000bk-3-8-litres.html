Low-oil cooking

An air fryer cooks food by circulating very hot air around it, which gives a crisp outside with little or no oil. This lets you enjoy chips, chicken, samosas, fish and snacks with far less oil than deep frying.

What you can cook

  • Chips, potato wedges and other potato dishes
  • Chicken wings, drumsticks and fish
  • Samosas, spring rolls and frozen snacks
  • Vegetables, roast potatoes and sweet potatoes
  • Reheating leftovers so they stay crisp

Digital control and 7 presets

The digital LCD panel makes it simple to set the temperature and time. Seven preset programs give you a quick starting point for common foods, and you can adjust the temperature from 80 to 200 degrees and set the timer up to 60 minutes to suit your recipe.

Size and capacity

The 3.8 litre basket holds about 1.2 kg of food, which is enough for a couple or a small family. Do not overfill the basket, so hot air can move freely around the food and cook it evenly. For bigger meals, cook in batches.

Easy clean-up and safety

The removable bowl has a non-stick coating that is designed to be easy to clean. Overheat protection adds safety during use.

Tips

  • Preheat if the manual recommends it
  • Shake or turn the food halfway through cooking
  • Use a little oil for extra crispness
  • Place the fryer on a flat, heat-resistant surface with space around it
  • Let it cool before cleaning
