Plan portions and use the basket for even cooking

The 3.8-litre basket is listed to hold about 1.2 kg of food. For the most even result, arrange food in a loose layer where possible instead of packing the basket tightly. Hot air needs room to circulate around the pieces. If you are preparing chips, wedges or chicken for several people, cooking in two batches can be more reliable than filling the basket beyond its practical capacity. Cut ingredients into reasonably similar sizes so they finish at a similar time, and turn or shake them when the recipe or manual recommends it.

The seven preset programmes provide starting points for common foods, while the adjustable temperature range of 80–200°C and timer up to 60 minutes allow you to follow a recipe more closely. Presets are not a guarantee that every food or portion will cook identically. Check that food is thoroughly cooked before serving, and use the manual for recommended settings and safe handling.

Choose a safe countertop position and care for the non-stick bowl

Before ordering, measure the counter space where you plan to use the fryer and leave the ventilation clearance specified by Kenwood. The appliance is rated at 1500W, so check the rating label and your kitchen's electrical supply before use. Place it on a stable, level, heat-resistant surface, keep the air outlet clear and avoid positioning it directly beside curtains or other heat-sensitive items. Do not move the fryer while it is hot or operating.

After cooking, switch it off and allow the basket and removable bowl to cool before cleaning. Use a soft sponge or cloth and follow the manual's cleaning instructions to protect the non-stick coating; abrasive pads and metal utensils can damage the surface.
