About Protecta Micro Tamper-Resistant Rodent Bait Station

Protecta Micro tamper-resistant rodent Bait Station in Kenya

Protecta Micro is a compact tamper-resistant bait station designed primarily for controlled mouse baiting in tight locations where a larger station may not fit. Its small footprint makes it relevant to pest-management technicians working around narrow wall edges, corners, equipment spaces and other restricted points. The station is a reusable housing for compatible bait; it is not a rodenticide and does not control mice unless an appropriate bait or approved insert is selected and used according to its own label.

Compact design for restricted locations

The manufacturer describes Protecta Micro as a small, economical mouse bait station made from rigid heavy-duty polymer plastic. The compact body is intended to fit into locations where larger bait stations may be difficult to position. Entry openings at the ends provide a route for mice into the station. The manufacturer's listed dimensions are approximately 9.53cm long, 7.94cm wide and 3.18cm high. Confirm that these measurements suit the intended placement area and leave enough room to open and service the station.

Locking and controlled access

Protecta Micro is designed to lock automatically when closed and to be opened using the compatible two-prong key. A locking enclosure helps restrict casual access to bait when the station is correctly closed and positioned. It does not make rodenticide harmless. Keep the station secured away from children, pets, livestock and non-target wildlife, and never leave bait loose beside the housing. Confirm whether the correct key is included with this single-piece listing before ordering; do not assume that keys or additional accessories are supplied unless stated.

Bait compatibility and responsible use

Use only bait blocks or monitoring products that the manufacturer confirms are compatible with Protecta Micro. The manufacturer describes the station as suitable for its BLOX bait format, but customers should verify the exact bait dimensions and current instructions before use. Follow the bait label for approved target pests, placement, inspection, protective equipment and disposal. Never cut or modify bait unless the label permits it. If a non-toxic monitoring block is used, follow its instructions and replace it only with a product intended for that purpose.

Where to consider placing it

A professional site assessment should identify mouse activity before stations are installed. Possible monitoring points include wall edges, corners, service spaces and areas where droppings, gnaw marks or other signs have been found. Place stations where they will not be kicked, washed away, covered by stored goods or obstructed by cleaning. Do not position rodenticide bait in food-preparation areas or where bait could contact food, utensils or packaging. The exact location must comply with the bait label and the site's hygiene requirements.

Inspection and record keeping

Inspect each station at an interval suitable for the site risk and the product label. Check that the body remains intact, the lid is properly locked and the station has not been moved or tampered with. Record the location, station reference, date and any bait or monitoring observations. Replace damaged housings and address signs of spillage or non-target access immediately using the relevant safety instructions. A bait station should never be installed and forgotten.

Integrated mouse management

Bait stations are only one element of a mouse-management programme. Remove accessible food, store dry goods and animal feed in sealed containers, keep waste covered and clean crumbs and spills promptly. Inspect gaps around pipes, doors and service penetrations, then seal suitable openings with appropriate materials. Reduce clutter that offers nesting material or hiding places. Continue monitoring after control work because new mice may enter if access and food sources remain available.
