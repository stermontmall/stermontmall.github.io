Why Coccidiosis Prevention Matters Coccidiosis is one of the most common and costly diseases in poultry. It is caused by microscopic parasites that damage the lining of the gut, leading to bloody or watery droppings, weakness, poor growth, poor feed conversion, reduced egg production and deaths, especially in young birds raised on litter. Prevention through feed, along with good management, is far cheaper than treating an outbreak.
