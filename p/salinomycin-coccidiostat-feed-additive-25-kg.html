Why Coccidiosis Prevention Matters

Coccidiosis is one of the most common and costly diseases in poultry. It is caused by microscopic parasites that damage the lining of the gut, leading to bloody or watery droppings, weakness, poor growth, poor feed conversion, reduced egg production and deaths, especially in young birds raised on litter. Prevention through feed, along with good management, is far cheaper than treating an outbreak.

Using It Properly

Add the product to feed exactly as the label and your veterinarian or nutritionist advise, and mix it thoroughly so every bird gets an even amount. Uneven mixing can leave some birds under-protected and expose others to too much. Observe the withdrawal period before slaughter, and do not combine it with other products unless your vet advises it. Keep the mixing equipment clean to prevent carry-over into feeds for other animals.

Good Management Tips

Keep litter dry, avoid overcrowding, clean feeders and drinkers regularly and keep young birds separate from older ones. Clean and rest the house between batches and talk to a veterinarian about a vaccination or feed programme that suits your farm.
