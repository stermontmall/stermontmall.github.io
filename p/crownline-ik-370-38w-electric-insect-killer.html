Product overview

The Crownline IK-370 is a 38W electric insect killer designed for indoor spaces where flying insects need to be monitored and controlled with a powered UV attraction unit. The model uses two T8 18W tubes and a high-voltage grid. Its stated coverage is up to 150 square metres, making it a larger-format option for premises that need a wall- or ceiling-hung insect control appliance rather than a small bedside lamp. The IK-370 is listed with an aluminium-alloy and ABS housing, an easy-hanging arrangement and a removable collection tray.

Key features & benefits

The unit is identified by the Crownline IK-370 model number. Its 38W rating is paired with two 18W T8 tubes. The product listing specifies a 2000–2500V grid and a stated coverage area of 150m². The housing combines aluminium alloy and ABS, while the outer grid is described by the manufacturer as having a finger-safety test design. A removable lower tray collects insect remains and can be taken out for cleaning. The hanging design is intended to make installation straightforward where a suitable mounting point is available.

Uses & applications

The IK-370 may suit restaurants, food-preparation support areas, shops, warehouses, offices, reception areas and other indoor premises where flying insects are a nuisance. Its larger stated coverage makes it more appropriate for a defined room or work area than for personal, handheld use. It can be considered by pest-control operators, facilities teams and business owners who want a fixed electrical insect-control appliance as one part of a broader hygiene and pest-management programme. HOW IT WORKS The UV tubes provide light intended to attract flying insects towards the appliance. Insects that reach the internal electrified grid are killed and fall into the collection tray. The unit is an electrical trapping appliance, not a pesticide spray, residual treatment or substitute for removing breeding sources. Its effectiveness in a particular room depends on placement, competing light sources, airflow, insect behaviour and routine cleaning. The stated 150m² coverage is a manufacturer specification, not a guarantee that every insect in that area will be captured. INSTALLATION AND POSITIONING Choose a dry, suitable indoor position with access to the correct electrical supply. The product information describes an easy-hanging design, but the mounting surface and fixing method must be appropriate for the unit's weight and dimensions. Position the appliance where it can be reached safely for inspection and tray cleaning, while keeping it away from food, water splash, excessive dust and physical impact. Avoid placing it directly beside brighter competing lights if those lights draw insects away from the UV tubes. Follow the supplied manual and local electrical requirements; do not improvise wiring or mounting hardware. CLEANING AND ROUTINE CARE Switch off and disconnect the appliance before cleaning or servicing it. Allow the unit to become safe to handle, then remove the collection tray according to the manufacturer's instructions and dispose of the captured insects hygienically. Clean the tray and accessible surfaces without wetting electrical components. Inspect the tubes and grid periodically for dust or damage. Replace tubes only with compatible parts specified for the IK-370. Do not touch the electrified grid while the appliance is connected to power, and do not operate a unit with damaged wiring, casing or protective components.
