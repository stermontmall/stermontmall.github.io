About Amito 5.7WDG Insecticide – 1 kg

Amito 5.7WDG Insecticide (1 kg) Amito 5.7WDG is an emamectin benzoate insecticide in water-dispersible granule (WDG) form, supplied in a 1 kg pack. It is used to control fall armyworm, caterpillars and other chewing pests on vegetables and field crops. KEY FEATURES • Emamectin benzoate, an active ingredient widely used against caterpillars • Water-dispersible granules that mix into the spray tank with little dust • For fall armyworm, caterpillars and other chewing pests • Suitable for vegetables and field crops • 1 kg pack for farms and growers • Delivered to all 47 counties WHY CATERPILLAR CONTROL MATTERS Fall armyworm and other caterpillars feed on leaves, whorls, shoots and fruits. In maize, young larvae hide deep in the whorl and can strip a field quickly if not controlled. In vegetables, caterpillar feeding lowers quality and market value. Acting early, when larvae are small, gives the best protection for your yield. HOW TO USE IT Water-dispersible granules are weighed or measured and stirred into water in the spray tank, then applied as a spray. Follow the dose, water volume and number of applications on the label. Scout fields regularly, look for fresh feeding damage and frass, and spray when pests are first seen. Direct the spray into the whorl on maize and cover both sides of the leaves on vegetables. RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT Repeated use of the same insecticide group can lead to pests becoming harder to control. Rotate with products from other chemical groups and keep to the number of applications allowed on the label. SAFETY Read the label before use and keep out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when handling granules and spraying. Do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying, and wash your hands and face afterwards. Follow the pre-harvest interval on the label. Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and fish. STORAGE Keep in the original, tightly closed pack in a cool, dry, locked place away from food, feed, children and pets. Never reuse empty containers.
