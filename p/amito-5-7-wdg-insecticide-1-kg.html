About Reliable Amito 5.7WDG Insecticide

Amito 5.7WDG Insecticide (100 g, 250 g, 1 kg)

Amito 5.7WDG is an emamectin benzoate insecticide in water-dispersible granule form for fall armyworm, caterpillars and other chewing pests on vegetables and field crops. Act early, while larvae are small, to protect your yield.

Key specifications & features

  • Active ingredient: Emamectin benzoate
  • Target: Fall armyworm, caterpillars and other chewing pests
  • Formulation: Water-dispersible granules (WDG)
  • Application settings: Agricultural: vegetable farms and field crops such as maize

Mixing & application instructions

1. Dilution rate: use the dose and water volume printed on the manufacturer's label for your crop and growth stage.

2. Application method: Weigh or measure the granules and stir them into water in the spray tank, then apply as a spray. On maize direct the spray into the whorl; on vegetables cover both sides of the leaves.

3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Available in 100 g, 250 g and 1 kg.

4. Scout fields regularly, look for fresh feeding damage and frass, and spray when pests are first seen.

5. Rotate with products from other chemical groups and keep to the number of applications allowed on the label.

Safety & re-entry precautions

  • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.
