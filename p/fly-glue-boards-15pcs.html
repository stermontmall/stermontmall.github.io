About Universal UV Fly Trap Glue Boards – 15 Pack

Universal UV Fly Trap Glue Boards are replacement adhesive boards for compatible insect light traps. This listing identifies a 15-piece pack and a stated board size of approximately 400mm x 215mm. The gridded adhesive surface captures flying insects attracted by the light trap and can provide a visible record of catches for routine pest monitoring. Replacement boards are consumables; the UV insect light unit itself is not included unless the product listing specifically says otherwise.

Why replace Glue Boards

An insect light trap relies on a clean, effective capture surface. As insects accumulate, dust settles or the adhesive ages, the board may become less effective and harder to inspect. A fresh board restores the intended capture surface and gives the operator a clear area for recording new catches. The printed grid can assist with visual counting and trend monitoring, depending on the board design. Replacing boards at the correct interval is part of routine maintenance for an insect light trap.

Check size and model compatibility

The word universal does not mean that every glue board fits every insect light trap. Before ordering, compare the board dimensions, thickness, shape and retention method with the board currently installed in your unit. This listing states approximately 400mm x 215mm; the exact overall dimensions and usable adhesive area should be confirmed from the pack. Some boards can be trimmed or split for particular models, but do not cut or modify this product unless the supplier confirms that it is designed for that purpose. A poor fit may leave gaps or prevent the unit from closing correctly.

Where Glue-board light traps are used

UV glue-board insect light traps are used in suitable indoor environments where flying-insect monitoring and capture are needed. Examples may include warehouses, food-service support areas, retail premises, production rooms and other commercial spaces. The trap and board should be positioned according to the equipment manufacturer's instructions and the site's hygiene plan. In food-handling environments, avoid placing an insect light trap where it attracts insects across exposed food, utensils or preparation surfaces. Follow applicable food-safety requirements.

How to replace a board

Switch off and isolate the insect light trap before servicing it. Follow the equipment manual to open the front cover or access panel and remove the used board without spreading insects or debris. Handle the adhesive surface carefully and avoid touching the glue. Fit the replacement board in the correct orientation and make sure it is held securely in the designed position. Close the unit and restore power only after servicing is complete. If the unit has electrical damage or a broken cover, do not continue using it until it has been inspected.

Inspection and catch records

Record the date each board is installed and the date it is replaced. Where the grid is intended for counting, use a consistent method to record catches and compare trends between rooms or inspection periods. A sudden rise in catches may indicate a change in insect pressure, an open entry route or a nearby breeding source. Inspect doors, windows, drains, waste areas and stored materials as appropriate. The board captures insects but does not identify the source automatically.

Storage and disposal

Keep unused boards in their protective packaging until required. Store them flat in a clean, dry place away from heat, dust and direct sunlight. Keep adhesive surfaces away from children, pets, clothing and other objects. Dispose of used boards according to the equipment and board supplier's instructions and the site's waste procedures. Wear suitable gloves when handling used boards and wash hands after service.
