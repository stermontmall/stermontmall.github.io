Formulation and intended role

The supplier identifies sodium 1-naphthyl acetic acid (NAA) at 45g/L as the active ingredient. The listing describes use in flowering and fruit-set management. It is a growth regulator, not a general-purpose fertilizer, and should not be substituted for crop nutrition products.

For crop inputs, formulation and concentration matter more than the brand name alone. Compare the active ingredient or nutrient analysis, formulation type, crop, application method and pack size on the label before use. Do not transfer directions between different products or concentrations.

Planning crop care in Kenya

Kenyan farms differ in soil, rainfall, irrigation and crop stage. Match the formulation to the crop and identified need, using a soil or plant test where available. A foliar fertilizer normally supplements a wider nutrition plan, not an automatic replacement for basal fertilizer.

Adjuvants and growth regulators have a different role: use them only for the purpose and crop stages stated on the label.

Application and compatibility

The supplier lists 1ml in 4.5 litres of water, equivalent to about 4ml in 20 litres or 20ml in 100 litres. Confirm the crop, growth stage, timing and any repeat-application limits on the delivered label. Confirm the instruction applies to this formulation and crop on the delivered label.

Measure accurately with clean, calibrated equipment; never increase the rate to force a faster response. Do not tank-mix with pesticides, fertilizers, oils or adjuvants unless the label or a qualified agronomist confirms compatibility. Follow any compatibility test, protective-equipment, re-entry or harvest-interval directions.

Storage and safe handling

Keep the original labelled pack tightly closed in a cool, dry, secure place as directed. Protect it from heat, sunlight, moisture and contamination. Store away from food, feed, drinking water, medicines, children and animals.

Wear the label-specified protective equipment and wash after handling. Never reuse packaging for food or water. Dispose of empty packs as directed; do not use damaged or unlabelled packs.

Keep a simple crop-treatment record with the product name, pack size, crop, application date, treated area and method. Note the water volume and follow-up observations so future decisions can be compared with the label and the wider crop programme.
