Product Overview & Key Benefits

The Milano Italia 16HP Diesel High-Pressure Water Pump is a 3-inch engine-driven pump listed for high-elevation water transfer and large-scale irrigation. The supplier specifies a 16HP industrial diesel engine, 3-inch (80mm) inlet and outlet, maximum head of 100 metres and maximum flow range of 45–55 cubic metres per hour. It is described as a direct-injection diesel pump with electric key start and manual recoil backup.

The supplier also lists a self-priming high-pressure centrifugal design and a reinforced anti-vibration steel cradle. These specifications make it a high-capacity option for farms and industrial sites that need a substantial water flow over a long or elevated route. The 16HP diesel engine is the main power source, while the 3-inch ports support high-volume transfer.

The 100m maximum head indicates the maximum lifting capability under specified conditions. The 45–55m³/h flow range is the listed maximum range; actual output depends on the operating head and the resistance created by the pipe route. The self-priming centrifugal design is described by the supplier as helping with priming, but the correct initial priming procedure must still be followed.

Electric key start reduces the effort required to start the engine, with recoil backup available as an alternative. The reinforced steel cradle is intended to support the equipment and reduce vibration during operation.

Where & How to Use in Kenya

This pump may suit long-distance irrigation, transferring water to elevated reservoirs, large farms and industrial water delivery where a high head and substantial flow are needed. It can be considered for upland fields where the water source is lower than the irrigation area, provided the flow at the actual delivery head meets the crop-water plan. It may also support construction-site water transfer and filling large tanks.

HEAD AND FLOW PLANNING Calculate total dynamic head by combining the vertical rise with losses from pipe length, bends, valves and fittings. The listed 100m head is a maximum rating, not a guarantee of 45–55m³/h at that height.

Request a performance curve or written flow estimate for the expected operating point. Use compatible 3-inch pipework to avoid excessive friction losses. Confirm the suction lift with the supplier and keep the pump near the water source.

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Set the pump on stable, level ground and secure the steel cradle. Support the suction and discharge hoses so their weight does not strain the pump ports. Direct the discharge to a safe location and prevent erosion, flooding or backflow.

Do not run the pump dry if the manufacturer requires priming or water in the casing. Keep hands and loose clothing clear of rotating engine components. Use proper lifting equipment when transporting the unit and never lift it by its hoses.
