Product overview

Tehno Mss G009 8-Litre Gravity Poultry Drinker in Kenya The Tehno Mss G009 is an eight-litre gravity-fed poultry drinker designed to provide a steady supply of drinking water to chickens and other domestic poultry. The reservoir holds water above a drinking rim, and gravity allows water to flow down as birds drink.

Product details

The system does not require electricity, a pump or a direct water-line connection. For poultry keepers who want a simple manual drinker with a visible water reserve, the G009 offers a practical way to reduce repeated refilling while keeping the drinking point accessible. Gravity-fed water delivery The G009 uses gravity to replenish the drinking rim as the water level falls.

Because it has no electric pump or powered valve, it can be used in poultry houses without an electrical connection. Translucent tank and water-level checks The white reservoir is translucent, allowing the keeper to see the remaining water without opening the drinker. This is useful during routine rounds because a quick visual check can show whether a refill is needed.

Care, cleaning & storage

Actual water use varies with flock size, age, breed, feed, egg production, temperature and humidity. During hot weather, birds may drink substantially more, so inspect the level frequently and refill before the reservoir is empty. After assembly, check that the tank is seated evenly and that water flows into the rim at a steady level.

If the drinker does not refill correctly, inspect the clips and contact surfaces for dirt or misalignment. The red base makes the drinking area easy to identify in the poultry house and may help birds locate the water point. Keep the reservoir clean so the water remains visible and the plastic does not develop a film.

Empty and rinse the saucer daily, and clean the reservoir regularly using a suitable poultry-equipment cleaning method. Rinse thoroughly before refilling so that no cleaning residue remains. In warm weather, inspect more frequently for algae or slime.

Keep drinkers away from perches and leaking feed or water equipment. Clean water supports flock health, but this drinker does not treat contaminated water or replace a farm's wider biosecurity and sanitation routine. Who may find this drinker useful The Tehno Mss G009 may suit smallholder poultry farmers, backyard chicken keepers and medium-sized poultry units that need a manual water dispenser with a larger reserve.

Safety information

The supplier describes a three-clip assembly: the tank is filled and then secured to the saucer with clips. Follow the manufacturer's filling sequence carefully to avoid spills. UV resistance does not make the unit immune to cracking, impact or wear.

Avoid leaving the drinker in unnecessary direct sun for long periods, and inspect the tank, saucer and clips for damage.
