Why Bait stations are best

A bait station is a small covered box or tube that holds the bait. It keeps the bait dry and clean, makes it harder for children, pets and livestock to reach it, and gives rodents a dark, sheltered place where they feel safe to feed. Stations also make it easy to check how much bait has been eaten, so you know where the rodents are active.

How to use it

Place bait stations along walls, near holes and burrows, behind stores and stacks, and on rodent runs where you see droppings and gnaw marks. Put several stations across a larger area, spaced as the label advises. Check them regularly, top up the bait that has been eaten and replace bait that is wet or mouldy. Continue until no more bait is taken, then remove stations and leftover bait as the label directs.

For stores and farms

Larger premises usually have more than one nesting area. Walk around the building, inspect the outside walls, drains, roof edges and nearby vegetation, and place stations at each point where there is activity. Keep grain and feed in closed containers, stack bags away from walls, and clear rubbish.

Safety

Check the PCPB registration number on the label, read the label before use, and keep away from children, pets and livestock. Do not place bait where food, feed or water can be contaminated. Wear gloves when handling bait or stations, and wash your hands afterwards. Collect dead rodents regularly and dispose of them safely. If anyone swallows the bait, get medical help at once and show the label. For animals, contact a vet.

Storage

Keep the pack tightly closed in a cool, dry, locked place away from children, pets and food.
