Where & How to Use in Kenya

A bait station is a small covered box or tube that holds the bait. It keeps the bait dry and clean, makes it harder for children, pets and livestock to reach it, and gives rodents a dark, sheltered place where they feel safe to feed. Stations also show you how much has been eaten, so you learn where the rodents are most active.

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Check the PCPB registration number on the label and read the label before use. Keep children, pets and livestock away from the bait and the stations, and never put bait where food, feed or drinking water could be contaminated. Wear gloves, wash your hands afterwards and dispose of dead rodents safely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why use a bait station instead of leaving bait on the floor?
A station keeps the bait dry and clean, makes it harder for children, pets and livestock to reach it, and gives rodents a sheltered place to feed.

Which Bromarat pack suits my building?
The 25 g pack suits a home or a small shop with a few stations. The 100 g pack suits stores, farms and larger premises with many.

How long should I keep baiting?
Keep checking and topping up until no more bait is taken, then remove the stations and leftover bait as the label directs.

How to use

Place stations along walls, near holes and burrows, behind cupboards and stacked goods, and on runs where you see droppings and gnaw marks. A home or small shop usually needs a few; a store or farm needs more, spaced as the label advises. Check them regularly, top up bait that has been eaten and replace bait that is wet or mouldy.

Carry on until no more bait is taken, then remove the stations and leftover bait as the label directs.

Before you start

  • Walk around the building and look for droppings, gnaw marks and runs
  • Store food and grain in closed containers and stack bags away from the walls
  • Block holes around pipes and doors
  • Clear rubbish and long grass near the building
