Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

This is a pest-control or agricultural chemical product. Use it only for the crop, pest, site and application method expressly authorised on the current Kenya-approved product label. Read the full label before opening or use.

Follow the label exactly for personal protective equipment, dilution, application, ventilation, re-entry period, pre-harvest interval, environmental restrictions and any other precautions. Product overview

KAPI Flower Dust is a natural pyrethrum-based agricultural insecticide supplied in a one-kilogram powder pack. The Kenya Pest Control Products Board biopesticide register lists FLOWER DUST under registration number PCPB(CR)2370, with pyrethrins 0.25% w/w as the active ingredient. Its registered uses include control of the pupal and adult stages of thrips and false codling moth in roses, and thrips in chives.

The register gives a 12-hour re-entry interval and WHO Class U classification, meaning it is unlikely to present acute hazard under normal use. Always check the current Kenyan label for the approved crop, target pest and directions before use. Flower Dust is a dry, natural pyrethrum insecticide designed for agricultural use around the soil and crop surface.

Pyrethrins are naturally derived insecticidal compounds that affect insect nerve signalling. The PCPB registration identifies thrips and false codling moth in roses and thrips in chives. Flower Dust is particularly relevant to pest stages that occur in the soil or on the soil surface.
