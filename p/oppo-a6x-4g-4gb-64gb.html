A major practical feature is the 6,100mAh typical-capacity battery specified by OPPO for this model. The CPH2819 supports dual Nano-SIM operation, 4G LTE, Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5.0 and USB Type-C. It also includes a 3.5mm headphone jack and a side-mounted fingerprint sensor. NFC is listed as unsupported on OPPO Kenya's specification page. Mobile network performance depends on the exact frequency bands supported by the device and the coverage of the Kenyan network operator at the place of use. For Kenyan users, the OPPO A6x can serve as a practical primary or secondary phone for communication, online learning, mobile banking, customer follow-up, navigation and routine business administration. Small traders can use it to respond to WhatsApp enquiries, photograph products, check stock lists and access web-based services.
