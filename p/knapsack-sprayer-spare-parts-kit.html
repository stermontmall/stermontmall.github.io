Why Sprayer maintenance matters

A knapsack sprayer relies on a sealed tank, pump, hose, lance, trigger and nozzle assembly to deliver liquid safely and consistently. A worn seal or damaged connector can cause leaks, loss of pressure or uneven application. Using a mismatched replacement part can create the same problems or damage the equipment. Regular inspection and timely replacement of compatible components help maintain reliable operation and reduce the risk of accidental exposure to spray liquid.

Check compatibility before ordering

Identify the sprayer brand and model, then compare the connector thread, hose diameter, lance fitting, nozzle type and seal dimensions. Some sprayers use proprietary fittings that do not fit generic repair kits. If you are replacing a part from an Amiran, Solo, Jacto or other branded sprayer, confirm compatibility with the exact model rather than relying only on the tank capacity. A kit advertised for a 16-litre sprayer may not fit every 16-litre model.

Safe Repair procedure

Before servicing a sprayer, stop spraying, release pressure and empty the tank according to the equipment manual. Clean the tank and components using the method recommended for the chemical previously used. Do not service a pressurised sprayer. Wear suitable protective equipment if residues may remain inside the tank or hose. Replace only the component that is damaged and assemble it in the correct orientation. Tighten fittings according to the manufacturer's instructions; excessive force can damage plastic threads or seals.

Test with clean water

After replacing parts, test the sprayer with clean water before adding any chemical. Check the tank, hose, trigger, lance and nozzle for leaks. Confirm that the pump builds pressure and that the spray pattern is consistent. If a fitting leaks or pressure drops, stop and check the connection and seal again. Do not use the sprayer for chemical application until the leak has been corrected and the equipment is operating safely.

Cleaning and preventive maintenance

Clean the sprayer after each use according to the chemical label and equipment instructions. Do not discharge leftover pesticide or rinsate into drains, streams or water sources. Clean filters and nozzles with suitable tools and never use the mouth to clear a blockage. Inspect hoses for cracks, seals for wear and connectors for damage. Store the sprayer empty, clean and dry, and keep spare parts in a labelled container away from heat and contamination.
