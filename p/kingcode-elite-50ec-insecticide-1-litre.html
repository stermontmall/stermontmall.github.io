Greenlife Kingcode Elite 50EC Insecticide (1 Litre) Greenlife Kingcode Elite 50EC combines lambda-cyhalothrin 15 g/L and acetamiprid 35 g/L in a broad-spectrum insecticide for thrips, aphids and whiteflies on roses, French beans, kales, tomatoes and other crops. It is an emulsifiable concentrate (EC) in a 1 litre pack, and the label dose and pre-harvest interval should be followed. KEY FEATURES • Lambda-cyhalothrin 15 g/L + acetamiprid 35 g/L • Emulsifiable concentrate (EC) • Broad-spectrum insecticide • Controls thrips, aphids and whiteflies • For roses, French beans, kales, tomatoes and other crops • 1 litre pack • Greenlife brand PESTS IT CONTROLS • Thrips: tiny insects that scar leaves, flowers and pods • Aphids: soft-bodied sap suckers that curl leaves, produce honeydew and can spread viruses • Whiteflies: small white insects on the underside of leaves that weaken plants and spread viruses These pests multiply quickly in warm weather and can damage crops of roses, French beans, kales and tomatoes if they are not controlled early. TWO ACTIVE INGREDIENTS Lambda-cyhalothrin is a pyrethroid that works on contact and by ingestion and gives quick knockdown. Acetamiprid is a neonicotinoid that is taken up by the plant and works on sucking pests. Together they give broader coverage and support resistance management compared with a single active ingredient. HOW TO USE IT Follow the label dose, water volume, spray interval and maximum number of applications. Scout crops regularly and start spraying when pests first appear. Half-fill the tank with water, add the measured product, top up and agitate. Spray evenly and cover the underside of leaves, where whiteflies and aphids hide. Spray in calm weather, early morning or late afternoon. PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL Always follow the pre-harvest interval on the label before picking, which is especially important for kales, French beans and tomatoes that are harvested often. RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT Rotate with insecticides from other chemical groups and keep to the number of sprays allowed on the label. SAFETY Read the label before use and keep out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Both active ingredients can harm bees and aquatic life, so avoid spraying open flowers where bees are active and keep spray and rinse water out of streams and ponds. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
