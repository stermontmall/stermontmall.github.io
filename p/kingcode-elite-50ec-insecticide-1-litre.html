About Kingcode Elite 50EC Insecticide – 1 Litre

Kingcode Elite 50EC Insecticide (1 Litre)

Greenlife Kingcode Elite 50EC combines lambda-cyhalothrin and acetamiprid in a broad-spectrum insecticide for thrips, aphids and whiteflies. It protects roses, French beans, kales, tomatoes and other crops from pests that multiply quickly in warm weather.

Key specifications & features

  • Active ingredient: Lambda-cyhalothrin 15 g/L + Acetamiprid 35 g/L
  • Target: Thrips, aphids and whiteflies on roses, French beans, kales, tomatoes and other crops
  • Formulation: Emulsifiable concentrate (EC)
  • Application settings: Agricultural: rose, French bean, kale and tomato growers

Mixing & application instructions

1. Dilution rate: use the dose and water volume printed on the manufacturer's label for your crop and growth stage.

2. Application method: Half-fill the tank with water, add the measured product, top up and agitate. Spray evenly in calm weather, early morning or late afternoon, covering the underside of leaves where whiteflies and aphids hide.

3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Available in 1 Litre.

4. Scout crops regularly and start spraying when pests first appear.

5. Both active ingredients can harm bees and aquatic life: avoid spraying open flowers where bees are active and keep spray and rinse water out of streams and ponds.

Safety & re-entry precautions

  • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.

  • Read the whole label before you buy or mix. The label is the legal instruction for crops, pests, rates and safety.
  • Look for the PCPB registration number on the pack. Do not buy chemicals that carry none.
  • Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI) printed on the label, the number of days that must pass between the last spray and harvest. Use the product only on the crops the label lists.
