Why Choose Tecto 500SC

Botrytis, or grey mould, is one of the most damaging diseases in roses, especially in cool, humid greenhouses. It attacks buds, petals and stems and can cause heavy losses before and after harvest. A systemic fungicide moves into the plant tissue, giving protection from inside, which is useful for preventing disease before it spreads. Syngenta is a global leader in crop protection, and Tecto is a trusted name among flower growers.

Botrytis Management Tips

Keep humidity under control by ventilating greenhouses, avoid wetting flowers and leaves late in the day, and give plants enough space for air to move. Remove dead petals, infected buds and fallen debris, and keep tools and benches clean. Apply fungicide early, before disease builds up, and rotate with fungicides from other groups to protect their long-term effectiveness.

Safe use of Original Tecto 500SC Fungicide – 1 Litre

  • Read the whole label before you buy or mix. The label is the legal instruction for crops, pests, rates and safety.
  • Look for the PCPB registration number on the pack. Do not buy chemicals that carry none.
  • Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI) printed on the label, the number of days that must pass between the last spray and harvest. Use the product only on the crops the label lists.
  • Wear gloves, a mask or respirator, eye protection and long protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards.
  • Keep chemicals in their original container, out of reach of children, pets and livestock. Never reuse empty containers for food or water.
