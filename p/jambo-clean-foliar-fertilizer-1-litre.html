About Jambo Clean Foliar Fertilizer

Greenlife Jambo Clean Foliar Fertilizer (1 Litre) Greenlife Jambo Clean is a foliar fertilizer that cleans sooty mould and honeydew off crops while nourishing them. It contains water-soluble phosphorus pentoxide (P2O5) 8.2% and total nitrogen 10.0% (urea 8.5% plus ammoniacal 1.5%). It is supplied in a 1 litre pack. KEY FEATURES • Foliar fertilizer that cleans sooty mould and honeydew • Water-soluble phosphorus pentoxide (P2O5) 8.2% • Total nitrogen 10.0% (urea 8.5% + ammoniacal 1.5%) • Feeds the crop while it cleans the leaves • 1 litre pack • Greenlife brand • Delivered to all 47 counties WHAT ARE HONEYDEW AND SOOTY MOULD? Sucking insects such as aphids, whiteflies and scale insects feed on plant sap and pass out a sticky, sugary liquid called honeydew. It covers leaves, stems and fruits. A black fungus called sooty mould then grows on the honeydew and forms a dark coating. The coating blocks light, so the plant cannot photosynthesise well, and it makes the crop look dirty, which can lower quality and market value, especially for flowers, vegetables and fruit. HOW JAMBO CLEAN HELPS Jambo Clean is sprayed on the crop to help clean off sooty mould and honeydew. At the same time, the nutrients in the product nourish the plant. This makes it a practical choice for growers who need to clean up their crops and give them a boost. WHAT THE NUTRIENTS DO • Nitrogen supports leaf growth and green colour. Here it comes partly as urea and partly as ammoniacal nitrogen • Phosphorus supports root development, flowering and energy transfer in the plant HOW TO USE IT Follow the dose, water volume, timing and interval given on the label. Mix in clean water, agitate, and spray evenly so that leaves are well covered. Spray in the cool of the morning or late afternoon and avoid the heat of the day to reduce the risk of leaf scorch. Test on a few plants first if you are using it on a crop for the first time. IMPORTANT: TREAT THE CAUSE TOO Sooty mould returns if the sucking insects are still there. Scout for aphids, whiteflies and scale and control them with a suitable insecticide, following its label. Good crop hygiene, ant control and removing badly affected leaves also help. SAFETY Read the label before use. Wear gloves and avoid contact with eyes and skin. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Do not let spray or rinse water reach streams or ponds. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry place away from food and feed.
