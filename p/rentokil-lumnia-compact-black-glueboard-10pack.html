This product is intended for customers who already have a compatible Lumnia Compact unit and need replacement boards for routine inspection and servicing. HOW THE REPLACEMENT BOARD IS USED

The Lumnia Compact is an LED insect light trap. Its lamp attracts flying insects towards the unit, and insects that reach the adhesive board are retained on its sticky surface. The board provides a visible capture surface that can also help staff inspect flying-insect activity as part of a wider pest-monitoring programme. It must be fitted inside the compatible trap in the position and orientation specified in the equipment instructions. It should not be placed loose around a room, used as a food-contact surface or treated as a substitute for the complete insect light trap.

WHEN TO INSPECT AND REPLACE

Include the glueboard in the site's regular pest-monitoring and equipment-maintenance checks. Inspect it for captured insects, dust, debris, loss of tack or physical damage. Replace it when the adhesive surface is substantially covered, contaminated, damaged or no longer suitable for effective capture and monitoring. The actual replacement frequency depends on flying-insect pressure, the location of the unit, dust and airborne debris, seasonal conditions and the site's documented monitoring schedule. There is no single replacement interval that is appropriate for every premises. Follow the Lumnia Compact instructions and your organisation's pest-management procedures.

HOW TO REPLACE A LUMNIA COMPACT GLUEBOARD (STEP BY STEP)

Time needed: about 5 minutes. You will need: a replacement glueboard, disposable gloves and a bag for the used board.

Step 1: Switch off and unplug. Turn the unit off and unplug it at the wall. Leave the lamps to cool for a minute before opening the unit.

Step 2: Open the front cover. The cover is held by a catch or latch at the bottom or side. Press or slide it to release, then lift or swing the cover open.

Step 3: Remove the old glueboard. Slide it out of the guide rails or unclip it from the holders. Handle it by the edges only. Fold or wrap it with the sticky side inward before placing it in a bag and binning it.

Step 4: Fit the new glueboard. Peel off the protective film or backing. Make sure the glue side faces the lamps (the open, light-facing side), then slide the board fully into the rails or clips until it sits flat and secure.

Step 5: Check the lamps. Wipe off any dust and note whether a lamp looks dim or flickers. Lamp output falls over time, and many insect light traps call for lamp replacement about once a year, so check your unit's instructions.

Step 6: Close, plug in and switch on. Close the cover until it clicks, restore power and switch on. The indicator light on the unit should come back on.

Step 7: Update the service label. Write the date on the service sticker on the front of the unit so you know when the next check is due. If your site keeps a pest-control log, record the change there too.

Always wear gloves. The adhesive is very hard to remove from skin and clothes. If the equipment instructions differ from the steps above, follow the equipment instructions.

TIPS FOR GETTING THE BEST RESULTS FROM YOUR LUMNIA COMPACT

Position the unit away from competing light sources and out of direct sunlight, and keep it clear of open food-preparation areas. Do not touch the adhesive surface of a new board, because skin oils and dust reduce tack. Keep spare boards flat in their packaging in a cool, dry place. Check boards more often in warm months, when flying-insect activity is usually higher. Record the date of each change so you have a simple service history for audits.

SAFE REPLACEMENT AND

Storage & handling

Before opening or servicing the insect light trap, isolate its electrical supply and follow the manufacturer's instructions. Remove the used board carefully and avoid touching the adhesive or transferring insects and debris to nearby surfaces. Fit the replacement board correctly, without bending or damaging it. Keep unused boards flat in their protective packaging in a cool, dry storage area, away from direct contamination and excessive heat. Keep both unused and used adhesive boards away from children, pets, food and food-contact surfaces. Use suitable protective gloves when handling used boards, wash hands after servicing and dispose of used boards in line with the premises' hygiene procedures and applicable local waste requirements. Do not wash, clean for reuse or attempt to reactivate a used board.

WHERE THESE BOARDS MAY BE USED

A maintained Lumnia Compact unit may form part of an integrated flying-insect monitoring and control programme in suitable indoor premises. Depending on the site's risk assessment and equipment placement, examples can include food retail areas, hospitality premises, hotel service areas, offices, shops and other commercial environments. Correct positioning, housekeeping, exclusion measures, waste management and regular inspection remain important. A replacement glueboard alone does not guarantee a pest-free environment, and it does not replace professional pest-management advice where an infestation or recurring flying-insect issue is present.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I change a Lumnia Compact glueboard?
Replace it when it is substantially covered with insects or dust, when it has lost tack or is damaged, and at each scheduled service. Busy or warm periods may need more frequent changes.

Which way does the glueboard face?
The glue side faces the lamps, on the open, light-facing side of the board.

Do I need to change the lamps at the same time?
Check the lamps every time you change the board. Lamp output falls with use, and many units call for annual lamp replacement.

How do I dispose of a used glueboard?
Fold or wrap it with the sticky side inward and dispose of it in line with your premises' hygiene procedures and local waste requirements.

How do I remove glue from skin or clothes?
Glue is hard to remove, so wear gloves. If glue does get on skin or fabric, follow the advice on the product packaging.

Will this board fit other Lumnia models?
No. The black Compact board is reference 304858, 385 × 365 mm. Other Lumnia models use different references and sizes, so check your model label before ordering.
