Product Overview & Key Benefits

The Astramilano 7.5HP 2-Inch High-Pressure Petrol Water Pump is an engine-driven centrifugal pump listed for high-pressure water transfer. The supplier specifies a 7.5HP four-stroke OHV single-cylinder air-cooled petrol engine, 2-inch (50mm) inlet and outlet, maximum delivery head of 90–100 metres, maximum pressure of approximately 9–10bar and maximum water output of 35–36 cubic metres per hour. The listed suction lift is 7–8 metres, engine speed is 3,600RPM and the starting system is manual recoil.

This model is intended for buyers who need high delivery pressure from a portable petrol-powered pump. The 90–100m maximum delivery head is the main performance rating. The supplier describes it as the total physical vertical height plus horizontal pipe friction drop, so buyers should confirm how the manufacturer defines the rating and request a performance curve.

The listed maximum pressure is approximately 9–10bar. The stated maximum flow is 35–36m³/h, equivalent to about 580–600L/min under low-head conditions. The 2-inch ports define the suction and discharge connection size.

The 7.5HP petrol engine provides portable power for farms and sites without a fixed electrical supply.

Where & How to Use in Kenya

This pump may suit high-pressure irrigation, water transfer to elevated tanks, long-distance delivery and agricultural sites where water needs to be moved across sloping land. It may also be considered for construction water supply where the required head and flow fit the pump's performance range. For an irrigation system, calculate the pressure and flow required at the sprinkler, hose or distribution point.

For tank filling, account for the tank's height and the length of the delivery route. The pump is not described as a sewage or trash pump, so it should be used with water within the manufacturer's permitted quality range. UNDERSTANDING THE HIGH-HEAD RATING The listed 90–100m head is a maximum rating, not a promise of 35–36m³/h at that height.

Flow falls as total head and pipe resistance increase. Total system resistance includes vertical lift, horizontal pipe friction, elbows, valves, filters and changes in diameter. Ask the supplier for the performance curve or a written flow estimate at the intended operating head.

Use 2-inch discharge hose rated for the expected pressure. Avoid weak fittings, loose clamps and unnecessary reductions. SUCTION AND PRIMING The supplier lists a maximum suction lift of 7–8m.

Keep the pump close to the water source and use an airtight 2-inch suction hose. Confirm whether the unit is self-priming and how much water must be added to the pump casing before starting. Fit a suitable intake strainer and keep it clear.

If air enters the suction line, the pump may lose prime or deliver less water. Do not exceed the manufacturer's suction or priming limits. PETROL ENGINE OPERATION Use the fuel and oil grades specified in the engine manual.

Check engine oil before starting and follow the recoil-start procedure. Operate outdoors in a well-ventilated area because petrol exhaust is hazardous. Refuel only when the engine is stopped and cool.

Keep fuel away from flames, sparks and hot engine parts. Keep the cooling air path clear and do not operate the pump in an enclosed room or poorly ventilated pit. INSTALLATION AND

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Set the pump on firm, level ground. Secure the suction and discharge hoses to prevent movement when the pump starts. Ensure the discharge line and fittings are rated for the expected pressure.

Direct water to a safe outlet and avoid erosion or flooding. Do not run the pump dry if the manufacturer requires priming. Keep hands and clothing away from moving engine components and hot surfaces.

Isolate the pump before cleaning or maintenance. MAINTENANCE AND
