About B&G 1150 Bulb Dust-R Hand Powder Applicator

The B&G Bulb Dust-R is a small squeeze-bulb applicator designed to deliver measured amounts of compatible dry dust into narrow treatment points. Its compact shape is convenient for spot work around service penetrations, equipment voids and other hard-to-reach locations identified during inspection. The nozzle directs the material more precisely than a loose hand shaker. This is an application tool, not a pesticide; select only a product labelled for the intended site and use. Keep the applicator dedicated to one formulation, avoid cross-contamination and clean it in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions.
