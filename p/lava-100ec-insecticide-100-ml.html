Lava 100EC Insecticide (100 ml) Lava 100EC is a public health insecticide concentrate for bedbugs, cockroaches, houseflies, ants, fleas and other crawling insects in homes, hotels and offices. It comes in a 100 ml pack and is diluted with water before spraying. KEY FEATURES • Public health insecticide concentrate • Controls bedbugs, cockroaches, houseflies, ants and fleas • Also works on other crawling insects • For homes, hotels and offices • Emulsifiable concentrate that is diluted with water • 100 ml pack • Delivered to all 47 counties PESTS IT CONTROLS • Bedbugs: hide in mattress seams and bed frames and bite at night • Cockroaches: hide in kitchens, drains and cupboards and contaminate food • Houseflies: land on food and waste and spread germs • Ants: follow food trails into kitchens and stores • Fleas: live in bedding, carpets and pet areas and bite people and animals HOW TO USE IT Dilute with water before spraying, using the amount stated on the label. Apply to cracks, crevices, skirting boards, bed frames, behind appliances, door and window frames, bins and other places where insects hide or walk. Keep people, pets and food away until the spray has dried, and repeat if the label advises it. FOR HOTELS AND OFFICES In places with many rooms, plan the treatment so the whole building is sprayed in a short time, otherwise insects move from treated to untreated rooms. Focus on kitchens, stores, bathrooms, bedrooms, staff areas and drains. Keep a short record of the dates and areas treated. For large or ongoing problems, consider a licensed pest control operator. HOUSEHOLD TIPS • Clean before you spray, and wash bedding at a high temperature • Cover food, dishes and cooking surfaces • Store food in sealed containers • Take out rubbish daily • Seal cracks around pipes, doors and windows SAFETY Read the label before use. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Cover or remove fish tanks and pet bowls. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle. STORAGE Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place.
