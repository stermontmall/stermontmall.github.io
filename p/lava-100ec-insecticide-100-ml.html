Where it helps

Bedbugs hide in mattress seams and bite at night. Cockroaches contaminate food in kitchens, drains and cupboards. Houseflies land on food and waste and spread germs. Ants follow food trails into kitchens and stores, and fleas live in bedding, carpets and pet areas. A single multi-pest concentrate is convenient where several of these problems appear in the same building.

How to use it

Dilute with water before spraying, using the amount stated on the label. Apply to cracks, crevices, skirting boards, bed frames, the backs of appliances, door and window frames, bins and other places where insects hide or walk. Keep people, pets and food away until the spray has dried, and repeat if the label advises it. In buildings with many rooms, spray the whole building in a short time so insects do not move from treated to untreated rooms.

Before you start

  • Clean first, and wash bedding at a high temperature
  • Cover food, dishes and cooking surfaces
  • Store food in sealed containers and take out rubbish daily
  • Seal cracks around pipes, doors and windows

Safety

Read the label before use. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Keep it away from children, pets, food and water, and cover or remove fish tanks and pet bowls. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle.

Storage

Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place.
