How to use

1. Fill the kettle with clean water above the 0.5 L minimum mark and below the 2 L maximum mark. 2. Press the button to open the lid if needed, then close it firmly. 3. Place the kettle on its base and switch on. 4. Lift it off the base to pour. Never switch it on empty or overfill it. CARE AND CLEANING Unplug and let the kettle cool before cleaning. Wipe the outside with a soft damp cloth and never put the base in water. If limescale forms inside, boil water mixed with white vinegar, leave it for a short while, then rinse well and boil clean water twice before drinking. Empty the kettle after use.
