Cooking when Gas or power runs out

Kenyan households often face a gas cylinder running empty or a power cut at an awkward time. With gas burners and an electric hot plate on the same cooker, you can switch to the other fuel and keep the meal going. The electric oven handles baking and roasting when electricity is available.

The Oven

The 50 litre oven has top and bottom heating, which gives even heat for baking cakes, bread and pastries, roasting chicken and vegetables, and grilling when the oven is used as directed in the manual. The built-in timer helps you keep track of cooking time so food does not overcook.
