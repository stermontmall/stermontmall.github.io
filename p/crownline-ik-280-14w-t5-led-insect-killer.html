Product overview

The Crownline IK-280 is a compact 14W electric insect killer for indoor rooms where flying insects are a concern. It uses two T5 LED tubes rated at 6W each to attract flying insects towards an internal high-voltage grid. Crownline lists a coverage area of 30m² and describes the unit as suitable for rooms such as bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, toilets, offices and hotels. Its aluminium-alloy and ABS housing, hanging design and removable collection tray make it a practical fixed appliance for routine indoor insect control.

Key features & benefits

The IK-280 is a specific Crownline model, not a generic replacement lamp or a hand-held zapper. Its two T5 tubes provide the UV light source, while the internal grid is specified at 2000–2500V. The lower collection tray gathers insects that fall from the grid and can be removed for cleaning. Crownline describes the housing as aluminium alloy combined with durable ABS. The product information gives a tube working-life estimate of 5,000–8,000 hours; actual service life depends on operating conditions and replacement requirements. The easy-hanging design allows the unit to be positioned on a suitable wall or ceiling mounting point.

Uses & applications

The IK-280 is intended for indoor areas where flying insects such as flies, mosquitoes and moths are unwelcome. Its 30m² stated coverage makes it suited to a defined room or smaller work area rather than a large warehouse or open outdoor space. It may be considered for homes, offices, hotel rooms, kitchens and other indoor premises. In food-related businesses, position and operate the appliance in accordance with the premises' hygiene procedures and any applicable requirements; the product listing does not itself establish food-safety certification. HOW IT WORKS The UV tubes emit light intended to attract flying insects. When insects reach the internal electrified grid, they are killed and drop into the collection tray. This is a physical electrical insect-control device: it does not apply residual pesticide, disinfect a surface or remove insect breeding sites. Results in a particular room can vary with competing light, room layout, airflow, insect species and placement. Treat the stated 30m² coverage as the manufacturer's guide rather than a guarantee that every insect within that area will be caught. POSITIONING AND INSTALLATION Choose a suitable indoor location with access to a compatible mains outlet and enough clearance for safe servicing. The hanging arrangement requires a secure mounting point appropriate for the appliance's weight. Place the unit where flying insects are likely to travel, but away from excessive dust, water splash, cooking grease and accidental impact. Avoid positioning it immediately beside brighter competing lights that may draw insects away from the UV tubes. Keep the appliance accessible for inspection and tray cleaning. Follow the supplied manual for installation and electrical connection; do not modify the cord or mounting hardware. CLEANING AND CARE Switch off and unplug the IK-280 before cleaning, moving or servicing it. Follow the manufacturer's instructions when removing the collection tray. Dispose of captured insects hygienically and clean the tray without allowing moisture to enter electrical components. Inspect the housing, tubes and protective grille periodically. Replace worn tubes only with compatible parts specified for this model. Do not operate the appliance if its cable, casing, grille or other electrical components are damaged.

Safety information

The IK-280 is a mains-powered appliance containing a high-voltage grid. Keep it away from children and do not insert fingers or objects through the grille. Disconnect power before any maintenance. Keep it dry and use it only in the conditions specified by Crownline. It should complement, not replace, good sanitation, waste control, screens, exclusion and professional pest-management measures where required.
