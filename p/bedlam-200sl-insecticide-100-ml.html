Bedlam 200SL Insecticide (50 ml and 100 ml) Bedlam 200SL is an insecticide for bed bugs, cockroaches and fleas in Kenya, available in 50 ml and 100 ml packs. These sizes suit homes, rentals, hostels and small premises that need to treat several rooms or repeat a treatment. KEY FEATURES • Insecticide for bed bugs, cockroaches and fleas • 50 ml and 100 ml packs for homes, hostels and small premises • Soluble liquid (SL) formulation that is mixed with water • Delivered to all 47 counties SPECIFICATIONS • Product: Bedlam 200SL • Use: Bed bugs, cockroaches and fleas • Pack sizes: 50 ml and 100 ml WHERE IT HELPS Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards and cracks in walls, and they bite at night. Cockroaches shelter in kitchens, drains, cupboards and behind appliances, and spread germs over food and surfaces. Fleas live in bedding, carpets, pet resting places and cracks in floors, and bite people and animals. A larger pack gives enough product to treat a full house, several hostel rooms or a rental unit between tenants. CHOOSING THE RIGHT SIZE Pick the 50 ml pack for a small house or a few rooms. Choose the 100 ml pack if you manage several rooms or units, or want some product in reserve for a follow-up treatment, if the label advises one. GETTING THE BEST RESULTS • Clean first: wash bedding at a high temperature and vacuum mattresses, floors and corners • Dilute exactly as directed on the label • Spray cracks, crevices, mattress seams, bed frames, skirting boards and other hiding places • Keep people, pets and food away until the spray has dried • Treat all affected rooms at the same time, so pests do not move from one room to another • For large or persistent infestations, consider a licensed pest control operator SAFETY Read and follow the label before use. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle. STORAGE Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place. ORDER Pay by M-Pesa.
