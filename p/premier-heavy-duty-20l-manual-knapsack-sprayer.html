CAPACITY AND PRACTICAL FIT The 20-litre capacity is intended to reduce the number of refill stops compared with smaller hand-held pressure sprayers. The actual area covered by one tank cannot be fixed from capacity alone. It varies with the application rate, crop spacing, nozzle type, walking speed, spray pattern and the amount of liquid that must reach each target. A user treating a small home garden may find a 20-litre tank larger than necessary, while a farmer or grounds worker treating several beds or rows may value the reduced refill frequency. Plan the amount of liquid from the treatment label rather than filling the tank simply because space is available. BACKPACK DESIGN The supplier describes an ergonomic knapsack design intended to make the unit more comfortable during extended use. A backpack arrangement places the tank on the operator's back and allows the lance to be directed with the hands. Fit and comfort depend on the operator, clothing, tank fill level and adjustment of the straps. Before beginning a long task, try the empty sprayer on a level surface, adjust the straps evenly and check that the tank remains stable when walking. Do not assume that a 20-litre full load is light; the combined weight of the tank, liquid and fittings can be substantial. CONSTRUCTION AND INTENDED USE MyAgrovet describes the unit as being built from durable materials, but does not publish a specific tank polymer, pressure rating, pump design, nozzle type or seal material in the available product details. Those technical details should therefore be checked on the delivered unit or with the supplier if they are important to your work. The listing names fertilizers, pesticides and water as intended liquids. Compatibility is not universal: the treatment label and sprayer instructions must both permit the chosen liquid. Do not use the sprayer with solvents, fuels, corrosive mixtures or any formulation that the equipment manufacturer does not approve. APPLICATION PLANNING For crop spraying, first identify the crop, target pest or disease and approved product. Read the label for the correct dilution, target coverage, application timing, re-entry interval and personal protective equipment. Calibrate the sprayer using clean water before mixing a treatment. Walk at a consistent pace, maintain a steady lance position and avoid spraying when wind could carry droplets onto people, neighbouring crops, animals or water. For household or premises work, confirm that the product is approved for the target surface and location. Keep children, pets, food, utensils and uncovered water away from the treatment area as directed by the label. PRE-USE INSPECTION Before every use, check the tank body, cap, straps, hose, pump, trigger, lance and nozzle for visible damage. Tighten fittings only as instructed; overtightening can damage plastic threads or seals. Test the system with clean water and check for leaks before adding a chemical. If pressure cannot be maintained, liquid escapes from a connection or the trigger sticks, stop and identify the fault before continuing. Do not try to clear a blocked nozzle by blowing through it with your mouth. Use the cleaning method and tools recommended by the equipment manufacturer. CLEANING AND SAFE

Storage & handling

After finishing, follow the label instructions for leftover mixture, cleaning and rinse-water disposal. Never empty residues into a storm drain, stream, pond or drinking-water source. Clean the tank and liquid path only in the manner allowed for the formulation used. Remove and rinse the nozzle or filter as directed, then allow parts to dry. Keep the sprayer empty and stored upright in a secure, shaded, ventilated place. Store it separately from food, animal feed, medicines and household utensils.
