About Amaze Fridge and Freezer Guard with 6-Way Extension Socket

Product overview

Buy the Amaze Fridge and Freezer Guard with a 6-way extension socket online in Kenya. Power cuts, sudden returns of electricity and low voltage are some of the most common reasons fridge compressors fail. This guard sits between your wall socket and your fridge or freezer, watches the supply, and cuts the power when it is unsafe so your appliance is not damaged. How it works: the guard has three indicator lights marked OFF, WAIT and ON. When the supply is unsafe it disconnects the appliance. When power returns, the guard waits for a short delay before switching back on, which gives the compressor time to settle. This protects against the damaging quick restart that often happens when electricity comes back after an outage. A bypass button lets you override the guard if you need to. The bundle also includes an Amaze 6-way extension socket with a long white cable and a UK-style plug, so you can power your TV, decoder, phone chargers and other small devices from a single wall point. The sockets are clearly spaced, and the cable is long enough to reach across most rooms. Who needs it: any household or small business with a fridge, freezer, water dispenser or display cooler. In areas where voltage drops or blackouts are common, a guard is a small cost compared with replacing a compressor or losing a freezer full of food, milk or drinks. Pack size: 1 Amaze fridge and freezer guard plus 1 Amaze 6-way extension socket with cable. Colour: white with blue label. Plug type: UK three-pin. Please check the rating printed on the guard against your appliance before you buy, and use the guard for refrigeration appliances as shown on its label. Installation is simple: plug the guard into the wall, plug your fridge into the guard, and wait for the ON light. No tools needed.
