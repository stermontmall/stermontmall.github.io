144HZ AMOLED DISPLAY

Infinix lists a 6.78-inch Full HD+ AMOLED display with a 1080 x 2436 resolution and refresh rates up to 144Hz. The large screen gives room for reading, video, documents, social media and games. AMOLED technology produces strong contrast and deep blacks, while the high refresh rate can make supported motion and scrolling appear smoother. Not every application or game will run at 144Hz, and the setting can affect power consumption. Display brightness and colour appearance also depend on settings, content and lighting conditions.

CAMERAS FOR PHOTOGRAPHY AND CONTENT

The official specification lists a 50MP main rear camera with optical image stabilisation, an 8MP secondary camera and a flicker sensor. The front camera is 32MP. Optical stabilisation helps counter small hand movements and can improve the chance of sharper still photos or steadier supported video, particularly when light is limited. The additional rear camera supports wider scene capture, while camera modes include portrait, night, slow motion, time-lapse and document photography. Results depend on lighting, subject movement and software processing; a high megapixel count is not a guarantee of professional results.

PROCESSOR AND DAILY PERFORMANCE

The Note 50 Pro uses the MediaTek Helio G100 Ultimate platform, an octa-core chipset intended for everyday apps, multitasking, browsing, streaming and supported mobile games. This is a 4G-class platform rather than a 5G chipset. Performance depends on application requirements, software updates, background tasks, device temperature and available memory. Customers who need demanding 3D gaming or specialist professional workflows should compare independent performance tests and higher-performance models before choosing.

8GB RAM AND 256GB

Storage & handling

This listing is for 8GB physical RAM and 256GB internal storage. Physical RAM helps the operating system keep active applications available when switching between tasks. Infinix also describes extended-memory functionality on this model; extended RAM uses part of internal storage and should not be confused with additional physical RAM. The 256GB storage capacity provides room for photos, videos, applications, downloads and work files, although system software occupies some space. Actual available storage is therefore less than the headline capacity.

BATTERY AND CHARGING

Infinix specifies a typical 5,200mAh battery and support for up to 90W wired charging. The manufacturer also lists wireless charging capability on the Note 50 Pro range. Maximum charging performance depends on a compatible charger, cable, regional package and power conditions. Confirm which charger and accessories are included with the supplied Kenyan-market unit. Battery life varies with screen refresh rate, brightness, mobile signal, camera use, gaming and background applications. Use compatible charging equipment and follow the manufacturer's safety instructions.

CONNECTIVITY, AUDIO AND PROTECTION

The published specification includes dual Nano-SIM, 4.5G/4G/3G/2G network support, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.4, USB Type-C and NFC. Actual mobile bands and NFC availability can vary by market, so check the model variant and operator compatibility. Infinix lists stereo speakers with JBL tuning and an IP64 rating for dust and splash resistance. IP64 does not mean waterproof: do not submerge the phone or expose it to high-pressure water. Physical damage and normal wear can reduce resistance.

WHO SHOULD CONSIDER THE NOTE 50 PRO?

The Note 50 Pro is worth considering for users who value a large 144Hz AMOLED screen, a stabilised 50MP main camera, fast wired charging and 256GB of storage. It can suit students, entrepreneurs, office users and content consumers who want a capable 4G smartphone. It is not the right choice for someone whose essential requirement is 5G connectivity.
