Product overview

The IPCONE IP20LMN is a 20-litre manual knapsack sprayer listed by MyAgrovet under WQ products. It is designed for users who want a hand-operated backpack sprayer for approved agricultural and general liquid applications. The product listing identifies an internally mounted brass piston pump, a Santoprene piston cup, a UV-resistant tank and an internal agitator. The tank is marked in litres and gallons, helping the operator estimate the amount of liquid remaining. The supplier lists a maximum pressure of 65 PSI and states that five pumps can build approximately 45 PSI. 20-LITRE UV-RESISTANT TANK The nominal tank capacity is 20 litres. The tank is described as high-density polyethylene with UV resistance and visible gallon and litre markings. These markings are useful when preparing a measured volume, but users should still follow the chemical label and use accurate measuring tools for concentrate. A full 20-litre tank is heavy, so plan the route and refill point before starting. Fill only the quantity required for the task, keep the cap secure and avoid carrying a full sprayer over steep, slippery or unstable ground. Store the unit out of direct sunlight when not in use, even though the tank is described as UV-resistant. INTERNALLY MOUNTED BRASS PISTON PUMP The supplier identifies an internally mounted brass piston pump. Its placement is intended to reduce external leakage around the pump assembly. The piston cup is listed as Santoprene, a material chosen for resistance to chemical wear. The supplier states that the sprayer can reach up to 65 PSI and that approximately 45 PSI is achieved after five pumps. Actual pressure and output depend on the pump condition, seals, nozzle, liquid and operating technique. Use a steady pumping rhythm and do not force the lever if it binds or becomes difficult to move. Stop and inspect the sprayer if pressure cannot be maintained or liquid escapes from a connection. BUILT-IN AGITATOR The product has an internal agitator designed to help keep the liquid mixture blended while the sprayer is operating. This can be useful for formulations that require mixing, but it does not remove the need to prepare the solution correctly. Follow the product label for agitation, dilution and application instructions. Some products must be mixed in a separate container before being poured into the sprayer. Do not assume that every suspension, powder, concentrate or fertilizer can be used in the tank. Confirm compatibility from the formulation label and equipment instructions, and clean the unit thoroughly between different products. APPLICATION AREAS MyAgrovet lists pesticides, herbicides, fertilizers and general spraying among the intended application categories. Use only a formulation whose label permits application with a manual knapsack sprayer and whose target crop, pest, disease or surface matches the intended task. For herbicides, prevent drift and accidental contact with crops or ornamentals. For insecticides and fungicides, observe the label's crop-specific dose, timing, protective equipment, re-entry interval and harvest interval. For liquid fertilizers, follow the recommended dilution and avoid applying during unsuitable weather. The sprayer itself does not determine the correct treatment or dose. PREPARATION AND SAFE OPERATION Before filling, inspect the tank, cap, pump lever, hose, trigger, lance, nozzle, filter and seals. Test the sprayer with clean water and check all connections for leaks. Measure and mix the treatment exactly as instructed on its label. Wear gloves, eye protection, protective clothing and respiratory protection where required. Keep children, bystanders, pets, livestock, food, animal feed and drinking water away from the treatment area. Do not spray in strong wind or toward people, animals, open windows, roads or water sources. Keep a steady pace and avoid exceeding the label's application rate. CLEANING AND

Clean the tank, internal pump, hose, filter, lance and nozzle following the equipment instructions. Flush the liquid path before changing formulations, especially before switching from herbicide to a crop treatment. Do not clear a blocked nozzle by mouth.
