The WQ 6 kg Poultry Feeder is a red-and-white plastic gravity feeder for chickens, ducks, turkeys and similar poultry. Its feed container holds up to 6 kg, allowing a keeper to replenish feed less frequently than with a small chick tray. Uses & applications

For a backyard chicken keeper, a 6 kg reservoir can reduce the number of times feed needs topping up during the day. In a brooder or grower area, use a feeder size and height appropriate to the birds' age so chicks can reach the trough without climbing into it. For mature layers, position the feeder where birds can approach from several sides without crowding. Duck and turkey keepers should check that the trough dimensions and access suit the size of their birds before buying. HOW TO SET UP Place the feeder on a stable, level surface or suspend it only if the design and supplied fittings allow. Fill the reservoir with a suitable dry poultry ration and make sure the base is correctly seated before placing it with the flock. Set the feeding height so birds can eat comfortably while limiting scratching and litter entry. Observe the flock after installation: if birds struggle to reach the feed, crowd around one side or scatter large amounts, adjust the height and position or add more feeding points. Refill before the reservoir is empty and check that feed flows freely into the trough. CLEANING AND BIOSECURITY Empty remaining feed before washing. Remove loose feed and litter from the tray, then clean and dry the parts using a poultry-house hygiene routine appropriate to the farm. Do not refill a wet feeder because damp feed can spoil or cake. Inspect the container, tray and attachment points for cracks, sharp edges or damage. Replace a damaged feeder rather than allowing broken plastic to injure birds or contaminate feed. Keep equipment for different houses or age groups separate where the farm's biosecurity plan requires it. BEFORE YOU BUY Check that 6 kg is an appropriate capacity for your flock and that the dimensions fit the available floor space. A single feeder may not provide enough access for a large group; the correct number depends on bird age, flock size and the feeding space recommended for the production system. This listing is for one feeder, not a pack of ten and not an automatic feeding line. Confirm included accessories with the seller if you require a hanging cord or spare parts.

Buying guide

Choose a 6 kg feeder when you want a straightforward reservoir-and-trough design for routine poultry feeding. A smaller feeder may suit a small brooder or limited flock, while larger farms may prefer multiple feeding points or a silo-style system. If your main concern is feed wastage, observe the birds and adjust feeder height, fill level and access rather than assuming capacity alone will prevent spillage. If you need a feeder with a grill, anti-scatter ring, legs or a metal trough, compare those specific designs before ordering.
