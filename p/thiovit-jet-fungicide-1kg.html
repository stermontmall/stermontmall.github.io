Product Overview & Key Benefits

Thiovit Jet 1kg is a Syngenta sulphur-based wettable-granule fungicide supplied in a one-kilogram pack for crop protection programmes where powdery mildew and mite pressure need to be managed. The product is listed by Syngenta Kenya as Thiovit Jet Wettable Powder, with elemental sulphur as its active ingredient and a wettable-granule formulation. Its official product information describes contact, fumigant and residual activity against powdery mildew and mites.

The 1kg pack is a practical size for growers, horticultural operators and agro-dealers who need a manageable quantity for planned crop-protection work. The formulation is designed around finely processed sulphur particles. Syngenta identifies optimum particle size as an important product characteristic, supporting contact and vapour activity.

The manufacturer describes three modes of activity: contact, fumigant and residual. Actual performance depends on the crop, target organism, weather, coverage, timing and correct use according to the current label. Thiovit Jet is identified for use against powdery mildew and mites.

Powdery mildew is a fungal disease that can appear as a pale, powder-like growth on plant surfaces. Mites are small pests that may cause stippling, bronzing or other feeding damage depending on the crop and species. Where the diagnosis is uncertain, consult a qualified agronomist or crop-protection adviser before choosing a treatment.
