About Abasi 5% EC Insecticide (1 Litre)

Abasi 5% EC (1 litre) Spider mites, thrips and leafminers are small, but they reduce yield and spoil the look of roses, tomatoes, cucumbers and French beans, which is what buyers pay for. Abasi 5% EC is an abamectin insecticide that kills by contact and stomach action and moves through the leaf (translaminar), so pests feeding on the underside are reached. It works at very low rates. KEY BENEFITS • Strong knockdown of mites and leaf-mining insects • Contact and stomach activity stops pests feeding quickly • Translaminar movement protects both leaf surfaces • Reliable on thrips and other small sucking pests • Cost-efficient: only 4 to 6 ml per 20 L knapsack TECHNICAL SPECS • Active ingredient: Abamectin 5% (50 g/L) • Formulation: Emulsifiable concentrate (EC) • IRAC group 6: activates glutamate-gated chloride channels, causing paralysis • Pack: 1 litre (also available in 50 ml, 100 ml, 250 ml, 500 ml and 1 L) • Crops and pests: thrips, spider mites and nematode dips on roses; spider mites and caterpillars on tomatoes; leafminers and aphids on cucumbers; leafminers, thrips, spider mites and aphids on French beans MIXING AND APPLICATION DOSAGE (per 20 L knapsack) Rate: 200 to 300 ml per hectare in 1,000 L of water, which is 4 to 6 ml per 20 L knapsack. A 1-litre bottle mixes about 166 to 250 knapsacks. 1. Half-fill the knapsack with clean water. 2. Add the measured Abasi, then top up to 20 L and agitate well. 3. Spray immediately after mixing, covering the undersides of leaves where mites and thrips hide. 4. Follow the repeat interval and harvest waiting period printed on your pack. SAFETY Toxic to bees and aquatic life, so avoid spraying during flowering or near water. Wear gloves, mask and overalls. Do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying.
