Abasi 5% EC Insecticide and Miticide Abasi 5% EC is an abamectin insecticide and miticide for growers of roses and vegetables. It controls thrips and spider mites, and it can be used as a dip to control nematodes on roses. It also protects tomatoes against spider mites and caterpillars, cucumbers against leaf miners and aphids, and French beans against leaf miners, thrips, spider mites and aphids. ACTIVE INGREDIENT Abamectin 5%, in an emulsifiable concentrate (EC) formulation. WHY GROWERS CHOOSE ABASI 5% EC • Highly effective against mites and leaf-mining insects, with strong knockdown action • Works by contact and stomach activity, so pests stop feeding quickly • Translaminar movement protects both the upper and lower leaf surfaces • Reliable control of thrips and other small sucking pests • Effective at low use rates, which keeps the cost per hectare down CROPS AND PESTS • Roses: thrips and spider mites, and nematodes when used as a dip • Tomatoes: spider mites and caterpillars • Cucumbers: leaf miners and aphids • French beans: leaf miners, thrips, spider mites and aphids HOW TO MIX Half fill the spray tank with water. Add the required amount of Abasi 5EC, then top up with water to the required level and mix or agitate thoroughly until uniform. Spray immediately after mixing. The application rate for this product is 200 to 300 ml per hectare in 1000 litres of water. HOW IT WORKS Abamectin activates glutamate-gated chloride channels in the pest, increasing chloride ion flow and causing paralysis. This is why treated mites and insects stop feeding soon after they contact or eat the spray. SAFETY Always read the label before use and follow the pre-harvest interval for your crop. Wear protective clothing, gloves, a mask and eye protection when mixing and spraying. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Do not let spray or rinse water reach streams or ponds.
