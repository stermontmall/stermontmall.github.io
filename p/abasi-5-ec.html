About Abasi 5% EC

Abasi 5% EC Insecticide and Miticide (50 ml, 100 ml, 250 ml, 500 ml, 1 Litre)

Abasi 5% EC is an abamectin insecticide and miticide for growers of roses and vegetables. It controls thrips and spider mites, can be used as a dip against nematodes on roses, and protects tomatoes, cucumbers and French beans from mites, caterpillars, leaf miners and aphids.

Key specifications & features

  • Active ingredient: Abamectin 5%
  • Target: Thrips and spider mites on roses (nematodes as a dip); mites and caterpillars on tomatoes; leaf miners and aphids on cucumbers and French beans
  • Formulation: Emulsifiable concentrate (EC)
  • Application settings: Agricultural: roses and vegetables

Mixing & application instructions

1. Dilution rate: Application rate 200 to 300 ml per hectare in 1000 litres of water.

2. Application method: Half-fill the spray tank with water, add the required amount of Abasi 5EC, top up to the level and agitate until uniform. Spray immediately after mixing, covering both leaf surfaces.

3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Available in 50 ml, 100 ml, 250 ml, 500 ml and 1 Litre.

4. Abamectin acts by contact and stomach action with translaminar movement, so treated pests stop feeding soon after contact.

5. Rotate with insecticides from other chemical groups.

Safety & re-entry precautions

  • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.

Safe use of Original Abasi 5% EC

  • Read the whole label before you buy or mix. The label is the legal instruction for crops, pests, rates and safety.
  • Look for the PCPB registration number on the pack. Do not buy chemicals that carry none.
  • Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI) printed on the label, the number of days that must pass between the last spray and harvest. Use the product only on the crops the label lists.
  • Wear gloves, a mask or respirator, eye protection and long protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards.
  • Keep chemicals in their original container, out of reach of children, pets and livestock. Never reuse empty containers for food or water.
