Features & specifications

Bacillus thuringiensis, commonly called Bt, is a naturally occurring soil bacterium used in biological insect control. Baciguard is a stomach-acting microbial insecticide: susceptible larvae must ingest treated plant material for the Bt toxins to become active in their gut.

Care, cleaning & storage

Scout the crop regularly, inspect growing points and leaf undersides, and look for eggs, young larvae, fresh feeding damage and frass. Identify the pest before treatment because different caterpillars and other insects may require different products. Bt products should be stored and handled according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Keep Baciguard in its original, tightly closed package in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Do not store a prepared spray mixture longer than the label permits. Use clean application equipment and do not mix Baciguard with fungicides or other pesticides unless compatibility is confirmed by the label or manufacturer.
