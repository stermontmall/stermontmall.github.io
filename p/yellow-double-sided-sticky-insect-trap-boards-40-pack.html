WHAT THIS PRODUCT IS

These boards are passive adhesive monitors. They do not use an electrical grid, UV lamp, liquid, aerosol or pesticide treatment. Insects that contact the adhesive surface may become stuck, allowing the board to provide a visible indication of insect activity. Because the product is a monitor rather than a complete pest-control programme, it should be considered alongside cleaning, waste management, physical exclusion and regular inspection. A board cannot identify every insect species, explain where insects are breeding or guarantee that an infestation has been resolved.

WHERE IT MAY BE CONSIDERED

The 40-board pack may be relevant to greenhouse operators, plant-growing areas, stores, warehouses and other suitable locations where monitoring flying-insect activity is useful. The appropriate location depends on the site, the insects being monitored and the instructions supplied with the product. In a greenhouse or nursery, monitoring can help staff notice changes in flying-insect activity around plants. In a storage area, boards may form one part of a documented inspection routine. In homes or shared buildings, use should be limited to suitable areas where the boards can be placed securely and kept away from children, pets, food and surfaces used for food preparation.

HOW TO CHOOSE THE RIGHT MONITOR

Before ordering, consider the area to be monitored, the type of flying insect that has been observed and whether an adhesive board is suitable for that setting. Confirm the board dimensions and whether the adhesive is exposed on both sides as expected. Ask the supplier whether holders or mounting accessories are included; they are not confirmed in the current listing details. If the site requires monitoring for a particular insect, ask a qualified pest-management professional whether this board is appropriate. Different insects respond differently to monitoring methods, and a yellow adhesive board should not be assumed to attract every flying pest.

PLACEMENT AND ROUTINE CHECKS

Follow the instructions printed on the supplied packaging. Choose a stable, appropriate position where the board can be inspected without being accidentally touched or knocked down. Keep it away from food, food-contact surfaces, cooking areas and places where children or pets can reach it. Record the date and location when a board is placed so that observations can be compared over time. Inspect it according to the product directions and the site's hygiene procedures. If a board becomes dusty, full, damaged or loses its adhesive condition, follow the packaging guidance for replacement and disposal. Do not touch the adhesive surface directly.

USING MONITORING INFORMATION RESPONSIBLY

A catch on a board is an observation, not a diagnosis. Similar-looking insects can be difficult to distinguish, and a board may catch non-target insects as well as the insects of concern. If identification matters, ask a qualified person to examine a clear photograph or specimen. Compare observations from the same locations over time rather than drawing conclusions from one board. If insect activity continues, investigate possible entry points, standing water, waste handling, stored goods, plant conditions and other site-specific sources with appropriate professional support.

CARE,

Storage & handling

Keep unused boards in their original packaging and store them in a clean, dry place as directed by the supplier. Protect the adhesive from dust, moisture and accidental contact before use. Do not place boards where they may obstruct walkways, contact machinery or become mixed with food, stock or packaging. Handle used boards carefully and dispose of them according to the packaging instructions and local waste procedures. Do not wash and reuse an adhesive board unless the manufacturer explicitly states that it is reusable.
