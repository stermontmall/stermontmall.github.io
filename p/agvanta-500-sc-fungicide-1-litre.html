Product overview

Agvanta 500 SC Fungicide 1 litre is a dual-active fungicide containing azoxystrobin 250 g/L and flutriafol 250 g/L in a suspension concentrate formulation. Kenya's Pest Control Products Board crop register lists AGVANTA 500 SC under PCPB(CR)1741. Its registered uses cover several important crop diseases: Northern and Southern leaf blight, grey leaf spot and leaf rust on maize; powdery mildew and Botrytis in roses; rust in carnations; early and late blight in tomatoes; powdery mildew and leaf spots in peppers; rust in soybeans; yellow rust and stem rust in wheat; and rice blast in rice. This page is specifically for one 1 litre pack. The selected pack should be matched to the farm's label-based requirement and not confused with other sizes or fungicide formulations. ACTIVE INGREDIENTS AND FORMULATION Agvanta 500 SC combines azoxystrobin 250 g/L with flutriafol 250 g/L. Azoxystrobin belongs to the QoI fungicide group, while flutriafol is a triazole fungicide. The combination gives the product two different modes of action, but it does not remove the need for resistance management. The SC designation means suspension concentrate. Confirm both active ingredients and their concentrations on the original container. Do not confuse Agvanta 500 SC with products that contain only azoxystrobin, only flutriafol or a different concentration. It is a concentrate, not a ready-to-use spray; preparation and application must follow the current Kenyan label. KENYAN REGISTRATION AND APPROVED USES The PCPB crop register lists Agvanta 500 SC as PCPB(CR)1741 and specifies the registered crop and disease uses. The maize uses include Northern and Southern leaf blight, grey leaf spot and leaf rust. The flower uses include powdery mildew and Botrytis in roses and rust in carnations. The vegetable uses include early and late blight in tomatoes and powdery mildew and leaf spots in peppers. The register also lists rust in soybeans, yellow rust and stem rust in wheat, and rice blast in rice. These uses are crop-specific. A registration for one crop does not automatically authorize treatment of another crop or disease. Check the crop, disease, growth stage and current label before purchase and before use. The PCPB register states a 12-hour restricted-entry interval with work wear and chemical-resistant gloves. It also gives crop-specific pre-harvest intervals: three days for tomatoes and peppers, 21 days for soybeans, 35 days for wheat, seven days for maize and 28 days for rice. Always verify the current label for the exact crop, any updated restrictions and additional personal protective equipment. Do not transfer a pre-harvest interval from one crop to another. 1 LITRE PACK SIZE AND BUYING FIT This listing is for one 1 litre container. It is not another pack size. Select only the quantity required by the label and avoid storing excess concentrate unnecessarily. DISEASE IDENTIFICATION AND RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT Accurate disease identification is important before selecting a fungicide. Leaf spots, blights, rusts and powdery mildew can have different causes and may require different management. Inspect the crop and confirm the disease with a qualified agronomist where symptoms are uncertain. Agvanta's two active ingredients do not mean that it should be applied repeatedly without a plan. Follow resistance-management guidance, rotate with fungicides from different FRAC groups when recommended, and avoid unnecessary repeat applications. Good field hygiene, removal of infected crop residues, adequate crop spacing, balanced nutrition and monitoring can support an integrated disease-management programme. Use Agvanta only for the crop and disease combinations listed on the current Kenyan label. BEFORE PURCHASE AND DELIVERY Confirm the product name, both active ingredients, concentration, SC formulation and exact 1 litre pack size. Check that the original seal is intact, the label is legible and the batch, manufacturing and expiry details can be read. Do not accept a leaking, damaged, unlabelled or tampered container. If the delivered pack is a different size or formulation, do not open or apply it; contact the supplier for clarification. Keep the label available during storage, mixing and application.

Safety information

Read and follow the complete, current product label and safety data sheet before opening or using this pesticide. Confirm that the intended crop, target and application method are approved. Do not guess a dose, dilution, tank mix or treatment interval.

Wear the protective clothing and equipment required by the label and safety data sheet during handling, mixing, loading, application and cleaning. Avoid skin and eye contact and inhalation of spray mist. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. Wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly after work.

Keep children, animals and unprotected people away. Prevent spray drift, spills and run-off, and protect food, feed, medicines, drinking water, drains and natural water sources. Observe the exact restricted-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the approved label.

If exposure or suspected poisoning occurs, follow the label first-aid instructions and seek prompt medical advice. Keep the original container and label available.

Storage & handling

Keep the container closed in its original labelled packaging in a secure, cool, dry and well-ventilated chemical store. Protect it from heat, direct sunlight and physical damage. Store separately from food, feed, medicines and household goods, and prevent access by children and unauthorized persons. Never transfer pesticide into a food or drink container. Do not reuse empty packaging. Dispose of containers, unused product and washings only as directed by the label and applicable Kenyan requirements; never pour them into drains or water sources.
