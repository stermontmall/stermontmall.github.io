Product overview

Fendona 60 SC Insecticide 250ml is a BASF suspension concentrate for professional and public-health pest management. The product contains alpha-cypermethrin at 60 g/L and is registered in Kenya under PCPB(CR)0166. The Pest Control Products Board public-health register lists its use against mosquitoes, cockroaches and houseflies, and for protection of store fabric in grain-storage outlets against storage insect pests. This listing is for one 250ml bottle. It is intended for pest-management professionals, institutions, hospitality facilities, grain-storage operators and other buyers who have confirmed that the registered use and product label match their treatment requirement. ACTIVE INGREDIENT AND FORMULATION Fendona 60 SC contains alpha-cypermethrin 60 g/L. The SC designation identifies a suspension concentrate formulation. The active ingredient and formulation should be checked on the original bottle before purchase. Fendona 60 SC should not be confused with Fendona products carrying a different concentration, pack size or formulation, or with other pyrethroid insecticides. The 250ml bottle is a concentrated product and is not ready to apply directly to surfaces. Preparation, dilution, application equipment, treatment surfaces, target pest and safety measures must follow the current Kenyan label. Do not transfer rates from another Fendona pack or another alpha-cypermethrin product. KENYAN REGISTRATION AND TARGET PESTS The PCPB public-health register identifies Fendona 60 SC as PCPB(CR)0166 and lists mosquitoes, cockroaches and houseflies as target pests. It also lists protection of store fabric in grain-storage outlets against storage insect pests. These are public-health and stored-grain-facility uses, not a general approval for application to crops or food commodities. The registered use should guide the purchase decision: identify the pest, the treatment site and whether the site is a domestic, institutional, commercial or grain-storage environment. Do not apply the product to food, feed, crops, people or animals. Safety information

Read and follow the complete, current product label and safety data sheet before opening or using this pesticide. Confirm that the product, formulation, intended crop or site, target pest or disease and application method are approved for the intended use. Do not guess a dose, dilution, tank mix, application interval or treatment method, and never use the product for an unlisted purpose.

Wear all personal protective equipment specified on the label and safety data sheet during handling, measuring, mixing, loading, application and equipment cleaning. Avoid contact with skin and eyes and avoid inhaling dust, vapour or spray mist. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. Wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly after work, and remove and launder contaminated clothing separately.

Keep children, animals and unprotected people away from the product and treated areas. Prevent spray drift, spills and run-off. Do not contaminate food, animal feed, medicines, drinking water, drains, ponds, rivers or neighbouring crops. Observe the exact restricted-entry interval and pre-harvest interval stated on the approved label; do not re-enter or harvest earlier.

If exposure, a spill or suspected poisoning occurs, stop work, follow the first-aid and emergency instructions on the product label and safety data sheet, and seek prompt medical or professional advice. Keep the original container and label available for medical personnel. Do not induce vomiting or administer treatment unless instructed by the label or a qualified medical professional.

Storage & handling

Store the 250ml bottle tightly closed in its original labelled container in a secure, cool, dry and ventilated pesticide store. Protect it from direct sunlight, heat and physical damage. Keep it away from food, feed, household goods and water sources. Never transfer the concentrate into a beverage bottle or an unlabelled container. Do not reuse empty pesticide packaging. Follow the label and applicable Kenyan requirements for collection and disposal. Do not pour unused product or washings into drains, rivers, ponds or other water sources.
