Ocean Blue Nitrile Powder-Free Examination Gloves are disposable hand-protection gloves intended for suitable examination, laboratory, hygiene and general handling tasks. Nitrile is a synthetic rubber material and is often selected as an alternative to natural rubber latex. Powder-free gloves can reduce powder residue during use. Why choose Nitrile Gloves

Nitrile gloves provide a flexible barrier for tasks that require frequent glove changes and good hand dexterity. They are commonly used in medical examination, laboratory work, cleaning, food-handling support and other hygiene-sensitive activities. Nitrile is not natural rubber latex, which may be relevant for users who need to avoid latex proteins. However, the exact formulation and allergy information should be checked on the product packaging. Not every nitrile glove is suitable for every chemical, medical procedure or food-contact application.

Powder-free design

Powder-free gloves are manufactured without a deliberately added donning powder. This can help reduce powder residue on hands, equipment and work surfaces. The glove should be easy to put on when the hands are clean and dry, but fit and ease of donning vary by size and product finish. Avoid using lotion or oil that may affect glove integrity unless the manufacturer confirms compatibility. If the glove tears during donning, replace it before starting the task.

Select the correct size

Choose a size that fits the palm and fingers without excessive tension. A glove that is too small may split or cause hand fatigue, while a loose glove may reduce tactile control. Check the supplier's size chart and confirm whether Small, Medium, Large or Extra Large options are available. For a workplace team, maintain size-specific stock so workers are not forced to use an unsuitable fit. Inspect gloves for tears, pinholes, discolouration or damaged packaging before use.

Single-use handling

Examination gloves are generally intended for single use unless the manufacturer explicitly states otherwise. Change gloves between tasks, between individuals and whenever they become torn, heavily contaminated or otherwise compromised. Do not wash or disinfect disposable gloves for reuse. Remove gloves carefully to avoid touching the contaminated outer surface with bare skin, then wash or sanitise hands. Dispose of used gloves according to the workplace's hygiene and waste-management procedure.

Chemical and biological limitations

Nitrile gloves can resist some chemicals, but resistance varies significantly with formulation, concentration, temperature and exposure time. Do not assume that a standard examination glove is suitable for concentrated chemicals, solvents, pesticides or fumigation products. For chemical handling, consult the glove manufacturer's compatibility chart and the chemical safety data sheet. For medical or laboratory procedures, verify the required medical-device status, sterility and barrier performance. This listing does not state that the gloves are sterile or certified for chemotherapy, infectious agents or all chemical applications.

Storage and Pack details

Keep unused gloves in their original dispenser packaging in a clean, dry place away from direct sunlight, excessive heat and sharp objects. Check the manufacturer's expiry date and packaging condition before use.
