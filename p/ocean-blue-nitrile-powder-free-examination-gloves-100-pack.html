Before you start

Choose a size that fits the palm and fingers without excessive tension. A glove that is too small may split or cause hand fatigue, while a loose glove may reduce tactile control. Check the supplier's size chart and confirm whether Small, Medium, Large or Extra Large options are available.

For a workplace team, maintain size-specific stock so workers are not forced to use an unsuitable fit. Inspect gloves for tears, pinholes, discolouration or damaged packaging before use.

How to use

Examination gloves are generally intended for single use unless the manufacturer explicitly states otherwise. Change gloves between tasks, between individuals and whenever they become torn, heavily contaminated or otherwise compromised. Do not wash or disinfect disposable gloves for reuse.

Remove gloves carefully to avoid touching the contaminated outer surface with bare skin, then wash or sanitise hands. Dispose of used gloves according to the workplace's hygiene and waste-management procedure.
