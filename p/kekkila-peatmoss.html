Choose peat moss for the job you have planned

Kekkila Peatmoss is listed for plant propagation, seed starting and use in potting mixes. Peat moss is valued for its ability to hold moisture and contribute to a lighter growing medium. Before ordering, decide whether you need it for starting seeds, preparing a potting blend or another gardening project, and check that the product instructions match that purpose.

Moisture, storage and responsible use

Peat moss can become difficult to wet evenly if it dries out completely. Follow the manufacturer's directions for preparing and moistening it, and mix it thoroughly with the other ingredients specified for your growing project. Avoid compacting the medium; roots need both moisture and air around them.

Keep the bag closed and store it in a dry, sheltered place so rain and dirt do not contaminate the contents. Use only the amount required for the project and keep the packaging information for reference. Peat is a natural resource formed over long periods, so consider whether a suitable lower-peat or peat-free growing medium is available for your plants and local conditions.
