KEY FEATURES • Ready-to-use rodent bait • For rats and mice • Best used inside bait stations • 25 g pack for homes, small stores and farms • Delivered to all 47 counties SPECIFICATIONS • Product: Bromarat 0.005 RB • Use: Rats and mice • Pack size: 25 g WHY BAIT STATIONS ARE BEST A bait station is a small covered box or tube that holds the bait. It keeps the bait dry and clean, makes it harder for children, pets and livestock to reach it, and gives rodents a dark, sheltered place where they feel safe to feed. A simple station can be made from a sturdy covered container placed against a wall, as long as the bait is kept out of reach of other animals. HOW TO USE IT Place bait stations along walls, near holes and burrows, behind cupboards and stores, and on rodent runs where you see droppings and gnaw marks. For a home or a small shop, a few stations are usually enough. Check them regularly, top up the bait that has been eaten and replace bait that is wet or mouldy. Continue until no more bait is taken, then remove stations and leftover bait as the label directs. FOR HOMES AND SMALL PREMISES Rodents come into homes looking for food and shelter. Store food and grain in closed containers, keep rubbish bins covered, block holes around pipes and doors, and keep the area outside the house clear of heaps and long grass. SAFETY Check the PCPB registration number on the label, read the label before use, and keep away from children, pets and livestock. Do not place bait where food, feed or water can be contaminated. Wear gloves when handling bait or stations, and wash your hands afterwards. Collect dead rodents regularly and dispose of them safely. If anyone swallows the bait, get medical help at once and show the label. For animals, contact a vet.
