About TVS HLX 125/150 Choke Cable N5170130

Product overview

TVS HLX 125/150 choke cable N5170130 is a replacement control cable for the choke mechanism on compatible carburettor-equipped motorcycles. It connects the rider-operated choke control to the carburettor enrichment mechanism, allowing the rider to select the starting setting when the engine is cold. This listing is intended for buyers checking the N5170130 reference for a TVS HLX 125 or 150 application.
