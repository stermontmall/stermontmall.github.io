Product Overview & Key Benefits

The APC Easy UPS BV 650VA, AVR, Universal Outlet, 230V is a line-interactive uninterruptible power supply intended to provide battery-backed power and voltage regulation for compatible electronic equipment. It is a practical option for a home office, small office desk, router-and-computer corner or workstation where a short interruption can interrupt work or cause equipment to shut down unexpectedly. The supplier lists a 650VA capacity and 375W rated output, four universal receptacles, a replaceable lead-acid battery and automatic voltage regulation. A UPS is not a substitute for a generator or a long-duration power station. Its purpose is to bridge short interruptions and help connected equipment respond more safely to changes in mains supply. The time available on battery depends on the load connected, battery condition and other operating conditions. SPECIFICATIONS

Before you start

SAFETY

Keep the UPS dry, upright and away from direct heat, moisture and blocked ventilation openings. Do not open the casing or attempt internal repairs unless qualified and authorised. Lead-acid batteries can deliver high fault current and should be handled according to the manual and local battery-recycling requirements.

Never short battery terminals or dispose of the battery with ordinary household waste. Do not overload the unit, cover its vents or connect it to a damaged mains socket. Important: the supplier and manufacturer pages disagree on input plug type and cable length.
