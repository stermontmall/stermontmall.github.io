About Promax 20EC Insecticide – 1 Litre

Bed bugs (kunguni) hide in mattress seams, bed frames and skirting boards, then bite while you sleep. Promax 20EC is a propoxur-based, odourless insecticide concentrate that you dilute and spray straight into those hiding places. It also tackles cockroaches (mende), fleas, ants and houseflies, so one 1 litre bottle covers a house, hostel, hotel room or office.

Key features

  • Odourless formulation suited to bedrooms and other indoor spaces (let surfaces dry fully)
  • Residual action on treated surfaces, so pests that crawl across later are still affected
  • Multi-pest: bed bugs, cockroaches, fleas, ants and houseflies
  • Concentrated: 25 ml makes 1 litre of ready-to-spray liquid
  • Works with any hand-pump or knapsack sprayer

Specifications

  • Product: Promax 20EC Insecticide
  • Active ingredient: Propoxur 20% w/v (carbamate insecticide)
  • Formulation: Emulsifiable concentrate (EC)
  • Use: Household and public-health crawling-insect control
  • Mode of action: blocks nerve signal transmission, causing paralysis
  • Pack size: 1 litre

Where it helps

Most homes need only a small amount of spray, so a concentrate goes a long way: one 1 litre bottle makes 40 litres of ready-to-spray liquid. Bed bugs, cockroaches, fleas, ants and houseflies are the main targets, and the residual film means that pests which crawl across a dry surface later are still affected.

Mixing rates (from the product label)

  • 1 litre hand sprayer: 25 ml Promax 20EC, topped up with water to 1 litre
  • 5 litre garden sprayer: 125 ml, topped up to 5 litres
  • 20 litre knapsack: 500 ml, topped up to 20 litres
  • Most homes need only 1 to 5 litres; the knapsack rate is for hotels, hostels and large premises
  • Always confirm the rate on your pack's label before mixing

How to use it

Half-fill the sprayer with clean water, add Promax, top up and shake well. Spray mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, skirting boards, cracks, behind sockets and furniture joints. Wet surfaces lightly; do not soak them. Bed bug eggs can hatch later, so inspect after about two weeks and treat again if needed.

Before you start

  • Keep children, pets and fish tanks out of the room until surfaces are dry and the room is ventilated
  • Do not spray food, utensils or bedding you will use before it dries

Safety

Wear gloves and wash your hands after use. Read the label before you begin, and keep the bottle out of reach of children.

Storage

Keep the bottle closed in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food.
