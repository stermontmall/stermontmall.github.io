How to Get Better Results

Clean and declutter before spraying, and move food, utensils and children's items out of the area. Spray cracks and crevices, behind cookers and fridges, under sinks, along skirting boards, on bed frames and in corners where insects hide. Wash bedding and treat mattress seams and bed joints for bedbugs, and repeat treatment as the label advises. Seal gaps, fix leaks and keep food stored in closed containers.
