Greenlife Bazooka 18EC Insecticide and Miticide (1 Litre) Greenlife Bazooka 18EC contains abamectin 18 g/L and is a broad-spectrum miticide and insecticide. It is used to control spider mites and leaf miners on French beans, tomatoes and other crops. It is an emulsifiable concentrate (EC) in a 1 litre pack. KEY FEATURES • Abamectin 18 g/L in an emulsifiable concentrate • Broad-spectrum miticide and insecticide • Controls spider mites and leaf miners • For French beans, tomatoes and other crops • 1 litre pack • Greenlife brand PESTS IT CONTROLS Spider mites are tiny sap feeders that live on the underside of leaves. They cause fine pale speckling and, in heavy attacks, webbing, leaf drop and poor yields. They multiply fastest in hot, dry weather. Leaf miners are small fly larvae that tunnel inside leaves, leaving winding white trails that reduce the plant's ability to photosynthesise and lower crop quality. HOW IT PROTECTS YOUR CROP Abamectin is taken in by the pest through contact and feeding, and it paralyses it so that it stops feeding. Many abamectin products also move into the leaf tissue, which helps reach pests that feed inside or under leaves. Good spray coverage on both sides of the leaf is important. HOW TO USE IT Follow the dose, water volume, spray interval and maximum number of applications on the label. Scout regularly, checking the underside of leaves for mites and mines, and begin spraying when pests first show up. Mix by half-filling the tank with water, adding the measured product, topping up and agitating. Spray in calm weather, early morning or late afternoon. RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT Mites can become resistant quickly if the same product is used repeatedly. Rotate with products from other chemical groups and keep to the number of sprays on the label. SAFETY Read the label before use and keep out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying. Follow the pre-harvest interval on the label, which is especially important for French beans and tomatoes picked often. Keep spray and rinse water out of streams, ponds and fish, and avoid spraying where bees are working. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
