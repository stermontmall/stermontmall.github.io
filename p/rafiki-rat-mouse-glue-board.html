About Rafiki Rat and Mouse Glue Board

The Rafiki Rat and Mouse Glue Board is a foldable adhesive board intended for rodent monitoring and capture in selected indoor situations. It provides a non-bait alternative to rodenticide and can help identify activity in stores, offices and other suitable premises. Glue boards are not appropriate for every site or every situation, and their use must take account of animal-welfare requirements, children, pets and non-target wildlife. Check local guidance and the product instructions before deciding whether an adhesive board is suitable.

How an adhesive Board works

The board uses a sticky surface to hold a rodent that contacts it. Its foldable design can help form a shallow tray or protected placement shape, depending on the product instructions. A glue board does not attract rodents from long distances and should not be expected to solve an infestation by itself. Placement needs to be based on signs of activity and an understanding of rodent movement. Use the board only in a way that complies with the supplier's directions and applicable rules.

Assess the site first

Look for droppings, gnaw marks, rub marks, tracks, damaged food packaging and other evidence before placing monitoring equipment. Identify likely routes along walls and behind equipment, while considering whether the location can be checked frequently. Do not put a glue board where children, pets, birds or other non-target animals can reach it. Avoid damp, dusty or high-traffic areas where the adhesive may become ineffective or the board may be disturbed. Do not place it near food, utensils or food-contact surfaces.

Inspection is essential

A glue board must be checked frequently and should never be left unattended for extended periods. Establish an inspection schedule appropriate to the site and product instructions. If an animal is captured, follow the applicable animal-welfare and local handling requirements; do not improvise or leave it to suffer. If the board becomes dusty, wet, damaged or contaminated, replace it and dispose of it responsibly. Wear suitable protective equipment when handling used boards and wash hands after service.

When to consider an alternative

For sites with children, pets, wildlife or high non-target exposure risk, a secured mechanical trap or a professionally managed rodent programme may be more appropriate. In some situations, tamper-resistant bait stations or live-capture equipment may be selected after a risk assessment. No single device is suitable for every building. A pest-management professional can help compare options based on the rodent species, activity level, hygiene requirements and site users.

Reduce food and shelter

Rodent control should include practical prevention. Store food, animal feed and ingredients in sealed, rodent-resistant containers. Keep refuse bins closed and clean spills promptly. Remove clutter and nesting material, repair leaks and close suitable gaps around doors, pipes and wall penetrations. Check delivery areas and storerooms regularly, because new stock and packaging can conceal signs of rodent activity. Continue monitoring after the immediate problem appears to have reduced.

Product details to confirm

This listing is for one Rafiki Rat and Mouse Glue Board. Confirm the board dimensions, fold configuration, adhesive type and any stated service or disposal instructions from the current pack. Do not assume that the board is suitable for outdoor use or for every rodent size. Keep unused boards in their packaging and store them away from heat, dust, children and pets.
