Hose dimensions and connections

Before ordering a braided water hose, confirm the roll length, internal diameter, wall construction and rated working pressure with the supplier. These details determine flow, reach and compatibility with taps, pumps, sprayers or irrigation fittings. Use clamps and connectors sized for the hose rather than forcing an incompatible fitting. Avoid sharp bends, dragging across abrasive edges or leaving the hose under pressure when not in use. Drain and store it away from direct damage and excessive heat. Inspect for bulges, cracks, leaks and worn ends before connecting it to a pump or pressurised supply.
