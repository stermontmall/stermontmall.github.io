### Why External Parasite Control Matters

Product details

Ticks, mites and fleas feed on the blood and skin of birds. Infested chickens become restless, stop laying, lose weight, develop rough feathers and scaly legs, and can become anaemic. Heavy infestations may even kill young birds.
