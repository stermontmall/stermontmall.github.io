  • Why External Parasite Control Matters Ticks, mites and fleas feed on the blood and skin of birds. Infested chickens become restless, stop laying, lose weight, develop rough feathers and scaly legs, and can become anaemic. Heavy infestations may even kill young birds. These parasites also hide in cracks in poultry houses, perches and nest boxes, so treating both the birds and their housing gives the best results.
