Product overview

The Non-Return Teat Dip Cup is a dairy hygiene accessory used to apply teat-dip solution during a cow-milking routine. Teat dipping is commonly included in udder hygiene programmes before or after milking, depending on the product label and the farm’s veterinary or dairy-management protocol.

Care, cleaning & storage

A non-return design is intended to reduce the chance that used solution flows back into the clean reservoir, helping keep the unused liquid separate from the portion applied to the teat. The reference product image shows a teat dip cup with a translucent reservoir and a green upper applicator. Apply the solution evenly as directed, ensuring the teat is treated without contaminating the reservoir. If the design has a non-return valve, keep it clean and check that it is functioning properly. Do not pour used solution back into the original chemical container or clean reservoir. After the milking routine, empty or retain the remaining solution only as directed by the teat-dip manufacturer. Wash the cup using a method compatible with its material and the chemical used. Rinse thoroughly if the manufacturer’s instructions allow, then allow it to dry in a clean protected place. However, it does not diagnose or treat mastitis, and it cannot replace clean bedding, machine hygiene, correct milking technique, regular udder checks or veterinary intervention. Farmers should contact a veterinarian when cows show abnormal milk, udder heat, swelling, pain or other signs of disease. Keep a written milking hygiene routine near the parlour so that every operator follows the same steps. Replace a damaged valve promptly; a valve that allows used liquid to flow back into the reservoir defeats the main hygiene advantage of a non-return design.

Features & specifications

The exact capacity, valve arrangement, material and supplied colour may vary by model. Confirm the cup’s capacity and compatibility with the teat-dip product you use before ordering. Keep the cup assigned to its intended use, clean it regularly and follow the product label and farm hygiene protocol to support safe dairy handling. The pictured reference model is a non-return type, but customers should still confirm the supplied design and reservoir capacity before ordering.

How to use & application

The pictured model is a non-return style; it should not be assumed to have the same features as a simple open dipping cup or spray applicator. Use the cup only with a teat-dip product whose label permits the intended application method. Follow the chemical manufacturer’s directions for dilution, contact time, storage, handling and any precautions. Never mix products or dilute them by guesswork. Before milking, prepare the cup according to the product instructions and inspect it for cracks, leaks, blocked tubes or a sticking valve. Fill only to the recommended level.

Safety information

Wear the protective equipment specified on the label and keep teat-dip chemicals away from children, feed and drinking water. A dip cup is an application tool, not a disinfectant by itself; its effectiveness depends on the solution and correct farm procedure. Avoid harsh solvents or abrasive brushes that could damage the valve or reservoir. Replace the cup if it becomes cracked, brittle, persistently contaminated or unable to dispense solution correctly. A cup that is dedicated to one teat-dip product can make routine work easier and reduce accidental mixing between chemicals. Label the cup if more than one solution is used on the farm, and never refill it from an unlabelled container.

Uses & applications

A non-return teat dip cup can be useful for smallholder dairy units, zero-grazing farms, commercial milking sheds and dairy training institutions. It supports a repeatable hygiene routine and helps staff use a dedicated applicator rather than improvised containers.
