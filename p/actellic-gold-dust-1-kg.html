About Actellic Gold Dust

Actellic Gold Dust is a ready-to-use dust for protecting stored grain from insect damage. It combines pirimiphos-methyl (16 g/kg) and thiamethoxam (3.6 g/kg) in a dustable powder, and it is registered in Kenya by the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB(CR) 1149). The 100 g, 500 g and 1 kg packs suit households and small farms storing maize and other cereals.

Key features

  • Ready to use, with no mixing in water
  • Two active ingredients working together against stored-grain pests
  • Registered in Kenya by the Pest Control Products Board
  • Three pack sizes: 100 g, 500 g and 1 kg
  • Suits household and small farm storage
  • Delivered to all 47 counties

  • Registration: PCPB(CR) 1149
  • Pack sizes: 100 g, 500 g, 1 kg

Where it helps

The catalog lists the larger grain borer, grain weevil, red flour beetle, lesser grain borer and other stored-grain beetles and moths. These pests can ruin a harvest in a few months, hollowing out kernels, leaving dust and spoiling grain for food, seed and sale. A dust that goes in as the grain is stored keeps working inside the bag.

Rate from the pack

  • About 50 g per 90 kg of grain, the size of a standard bag, as printed on the pack
  • Mix the dust evenly through the grain as it goes into storage
  • Do not use more than the label directs

How to use it

Mix the dust evenly through the grain as it goes into storage. At the pack rate of about 50 g per 90 kg bag, a 100 g pack treats about two bags, a 500 g pack about ten and a 1 kg pack about twenty. Even mixing matters, because pests survive in untreated pockets, so turn the grain until the dust is spread through every bag. Follow the label exactly.

Before you start

  • Clean and dry the store, and use dry, clean bags
  • Remove old grain, sweep out dust and fix cracks where insects can hide
  • Make sure the grain is properly dry before storing, because damp grain spoils even when treated

Safety

Avoid breathing the dust, and wear a mask and gloves when mixing. Keep it away from children, pets and food, and wash your hands afterwards. Follow the label's waiting period before using treated grain, and do not rinse containers into water sources.

Storage

Keep the pack closed in a cool, dry place out of reach of children.
