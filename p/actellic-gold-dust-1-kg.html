Actellic Gold Dust 1 kg Grain Protectant Actellic Gold Dust is a ready-to-use dust for protecting stored grain from insect damage. It combines pirimiphos-methyl (16 g/kg) and thiamethoxam (3.6 g/kg) as a dustable powder, and it is registered in Kenya by the Pest Control Products Board (PCPB(CR) 1149). The 1 kg pack suits farmers and households storing maize and other cereals. WHY FARMERS CHOOSE ACTELLIC GOLD DUST • Ready to use, with no mixing in water • Two active ingredients working together against stored-grain pests • Registered in Kenya by the Pest Control Products Board • 1 kg pack that suits household and small farm storage • Delivered to all 47 counties WHAT IT CONTROLS Larger grain borer, grain weevil, red flour beetle, lesser grain borer and other stored-grain beetles and moths. These pests can ruin a harvest in a few months, hollowing out kernels, leaving dust and spoiling grain for food, seed and sale. PREPARE BEFORE YOU STORE Clean and dry the store and use dry, clean bags. Remove old grain, sweep out dust and fix cracks where insects can hide. Make sure grain is properly dry before storing, because damp grain spoils even when treated. HOW TO USE IT Mix the dust evenly through the grain as it goes into storage. The usual rate on the pack is about 50 g per 90 kg of grain, the size of a standard bag. Follow the label exactly and do not use more than directed. Even mixing matters, because pests survive in untreated pockets. SAFETY Avoid breathing the dust and wear a mask and gloves when mixing. Keep it away from children, pets and food, and wash your hands afterwards. Follow the label's waiting period before using treated grain, and do not rinse containers into water sources. STORAGE Keep the pack closed in a cool, dry place out of reach of children.
