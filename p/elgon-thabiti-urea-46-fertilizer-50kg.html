Wear the protective clothing and gloves specified by the manufacturer; avoid contact with eyes and skin and avoid breathing dust, mist or vapour. Do not exceed the recommended application rate or mix with other products unless the label permits it.

Keep in the original closed container, away from children, food, drink, animal feed and water sources. Product overview

Elgon Thabiti Urea 46% is a granular nitrogen fertiliser supplied in a 50kg bag for crop nutrition programmes that require a concentrated source of nitrogen. Nitrogen supports chlorophyll formation, leaf development and the production of proteins needed for plant growth. The product is used by farmers growing maize, vegetables, sugarcane, pasture and other crops with a significant nitrogen requirement.

It is commonly considered for top-dressing after crop establishment, when plants are actively developing stems and leaves. The stated analysis is 46% nitrogen, making urea a concentrated fertiliser. That concentration is useful when a farmer needs to supply nitrogen without transporting a much larger quantity of a lower-analysis product.

It also means correct handling and application matter. The appropriate amount depends on crop type, soil condition, previous fertiliser use, expected yield, rainfall and the stage of growth. A fertiliser plan should be based on soil testing or reliable local agronomic recommendations rather than a single rate applied to every field.

For maize, nitrogen is important during vegetative growth, but the crop's total requirement is not normally supplied in one careless application. Farmers often split nitrogen applications so nutrients are available as the crop develops. The timing and quantity should follow the local crop recommendation and the fertiliser label.

Applying too early, too late or in excessive amounts can reduce nutrient-use efficiency, increase losses or damage plants. Where a field has uneven soil fertility, consider field-specific management instead of applying one uniform rate across the entire farm. Urea can be applied by broadcasting or by placing it near the crop as a side-dressing, where the recommended practice allows.

When side-dressing, avoid direct contact with stems, leaves and exposed roots. Fertiliser granules concentrated against plant tissue can cause injury. Where the product is used in a fertigation system, first confirm that the system and crop programme are suitable for urea and follow the agronomist's instructions.

Do not assume that every irrigation setup can safely handle every fertiliser. Moisture affects how urea behaves after application. In suitable conditions, it dissolves and the nitrogen becomes available to the crop.

If it remains on a hot, dry soil surface for an extended period, nitrogen losses may occur. Application timing should therefore take account of rainfall forecasts, soil moisture and irrigation plans. Avoid applying immediately before heavy runoff or on saturated ground where nutrients may be washed away.

Where rainfall is uncertain, seek local advice on placement and incorporation. Elgon Thabiti Urea should be stored in a dry, cool, well-ventilated place, protected from rain and ground moisture. Keep the bag closed when not in use and store it on a raised pallet rather than directly on a damp floor.

Do not store it with food, animal feed, seed or household items. Keep it away from children, animals and unauthorised handlers. Use gloves and avoid inhaling dust when handling fertiliser.

Wash hands after use and follow the safety information on the bag. A 50kg pack is suitable for farms that need a substantial quantity for field application or that share inputs across several plots. Before buying, estimate the area to be treated and check the recommendation for each crop.

For small kitchen gardens or limited plots, a smaller pack may be easier to handle and store. Keep a written note of the date, field, crop stage and quantity applied. These records help avoid double application and make it easier to assess crop response.

Nitrogen fertiliser should be part of balanced crop nutrition, not a substitute for all other nutrients. Phosphorus, potassium, sulphur and micronutrients may also be needed depending on soil and crop. A crop that receives excess nitrogen but lacks other nutrients may not reach its potential.

Good results also depend on suitable seed, weed control, water management, timely planting and protection from pests and diseases. Elgon Thabiti Urea 46% Fertilizer is intended for farmers who need a concentrated nitrogen source for top-dressing and related crop nutrition uses. Apply it only at recommended rates and stages, protect it from moisture during storage, and keep it away from plant tissue unless the approved method specifies otherwise.

For exact application rates, consult the current product label, soil-test results and a qualified local agronomist. When comparing fertilisers, read the nutrient analysis rather than relying only on the brand or bag size. Urea supplies nitrogen, while other fertilisers may provide phosphorus, potassium or additional nutrients.

A crop plan should match the product to the field's actual requirement. Keep the original bag label available so workers can confirm the product name and analysis before application. Never mix fertilisers or crop-protection products unless the labels or a qualified agronomist confirm that the combination is suitable.

During field work, use a calibrated scoop or weighing method so that the intended quantity is applied consistently. Avoid leaving open bags in the rain or transferring fertiliser into unlabelled containers. If a bag becomes wet or the granules cake, follow supplier guidance before use.

Keep fertiliser away from wells, streams, drainage channels and animal watering points. Responsible handling helps protect workers, crops and the surrounding environment while supporting a more predictable crop nutrition programme.
