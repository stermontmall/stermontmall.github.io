Premise 200 SC Termiticide (50 ml, 1 litre) Termites (mchwa) rarely announce themselves. They eat door frames, roof timbers and cupboards from the inside until the damage is expensive. Bayer Premise 200 SC is a non-repellent, odourless termiticide with imidacloprid 200 g/L. Where & How to Use in Kenya

It suits a builder treating hardcore and soil before the slab is cast, a homeowner treating the foundation lines of an existing house, and anyone dealing with a termite mound or wall void. MIXING RATES (FROM THE PRODUCT LABEL)

  • Dilution: 50 ml Premise 200 SC per 20 L knapsack of water (0.25%)
  • Application volume: 5 L of mixed solution per m² of soil, or per linear metre of trench along foundations
  • One knapsack covers about 4 m² or 4 linear metres
  • A 1 litre bottle mixes 20 knapsacks (400 L), enough for about 80 m² or 80 linear metres

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Harmful. Wear gloves, overalls, boots and a mask when mixing and spraying. It is toxic to bees and aquatic life, so never let spray or wash water reach streams, ponds, wells or drains.

Store the unused product in its original, closed pack in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food, feed and children. This is a pest-control or agricultural chemical product. Use it only for the crop, pest, site and application method expressly authorised on the current Kenya-approved product label.

Read the full label before opening or use. Follow the label exactly for personal protective equipment, dilution, application, ventilation, re-entry period, pre-harvest interval, environmental restrictions and any other precautions. Do not guess a dose, mix it with another product, or use it on food, animals, people or surfaces unless the label specifically permits that use.

Keep the original container tightly closed and locked away from children, unauthorised persons and animals, and separate from food, drink and animal feed. Prevent spills and contamination of drains, wells, streams and other water sources. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling; wash hands and exposed skin after handling.

Never reuse an empty pesticide container; follow the label and approved local hazardous-waste collection or disposal instructions. In case of suspected poisoning or significant exposure, seek urgent medical attention and show the product label. Frequently Asked Questions

How does Premise 200 SC kill the whole colony?
It is non-repellent, so termites cross the treated soil without detecting it. They carry the active ingredient back and spread it through the colony.

How much does one knapsack of Premise cover?
Mixed at 50 ml per 20 L and applied at 5 L per m², one knapsack treats about 4 m² of soil or 4 linear metres of trench. Confirm the rates on your label.

Which pack should I choose, 50 ml or 1 litre?
The 50 ml pack makes one 20 L knapsack, which suits a small job. The 1 litre bottle makes twenty, which suits a house or a building site.

How to use

Shake the bottle vigorously, half fill the knapsack with clean water, add the measured Premise, top up to 20 L and stir. Pre-construction: treat the hardcore and soil before the slab is cast. Trenches: apply along the inside and outside of the foundation lines.

Existing buildings: drill and inject along foundations and slab joints, and for a mound, drill into the nest and drench it thoroughly. Do not apply in rain or when rain is expected, and wash all equipment after use. Check the rates against the label on your pack.

Before you start

  • Fix leaks and remove old stumps and buried timber near the building
  • Keep treated soil away from wells, boreholes and drains
  • For a large building, consider a licensed pest control operator
