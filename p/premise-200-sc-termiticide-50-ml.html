Premise 200 SC Termiticide (50 ml, 1 litre) Termites (mchwa) rarely announce themselves. They eat door frames, roof timbers and cupboards from the inside until the damage is expensive. Bayer Premise 200 SC is a non-repellent, odourless termiticide with imidacloprid 200 g/L. Termites walk through the treated soil without detecting it, carry the active ingredient back and spread it through the colony.

Uses & applications

It suits a builder treating hardcore and soil before the slab is cast, a homeowner treating the foundation lines of an existing house, and anyone dealing with a termite mound or wall void. MIXING RATES (FROM THE PRODUCT LABEL)

  • Dilution: 50 ml Premise 200 SC per 20 L knapsack of water (0.25%)
  • Application volume: 5 L of mixed solution per m² of soil, or per linear metre of trench along foundations
  • One knapsack covers about 4 m² or 4 linear metres
  • A 1 litre bottle mixes 20 knapsacks (400 L), enough for about 80 m² or 80 linear metres

How to use

Shake the bottle vigorously, half fill the knapsack with clean water, add the measured Premise, top up to 20 L and stir. Pre-construction: treat the hardcore and soil before the slab is cast. Trenches: apply along the inside and outside of the foundation lines. Existing buildings: drill and inject along foundations and slab joints, and for a mound, drill into the nest and drench it thoroughly. Do not apply in rain or when rain is expected, and wash all equipment after use. Check the rates against the label on your pack.

Before you start

  • Fix leaks and remove old stumps and buried timber near the building
  • Keep treated soil away from wells, boreholes and drains
  • For a large building, consider a licensed pest control operator

Safety information

Harmful. Wear gloves, overalls, boots and a mask when mixing and spraying. It is toxic to bees and aquatic life, so never let spray or wash water reach streams, ponds, wells or drains.

Storage & handling

Store the unused product in its original, closed pack in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food, feed and children.
