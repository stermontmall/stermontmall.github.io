About Ampligo 150ZC Insecticide

Ampligo 150ZC Insecticide (100 ml)

Fall armyworm (viwavi jeshi) in the maize whorl and Tuta absoluta in tomatoes can finish a crop before you notice. Syngenta Ampligo 150 ZC combines two modes of action in one bottle: chlorantraniliprole stops caterpillars feeding almost at once, and lambda-cyhalothrin gives fast knockdown. It is registered in Kenya for maize, tomatoes, cabbage, French beans and snow peas.

Key benefits

  • Two active ingredients from different chemical groups, so it works well in resistance-management rotations
  • Feeding stops quickly, so the crop stops being damaged
  • Long-lasting protection that holds up in rain
  • Controls caterpillars, aphids, thrips and whitefly nymphs on the crops listed on the label
  • Very low rates: 6 to 12 ml per 20 L knapsack

Technical specs

  • Active ingredients: Chlorantraniliprole 100 g/L + Lambda-cyhalothrin 50 g/L
  • Formulation: Mixed capsule suspension and suspension concentrate (ZC)
  • WHO Class II (moderately hazardous), PCPB(CR)1702
  • Shelf life: 2 years in the original sealed container
  • Pack: 100 ml

MIXING AND APPLICATION DOSAGE (per 20 L knapsack)

  • Maize (fall armyworm): 10 to 12 ml (350 ml/ha in heavy infestation). Harvest wait: 14 days
  • French beans (aphids, thrips, whiteflies): 6 to 8 ml. Harvest wait: 7 days
  • Cabbage (diamondback moth, aphids): 12 ml. Harvest wait: 3 days
  • Snow peas (aphids, caterpillars, thrips): 12 ml. Harvest wait: 7 days
  • Tomatoes (Tuta absoluta, aphids, whitefly nymphs, thrips): 6 ml. Harvest wait: 3 days

Spray timing: start at early infestation. Maize: repeat after 7 days, maximum 2 sprays per season. Other crops: repeat after 10 days, maximum 3 sprays per season.

A 100 ml pack mixes about 8 to 16 knapsacks, depending on the crop rate.

1. Fill the knapsack 1/10 to 1/4 full with clean water and add the measured Ampligo.

2. Agitate while topping up, shake well before spraying and keep agitating while you spray.

3. Cover the maize whorl and the undersides of leaves. Do not spray in the hottest hours, on wet foliage, or when rain is expected within 2 hours.

Safety

Toxic to bees and fish. Do not spray flowering crops or near water. Wear gloves, overalls, a face mask and gumboots, and triple-rinse empty containers.

Safe use of Original Ampligo 150ZC Insecticide

  • Read the whole label before you buy or mix. The label is the legal instruction for crops, pests, rates and safety.
  • Look for the PCPB registration number on the pack. Do not buy chemicals that carry none.
  • Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI) printed on the label, the number of days that must pass between the last spray and harvest. Use the product only on the crops the label lists.
  • Wear gloves, a mask or respirator, eye protection and long protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards.
  • Keep chemicals in their original container, out of reach of children, pets and livestock. Never reuse empty containers for food or water.
