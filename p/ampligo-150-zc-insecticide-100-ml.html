Syngenta Ampligo 150ZC Insecticide (100 ml) Syngenta Ampligo 150ZC is a dual-action insecticide that combines chlorantraniliprole 100 g/L and lambda-cyhalothrin 50 g/L. It is used to control fall armyworm, bollworm, diamondback moth, cutworms, thrips, aphids and whiteflies. It is sold in a 100 ml pack and is classified WHO Class II. KEY FEATURES • Two active ingredients from different insecticide groups in one product • Chlorantraniliprole 100 g/L plus lambda-cyhalothrin 50 g/L • Targets caterpillars as well as sucking pests • Controls fall armyworm, bollworm, diamondback moth, cutworms, thrips, aphids and whiteflies • 100 ml pack • Syngenta brand TWO MODES OF ACTION Chlorantraniliprole is mainly used against caterpillars, which stop feeding soon after they take it in. Lambda-cyhalothrin is a pyrethroid that works on contact and ingestion and also helps against sucking insects such as aphids, thrips and whiteflies. Combining the two gives wider coverage when several pests attack the same crop, and using two modes of action supports resistance management. PESTS IT COVERS • Caterpillars: fall armyworm, bollworm, diamondback moth and cutworms • Sucking pests: thrips, aphids and whiteflies HOW TO USE IT Follow the dose, water volume, timing and maximum number of applications on the label. Scout your crop regularly and begin spraying when pests first appear, because small larvae are easier to control. Spray in calm weather, early morning or late afternoon, and make sure the spray reaches where pests feed. SAFETY This product is WHO Class II, so handle it with care. Read the label before use. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Lambda-cyhalothrin is harmful to bees and fish, so do not spray open flowers where bees are active and keep spray and rinse water out of streams and ponds. Follow the pre-harvest interval on the label. Keep out of reach of children and pets. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
