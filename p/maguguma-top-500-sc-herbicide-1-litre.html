Product overview

Maguguma Top 500 SC Herbicide (1 Litre) Maguguma Top 500 SC is a pre-emergence herbicide for maize that stops broadleaf weeds and grasses from germinating. It can also be used early post-emergence on newly germinated weeds, giving maize a clean start and cutting hand-weeding rounds. KEY

Product information

& FEATURES

  • Active ingredient: Metolachlor 300 g/L + Atrazine 200 g/L
  • Target: Broadleaf weeds and grasses in maize
  • Formulation: Suspension concentrate (SC)
  • Application settings: Agricultural: maize fields (pre-emergence, or early post-emergence on newly germinated weeds) MIXING & APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS 1. Dilution rate: use the dose and water volume printed on the manufacturer's label for your crop and growth stage. 2. Application method: Shake the container well, half-fill the tank with water, add the measured product, top up and agitate throughout. Spray evenly on a fine, moist, clod-free seedbed. 3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Available in 1 Litre. 4. Keep spray off neighbouring broadleaf crops such as beans and vegetables, and check the label for rotation restrictions before the next crop.

Product information

& RE-ENTRY PRECAUTIONS

  • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.
