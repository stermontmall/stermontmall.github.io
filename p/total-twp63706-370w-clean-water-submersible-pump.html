Where & How to Use in Kenya

This pump may be considered for drawing clean water from a suitable tank or well, moving water around a garden, supporting small irrigation tasks or draining clean water from a collection area. The supplier's listing does not provide a flow curve, so it is not possible to promise a particular number of litres per hour at a particular height. For a household or farm installation, calculate the required daily water volume and ask the seller whether the pump's performance matches the intended use.

It is not a replacement for a high-head borehole pump or a dirty-water sewage pump. CHOOSING THE RIGHT SETUP Before ordering, identify the water source and the vertical distance to the delivery point. Check the well or tank dimensions and confirm that the pump can fit through the access opening.

Ask for the pump diameter, cable length, outlet size and maximum submergence depth. Determine whether the pump will fill a storage tank, supply a short irrigation line or drain a shallow area. The head and flow requirements for each task are different.

A pump that works well for draining a shallow tank may not deliver enough water through a long pipe to an elevated tank. WATER QUALITY The supplier lists this as a clean-water pump. Use it only with water within the manufacturer's permitted quality range.

Do not assume it can handle sewage, fibrous waste, large debris, abrasive sand or thick sediment. Where water contains visible particles, ask whether a suitable intake screen is permitted and how often it should be cleaned. If the pump is intended for a well, ensure that it is positioned clear of settled sediment and that the intake remains submerged during operation.

INSTALLATION AND

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Follow the manufacturer's instructions for immersion depth, electrical connection and operation. Use a suitable rope or support to lower and retrieve the pump; never lift it by its power cable. Keep electrical joints dry and protected.

A qualified electrician should check the supply, cable condition, earthing and protective device. Do not operate the pump dry or outside its permitted water conditions. Secure the discharge pipe so its weight does not strain the pump outlet.
