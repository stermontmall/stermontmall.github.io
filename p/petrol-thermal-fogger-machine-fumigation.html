Petrol Pulse Jet Thermal Fogger Machine for Fumigation A portable petrol thermal fogger for fumigation and pest control jobs that need a fine, far-reaching fog. It uses a pulse jet engine with a stainless steel frame, a shoulder-carried solution tank and a hand-held barrel, so one person can fog a store, a greenhouse, a poultry house or a compound without dragging a heavy unit. Key Features - Pulse jet engine, petrol powered - Fine fog with droplets under 30 microns - Spray output of up to 90 litres per hour - Manual start and 12 V electric start with a lithium battery - Stainless steel frame with a perforated heat shield around the barrel - Shoulder-carried tank with a hand-held barrel for easy movement Specifications Engine: pulse jet, petrol Fuel: petrol rated 90 or higher, no oil mixed in Fuel use: 1.2 to 1.5 litres per hour Solution tank: about 15 to 16 litres (sellers list it slightly differently, so confirm with us before ordering) Spray output: up to 90 litres per hour Droplet size: under 30 microns Weight: about 6.5 kg empty, about 13.5 kg packed Size: 1100 x 280 x 380 mm Working temperature: -10 to 35 degrees Celsius Where it helps Thermal foggers carry a liquid into every corner of a closed space, which is why they are used by pest control teams and farm managers for stores, warehouses, poultry and livestock houses, greenhouses and large rooms.

The fog reaches ceilings, cracks and gaps that a hand sprayer misses, and it covers a big area in a short time. How to use 1. Work outdoors or in a well ventilated area and put on gloves, a mask rated for the chemical and eye protection.

2. Fill the fuel tank with clean petrol only, and fill the solution tank with a product that is made for thermal fogging, mixed as its label directs. 3.

Start the engine, let it warm up, then open the solution valve and fog while walking backwards out of the room. 4. Close the space for the time on the product label before letting people or animals back in.

Before you start - The barrel gets very hot. Never touch it, and keep it away from fuel, dry grass, fabric and open flames. - Never refuel while the engine is hot or running.
