How to Use

Apply generously and evenly to a clean face and neck about 20 minutes before going out in the sun. Reapply regularly, at least every two hours, and after sweating, swimming or towelling dry. Using too little lowers the protection, so do not skimp. Sunscreen is only one part of sun safety, so also wear a hat or sunglasses and look for shade at midday.

Care and Storage

Keep the cap closed tightly and store the tube in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Do not leave it in a hot car. Check the expiry date and batch information printed on the tube before use.

Safety Tips

For external use only. Avoid contact with the eyes, and rinse with water if it happens. Stop using the product if irritation develops. Do not apply to broken or damaged skin. Keep out of reach of children, and keep babies and young children out of direct sun. Read the ingredients on the pack if you have allergies.
