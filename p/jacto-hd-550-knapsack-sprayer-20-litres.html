Where & How to Use in Kenya

A strong, well-built sprayer gives more even coverage, lasts through many seasons and wastes less product. The Jacto HD 550 is designed for repeated, regular use by commercial growers, farm workers and spraying contractors as well as home gardeners. The piston pump builds pressure with each stroke of the lever, which gives a steady spray.

The internal agitator stirs the mixture as you pump, which helps keep products that settle, such as suspensions and powders, evenly mixed. The reinforced hose is made to take pressure and daily handling, and the adjustable nozzle changes the spray pattern from fine coverage on leaves to a more directed spray.

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when spraying, and follow the product label. Do not spray in windy weather. Do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying.

Release the pressure before opening the lid, and keep the sprayer away from children. Rinse the tank, hose and nozzle with clean water after every use, clean the nozzle and filter regularly, and store the sprayer clean and dry, away from direct sun and children.
