Why choose a heavy-duty Sprayer

A sprayer is one of the most used tools on a farm. A strong, well-built model gives more even coverage, lasts through many seasons and wastes less product. The Jacto HD 550 is designed for repeated, regular use by commercial growers, farm workers and spraying contractors as well as home gardeners.

How the features help

  • Piston pump: builds pressure with each stroke of the lever, which gives a steady spray
  • Internal agitator: stirs the mixture inside the tank as you pump, which helps keep products that settle, such as suspensions and powders, evenly mixed
  • Reinforced hose: made to take pressure and daily handling
  • Adjustable nozzle: lets you change the spray pattern for different jobs, from fine coverage on leaves to a more directed spray
  • 20 litre tank: holds enough for a good stretch of spraying before you need to refill

What you can spray

Pesticides, herbicides and foliar fertilizers on crops, orchards, flower farms and gardens, and for pest control around homes, stores and compounds. For best results and to avoid contamination, use a separate sprayer for herbicides where possible.

How to use it

Fill the tank to the level you need, add the product at the label rate, close the lid and pump to build pressure. Spray evenly and walk at a steady pace. Calibrate your sprayer so that you know how much it delivers per area.

Care and maintenance

  • Rinse the tank, hose and nozzle with clean water after every use
  • Release the pressure before opening the lid
  • Clean the nozzle and filter regularly
  • Check seals and hose for leaks
  • Store clean and dry, away from direct sun and children

Safety

Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when spraying, and follow the product label. Do not spray in windy weather. Do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying.
