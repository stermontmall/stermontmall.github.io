About Compliant 560SC Fungicide (1 Litre)

Compliant 560SC Fungicide (1 Litre) Compliant 560SC combines azoxystrobin and chlorothalonil in one broad-spectrum fungicide. It controls powdery and downy mildew on roses, leaf spot, black leg and club root on cabbages, rusts and anthracnose on French beans, and coffee berry disease. KEY SPECIFICATIONS & FEATURES • Active ingredient: Azoxystrobin 60 g/L + Chlorothalonil 500 g/L • Target: Powdery and downy mildew on roses; leaf spot, black leg and club root on cabbages; rusts, anthracnose and angular leaf spot on French beans; coffee berry disease • Formulation: Suspension concentrate (SC) • Application settings: Agricultural: flower farms, vegetable and bean fields, and coffee MIXING & APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS 1. Dilution rate: use the dose and water volume printed on the manufacturer's label for your crop and growth stage. 2. Application method: Shake well, half-fill the tank with water, add the measured product, top up and keep agitating while you spray. Cover leaves, stems, pods and berries evenly. 3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Available in 1 Litre. 4. Start protection early, before disease is widespread, and repeat at the label interval while humid weather, heavy dew and frequent rain favour disease. 5. Rotate crops, use clean seed and healthy seedlings, and keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and drains: chlorothalonil and azoxystrobin can harm fish. SAFETY & RE-ENTRY PRECAUTIONS • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying. • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label. • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.
