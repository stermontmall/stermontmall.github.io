About Compliant 560SC Fungicide – 1 Litre

Greenlife Compliant 560SC Fungicide (1 Litre)

Greenlife Compliant 560SC combines azoxystrobin 60 g/L and chlorothalonil 500 g/L in one fungicide. It is used for powdery and downy mildew on roses, leaf spot, black leg and club root on cabbages, rusts, anthracnose and angular leaf spot on French beans, and coffee berry disease. It is a suspension concentrate (SC) supplied in a 1 litre pack.

Diseases and crops

  • Roses: powdery mildew and downy mildew
  • Cabbages: leaf spot, black leg and club root
  • French beans: rusts, anthracnose and angular leaf spot
  • Coffee: coffee berry disease

Two active ingredients

Azoxystrobin belongs to the strobilurin group, which protects leaves and has movement in the plant tissue. Chlorothalonil is a protectant that stays on the surface of the plant and works against a wide range of fungi. Using two active ingredients together gives broader coverage than a single one and supports resistance management.

How to use it

Follow the dose, water volume, spray interval and maximum number of applications on the label. Start protection early, before disease is widespread, and repeat at the interval on the label while conditions favour disease, such as humid weather, heavy dew and frequent rain. Shake well, half-fill the tank with water, add the measured product, top up and agitate throughout. Spray evenly to cover leaves, stems, pods and berries.

Good disease management

  • Rotate crops, especially for cabbages, to lower the build-up of soil-borne disease
  • Use clean seed and healthy seedlings
  • Remove and destroy infected plant material
  • Avoid watering late in the day and keep good airflow in the crop

Safety

Read the label before use and keep out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Follow the pre-harvest interval on the label for each crop. Chlorothalonil and azoxystrobin can be harmful to fish and aquatic life, so keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and drains.

Storage

Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
