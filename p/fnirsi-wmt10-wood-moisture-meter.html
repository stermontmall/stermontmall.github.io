Product Overview & Key Benefits

The FNIRSI WMT-10 is a non-destructive moisture meter for checking moisture levels in timber and selected building materials during property inspections. Its pinless sensing approach helps technicians scan surfaces without leaving probe holes, while the display provides a quick reading for comparison across inspected areas. Moisture readings can help identify conditions that may contribute to timber deterioration and attract wood-damaging pests, but the meter does not detect termites directly and cannot confirm an infestation.

Use it as one part of a wider inspection that includes visual checks, tapping and appropriate specialist tools. Verify material mode and calibration before recording readings. FNIRSI identifies the WMT-10 as an inductive, pinless instrument with a 2.

4-inch colour screen, 0. 1% display resolution and a maximum sensing depth of up to 17mm. The sensor area is listed as 40.

5 by 40. 5mm. Published ranges are 0–75% for hardwood and softwood, 0–25% for plaster wall and 0–43% for brick wall, with a stated measurement error of ±1.

5%. These are instrument specifications, not universal building moisture limits or a guarantee that every surface can be measured accurately. The instrument provides separate modes for hardwood, softwood, plaster wall and brick wall.

Select the mode that best matches the surface being tested before taking a reading. A timber setting should not be used as though it were a calibrated plaster-wall setting. Different species, density, coatings and construction assemblies can influence readings.

Compare like materials under similar conditions and consult the manufacturer's instructions when a surface is composite, painted, laminated or otherwise outside the listed measurement modes. Begin a building survey with a visual assessment of the timber, wall finish, plumbing routes, roof junctions, bathrooms, kitchens and other locations where moisture may accumulate. Use the meter to scan accessible areas and record the location, material mode and displayed value.

Take several readings across the inspection area rather than relying on one point. Repeat measurements around an area of concern to understand whether the condition is localised or spread across a wider surface. Dated photographs and written notes help explain why a location was tested.

The WMT-10 can help identify differences in moisture conditions in selected materials, but it does not locate a leak, identify the source of water or diagnose termite activity. A damp reading may relate to plumbing, roof leakage, condensation, ground moisture or another building issue. Termite confirmation requires other evidence, such as mud tubes, galleries, damaged timber or live insects.

Use the meter as supporting information in a building or pest inspection, not as a stand-alone verdict or substitute for a qualified specialist. The 2. 4-inch colour display is intended to make readings easier to view during field work.

FNIRSI lists sound and colour alarms, a temperature display, a built-in flashlight and the ability to save and review up to 30 sets of readings. The alarm thresholds should be understood in relation to the selected material mode. Stored readings can support a small inspection route, but larger jobs should also use a written report with room references, photographs and dated observations.

Do not interpret an alarm as an automatic instruction to treat a building. Pinless meters sense a zone beneath the sensor rather than a single point at the surface. The maximum depth is specified as up to 17mm, but actual response can vary with material, contact, surface flatness and construction.

Metal behind a wall, foil layers, dense coatings or uneven surfaces may affect the reading. Do not compare results from different modes as if they were directly equivalent. When a result has legal, structural or contractual importance, obtain confirmation using an appropriate reference method and follow the instrument manual.

Keep the sensor area clean and avoid scratching or striking it against masonry. FNIRSI lists a 1000mAh rechargeable battery and adjustable auto-power-off intervals of 5, 10 or 15 minutes. Charge using the supplied or manufacturer-approved cable and store the meter dry, away from extreme heat and impact.

Check the device's response and settings before beginning a site survey. Keep the manual and purchase details so the correct procedure can be followed if the firmware or measurement behaviour changes. The WMT-10 may support inspections in Kenyan homes, hotels, rental properties, schools, timber stores, warehouses and building-maintenance projects.

Damp areas near bathrooms, roofs and plumbing can be worth checking when investigating timber deterioration or conditions that may attract wood-damaging pests. For pest-control teams, moisture observations can strengthen a report when combined with visual evidence and building history. It does not replace a qualified building surveyor, plumber or termite specialist where those services are needed.
