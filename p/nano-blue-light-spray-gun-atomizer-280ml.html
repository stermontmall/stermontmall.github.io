About Nano Blue Light Spray Gun Atomizer 280 ml

A handheld, battery-powered spray gun that turns the liquid in its bottle into a fine mist, with a blue light that glows as it sprays. It is light, cordless once charged and simple to use, which makes it handy for quick misting jobs around the home, shop, salon or small office.

Key Features

  • Fine mist spray with a blue light
  • 280 ml transparent liquid bottle, so you can see how much is left
  • 1200 mAh rechargeable battery
  • One power button: press once to start, press again to stop
  • Lightweight at about 270 g, comfortable to hold for longer jobs

Specifications

Bottle capacity: 280 ml

Battery: 1200 mAh rechargeable, about 2 hours for a full charge

Run time: about 25 minutes of spraying per bottle of liquid

Weight: about 270 g

Control: single power button

Where it helps

Use it to apply a fine mist of a liquid you have chosen for the job, for example fabric or room freshener, or a cleaning or sanitising solution that is made for spraying. Because the mist is fine and even, a small amount of liquid covers a surface quickly and you waste less than with a trigger spray bottle.

How to use

1. Charge the battery fully before first use.

2. Fill the bottle with a suitable liquid, no more than the 280 ml capacity, and screw it on firmly.

3. Hold the gun upright, point it away from your face and press the power button once to start spraying.

4. Press the button again to stop. Empty and rinse the bottle after use so the nozzle does not clog.

Before you start

  • Use only liquids that are safe and made for spraying. Read the label of any chemical, and do not use thick, oily or sticky liquids.
  • Do not spray towards people, pets, food, electrical sockets or open flames.
  • Spray in a ventilated room and keep the gun away from water while charging.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
