About Magnetic Mosquito Door Screen 120 x 210cm

Product overview

Magnetic Mosquito Door Screen 120 x 210cm is a larger removable mesh curtain designed for a compatible doorway. The fine mesh provides a physical barrier intended to reduce the entry of mosquitoes, flies and other flying insects through the covered opening while allowing air to move between spaces. This is a non-chemical screening accessory: it does not contain insecticide, release repellent vapour or kill mosquitoes already inside a room.

LARGE SIZE AND DOORWAY CHECK

The listed curtain dimensions are 120 x 210 cm. Measure the full doorway opening, including the clear width and height, and check the amount of overlap required by the supplier. A curtain measuring 120 cm wide may need additional frame space for fastening and secure coverage. Confirm that the top and side frame surfaces are suitable for the supplied mounting method. If your entrance is wider or taller than the recommended opening, do not stretch the mesh to force a fit.

HOW THE SCREEN OPERATES

The magnetic curtain uses two mesh panels that part at the centre for passage. Magnetic closure points are intended to bring the panels together again after someone walks through. This can provide convenient hands-free access in suitable doorways. The screen needs to hang straight and freely, with its top and side edges firmly secured and the centre edges aligned. A clear path at the bottom helps prevent the curtain from snagging and allows the panels to reconnect.

POSSIBLE APPLICATIONS

Consider this size for wider bedroom, kitchen, sitting-room, dining-room, patio or balcony entrances where a lightweight mesh curtain is suitable. It can support ventilation while helping reduce insect entry through the screened doorway. It does not cover other openings in the building. Windows, vents, roof spaces and gaps around other doors should be checked separately. Combine screening with removal of standing water, good drainage and regular household inspection for a more complete mosquito-prevention routine.

INSTALLATION

Read the supplied fitting instructions before starting. Clean the frame surface and let it dry. Remove dust, grease and loose paint that could weaken adhesive fasteners. Position the screen centrally and level along the top. Secure the fixing strips or supplied accessories as directed, then align the centre magnets and check the full perimeter. Walk through the screen at a normal pace several times to test whether the panels close by themselves. Make sure the curtain does not interfere with the operation of the actual door, create a trip hazard or obstruct an emergency exit. If the mounting surface is unsuitable, seek a compatible installation option.

MAINTENANCE

Inspect the mesh for tears, pulled seams and loose edges. Check the magnets and fastening strips periodically, especially on a frequently used entrance. Clean the mesh gently according to the product care instructions. Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive brushes and excessive pulling. Refit or repair damaged sections before relying on the screen as an insect barrier. Keep it away from sharp edges and hot surfaces.

CHOOSING THE RIGHT SCREEN

Before buying, compare your measured opening with the 120 x 210 cm product size and confirm the usable doorway dimensions with the seller. Check the colour, mesh material, included fixing accessories and whether the screen is suitable for your frame type. A standard magnetic curtain may not suit sliding doors, unusually shaped entrances or doors that require a rigid closure. In those cases, a purpose-made or professionally fitted screen may be more appropriate.
