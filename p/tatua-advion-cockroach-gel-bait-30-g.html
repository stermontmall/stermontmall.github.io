Why Use a Cockroach Gel Bait Cockroaches hide in dark, tight places such as behind cookers, under sinks, inside cupboard hinges and around drains. They contaminate food and surfaces and can spread germs. A gel bait works differently from a spray: cockroaches feed on the bait and return to their hiding places, which helps reach insects that never come out into the open. Because only small dots are used, gel baits are neat, precise and ideal for kitchens and food areas.
