Why Use a Cockroach Gel Bait

Cockroaches hide in dark, tight places such as behind cookers, under sinks, inside cupboard hinges and around drains. They contaminate food and surfaces and can spread germs. A gel bait works differently from a spray: cockroaches feed on the bait and return to their hiding places, which helps reach insects that never come out into the open. Because only small dots are used, gel baits are neat, precise and ideal for kitchens and food areas.

How to Apply

Clean the area first and remove food and crumbs so the bait is the most attractive food source. Apply small dots, not long lines, near hinges, behind appliances, under sinks, along the edges of cupboards and where you see droppings or cockroach activity. Do not spray insecticide on top of the bait, because it can put cockroaches off. Check after a few days and re-apply where the bait has been eaten or has dried out.

Prevention Tips

Seal cracks, fix leaks, store food in closed containers, empty bins daily and wash dishes before night. Good hygiene together with gel baiting gives the best long-term control.
