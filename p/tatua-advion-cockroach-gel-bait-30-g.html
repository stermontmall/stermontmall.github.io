Where it helps

Cockroaches hide in dark, tight places such as behind cookers, under sinks, inside cupboard hinges and around drains, and they contaminate food and surfaces. A gel bait works differently from a spray: cockroaches feed on the bait and return to their hiding places, which helps reach insects that never come out into the open. Because only small dots are used, it is neat and precise.

How to use it

Clean the area first and remove food and crumbs so the bait is the most attractive food source. Apply small dots, not long lines, near hinges, behind appliances, under sinks, along cupboard edges and where you see droppings or cockroach activity. Do not spray insecticide on top of the bait, because it can put cockroaches off. Check after a few days and re-apply where the bait has been eaten or has dried out.

Before you start

  • Empty and wipe the cupboards and counters where you will place gel
  • Fix leaks and seal cracks around pipes
  • Choose spots children and pets cannot reach

Safety

Read the label before use. Keep away from children and pets, and apply only in places they cannot reach. Do not apply on food-contact surfaces, and wash your hands after use.

Storage

Close the tube after use and keep it in a cool, dry place away from food, children and pets.

Why use a gel bait instead of a spray?
