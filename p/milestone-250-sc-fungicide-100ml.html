DISEASE IDENTIFICATION AND CROP PLANNING Effective disease management starts with accurate diagnosis. Leaf spots, yellowing, wilting and surface growth can result from fungal, bacterial or viral pathogens, nutrient imbalance, water stress or physical injury.

Inspect the crop and seek advice from an extension officer or qualified crop adviser when diagnosis is uncertain. Match the identified disease to the crop and target stated on the label. Plan applications around crop stage, weather, spray coverage and pre-harvest restrictions.
