Product Overview & Key Benefits

This magnetic mosquito-proof screen door curtain is a polyester mesh panel designed to provide a physical barrier across a doorway while allowing air to pass through. The Jumia Kenya listing identifies the size as 210 × 100 cm and describes the item as a mosquito-proof door curtain made from polyester. It is a non-chemical option for households looking to screen a doorway against flying insects.

- Door-screen format: intended to cover a doorway rather than a window. - Listed size: 210 cm high × 100 cm wide. - Material: polyester, described by the supplier as polyester fibre (dacron).

This is a pest-control or agricultural chemical product. Use it only for the crop, pest, site and application method expressly authorised on the current Kenya-approved product label. Read the full label before opening or use.

Follow the label exactly for personal protective equipment, dilution, application, ventilation, re-entry period, pre-harvest interval, environmental restrictions and any other precautions. Buying & Pack-Size Guide

Before choosing a screen curtain, check the doorway measurements, the direction in which the door opens, the amount of clearance around the frame and whether people will pass through frequently. Consider whether the screen location is sheltered from strong wind and whether pets or children may pull at the mesh. A screen that is too narrow or too short may leave gaps, while a panel that is too large may interfere with the doorway or its fixing points.
