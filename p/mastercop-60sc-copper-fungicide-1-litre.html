About Mastercop 60SC Copper Fungicide – 1 Litre

Mastercop 60SC Copper Fungicide (1 Litre)

Mastercop 60SC is a copper-based protective fungicide for fungal and bacterial diseases on tomatoes, beans and other vegetables. It forms a protective layer on leaves and stems that helps stop spores and bacteria before they enter the plant.

Key specifications & features

  • Active ingredient: See the product label
  • Target: Fungal and bacterial diseases on tomatoes, beans and other vegetables (protective control)
  • Formulation: Suspension concentrate (SC)
  • Application settings: Agricultural: small and medium farms, kitchen gardens and greenhouses

Mixing & application instructions

1. Dilution rate: use the dose and water volume printed on the manufacturer's label for your crop and growth stage.

2. Application method: Mix as the label directs and spray evenly so leaves and stems are covered. Do not spray in strong wind or just before rain.

3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Available in 1 Litre.

4. Apply before disease becomes heavy and scout the crop regularly: this is a protective product.

5. Use copper in rotation with other fungicide groups, especially in wet, humid weather when blight, leaf spots and bacterial problems spread fast.

Safety & re-entry precautions

  • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.
