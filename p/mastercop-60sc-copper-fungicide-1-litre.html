Why Choose Mastercop 60SC

Copper fungicides are widely used by Kenyan farmers because they act on the surface of leaves and stems, forming a protective layer that helps stop disease spores and bacteria before they enter the plant. Because this is a protective product, it works best when it is applied before disease becomes heavy, so regular scouting of your crop is important. Mastercop 60SC comes as a suspension concentrate (SC), which mixes into a smooth spray liquid in the tank when you follow the label.

Where It Fits in Your Crop Programme

Many growers include a copper fungicide in rotation with other fungicide groups to reduce the risk of resistance and to protect crops during wet, humid weather, when blight, leaf spots and bacterial problems spread quickly. Good farm practices help the product work better: use clean seed or seedlings, space plants for airflow, remove badly infected leaves, rotate crops and avoid watering from above late in the day.
