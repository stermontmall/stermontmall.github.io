About Polti Cimex Eradicator Professional Bed Bug Steam Cleaner

Product overview

The Polti Cimex Eradicator is a specialist steam-cleaning appliance designed for targeted bed bug management on suitable surfaces. Its patented steam-dispenser system superheats dry saturated steam to a stated temperature of up to 180°C, allowing trained operators to direct heat into seams, joints and other potential harbourage points. It is a heat-based tool rather than a conventional insecticide spray. Correct use requires careful technique, surface compatibility checks and a planned inspection and follow-up programme. Polti's published technical information lists a 2-litre working capacity, steam output of up to 110g per minute, pressure up to 4 bar and a steam temperature up to 180°C. The manufacturer lists a steam-ready indicator and an out-of-water signal. Specifications and accessories can vary by regional version, so confirm the exact model number, electrical rating and package contents on the unit supplied. Do not assume that a US-market voltage specification applies to a different regional version intended for another power supply. The Cimex Eradicator is intended to deliver superheated dry saturated steam through a dedicated dispenser rather than behave like an ordinary household garment steamer. The controlled expansion and heat-exchange arrangement is designed to raise the steam temperature at the outlet. Polti describes this approach as a method for treating bed bugs and eggs without relying on an insecticide application. Actual results depend on correct treatment of the target surface, steam delivery, movement speed and access to the insect harbourage. It should not be described as a guaranteed whole-room eradication device. Bed bug work begins with inspection. Check mattress seams, bed-frame joints, screw holes, headboards, bedside furniture, upholstered furniture, skirting and nearby cracks. Identify which materials can tolerate steam and which may be damaged by heat or moisture. A technician should plan a route that allows the nozzle to reach appropriate surfaces without directing steam toward electrical equipment, delicate finishes, people or pets. Steam treatment should be part of an integrated programme that includes monitoring, laundering where appropriate and follow-up inspections. Use the appliance only after reading the exact operating manual supplied with the machine. Fill the boiler as instructed, connect the hose and dispenser securely, and wait for the steam-ready indication. Use the correct attachments for the surface and keep the nozzle moving at the pace recommended by Polti. Do not hold the steam in one place longer than instructed. Excess heat and moisture can damage adhesives, finishes, timber, plastics, fabrics and other sensitive materials. Test a discreet area where the manual advises before treating a larger surface. The machine is a powered pressure-and-heat appliance. Steam at the outlet can cause serious burns, and the hose, gun and metal fittings may remain hot after use. Wear the protective equipment specified by the manufacturer, keep bystanders clear and never point the dispenser at skin or animals. Do not use it on live electrical equipment, sockets, powered appliances or materials that are not suitable for steam. Follow the manual for cooling, depressurising, refilling and moving the unit; never open a pressurised boiler. Polti lists accessories for the Cimex Eradicator that may include a flexible steam hose with gun, straight and curved extensions, wheels, gloves, cleaning accessories and consumables such as HPMed and Kalstop, depending on the regional package. The contents in this listing must be confirmed with the seller. Do not assume that every accessory shown in manufacturer marketing images is included in the unit being sold. HPMed is a separate detergent used with the system for odour-related support; it is not the steam generator itself and should be checked as a package component. After use, allow the machine to cool and follow the manufacturer's cleaning and descaling instructions. Use only the recommended maintenance products and intervals. Check the hose, steam gun, nozzle, seals, power cable and boiler indicators before the next job. Store the appliance dry and protect the attachments from impact or blockage. Maintain a service record, especially when the unit is used by several technicians. Do not improvise boiler repairs or substitute unapproved parts in a pressurised heating appliance. For Kenyan pest-control contractors, hotels, guest houses, hospitals and residential service teams, the Cimex Eradicator can support targeted bed bug work where heat treatment is appropriate. It should be used by trained operators who understand material limits, burn hazards and the need to inspect adjoining furniture and rooms. A steam tool does not replace a complete bed bug management plan, and not every mattress, upholstery fabric or building finish can safely receive steam. Document the treated locations and schedule follow-up checks. The Polti Cimex Eradicator is listed at KSh 65,000.
