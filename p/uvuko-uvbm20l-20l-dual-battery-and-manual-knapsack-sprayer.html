WHY A DUAL-MODE 20-LITRE SPRAYER?

A dual-mode sprayer combines battery-assisted pumping with a manual option. Electric operation can reduce the repeated effort of hand pumping during longer spraying sessions. Manual operation offers an alternative if the battery is discharged or charging is unavailable, provided the operator follows the correct procedure. This can be useful for farms, gardens, nurseries and outdoor maintenance where the operator needs to move between treatment sections. The 20-litre tank provides a substantial liquid capacity, but the filled unit is heavy. Plan carrying distance, terrain, refill points and work duration before filling.

BATTERY CAPACITY AND RUNTIME

The supplier lists a 12V battery in the 7Ah–12Ah range. Because the capacity is given as a range, ask which battery is fitted to the actual unit being sold. The listing's estimate of approximately two 20-litre spray operations per full charge is not a guaranteed runtime for every job. Actual performance depends on battery age, charge condition, nozzle, pressure, liquid viscosity, pump demand and operator use. Confirm the charging time, battery type, replacement cost and availability with the seller. Use only the supplied compatible charger or an approved equivalent, and charge in a dry, ventilated place away from excessive heat and moisture.

MANUAL BACKUP PUMPING

The UVBM20L is listed with manual pumping as an alternative to the battery mode. This can be useful when electrical power is unavailable, but the exact changeover procedure must be taken from the equipment manual. Do not force the pump or operate controls in an unintended sequence. Inspect the pump handle and seals regularly. If the unit cannot maintain pressure, stop and identify the cause rather than applying excessive force. Manual operation still requires safe pressure handling and correct calibration.

TANK, PISTON PUMP AND FILTER

The supplier describes a reinforced tank, heavy-duty poly piston and inline filter. The tank holds the spray liquid within its marked fill limit. The piston pump creates pressure for liquid delivery, while the inline filter helps keep debris from reaching the downstream components. Keep the filter clean and correctly fitted. Sediment can block a nozzle or affect pump performance. Tank material and pump construction do not guarantee compatibility with every chemical. Check the product label and equipment manual before use, particularly when a formulation contains solvents or other ingredients that may affect seals and plastics.

SHOULDER STRAPS AND HANDLING

Wide padded shoulder straps are listed to support carrying. Adjust them so the tank sits securely and the load is distributed as intended. A filled 20-litre sprayer can be heavy, so consider the operator's physical ability and avoid lifting it onto the back without assistance if necessary. Use a stable surface for filling and keep the cap secure before carrying. Do not overfill. When treating a large property, use planned refill points to reduce the distance travelled with a full tank. Avoid rough handling that could damage the tank, pump, filter or battery compartment.

PREPARING AND CALIBRATING A SPRAY JOB

Read the product label and sprayer manual before filling. Measure the treatment accurately and prepare only the amount required. Do not calculate chemical dilution from the tank size alone. Test the sprayer with clean water to check for leaks, filter seating, pump action and nozzle pattern. Calibrate the application rate using the method recommended for the product and equipment. Maintain the correct distance and walking pace. Avoid windy conditions that could carry droplets off target, and protect water sources, drains, neighbouring crops, food and animal feed. Keep people and animals away for the label-specified period.

Do not eat, drink or smoke while mixing or spraying. Keep the lance pointed away from people and animals. Release pressure as directed before opening the tank or disconnecting the hose. Keep children and untrained people away from the sprayer and chemical containers. Never use the tank for drinking water, food or beverages. Store chemicals in their original labelled containers and follow the stated first-aid and emergency instructions.

CLEANING AFTER USE

Follow the label and equipment manual for safe emptying, rinsing and disposal. Never pour pesticide residues or rinse water into drains, streams, ponds, wells or storm-water channels. Clean the tank, inline filter, hose, lance and nozzle according to the recommended procedure. If changing to another product, use the specified decontamination process to prevent residues from affecting the next treatment. Keep the battery, charger and electrical connectors dry. Remove or isolate the battery before cleaning electrical parts when the manual instructs. Inspect seals, filter, straps and hose after cleaning.

Storage & handling

Before each use, check the tank, cap, pump, filter, straps, hose, trigger and nozzle. Replace damaged seals and hoses with compatible parts. Store the sprayer empty, clean and dry in a secure location away from children, direct sunlight, food and animal feed. Store and charge the battery according to the manufacturer's instructions. Do not leave the battery in excessive heat or allow electrical contacts to become wet. If the pump loses pressure, the battery overheats or the tank leaks, stop using the unit and seek appropriate service.
