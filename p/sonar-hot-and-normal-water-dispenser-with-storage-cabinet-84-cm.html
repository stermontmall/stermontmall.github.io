How to Set Up

Place the dispenser on a flat, firm floor near a power socket, leaving a little space around it for air to circulate. Wipe the top, then load a clean bottle of water. Switch on the heating and wait for the hot water to be ready. Keep the dispenser away from direct sunlight, and let it stand upright for a while after delivery before you plug it in.

Cleaning and Care

Unplug the dispenser before cleaning. Wipe the outside with a soft damp cloth, and clean the taps and the drip tray regularly to keep them hygienic. Use clean drinking water, replace empty bottles promptly so the heater does not run dry, and descale as needed if you live in a hard-water area. Keep the hot tap away from children, because the water can scald.
