Where it helps

Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards and cracks in walls, and they bite at night. Cockroaches shelter in kitchens, drains, cupboards and behind appliances, and they spread germs on food and surfaces. Ants follow food into kitchens, stores and bedrooms and nest in walls, floors and gardens. With one product you can treat several of the most common household pests.

Getting the best results

  • Clean first: wash bedding at a high temperature and vacuum mattresses, floors and corners
  • Dilute exactly as directed on the label
  • Spray cracks, crevices, bed frames, skirting boards, door frames and other hiding places
  • Spray ant trails and entry points
  • Keep people, pets and food away until the spray has dried
  • Repeat the treatment if the label advises it
  • Treat all affected rooms at the same time, so pests do not move to other rooms

Preventing a return

Store food in closed containers, wipe up crumbs and spills, keep rubbish bins covered, and seal cracks and gaps around pipes and doors. Check second-hand furniture and luggage for bed bugs before bringing them indoors.

Safety

Read and follow the label before use. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle. For large or persistent infestations, consider a licensed pest control operator.

Storage

Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place.
