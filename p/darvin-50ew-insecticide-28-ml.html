Where it helps

Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards and cracks in walls, and they bite at night. Cockroaches shelter in kitchens, drains, cupboards and behind appliances, and they spread germs on food and surfaces. Ants follow food into kitchens, stores and bedrooms and nest in walls, floors and gardens. With one small pack you can treat several of the most common household pests without buying a different product for each.

How to use it

Dilute exactly as directed on the label. Spray cracks, crevices, bed frames, skirting boards, door frames and other hiding places, and spray ant trails and entry points. Keep people, pets and food away until the spray has dried. Repeat the treatment if the label advises it. Treat all the affected rooms at the same time so that the pests do not move to the rooms you have not sprayed.

Before you start

  • Wash bedding at a high temperature and vacuum mattresses, floors and corners
  • Remove food and cover utensils before you spray
  • Check second-hand furniture and luggage for bed bugs before bringing them indoors
  • For a large or persistent infestation, consider a licensed pest control operator

Safety

Read and follow the label before use. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle.

Storage

Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place.
