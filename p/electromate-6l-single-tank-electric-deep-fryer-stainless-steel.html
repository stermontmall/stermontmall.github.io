Frying Tips

Fill the tank with cooking oil between the minimum and maximum marks, and let the oil heat before you lower the basket. Dry the food well before frying so the oil does not spit, and fry in small batches so the oil temperature stays high and food comes out crisp rather than greasy. Lift the basket and let the oil drain before serving. Filter the oil through a fine strainer after it cools to extend its life.

Cleaning and Care

Switch off, unplug and let the oil cool completely before cleaning. Remove the basket and wash it with warm soapy water. Take care when emptying the oil, and wipe the tank with a soft cloth. Do not put the heating unit in water, and keep the control panel dry.

Safety Tips

Never leave hot oil unattended, and never add water to hot oil. Keep children away, place the fryer on a stable, flat, heat-resistant surface and use a socket that can handle the load. Follow the user manual.
