Resolve 6% Dust 12 x 10g pack details

This Resolve 6% Dust listing is for one display box containing 12 individual 10g sachets, giving 120g of product in total. Osho Chemical Industries lists it as a public-health insecticide for household control of cockroaches, ants, fleas and houseflies. The Kenyan Pest Control Products Board public-health register lists Resolve 6% Dust under PCPB(CR)2009.

The registered formulation is listed with carbaryl 50g/kg and lambda-cyhalothrin 10g/kg. This is a dust formulation, not a spray or liquid concentrate. Check the pack and the label before use, and do not substitute instructions from another insecticide or another Resolve pack.

  • 12 sachets per display box.
  • Each sachet contains 10g; 120g total.
  • Listed for household cockroaches, ants, fleas and houseflies.

Use the insecticide dust with care

Apply only by the method and at the rate stated on the product label. Keep the dust away from food, drinking water, utensils, children's toys and animal feed. Keep children and pets away from treated areas for the period stated on the label. Store unopened sachets in their original box in a cool, dry, locked place, separate from food and medicines. Dispose of empty sachets and leftover product according to the label and local requirements.
