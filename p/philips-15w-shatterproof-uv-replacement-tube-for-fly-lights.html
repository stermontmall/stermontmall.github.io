Product overview

Maintain a professional flying-insect control unit with a Philips 15W shatterproof UV replacement tube. This replacement lamp is intended for compatible insect light traps that use a 15-watt actinic ultraviolet tube. It is a consumable maintenance part rather than a complete fly-killing machine: the lamp provides the UV-A attraction source, while the insect light unit's own capture system—such as a glue board or other designed mechanism—does the actual trapping.

Designed for insect light traps Flying-insect light traps use a specialised UV-A light source to attract susceptible flying insects towards a capture area. In glue-board units, insects are retained on a replaceable adhesive board positioned inside the housing. In other designs, the unit may use a different capture method.

The replacement tube supports the attraction function of a compatible device, helping a maintained unit continue operating as intended. This product is listed as a 15-watt Philips shatterproof tube for replacement in insect fly light units. Shatter-resistant sleeve The protective shatterproof construction is useful in locations where broken glass contamination is a concern.

Commercial kitchens, food preparation rooms, food processing premises, packing areas and other hygiene-sensitive workplaces may operate glass-control procedures. A shatter-resistant sleeve is designed to help retain glass fragments if the lamp breaks. It does not make the tube unbreakable, remove the need for careful handling or replace a site's glass and brittle-plastic policy.

When to replace a UV tube UV output can decline during service even when a fluorescent tube still appears to illuminate. A lamp that looks bright to the eye may no longer deliver the same useful attraction output for flying insects.

Follow the equipment manufacturer's recommended replacement interval and the maintenance schedule used at your premises. Replace a tube that is damaged, flickering, blackened at the ends or no longer operating reliably. Switch off and isolate the insect light unit before opening its housing or handling the lamp.

Allow the tube to cool before removal. Suitable maintenance settings A compatible replacement UV tube may be useful for pest management contractors, restaurants, hotels, supermarkets, food warehouses, bakeries, institutional kitchens, factories and other premises that maintain indoor insect light traps. It can also support planned maintenance programmes where several units are installed and spare lamps are kept in stock.

The tube is not a stand-alone mosquito lamp, room light or general-purpose household bulb. Its purpose is to operate in a compatible flying-insect control device. Installation and care Only a competent person should replace the tube where required by the equipment instructions or workplace safety procedures.

Disconnect power before opening the unit. Avoid touching the glass surface with bare hands, do not bend or twist the tube, and make sure the pins are correctly aligned with the holder. Never use a tube with visible cracks, a damaged sleeve or bent pins.

After installation, close and secure the unit's cover or guard before restoring power. Dispose of spent fluorescent lamps through an appropriate local waste or recycling route; do not crush them or place broken lamp fragments where people, food or animals may come into contact with them. Pest management role An insect light trap is one component of an integrated flying-insect management plan.

Good results also depend on correct placement, sanitation, waste management, exclusion of insects from buildings, maintenance of doors and screens, and timely replacement of capture boards where applicable.
