Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Lower the pump using a suitable support rope or installation tool; never lift it by the electrical cable. Support the rising pipe so its weight does not strain the pump outlet. Use a compatible non-return arrangement and secure the pipework.

Do not operate the pump dry or outside the manufacturer's immersion limits. Isolate power before retrieving the pump or clearing an obstruction. Use an installer familiar with submersible pump systems for borehole installation.
