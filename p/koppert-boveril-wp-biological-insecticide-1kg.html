Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

This is a pest-control or agricultural chemical product. Use it only for the crop, pest, site and application method expressly authorised on the current Kenya-approved product label. Read the full label before opening or use.

Follow the label exactly for personal protective equipment, dilution, application, ventilation, re-entry period, pre-harvest interval, environmental restrictions and any other precautions. Do not guess a dose, mix it with another product, or use it on food, animals, people or surfaces unless the label specifically permits that use. Keep the original container tightly closed and locked away from children, unauthorised persons and animals, and separate from food, drink and animal feed.

Prevent spills and contamination of drains, wells, streams and other water sources. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling; wash hands and exposed skin after handling. Product overview

Koppert Boveril WP is a microbial insecticide supplied in a one-kilogram wettable powder pack. The Kenya Pest Control Products Board biopesticide register lists BOVERIL WP under registration number PCPB(CR)2159, with Beauveria bassiana as the active biological agent. The register identifies it for control of whiteflies in roses.

The product contains the PL 63 strain of the insect-pathogenic fungus Beauveria bassiana. Always check the current Kenyan label for the approved crop, target pest, pack details and directions before purchase or use. Beauveria bassiana is an entomopathogenic fungus that infects susceptible insects through contact.

Spores attach to the insect’s outer surface, germinate and penetrate the cuticle. The fungus then develops inside the insect and eventually causes its death. Because it works differently from conventional chemical insecticides, Boveril can be considered as part of an integrated pest-management and resistance-management programme.

It is not a conventional contact poison, a public-health room spray, a rodenticide, a herbicide or a fungicide. The one-kilogram pack is intended for commercial flower farms, nurseries and professional crop-protection users who require a larger quantity for planned greenhouse or rose-production programmes. The Kenyan registration specifically identifies whitefly control in roses.

Do not assume that registration for roses authorises use on other crops or against other pests. If a grower needs to control thrips or another insect on a different crop, check the current Kenyan label and obtain advice from a qualified agronomist before selecting a product. Whiteflies can reproduce rapidly in protected crops and may cause direct feeding damage, honeydew deposits and secondary sooty mould.

They are often found on the undersides of leaves, especially on younger growth. Regular scouting is important. Inspect representative plants, examine leaf undersides and growing points, use appropriate monitoring tools and record the level of infestation.

Correct pest identification matters because whitefly damage can be confused with other sucking pests, nutritional problems or plant stress. Boveril is a living microbial product, so storage and handling conditions can affect its viability. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions for temperature, shelf life, preparation and application.

Do not expose the product to excessive heat, direct sunlight or conditions that may reduce the viability of the fungal spores. Use clean application equipment and prepare the suspension only as directed. Do not keep a ready-made spray mixture longer than the label permits, and do not combine the product with fungicides or other products unless compatibility is confirmed by the label or manufacturer.

The product information supplied by Agropests gives a reference application rate of 2g per litre of water and recommends application in late afternoon or on cloudy days, with adequate humidity and direct contact between the product and target pests. Use these details only if they match the current approved Kenyan label for the pack supplied. The label controls the exact rate, water volume, crop stage, spray coverage, application interval, number of applications, re-entry conditions and any restrictions on tank mixing.

Do not increase the concentration or apply to an unlisted crop. Integrated whitefly management should combine scouting with removal of heavily infested plant material where appropriate, weed management, crop hygiene, screening, biological control and conservation of natural enemies. Avoid unnecessary broad-spectrum sprays that may disrupt beneficial organisms.

Record treatment dates, product batch numbers, target pest levels and follow-up observations so that the grower can evaluate performance and adjust the programme. Where whitefly pressure remains high, review pest identification, crop hygiene, spray coverage, humidity and possible reinfestation before deciding on additional control measures. Before purchase, check that the pack is sealed, genuine, correctly labelled and within its expiry date.

Confirm PCPB(CR)2159 and Beauveria bassiana on the current Kenyan label. The product photograph is for identification; the actual pack label supplied with the product is the authority for permitted use, storage, preparation, personal protective equipment and disposal. If the label is missing, damaged or inconsistent with the expected registration, do not use the product until the supplier or PCPB has clarified the discrepancy.

Wear the protective equipment stated on the label and avoid inhaling powder during mixing. Prevent contact with eyes and skin. Keep unprotected people and animals away during preparation and application.

Protect food, drinking water and animal feed from contamination. After application, clean equipment and protective clothing according to the label and wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly. Store Boveril in its original packaging in a cool, dry and secure place away from food, medicines, children and unauthorised persons.

Never transfer the powder into an unlabelled container or reuse empty packaging. Dispose of unused product and packaging through approved pesticide-waste arrangements. Keep the batch number, purchase record and expiry date for traceability.

Because Boveril is a microbial product, follow the manufacturer’s storage conditions carefully to maintain product quality until use.
