About Bat CRP Bat Repellent

Bat CRP Bat Repellent (1 Litre) Bat CRP is a heavy-duty bat repellent chemical for getting rid of bats from roofs, ceilings, halls and other hiding places in homes and buildings. It comes in a 1 litre pack and is diluted with water as directed on the label. KEY FEATURES • Heavy-duty bat repellent • For roofs, ceilings, halls and other roosting places • Dilute with water before use • 1 litre pack • Delivered to all 47 counties THE PROBLEM WITH BATS IN BUILDINGS Bats that settle in a roof or ceiling leave droppings and urine that stain ceilings, cause strong smells and attract insects. Droppings build up in roof spaces, and the noise at night can disturb families, guests and worshippers. Halls, churches, schools, hotels, offices and homes often need a way to move bats out. HOW TO USE IT Dilute Bat CRP with water as directed on the label. For roofing and hall areas, the label dilution is 1 part product to 4 parts water. Apply the diluted mixture to the areas where bats roost, such as the underside of roofs, ceiling spaces, beams and corners. Treat the roosting surfaces thoroughly and repeat if the label advises. Before treatment, clear old droppings and wear protective gear, because dried droppings can create dust. AFTER TREATMENT Once the bats have left, close entry points so that they cannot return. Check the eaves, gaps around the roof, vents and holes in walls and seal them. Clean up droppings safely and keep the roof space dry and clean. Regular inspections help you catch a new problem early. Bats are useful animals outdoors because they eat insects, so the aim is to move them out of buildings rather than harm them. Check local rules on handling bats if you are unsure. SAFETY Read the label before use. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and applying, and use a ladder or platform safely when working at height. Wash your hands after use and keep out of reach of children. Keep away from food, water, pets and livestock, and do not let the product reach streams or ponds. STORAGE Store in the original closed container in a cool, dry, locked place.
