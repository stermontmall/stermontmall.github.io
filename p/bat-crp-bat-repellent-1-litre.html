About Bat CRP Bat Repellent

Bats that settle in a roof or ceiling leave droppings and urine that stain ceilings, cause strong smells and attract insects, and their noise at night disturbs families, guests and worshippers. Bat CRP is a heavy-duty bat repellent chemical for moving bats out of roofs, ceilings, halls and other hiding places. It is diluted with water as the label directs and comes in 250 ml, 500 ml and 1 litre packs.

Where it helps

Droppings build up in roof spaces, and the smell and stains spread to the rooms below. Halls, churches, schools, hotels, offices and homes often need a way to move bats out. Bat CRP is made for that job: you apply the diluted mixture where bats roost, so that they leave and look elsewhere.

Dilution (from the product label)

  • Roofing and hall areas: 1 part Bat CRP to 4 parts water
  • Always confirm the dilution on your pack's label before mixing

How to use it

Dilute Bat CRP with water as directed on the label. Apply the diluted mixture to the places where bats roost, such as the underside of roofs, ceiling spaces, beams and corners. Treat the roosting surfaces thoroughly and repeat if the label advises. Once the bats have left, close the entry points so they cannot return: seal the eaves, gaps around the roof, vents and holes in walls. Then clean up droppings safely and keep the roof space dry and clean.

Before you start

  • Clear old droppings and wear protective gear first, because dried droppings can create dust
  • Check local rules on handling bats if you are unsure

Safety

Read the label before use. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and applying, and use a ladder or platform safely when working at height. Wash your hands after use and keep out of reach of children. Keep away from food, water, pets and livestock, and do not let the product reach streams or ponds.

Storage

Store in the original closed container in a cool, dry, locked place.
