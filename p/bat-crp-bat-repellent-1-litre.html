Before using a bat repellent product

Important: Bats are beneficial wildlife and may be protected. Do not poison, injure, trap or seal bats inside a building. Before using any chemical, check Kenyan wildlife rules and the product’s original label, registration status and approved use. Consider humane exclusion by a qualified professional.

Product listings do not replace the manufacturer’s label or professional advice. Confirm the product’s ingredients, registration details, intended application and safety directions directly from the packaging before purchase or use. Do not rely on online dilution rates or instructions unless they are confirmed on the current label.
