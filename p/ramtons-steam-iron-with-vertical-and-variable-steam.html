Ironing and Care Tips

Check the care label on each garment before ironing and choose the right temperature and steam setting. Start with delicate fabrics at lower heat and finish with thicker fabrics. Fill the tank with clean water, and use filtered or soft water if your tap water is hard, to reduce limescale. Empty the tank after use, let the iron cool, and store it upright. Keep the soleplate clean by wiping it with a damp cloth once it has cooled.
