Match steam and temperature to the garment

This Ramtons steam iron combines variable steam control with a vertical-steam function. Variable steam lets you adjust the output for different ironing tasks, while vertical steam can be useful for refreshing suitable hanging garments or curtains. The see-through water tank makes it easier to check the water level, and the non-stick soleplate is designed to glide across fabric.

Always read the care label before ironing. Start with the lowest suitable temperature for delicate fabrics and increase it only when the label allows. If you are unsure how a fabric reacts to steam, test a small hidden area or use the garment manufacturer's care instructions. Do not use steam on materials that the care label says should not be steamed.

  • Check the water-tank instructions and any water-quality guidance in the manual.

Storage, cleaning and safety

After ironing, switch off and unplug the iron, allow it to cool and empty the tank if the manual recommends doing so. Store it upright in a dry place, with the cord arranged loosely rather than tightly wrapped around a hot appliance. Follow the manual's instructions for cleaning the soleplate and managing mineral build-up.
