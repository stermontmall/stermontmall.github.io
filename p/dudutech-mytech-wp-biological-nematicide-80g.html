Product overview

Dudutech Mytech WP is a biological nematicide supplied in an 80g wettable powder pack. The Kenya Pest Control Products Board biopesticide register lists MYTECH WP under registration number PCPB(CR)1134.

Product details

Its active biological ingredient is Paecilomyces lilacinus at approximately 1 × 10^10 colony-forming units per gram. The registered use is control of plant-parasitic nematodes, including Meloidogyne species, on roses and tomatoes. It can parasitise nematode eggs and other susceptible stages. Spores attach to the host, germinate and form fungal structures that penetrate and colonise the egg or nematode body. Root-knot nematodes can cause galling on roots, reduced nutrient and water uptake, stunting, yellowing and uneven plant growth. In roses, nematode damage can affect plant vigour and flower production. Protect food, drinking water and animal feed from contamination. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. Uses & applications

This biological mode of action differs from conventional chemical nematicides and can be useful in integrated soil-health and crop-protection programmes. Mytech WP is not a public-health insecticide, a household aerosol, a rodenticide, a herbicide or a foliar insecticide for every pest. The 80g pack is suitable for growers, nurseries, greenhouse operators and professional crop-protection users who need a smaller pack for targeted soil or root-zone management. The PCPB registration specifically identifies plant-parasitic nematodes on roses and tomatoes. In tomatoes, root injury may reduce crop establishment and yield. Care, cleaning & storage

Where nematodes are suspected, inspect roots and use soil or root testing where necessary to confirm the species and level of infestation before choosing a treatment. Mytech WP should be part of an integrated nematode-management programme. Do not mix Mytech with fungicides or other pesticides unless the label or manufacturer confirms compatibility, because some treatments may affect the viability of the biological fungus. Because Mytech contains living fungal spores, storage and handling are important. Use clean application equipment and prepare the mixture only as directed. Do not store a prepared suspension longer than the label permits. After use, clean equipment and protective clothing according to the label and wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly. Store the pack in a cool, dry and secure place away from food, medicines, children and unauthorised persons.
