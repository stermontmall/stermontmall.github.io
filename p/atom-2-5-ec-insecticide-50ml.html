Product overview

Atom 2.5 EC Insecticide 50ml is an emulsifiable concentrate containing deltamethrin at 25 g/L. The product is listed by Kenya's Pest Control Products Board under registration number PCPB(CR)0760. The current PCPB crop register identifies its approved crop uses as control of thrips and aphids on French beans, and caterpillars and aphids on roses. The register also gives a 12-hour restricted-entry interval and a one-day pre-harvest interval. This listing is for one 50ml bottle and is intended for buyers who have confirmed that Atom 2.5 EC and its registered crop use match the intended crop-protection programme. Safety information

AND RESPONSIBLE HANDLING Atom 2.5 EC is a pesticide concentrate and must be handled with care. Keep it away from children, food, animal feed, medicines and drinking water. Avoid contact with skin and eyes and avoid inhaling vapour, mist or spray. Use the personal protective equipment stated on the label. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. Wash hands and exposed skin after handling and clean application equipment only as directed. Observe the 12-hour restricted-entry interval and the one-day pre-harvest interval stated in the PCPB register, as well as any additional precautions on the current label. Do not enter a treated area before the required interval has elapsed, and do not harvest earlier than the labelled interval. Never increase the concentration or repeat applications more frequently than the label permits.

Storage & handling

AND DISPOSAL Keep the 50ml bottle tightly closed in its original packaging and store it in a secure, dry, well-ventilated pesticide store. Protect it from direct sunlight, heat and physical damage. Keep it separate from food, feed, medicines, seed and household items. Store it where children and unauthorized persons cannot reach it. Never transfer the concentrate into a beverage bottle or any unlabelled container. Do not reuse the empty pesticide container. Follow the approved label and applicable Kenyan requirements for disposal and container management. Do not pour unused product or washings into drains, rivers, ponds or other water sources.
