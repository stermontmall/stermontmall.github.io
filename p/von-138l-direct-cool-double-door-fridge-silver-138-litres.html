What Direct Cool means

In a direct cool fridge, cooling comes from the freezer compartment and the walls rather than from a fan. Frost can build up over time, so the fridge needs to be defrosted regularly.

Use and care tips

  • Place the fridge on a level floor with space behind and at the sides for air to move
  • Keep it away from direct sun and heat sources such as cookers
  • Allow food to cool before storing, do not overfill and avoid opening the door often
  • Defrost when ice builds up: switch the fridge off first, then clean it with a soft cloth and mild soap
  • Keep your receipt for warranty claims
