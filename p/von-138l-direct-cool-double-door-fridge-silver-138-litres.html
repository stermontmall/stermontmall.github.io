Organise groceries and understand direct-cool refrigeration

The VON listing describes a 138-litre direct-cool fridge with a separate top-mount freezer. Use the door racks for bottles and smaller containers, and arrange heavier groceries on the glass shelves without blocking the interior's cooling surfaces. Leave space between items so cold air can circulate, and avoid packing food tightly against the back wall. Allow hot food to cool before storing it, and keep the door closed as much as possible to maintain a steady temperature.

Direct-cool models can develop frost over time and need manual defrosting. When frost builds up, switch the appliance off and follow the manual's defrosting procedure; do not chip ice with a knife or sharp object, as this can damage the fridge. The ice and egg trays listed with this model provide convenient storage, but check the supplied manual for their recommended placement.

Allow ventilation and protect the warranty

Measure the space where the fridge will stand, including the doorway and route into the room. Position it on a level floor, away from direct sunlight and heat-producing appliances, and leave the rear and side clearance specified by VON. After delivery, follow the manufacturer's guidance on how long to let the appliance stand before connecting it to power. Do not use a damaged plug or cable, and have electrical issues checked by a qualified technician.

The product listing states a one-year product warranty and a three-year compressor warranty. Keep the receipt and warranty documents, and confirm the start date, coverage, exclusions and service contact at purchase. Clean shelves and door racks with a soft cloth and mild detergent, dry them before refitting, and keep the door seal free of food residue so it closes properly.
