Use and Care Tips

Place the fridge on a level floor with some space behind and at the sides for air to move. Keep it away from direct sun and heat sources such as cookers. Allow food to cool before storing, do not overfill and avoid opening the door often. As a direct cool fridge, defrost it regularly when ice builds up, switch it off first and clean it with a soft cloth and mild soap. Keep your receipt for warranty claims.
