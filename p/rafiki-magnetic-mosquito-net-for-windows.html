WHY USE A WINDOW MOSQUITO SCREEN

Product details

Windows are often opened to improve ventilation, but an uncovered opening can also provide a route for mosquitoes and other flying insects. A mesh screen creates a physical boundary across the opening while still allowing air to pass. This can be useful in bedrooms, sitting rooms, kitchens, offices, rental apartments, guest rooms and other spaces where windows are opened during the day or evening. It is especially relevant where occupants want a non-chemical way to reduce insect entry through a window.

Product details

A screen works only on the opening it covers. It should not be described as whole-house pest control or as a substitute for repairing other entry points. Doors, roof spaces, ventilation openings and damaged wall or frame gaps may still allow insects inside. For a more complete prevention approach, inspect the room and property for other openings and keep screens on regularly used windows in serviceable condition.

CHECK THE WINDOW BEFORE BUYING

Product details

Measure the clear opening and the area where the screen is expected to sit. Record the width and height at more than one point if the frame appears uneven. Check whether the window is sliding, hinged, louvred or another design, and note handles, locks, hinges and projecting frame sections that could interfere with the screen. The supplier should confirm whether the magnetic attachment is suitable for the particular frame and whether a separate metal strip or other fixing surface is required.

Product details

Ask for the actual screen dimensions rather than relying on a generic product photograph. Confirm what is included in the package and whether the listed item is a ready-made screen or a made-to-measure option. If a window is unusually shaped or has a non-metallic frame, request compatibility information before purchase. A screen that is too small may leave gaps, while one that is too large may not sit neatly or may interfere with the window.

FITTING AND EVERYDAY USE

Product details

Follow the supplier's fitting instructions for the exact model. Before attaching the screen, make sure the mounting area is clean, dry and suitable for the supplied fixing method. Position the screen so that it covers the intended opening and check the full perimeter for visible gaps. Open and close the window carefully to make sure the screen does not obstruct the handles or moving sections. Do not force a magnetic edge or pull the mesh sharply if it becomes caught.

Product details

If the design is removable, detach it only as directed by the supplier. Refit it carefully and check that the edges meet correctly each time. A magnetic connection should not be assumed to compensate for a poor fit or an uneven frame. Where the window is exposed to wind, frequent movement or pets, inspect the attachment more often for looseness or damage.

CARE AND INSPECTION

Product details

Check the mesh for holes, pulled threads, tears and worn corners. Inspect the magnetic edge and mounting surface for dust, loose sections or signs that the screen is no longer sitting flat. Clean the mesh gently using a method recommended for its material; avoid harsh scrubbing or pulling that could distort it. Keep sharp objects and rough handling away from the screen. If a section is damaged or an edge no longer attaches securely, arrange a suitable repair or replacement instead of relying on a visibly open gap.
