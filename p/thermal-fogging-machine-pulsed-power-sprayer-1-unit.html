Where it helps

A fog spreads through a space, which is why fogging is used where pests hide in cracks and corners. This machine is the equipment, not the insecticide, so you pair it with a product whose label allows thermal fogging and follow that label for the amount to use.

How to use it

The catalog does not give the fuel, tank capacity or settings, so follow the manufacturer's manual for filling, start-up and shut-down. Before you fill a tank, read the insecticide label to confirm the product may be used in a thermal fogger, and follow its dilution and the time the space must stay closed. For example, the catalog advice for Actellic 50EC says it must not be used with thermal foggers. Treat only the space you have prepared, and let the machine cool before you handle or move it.

Before you start

  • Read the machine manual and the insecticide label before the first use
  • Remove people, pets, food, feed and animals from the space
  • Keep flammable materials away from the machine and its hot parts

Safety

A thermal fogger gets hot, so never touch the heat pipes while the machine is running or cooling. Wear the protective equipment named on the insecticide label, including a respirator if the label requires one, and do not enter a fogged space until the label says it is safe to do so. Keep the machine away from children.

Storage

Let the machine cool completely, empty the tanks as the manual directs, and store it dry and out of reach of children, away from food and feed.
