A battery-powered pump reduces the repeated hand-pumping associated with a manual knapsack sprayer. 16-litre tank capacity and practical handling

Where the job is spread across a large site, plan refill points and safe mixing locations in advance.

Keep the tank securely fastened and use both shoulder straps as intended. Do not exceed the marked fill level, and leave sufficient space if the product instructions require it. The backpack format can help distribute the load, but it does not remove the need for sensible lifting and carrying practices.
