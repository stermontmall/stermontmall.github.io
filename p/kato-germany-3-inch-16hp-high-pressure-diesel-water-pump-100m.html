Where & How to Use in Kenya

This pump may suit large farms, high-elevation irrigation, long-distance water transfer, elevated storage tanks and construction sites where water must be moved against a substantial head. It may be useful in remote areas where diesel power is preferred to a fixed electrical supply. For irrigation, calculate the required delivery volume, field elevation, pipe route and distribution method.

For construction work, confirm the water quality and duty cycle with the supplier. Do not assume it is a trash or sewage pump; the listing describes a high-pressure water pump and does not specify solids passage. HEAD, FLOW AND PIPEWORK The 100m maximum head should be treated as an upper rating, not a normal operating point.

Total dynamic head includes vertical rise and friction losses through the hose, pipe, elbows, valves and fittings. Request a performance curve or written flow estimate at the planned head. Use compatible 3-inch pipework and avoid reducing the diameter unnecessarily.

Keep the suction hose within the stated 7–8m lift and make all suction joints airtight. Position the pump near the source and use an appropriate intake strainer. DIESEL ENGINE OPERATION Use diesel fuel and engine oil as specified in the manufacturer's manual.

Check oil before starting and follow the priming and start-up procedure. Operate outdoors in a well-ventilated area because exhaust gases are hazardous. Refuel only when the engine is stopped and cool.

Keep fuel away from sparks, flames and hot components. If using electric start, confirm whether the battery is included and how it should be maintained. Keep the recoil-start mechanism clear and follow the manufacturer's safe starting procedure.

INSTALLATION AND

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Place the pump on firm, level ground. Secure suction and discharge hoses so they cannot move unexpectedly. Direct the outlet to a safe discharge location and avoid erosion or flooding.

Do not run the pump dry if the manufacturer requires priming. Keep hands and clothing away from rotating components and hot surfaces. Use suitable lifting assistance when transporting the unit; do not lift it by hoses or fittings.
