Why phosphorus and calcium matter

Phosphorus and calcium are two of the main minerals in animal nutrition. They are needed for strong bones, teeth and eggshells, and they take part in energy use and growth. A shortage of either can cause weak bones, poor growth, thin shells, low egg production and lower milk yield. Because most grains and cereals are low in available phosphorus and calcium, farmers and feed mixers add a mineral source to their rations.

Where it is used

  • Poultry rations: layers, broilers and chicks
  • Pig rations
  • Rations for cattle, goats, sheep and other livestock

Different animals need different amounts at different stages, so the correct inclusion rate depends on the species, age and production level.

How to use it

Add Kynofos 21 Elite to the ration at the inclusion rate recommended on the label or by your vet or feed nutritionist. Mix it evenly through the feed, because an uneven mix can leave some animals with too little and others with too much. Use a proper mixer or mix in stages for small batches. Do not exceed the recommended rate.

Who it suits

The 25 kg bag suits farms that mix their own feed, small commercial poultry and pig units, and feed mixers who need a steady supply. For very small flocks, a smaller supplement may be more practical.

Good practice

  • Keep a record of the formulation for each batch of feed
  • Make sure calcium and phosphorus are balanced in the ration
  • Keep clean water available at all times
  • Ask a nutritionist for help with formulations

Safety

Read and follow the label before use. Keep away from children and pets. Avoid breathing dust when handling the powder, and wash your hands afterwards.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place off the floor, away from moisture, rodents and direct sunlight. Close the bag tightly after use.
