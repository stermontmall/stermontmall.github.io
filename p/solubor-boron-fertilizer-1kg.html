Solubor for correcting boron deficiency

Solubor is a highly soluble borate fertiliser listed for soil and foliar application. Boron is an essential micronutrient for plant growth, but crops need it in small quantities. The 1kg pack can suit growers treating a limited area or preparing measured foliar mixes where boron deficiency has been identified.

Boron has a narrow range between deficiency and excess. Do not increase the dose in an attempt to accelerate results, and do not assume that every crop or field requires boron. Check the product label for crop suitability and application rate, and use soil or plant analysis where possible.

  • Measure accurately with a suitable scale.
  • Confirm the recommended rate for the specific crop.
