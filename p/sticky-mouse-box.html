What this rodent-control product is for

This product is listed in relation to mice. Rodent activity may be indicated by droppings, gnaw marks, damaged food packaging, tracks, nesting material or scratching sounds. Inspect kitchens, stores, roof spaces and service openings without touching droppings or nesting material with bare hands.

Identifying whether the problem involves mice or larger rats helps determine whether the selected device or bait format is appropriate. Use the product only for its stated purpose.

Inspection and prevention

Start with inspection and prevention. Store food and animal feed in sealed, rodent-resistant containers. Clear spilled grain and rubbish, use covered bins and keep storage areas tidy.

Seal suitable gaps around doors, pipes and service entries. Remove clutter that can provide nesting places, while avoiding direct contact with droppings. These steps reduce food and shelter and make it easier to monitor activity.

Placement and monitoring

Choose placement points only after checking the product instructions and site conditions. Keep any trap or station stable, protected from accidental disturbance and inaccessible to children, pets and non-target animals. Inspect devices at the frequency stated by the manufacturer and record activity, servicing and any bait replenishment.

Do not leave a damaged station, exposed bait or a full capture device unattended. For recurring infestations or sensitive sites, seek advice from a qualified pest-control professional.

Safety and responsible handling

Where the product includes rodenticide, treat it as a hazardous pesticide even in a small pack. Keep it in its original labelled packaging, locked away from children, pets, food, feed and drinking water. Wear label-required protective equipment and wash hands after handling.

Never leave loose poison where people or animals can reach it. Dispose of dead or affected rodents only as directed by the label, preventing access by pets and wildlife. If exposure is suspected, follow the emergency instructions and seek urgent medical or veterinary advice.
