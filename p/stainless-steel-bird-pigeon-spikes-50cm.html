About Stainless Steel Bird and Pigeon Spikes – 50cm Strip

The Stainless Steel Bird and Pigeon Spikes – 50cm Strip is a physical bird-deterrent product designed to make selected ledges and narrow building surfaces less suitable for birds to perch or roost on. It is an exclusion option for property owners and facilities managers dealing with repeated pigeon activity around roofs, parapets, window ledges, signage, beams and similar surfaces. Rather than using poison or relying on repeated cleaning, a properly selected and installed spike strip changes the available landing space and encourages birds to use other locations.

Where Bird Spikes may help

Pigeons often return to familiar elevated resting points where they can land, shelter or access nesting locations. Common problem areas include shopfront ledges, balcony edges, warehouse beams, roof projections, air-conditioning platforms and building signs. A 50cm strip can be useful for measuring and planning a run along a narrow section. For longer areas, measure the full length and calculate the number of strips required. For broad ledges, corners or irregular surfaces, a site-specific layout may be needed. Spikes are not a complete solution where birds can simply move to an untreated adjacent surface.

Measure before ordering

Measure the length and width of the target surface before purchasing. Check the full footprint of the strip, not only its length, and allow for corners, joints and any gaps that could leave a landing point. This listing identifies one 50cm strip. Do not assume that multiple strips, adhesive, screws or other mounting accessories are included unless the product pack says so. Confirm the exact base design and fixing method with the supplier before installation, especially if the surface is painted, tiled, metal, concrete or exposed to weather.

Installation planning

A bird-deterrent strip needs a clean, stable and suitable fixing surface. Remove loose debris and bird droppings using appropriate hygiene precautions before installation. Choose a fixing method compatible with the surface and the manufacturer's instructions. Adhesives, screws, clips or other fittings are not interchangeable on every substrate. Do not install where the strip could fall onto people, vehicles or public walkways. Work at height should be carried out by a competent person using safe access equipment and suitable fall protection. Do not lean from windows or improvise roof access.

Humane and responsible Bird management

Bird spikes are intended to deter perching by reducing comfortable landing space; they should not be installed in a way that deliberately injures birds or traps them. Before installation, check whether birds are actively nesting and whether local wildlife rules or building requirements apply. If a nest is present, seek appropriate guidance before disturbing it. Keep the installation tidy and ensure sharp components are not exposed where people may touch them during routine maintenance.

Maintenance after installation

Inspect the strip periodically to confirm that it remains firmly attached and has not been bent, loosened or covered by debris. Remove leaves and nesting material safely so that the deterrent surface remains clear. Check the adjoining roof, ledge or sign for alternative perching points, because birds may relocate rather than leave the property entirely. If droppings continue to accumulate, review the whole building perimeter and consider combining physical deterrents with waste control, exclusion and professional bird-management advice.
