About Indoking 300SC Insecticide – 1 Litre

Greenlife Indoking 300SC Insecticide (1 Litre) Greenlife Indoking 300SC contains indoxacarb 300 g/L and is a suspension concentrate insecticide for control of bollworms, caterpillars and leaf miners on roses and other crops. It is supplied in a 1 litre pack, and the label dose and pre-harvest interval should be followed. KEY FEATURES • Indoxacarb 300 g/L suspension concentrate • Controls bollworms, caterpillars and leaf miners • For roses and other crops • 1 litre pack • Greenlife brand • Delivered to all 47 counties PESTS IT CONTROLS • Bollworms: caterpillars that bore into buds, flowers, fruits and pods and cause heavy damage • Caterpillars: chew leaves, shoots and flowers and lower crop quality • Leaf miners: larvae that tunnel inside leaves and leave pale winding trails For rose growers, even small damage can make flowers unsellable, so early control of chewing pests is important. Caterpillar damage is also a concern in vegetables and field crops. HOW IT WORKS Indoxacarb is taken in by caterpillars when they feed on or contact treated plant parts. It stops them feeding soon after and they then die, which helps protect buds, flowers and leaves from further damage. Good spray coverage is important because the pest must contact or eat the treated tissue. HOW TO USE IT Follow the label dose, water volume, spray interval and maximum number of applications. Scout the crop regularly, checking buds, shoots and the underside of leaves, and start spraying when pests first appear. Shake the container well, half-fill the tank with water, add the measured product, top up and agitate throughout. Spray evenly and thoroughly in calm weather, early morning or late afternoon. PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL Always follow the pre-harvest interval on the label before harvesting edible crops. For cut flowers, follow the label on re-entry and handling. RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT Pests can become harder to control if the same insecticide is used again and again. Rotate with insecticides from other chemical groups and keep to the number of applications allowed on the label. SAFETY Read the label before use and keep out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Keep away from food, water and pets. Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and fish, and avoid spraying where bees are active. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
