How to use

1. Open the three legs and spread them wide on flat ground.

2. Lift the sections to the height you want and lock each clip firmly.

3. Place your phone in the clamp, tighten it and adjust the angle.

4. Switch on Bluetooth on your phone, pair the remote, then press the remote button to take a photo or start recording.

Keep the legs wide when the tripod is fully extended, and avoid using it in strong wind or on slippery surfaces.
