How to Use

Open the three legs and spread them wide on flat ground. Lift the sections to the height you want and lock each clip firmly. Place your phone in the clamp, tighten it and adjust the angle. Pair the remote by switching on Bluetooth on your phone, then press the remote button to take a photo or start recording. Keep the legs wide when the tripod is fully extended, and avoid using it in strong wind or on slippery surfaces.

Care Tips

Wipe the stand with a dry cloth and keep sand and dust out of the locking clips. Collapse the sections slowly when packing, and store the tripod in a dry place. Replace the remote battery when it stops responding. Do not overload the clamp with heavy equipment, and check the clamp is tight each time you mount your phone.
