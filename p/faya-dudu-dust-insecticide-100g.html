INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT A lasting pest-management plan combines inspection, sanitation, exclusion, monitoring and carefully selected treatments. Store food in sealed containers, clean spills and crumbs promptly, empty rubbish bins regularly and remove clutter that provides hiding places. Repair leaks and reduce unnecessary moisture. Seal suitable cracks and gaps around pipes, doors and service entries. For ants, trace trails and look for likely access points. For cockroaches, inspect behind appliances, under sinks and around drains. For fleas, assess animal resting places and seek veterinary advice for treatment of pets; a household insecticide must not be applied to an animal unless its label specifically permits that use. Monitor treated locations and record pest activity. Avoid repeated applications simply because pests are seen immediately after treatment; follow the label's monitoring and retreatment directions. Do not combine different pesticides or use multiple products together unless their labels and a qualified professional confirm compatibility. Repeated reliance on the same active ingredients can contribute to reduced susceptibility, so professional programmes should consider resistance management and non-chemical controls. SAFE HANDLING AND PERSONAL PROTECTION Read the entire label before opening the pack. Wear the protective equipment specified by the manufacturer, avoid breathing dust and prevent contact with skin and eyes. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling pesticides. Keep unprotected people and animals away from the treatment area. Wash hands and exposed skin after handling and follow label instructions for cleaning tools and clothing. Do not use domestic food containers for measuring or storing pesticide. If accidental exposure or suspected poisoning occurs, follow the first-aid directions on the pack and seek prompt medical advice. Take the container or label when seeking assistance.
