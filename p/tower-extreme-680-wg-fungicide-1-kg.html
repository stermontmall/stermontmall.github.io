Why Choose Tower Extreme 680 WG

Blight and downy mildew can destroy a potato, onion or rose crop in just days when the weather is cool, wet and humid. Tower Extreme combines two active ingredients. Mancozeb protects the surface of the leaf against spores, while metalaxyl-M moves inside the plant to stop infections that have already started. This mix of protective, curative and systemic action gives growers a wider window to control disease than a purely protective product.

Resistance Management and Good Practice

Fungicides containing metalaxyl-type ingredients work best when they are used as part of a rotation with other fungicide groups, and not applied repeatedly on their own. Follow the label on the number of sprays and the spray interval. Plant healthy seed or seedlings, space plants for airflow, avoid watering late in the day and remove infected plant material. Spray when disease risk is high or at the first signs of infection.
