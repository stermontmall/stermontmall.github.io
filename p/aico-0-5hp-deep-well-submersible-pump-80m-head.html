This pump may suit a compatible domestic borehole or deep well, a farm water source or a water-supply installation that needs a submersible pump with an 80m maximum head. It may be used to transfer water to a storage tank or support light agricultural use where the delivered flow meets demand. It should not be chosen on head rating alone; the actual flow at the required lift is essential for sizing a household or irrigation system. BOREHOLE AND PIPEWORK CHECKS Measure the borehole casing diameter and confirm the pump's physical diameter before purchase. The supplier lists the outlet as 1 inch or 1.

25 inches depending on model, so ask which exact variant is offered. Estimate the pumping water level while the borehole is operating, not only its total depth. Include the vertical rise to the tank and friction losses from the pipe, bends, valves and fittings.

Ask the supplier for a pump curve or a written flow estimate at the intended operating head. Ensure the pump is not placed deeper than the listed submergence limit. ELECTRICAL INSTALLATION The stated supply is 220–240V AC, 50Hz, single phase.

A qualified electrician should verify the supply, cable sizing, earthing, overload protection and isolation before installation. Confirm whether a control box is supplied or required, and whether the cable length suits the borehole. Keep electrical joints dry and protected from water.

Do not connect the pump through improvised wiring or undersized extension leads. Follow the manufacturer's installation and operating instructions. SAFE USE AND MAINTENANCE Use a suitable support rope to lower and retrieve the pump; never lift it by its electrical cable.

Ensure the intake remains submerged and do not allow dry running unless the manufacturer expressly permits it. Inspect the cable, pipework, non-return valve and electrical protection periodically. If output falls, check for a low water level, blocked intake, pipe leakage, excessive friction loss or a supply-voltage problem.

Service the pump only as directed by the manufacturer.
