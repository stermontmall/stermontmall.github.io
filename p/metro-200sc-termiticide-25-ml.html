About Metro 200SC Termiticide – 25 ml

Subterranean termites live in the soil and travel through tunnels to reach fence posts, roof members and other wooden structures. Metro 200SC is a suspension concentrate (SC) termiticide containing imidacloprid 200 g/L, used for soil treatment against subterranean termites. It comes in a small 25 ml pack, a practical choice for homeowners, small construction jobs, wooden posts and fences.

Where it helps

A soil treatment places a protective zone between the termites and the structure you want to protect. Because Metro 200SC is non-repellent, odourless and colourless, termites do not easily detect and avoid the treated area, and nothing smells strongly in your home or compound. It suits soil treatment around small structures and the base of fence posts and timber before they go into the ground.

How to use it

Find the termite activity first: look for mud tubes on walls, hollow-sounding timber and soil mounds nearby. Then treat the soil thoroughly as directed on the label, and treat the base of fence posts and timber before they are placed in the ground. The label gives the dilution. A 25 ml pack is small, so measure carefully and mix only what the job needs.

Before you start

  • Remove rotting wood and tree stumps close to your building
  • Repair leaks that keep the ground damp
  • Keep firewood and stored timber off the ground
  • For a whole building or a large infestation, consider a licensed pest control operator

Safety

Check the PCPB registration number on the label, and mix and apply exactly as directed. Wear protective gear, keep Metro 200SC away from children, pets, food and water, and never contaminate water sources when you mix or spray.

Storage

Keep the 25 ml pack tightly closed in its original container, in a cool, dry place out of reach of children and away from food.

Treat the 25 ml size as a pack choice, not a DIY instruction

Metro 200SC is listed as an imidacloprid 200 g/L suspension-concentrate termiticide in a 25 ml pack. The catalogue mentions subterranean termites and soil or timber settings, but the current approved Kenyan label determines the exact authorised uses and restrictions. A small bottle does not make structural termite treatment simple or safe for an untrained user. Have a qualified pest-management professional inspect the site and confirm whether this product and formulation are appropriate.

Before ordering, check the label, manufacturer, seal, batch number and expiry date. Ask the professional to explain why the treatment is needed, what parts of the property are involved and whether non-chemical measures such as moisture correction, timber repair or exclusion can reduce the problem. Do not infer a treatment area or dilution from the pack size.

  • Confirm current registration and approved use for the exact product.
  • Use qualified help for soil, foundation or building treatments.
  • Do not estimate dilution, coverage or application frequency.

Keep people, pets and water sources protected

Store the sealed bottle in its original labelled packaging inside a locked pesticide cabinet, away from children, pets, food, feed and medicines. Prevent leaks and keep the product away from wells, drains, streams, fish ponds and water tanks. Handling and use must follow the current label's protective-equipment, first-aid, spill and disposal directions.

Before any professional treatment, identify water sources, drainage channels, gardens, play areas and occupied rooms so that the contractor can plan suitable safeguards. Request clear written advice about when occupants and pets may return and any post-treatment restrictions. Keep the label and service report. If a spill or suspected exposure occurs, follow the emergency instructions and seek professional assistance immediately.
