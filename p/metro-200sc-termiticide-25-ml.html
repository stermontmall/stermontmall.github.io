Tips for Better Termite Control

Start by finding the termite activity: look for mud tubes on walls, hollow-sounding timber and soil mounds nearby. Treat the soil thoroughly as directed on the label, and treat the base of fence posts and timber before they are placed in the ground. Remove rotting wood and tree stumps close to your building, repair leaks and keep firewood and stored timber off the ground. For large infestations or whole buildings, consider a licensed pest control operator.
