Where it helps

Use it for crop protection sprays, foliar feeding, weed control and pest control jobs on small and medium farms, kitchen gardens, greenhouses and nurseries. The adjustable nozzle lets you use a tight jet to reach a target and a wider cone for covering foliage, so one sprayer handles several tasks. Spraying sessions can be long, so the back-contoured tank and the steady pumping action are designed to reduce fatigue. Even coverage is what makes a product work properly, because missed patches become the place where pests and weeds return.

How to use it

Fill through the wide filling mouth, where the built-in strainer basket helps prevent nozzle clogging, add the product as its label directs, close the tank and pump to a steady pressure. Switch between the concentrated jet and the broad cone pattern with the adjustable nozzle, depending on whether you are aiming at a target or covering foliage. Keep a regular pace so that every row is covered evenly.

Before you start

  • Check the seals and the strainer basket, and clean the strainer if it clogs
  • If you can, keep one sprayer for herbicides and another for insecticides and fungicides, so that herbicide residue never reaches your crop
  • Read the chemical label for the dose and re-entry times

Safety

Always wear overalls, boots, gloves and a face mask when spraying agricultural chemicals. Follow the chemical label for dose and re-entry times. Keep the sprayer and all chemicals out of reach of children.

Storage

Rinse the tank thoroughly with clean water after every use, then spray clean water through the hose and nozzle so that no residue dries inside. Store the sprayer upside down in a shaded, dry place, away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures.
