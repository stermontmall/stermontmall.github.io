Greenlife Commander 240EC Herbicide (1 Litre) Greenlife Commander 240EC contains oxyfluorfen 240 g/L and is a pre-emergence and early post-emergence herbicide for control of grasses, broadleaf weeds and sedges in onions. It is an emulsifiable concentrate (EC) supplied in a 1 litre pack. KEY FEATURES • Oxyfluorfen 240 g/L emulsifiable concentrate • Pre-emergence and early post-emergence action • Controls grasses, broadleaf weeds and sedges • For onions • 1 litre pack • Greenlife brand WHY WEED CONTROL IN ONIONS MATTERS Onions have narrow upright leaves and a shallow root system, so they compete poorly with weeds. Weeds take light, water and nutrients, lower bulb size and yield, and make harvest and curing harder. Early weed control is especially important in the first weeks after the crop is established. PRE-EMERGENCE AND EARLY POST-EMERGENCE Pre-emergence means the product is applied to the soil before weeds come up, forming a layer that stops many weed seeds from establishing. Early post-emergence means it can also be used on very young weeds that have just emerged. Timing is important, so follow the crop stage and weed stage given on the label. HOW TO USE IT Follow the dose, water volume and application timing on the label. Prepare a fine, level seedbed, because even coverage helps good control. Use a clean, calibrated sprayer and apply evenly. Avoid spraying in windy conditions, and keep spray away from nearby crops that are not listed on the label. Do not apply to crops under stress from drought, waterlogging or disease. If you are using the product for the first time, test it on a small area to see how your crop and soil respond. GOOD PRACTICE • Identify the weeds in your field before choosing a herbicide • Combine chemical control with good land preparation and hand weeding for escapes • Respect the label limits on rate and number of applications • Keep records of dates, rates and weather SAFETY Read the label before use and keep out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Keep away from food, water and pets. Do not let spray or rinse water reach streams, ponds or wells. Rinse the sprayer thoroughly away from crops and water sources. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
