How to Use

Place the subwoofer on a stable surface, set the side speakers on both sides and the centre speaker on top, then connect the cables and plug in the power. Switch on and choose Bluetooth, USB, SD card, FM or AUX with the source button or the remote. Pair your phone by Bluetooth, or plug in your flash drive, and raise the volume slowly.

Care and Cleaning

Unplug before cleaning. Wipe the cabinets and display with a soft dry cloth. Never spray liquid on the system and keep it away from water, damp and direct heat. Leave space around the subwoofer so sound and heat can move freely.

Safety Tips

Do not play at very high volume for long periods, because it can damage your hearing and the speakers. Keep the system on a stable surface out of reach of children, do not open the cabinets and use a properly rated socket. Follow the user manual.
