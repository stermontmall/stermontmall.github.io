WHO THIS SPRAYER SUITS A 20-litre backpack sprayer can suit smallholder farmers, market gardeners, landscapers, grounds teams and pest-management operators who need a hand-operated unit rather than a powered machine. It is relevant for planned work on vegetables, ornamentals, shrubs, paths, fences and other areas where the selected liquid treatment is approved for that surface or crop. The backpack format keeps the tank close to the operator and leaves both hands available for controlling the lance and trigger. The amount of work possible from one tank depends on the nozzle setting, walking speed, treatment rate and area being covered. KEY MODEL FEATURES The supplier lists a 20-litre tank volume. This is the nominal capacity stated for the product; buyers should still follow the fill markings and leave any headspace required by the manufacturer. The stated pressure is 4.5 bar. Pressure influences how liquid is delivered, but the actual spray pattern and coverage also depend on the nozzle, liquid properties, pumping rhythm and operating condition. The Y-trigger is the control handle used to start and stop discharge. The fibreglass lance is the extension between the trigger and nozzle, allowing the user to direct the spray without holding the nozzle immediately beside the tank. ADJUSTABLE SPRAY PATTERN The listed nozzle can be adjusted from a jet to a mist. A jet gives a more concentrated stream, while a mist breaks the liquid into smaller droplets and spreads it over a wider area. Neither setting is automatically correct for every job. Select a pattern that matches the approved product label, target surface, weather and risk of drift. Fine droplets can move away from the target in wind, while a concentrated jet may cause splash or uneven coverage if used too close. Test the setting with clean water in a safe area before adding a treatment solution. USING IT RESPONSIBLY Before filling, inspect the tank, cap, hose, trigger, lance, nozzle and seals for cracks, leaks or loose connections. Confirm that the liquid is approved for the sprayer materials and the intended use. Measure and mix products exactly as directed by their labels; never estimate chemical concentrations or combine products unless the manufacturer expressly permits it. Fill in a ventilated area, avoid splashing and close the cap securely. Build pressure using the method in the supplied instructions, then apply evenly while keeping the nozzle directed away from people, animals, food, water sources and unintended plants. COMFORT AND WORK PLANNING A full 20-litre tank is heavy once the liquid and sprayer itself are included. Buyers should consider their strength, working duration and terrain before choosing this capacity. Adjust the shoulder straps so the tank sits securely and does not swing while walking. Take breaks when needed and avoid carrying a full tank up unstable ladders or steep, slippery ground. For small indoor jobs or spot treatments, a smaller hand sprayer may be easier to control. Storage & handling

After use, follow the treatment manufacturer's cleaning directions and the sprayer manual. Do not pour leftover chemical into drains, streams, ponds or household sinks. Dispose of unused mixture and rinse water according to the product label and local requirements. Where the label allows rinsing, use the recommended procedure and direct the rinse water only to an approved treatment area. Clean the nozzle and filter carefully so residue does not dry inside the passages. Store the sprayer empty, clean, dry and protected from direct sunlight, heat and freezing conditions. Keep it secured away from children, pets, food and animal feed.
