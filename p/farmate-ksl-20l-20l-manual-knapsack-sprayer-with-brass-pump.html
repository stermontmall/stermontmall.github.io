Product overview

The Farmate KSL-20L is a 20-litre manual knapsack sprayer with a brass piston pump and two supplied nozzles. MyAgrovet lists it for crop protection, livestock care and general cleaning tasks where a compatible liquid can be applied with a manual backpack sprayer. The product has a hand-operated pump lever, allowing the user to build pressure without a petrol engine or battery. Its stated tank capacity and nozzle options make it a practical choice for mixed farm use, provided each treatment is approved for the target and the equipment materials. BRASS PISTON PUMP AND PRESSURE The supplier describes an internally mounted brass piston pump designed to reduce external leakage during use. It lists a maximum pressure of up to 65 PSI and says that five pumps can bring the system to approximately 45 PSI. These are the figures stated on the supplier page; actual performance depends on the condition of seals, the pump, liquid path and nozzle. Pump steadily and follow the manual rather than forcing the lever. If the sprayer cannot hold pressure, leaks around the fittings or the lever becomes unusually difficult to operate, stop and inspect the unit before continuing. TWO NOZZLES FOR DIFFERENT APPLICATIONS The Farmate KSL-20L is supplied with an adjustable brass cone nozzle and a plastic fan nozzle. The supplier describes the brass cone as intended for pesticide spraying and the fan nozzle for herbicide application. These nozzles create different spray patterns and should be kept separate and clearly identified. Herbicide residue can damage sensitive crops if the same nozzle or sprayer is later used on vegetables or other plants. Clean the equipment thoroughly according to the label and manual before changing application type. Never assume that the nozzle alone makes an unapproved chemical suitable for use. 20-LITRE TANK AND DIMENSIONS The nominal tank capacity is 20 litres. The supplier lists an empty weight of approximately 3kg and a full weight of approximately 23kg. It also gives a tank opening of 125mm and dimensions of approximately 390mm long, 170mm wide and 630mm high. The wide opening can make filling and cleaning easier, while the tank size can reduce repeated refill trips on larger jobs. A full tank is still a substantial load. Adjust the straps before lifting, fill only what is needed and avoid carrying the sprayer over steep or unstable terrain. TANK MATERIAL AND CHEMICAL COMPATIBILITY The supplier identifies the tank as polyethylene and describes it as resistant to agricultural chemicals. Compatibility must nevertheless be checked for every product used. Certain formulations, solvents or concentrates may have restrictions on equipment materials, seals or application methods. Read the chemical label and sprayer instructions before mixing. Do not use the tank for drinking water, food or animal feed after it has been used for agricultural chemicals. Label the sprayer clearly and store it separately from household containers. WHERE IT CAN BE USED The product page lists crop protection, livestock care and general cleaning among its application areas. On crops, use only registered products whose labels permit application with a manual knapsack sprayer. For livestock or premises cleaning, check that the formulation is specifically approved for the intended animal, surface or environment and follow all dilution, contact-time and rinsing instructions. The sprayer is not a substitute for correct product selection. Avoid spraying people or animals directly unless the treatment label expressly permits that use and gives the required procedure. PREPARATION AND SAFE OPERATION Before each job, inspect the tank, lid, straps, pump lever, hose, trigger, nozzles and seals. Test the unit with clean water and check for leaks. Measure and mix treatments according to the label, using suitable measuring equipment. Wear the required gloves, eye protection, protective clothing and respiratory protection. Keep children, bystanders, pets, livestock, food, animal feed and drinking-water sources away from the treatment area. Avoid windy conditions that can carry droplets off target. Maintain a steady walking pace and do not exceed the label's application rate. CLEANING AND MAINTENANCE After spraying, manage leftover solution and rinse water according to the formulation label and local disposal requirements. Never pour chemical residues into drains, streams, ponds or drinking-water sources. Clean the tank, pump, hose, filter and both nozzles according to the sprayer manual. Keep the pesticide and herbicide nozzles separate, label them clearly and flush the system thoroughly before changing products. Do not clear a blockage by blowing through a nozzle. Store the sprayer empty, clean and dry in a secure shaded area away from children, pets, food and animal feed. BUYER CHECKLIST AND
