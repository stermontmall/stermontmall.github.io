Revus 250SC

Revus 250SC is a fungicide for crop disease control that helps protect plants from fungal infections. Used in a regular spray programme, it supports yield and quality.

  • Formulation: Suspension concentrate (SC)
  • Application settings: Agricultural: field crops (crops and diseases to be confirmed on the label)

Mixing & application instructions

1. Dilution rate: use the dose and water volume printed on the manufacturer's label for your crop and growth stage.

2. Application method: Mix and apply exactly as the manufacturer's label directs, shaking the container well and agitating the tank while you spray. Never mix with other products unless the label allows it.

3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Check the pack for its size.

Safety & re-entry precautions

  • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.
