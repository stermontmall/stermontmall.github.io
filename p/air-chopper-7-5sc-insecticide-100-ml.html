Where it helps

Bed bugs, cockroaches and ants are among the most common household pests, and they thrive where there are cracks, crevices and warm, dark hiding places. Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames and wall joints and feed at night. Cockroaches shelter in kitchens, drains and behind cupboards. Ants follow food and trails into rooms and stores. The 100 ml pack gives a handy size for treating a single house, a few rooms or a small hostel block without buying a large container.

Getting the best results

  • Clean first: wash bedding and vacuum mattresses, floors and corners
  • Use the dilution and spraying instructions on the label
  • Spray cracks, crevices, bed frames, skirting boards and other hiding places
  • Keep people, pets and food away from treated areas until the label says it is safe
  • Repeat treatment if the label advises it, and keep the area clean afterwards
  • For large or persistent infestations, consider a licensed pest control operator

Safety

Read and follow the label before use. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle.

Storage

Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place.
