Where & How to Use in Kenya

Bed bugs, cockroaches and ants thrive where there are cracks, crevices and warm, dark hiding places. Bed bugs shelter in mattress seams, bed frames and wall joints and feed at night. Cockroaches settle in kitchens, drains and behind cupboards.

Ants follow food and trails into rooms and stores. The range of packs lets you match the size to the job, from a smaller pack for a single home to a larger bottle for a hostel block.

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Read the label and follow it before use. Keep the product away from children, pets, food and water. Wear gloves and a mask while mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards.

Never pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle. Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food. Use this product only for its intended purpose and follow the complete manufacturer instructions.

If the item is a pesticide or chemical, the current Kenya-approved product label takes precedence: follow its authorised use, PPE, application, ventilation, re-entry, storage, first-aid and disposal directions exactly. Do not improvise chemical mixtures or application rates. Keep products and active traps or devices away from children, pets, food and animal feed.

For professional fumigation or restricted-use treatments, use appropriately trained and authorised pest-management personnel.
