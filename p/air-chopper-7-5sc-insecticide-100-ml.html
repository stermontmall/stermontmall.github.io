Uses & applications

Bed bugs, cockroaches and ants thrive where there are cracks, crevices and warm, dark hiding places. Bed bugs shelter in mattress seams, bed frames and wall joints and feed at night. Cockroaches settle in kitchens, drains and behind cupboards. Ants follow food and trails into rooms and stores. The range of packs lets you match the size to the job, from a smaller pack for a single home to a larger bottle for a hostel block.

How to use

Read the label first and prepare the dilution exactly as it directs. Spray cracks, crevices, bed frames, skirting boards and the other places where the pests hide or walk. Keep people, pets and food away from treated areas until the label says it is safe to return. Repeat the treatment if the label advises it, and keep the area clean afterwards so that the pests have less to come back for.

Before you start

  • Clean first: wash bedding and vacuum mattresses, floors and corners
  • Remove food, and cover utensils and fish tanks before spraying
  • Treat all the affected rooms in the same visit so the pests do not simply move next door
  • Call a licensed pest control operator for a large or persistent infestation

Safety information

Read the label and follow it before use. Keep the product away from children, pets, food and water. Wear gloves and a mask while mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Never pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle.

Storage & handling

Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place, away from food.
