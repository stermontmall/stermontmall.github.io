Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames and wall joints and feed at night. Cockroaches shelter in kitchens, drains and behind cupboards. Ants follow food and trails into rooms and stores. The 100 ml pack gives a handy size for treating a single house, a few rooms or a small hostel block without buying a large container.
