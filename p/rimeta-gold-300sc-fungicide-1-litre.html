Why Choose a Dual-Action Fungicide

Botrytis, also known as grey mould, spreads fast in cool, humid greenhouses and can ruin rose buds and flowers before they reach the market. Early blight and soft rot cause leaf spots, rotting and heavy losses in tomatoes and French beans. A product that works both systemically (inside the plant) and by contact (on the surface) gives wider protection, and using two different active ingredients supports better resistance management when rotated with other fungicide groups.

Disease Management Tips

Spray early, at the first sign of disease or when conditions favour it, rather than waiting until the crop is badly infected. Improve air movement with good spacing and ventilation, avoid wetting flowers and leaves late in the day, remove infected plant parts and clean tools. Follow the label on the number of sprays and rotate with fungicides from other groups.

Safe use of Original Rimeta Gold 300SC Fungicide – 1 Litre

  • Read the whole label before you buy or mix. The label is the legal instruction for crops, pests, rates and safety.
  • Look for the PCPB registration number on the pack. Do not buy chemicals that carry none.
  • Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI) printed on the label, the number of days that must pass between the last spray and harvest. Use the product only on the crops the label lists.
  • Wear gloves, a mask or respirator, eye protection and long protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards.
  • Keep chemicals in their original container, out of reach of children, pets and livestock. Never reuse empty containers for food or water.
