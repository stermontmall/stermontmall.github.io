About Rimeta Gold 300SC Fungicide (1L) – 1 Litre

Rimeta Gold 300SC combines carbendazim and pyrimethanil in a dual-action systemic and contact fungicide. It controls botrytis in roses and early blight and soft rot in tomatoes and French beans, protecting flowers and vegetables before they reach the market.

Key specifications & features

  • Active ingredient: Carbendazim + Pyrimethanil
  • Target: Botrytis (grey mould) in roses; early blight and soft rot in tomatoes and French beans
  • Formulation: Suspension concentrate (SC)
  • Application settings: Agricultural: flower farms, greenhouses, tomato and French bean growers

Mixing & application instructions

1. Dilution rate: use the dose and water volume printed on the manufacturer's label for your crop and growth stage.

2. Application method: Mix as the label directs and spray evenly over leaves, flowers and fruits at the first sign of disease or when conditions favour it.

3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Available in 1 Litre.

4. Improve air movement with good spacing and ventilation, avoid wetting flowers and leaves late in the day, remove infected plant parts and clean tools.

5. Follow the label on the number of sprays and rotate with fungicides from other chemical groups.

Safety & re-entry precautions

  • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.
