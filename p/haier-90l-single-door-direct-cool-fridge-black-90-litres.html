With a 90 litre capacity, this fridge suits bedsitters, studio apartments, students, single people, couples, hostels, offices and small shops. It takes up little floor space and is a good second fridge for drinks and snacks.

What Direct Cool means

In a direct cool fridge, cooling comes from the freezer compartment and the walls, rather than from a fan. The freezer may build up frost over time, so it needs occasional defrosting. This design is simple and can make the fridge easier to maintain.

Built for unstable power

Voltage can rise and fall in many areas. The wide-range compressor is designed to cope with unstable power, so the fridge keeps working when the voltage is not steady. For extra protection, a surge protector is a good idea.
