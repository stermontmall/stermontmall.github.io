About Pentagon 50EC Insecticide 1 Litre

Thrips and aphids suck sap, curl and scar leaves, and spread plant viruses. Pentagon 50EC uses lambda-cyhalothrin, a fast-acting synthetic pyrethroid, to knock these pests down quickly and protect your yield. It is an affordable choice for vegetable, flower and field crop growers.

Mixing & application instructions

1. Use the dose and water volume printed on the label for your crop and growth stage.

2. Mix with water to form a milky spray liquid, as the label directs.

3. Scout your crop regularly and spray when pests reach damaging levels.

4. Avoid spraying in strong wind. Keep spray away from ponds, rivers and neighbouring crops.

5. Rotate with products that have a different mode of action, and do not spray when bees are foraging.

Safety & re-entry precautions

  • Wear gloves, a mask or respirator, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried. Follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells.
  • Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.
  • Check for the PCPB registration number on the pack. Always read the label before use.
