About Blue-Touch Jumbo Rat Glue Traps – 2 Pack

Blue-Touch Jumbo Rat Glue Traps are adhesive capture boards supplied as a two-piece pack for selected indoor rodent-monitoring situations. The ready-to-use sticky surface is designed to retain a rat or mouse that contacts it. The product is a non-bait adhesive trap and does not contain rodenticide. Glue boards are not appropriate for every site or every situation, and their use must consider animal welfare, children, pets and non-target wildlife.

How an adhesive rodent trap works

The board uses a high-tack adhesive surface to hold an animal that steps onto it. Unlike a spring trap, it has no mechanical striking bar. Unlike a bait station, it does not require rodenticide. The supplier listing describes Blue-Touch Jumbo glue traps as ready to use and disposable. The exact board dimensions, adhesive formulation and whether the trap is foldable should be confirmed from the current pack. Do not assume that a glue board attracts rodents from a distance; placement must be based on signs of activity.

Where to consider placement

Before using an adhesive trap, assess the site for rodent activity and consider whether this capture method is suitable. Look for droppings, gnaw marks, rub marks and damaged food packaging. If the product is used, place it only in a protected location where children, pets and non-target animals cannot reach it. Avoid areas where birds, reptiles or other wildlife may become trapped. Do not place the board near food, utensils or food-contact surfaces. In some settings, a secured mechanical trap or professional control programme may be more appropriate.

Inspection and welfare

Inspect adhesive traps frequently and never leave them unattended for extended periods. An animal caught on a glue board may suffer distress or injury. Follow local animal-welfare guidance and the product instructions for checking, handling and disposal. Do not attempt improvised removal methods that could cause further injury. If a non-target animal is caught, seek appropriate professional advice. If you are unsure whether glue boards are permitted or suitable in your location, select another method after consulting a qualified pest-management professional.

Handling and disposal

Avoid touching the adhesive surface with bare hands. Keep boards in their original packaging until use and store them away from heat, dust, children and pets. Once used, handle the board carefully and dispose of it according to the supplier instructions and local waste requirements. Wear suitable gloves when handling used boards and wash hands after service. Do not reuse a board once it is contaminated, dusty or has captured an animal.

Reduce rodent access and food sources

Adhesive traps alone do not resolve the conditions that attract rodents. Store food and animal feed in sealed containers, keep rubbish covered, clean spills and remove clutter. Inspect doors, walls, pipes and service penetrations for gaps and seal suitable openings. Check storerooms and incoming goods for signs of activity. If rodent signs persist, investigate the source and consider an integrated programme that combines proofing, sanitation and suitable monitoring.
