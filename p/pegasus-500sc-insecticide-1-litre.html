Why Choose Pegasus 500SC

Diamondback moth, whiteflies and spider mites are among the toughest pests on Kenyan vegetable farms. They multiply fast in warm, dry weather and can quickly damage leaves, reduce yields and lower the market value of cabbage, kale and other vegetables. Pests also develop resistance when the same chemical is used again and again. A product with a different active ingredient, used in rotation with others, helps keep your pest control programme working.

Integrated Pest Management Tips

Scout your fields at least weekly and spray when pests reach damaging levels rather than on a fixed calendar alone. Rotate insecticides with different modes of action, use clean seedlings, remove crop residues after harvest and encourage natural enemies where possible. Spray in the early morning or late afternoon and make sure the spray reaches the underside of leaves, where whiteflies and mites hide.

Safe use of Pegasus 500SC Insecticide – 1 Litre

  • Read the whole label before you buy or mix. The label is the legal instruction for crops, pests, rates and safety.
  • Look for the PCPB registration number on the pack. Do not buy chemicals that carry none.
  • Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI) printed on the label, the number of days that must pass between the last spray and harvest. Use the product only on the crops the label lists.
  • Wear gloves, a mask or respirator, eye protection and long protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards.
  • Keep chemicals in their original container, out of reach of children, pets and livestock. Never reuse empty containers for food or water.
