Product Fit and Crop Nutrition Planning

A balanced NPK foliar feed can be considered as one component of a crop nutrition programme where the crop needs nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium together. It does not replace soil fertility planning, basal fertilizer or other crop-specific management. Consider the crop stage and existing nutrient programme before choosing a foliar product.

A foliar fertilizer is one possible part of crop nutrition management, not a substitute for understanding the crop, soil and water conditions. Weak growth can have several causes, including nutrient shortage, root damage, poor drainage, irregular watering, unsuitable soil pH, pests or disease. Select a product because its confirmed label information matches an identified need.

Where the cause is uncertain, seek advice from a qualified agronomist or agricultural extension officer.
