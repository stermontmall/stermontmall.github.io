COCKROACH CONTROL AND SITE INSPECTION Cockroaches often shelter in warm, dark and protected spaces close to food, water and waste. Common inspection points include cabinet hinges, gaps behind appliances, pipe penetrations, floor-wall junctions, service ducts, waste storage areas and cracks around plumbing.

A careful inspection helps distinguish a localized infestation from a building-wide problem. It also helps identify moisture, food residues and access points that need to be addressed alongside any treatment. The supplier recommends placement around likely hiding areas, but the exact placement method and quantity must be taken from the current label.

Do not apply the paste to food, food-contact surfaces, utensils, children's play areas, pet bowls or surfaces where it could be accidentally transferred to people or animals unless the label expressly permits the use. Keep it out of reach of children and pets. Do not treat every surface indiscriminately; targeted placement in label-approved locations is safer and makes monitoring easier.

INTEGRATED COCKROACH MANAGEMENT Effective cockroach management combines sanitation, exclusion, monitoring and an appropriately selected treatment. Store food in sealed containers, clean crumbs and grease, empty bins regularly, remove cardboard clutter and repair leaks. Seal gaps around pipes and service entries where practical.

Use monitoring devices where appropriate to establish where activity is greatest and whether the infestation is declining. In multi-unit housing, restaurants, shops and offices, coordinate treatment across connected rooms or units when inspection indicates that cockroaches are moving between them. Avoid using repellent sprays directly around bait placements unless the product label and a qualified pest-control professional confirm compatibility.

Some sprays can discourage pests from reaching bait placements or interfere with a baiting programme. Rotate control approaches responsibly and avoid repeated use of products with the same active ingredient or mode of action where resistance is a concern. For persistent infestations, consult a licensed pest-control provider.
