Where it helps

Mosquitoes bite in the evening and at night and can carry disease. Flies land on food and rubbish and spread germs to kitchens and dining areas. Cockroaches hide in cracks, behind cupboards and under sinks and come out in the dark. An aerosol is convenient for quick, targeted treatment of these pests as soon as you see them.

How to use it

Spray into corners, cracks and around doors and windows, where insects enter and hide. For flying insects, spray into the air in the room and then close the door and window for a while, as the label directs. For cockroaches, spray along skirting boards, behind appliances and in cracks, taking care not to spray food or cooking surfaces. Keep food and people away while spraying, and wait until the room is ventilated before re-entering.

Before you Spray

  • Cover or remove food, dishes, cooking utensils and baby items
  • Cover fish tanks and move pets and birds out of the room
  • Open windows and doors afterwards to let fresh air in

For best results

Keep the home clean and dry. Take out rubbish daily, fix leaking taps and block gaps around pipes. Use mosquito nets and window screens, and clear stagnant water around the compound so mosquitoes have fewer places to breed.

Safety

  • Read the label before use
  • Do not spray towards people, pets or food
  • Do not spray near flames, cookers, heaters or sparks, because aerosol cans are flammable
  • Do not puncture or burn the can, even when empty
  • Do not leave the can in direct sunlight or a hot car
  • Keep out of reach of children

Storage

Store upright in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place away from heat and children.
