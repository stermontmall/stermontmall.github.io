Choosing the Correct Equipment

Before purchase, confirm whether the listing is for a complete sprayer or a replacement component. Check the supplied lance, nozzle, hose, straps, pump assembly, battery, charger or engine accessories where relevant. For replacement components, compare connection dimensions and the sprayer model.

For powered equipment, confirm fuel or battery requirements and whether routine service parts are available locally. Tank capacity alone does not determine the correct machine; also consider weight, reach, working pressure and the size of the area being treated.

Pre-Use Inspection and Setup

Before filling, inspect the tank for cracks and check that the cap, hose, seals, trigger, lance and nozzle are fitted correctly. Test the unit with clean water first, away from people, animals, food and water sources. Check for leaks and confirm that the spray pattern is even.

Do not exceed manufacturer pressure or capacity limits. Calibrate output with clean water and the equipment instructions before introducing any treatment solution. Never use a damaged pressure vessel, worn hose or leaking connection.

Operating Guidance

During operation, maintain a stable posture and keep the nozzle directed toward the intended treatment area. Avoid spraying into wind, toward people or animals, or near open food and drinking water. Use only a formulation compatible with the sprayer materials and explicitly labelled for the target use.

Do not mix products unless their labels expressly permit it. Never estimate chemical dosage from tank size alone; follow the label rate and the area being treated. Stop work if weather conditions create unsafe drift.

Safety and Responsible Handling

Wear the personal protective equipment stated on the pesticide or fertiliser label, which may include gloves, eye protection, protective clothing, boots or respiratory protection. Keep children, pets and bystanders away from the work area. For petrol equipment, observe engine and fuel safety instructions and allow hot components to cool before refuelling or maintenance.

For battery equipment, keep the battery and charger dry and use specified charging equipment. Depressurise the system before opening the tank or disconnecting components.

Cleaning, Maintenance and Storage

After use, follow the chemical label and equipment manual for rinsing, cleaning and disposal of wash water. Do not discharge chemical residues into drains, ponds, rivers or drinking-water sources. Clean filters and nozzles with suitable tools rather than blowing through them by mouth.

Inspect seals, hoses, straps, pump parts and fasteners regularly. Store the unit clean and dry where appropriate, protected from direct sun, dust and unauthorised access. Keep engine fuel and battery packs stored according to their manuals.
