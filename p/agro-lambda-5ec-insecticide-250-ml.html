About Agro-Lambda 5EC Insecticide

Agro-Lambda 5EC Insecticide (250 ml) Agro-Lambda 5EC is a lambda-cyhalothrin insecticide in an emulsifiable concentrate (EC) formulation, supplied in a 250 ml bottle. Vegetable growers use it to control aphids and thrips on tomatoes and other vegetables. Both pests weaken plants, spoil fruit quality and can spread disease if left unchecked. HOW IT WORKS Lambda-cyhalothrin belongs to the pyrethroid group of insecticides. It acts on contact and by ingestion, so pests are affected when they touch sprayed surfaces or feed on treated leaves. This makes it a practical choice for quick knockdown of visible pests. As an emulsifiable concentrate, it mixes into water in the spray tank to give an even spray. WHERE IT FITS ON YOUR FARM Aphids and thrips build up quickly in warm, dry weather and hide on the underside of leaves and inside flowers. Scout your crop regularly and start protection when you first see them, rather than waiting for heavy damage. The 250 ml pack suits small and medium plots, kitchen gardens and growers who want to try a product before buying larger sizes. USING IT SAFELY AND WELL • Follow the label dose and pre-harvest interval exactly • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and long protective clothing when mixing and spraying • Spray early morning or late afternoon, and avoid windy or rainy conditions • Pyrethroids are harmful to bees and fish. Do not spray open flowers where bees are working, and keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and fish • Rotate with insecticides from different chemical groups to slow resistance STORAGE Keep in the original, tightly closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food, feed, children and pets. Never reuse the empty container. Rinse and dispose of it as directed on the label.
