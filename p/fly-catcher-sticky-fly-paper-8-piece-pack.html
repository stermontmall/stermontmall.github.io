Product overview

The Fly Catcher Sticky Fly Paper 8 Piece Pack is a simple, ready-to-use monitoring and trapping accessory for homes, food-handling businesses, stores and professional pest management programmes in Kenya. The pack is listed as eight pieces by the supplier. Each strip uses a gummed adhesive surface to catch small flying insects that land on it. Unlike an aerosol spray, this product works as a passive physical trap: it does not require a pump, electrical connection or mixing of a liquid formulation. It can be used as part of an integrated pest management plan alongside sanitation, exclusion, waste control and inspection. WHAT THE PACK IS This listing is for an eight-piece pack of sticky fly catcher paper. The supplier describes the product as rolled, gummed sticky paper intended to attract, trap and retain flying insects, including common house flies and mosquitoes. The listing does not state the dimensions of each strip, adhesive life, replacement interval, colour, active attractant ingredient or a measured capture capacity. Buyers who need a particular roll length or hanging fitting should confirm those details before ordering. No unverified chemical ingredient or guaranteed catch rate is claimed here. WHERE IT MAY BE USED Sticky fly paper can provide supplementary monitoring in places where flying insects are regularly noticed. Potential settings include kitchens and dining areas, shops, food stores, butcheries, hotel service areas, garages, patios, stables and suitable sheltered spaces around a home. In commercial premises, placement should be selected carefully so the trap does not hang over exposed food, preparation surfaces, utensils or customer contact areas. Follow the premises' hygiene procedures and any applicable food-safety requirements. HOW TO SET IT UP Open the pack carefully and handle the adhesive surface by its edges. The supplier's directions are to pull out the ribbon and unroll it slowly, then hang it in a location where flies are frequently observed. Use a suitable hanging point that keeps the strip stable and away from people, pets, fans, strong draughts and direct contact with food or clean equipment. Avoid placing it where a person may walk into it or where clothing, hair or packaging can touch the adhesive. If a strip becomes dusty, overloaded or loses tack, replace it rather than trying to wash and reuse it. PLACEMENT AND MONITORING Start by inspecting the premises to identify likely fly entry routes and attractants. Check doors, windows, waste bins, drains, food residues, animal areas and outdoor organic waste. A sticky strip is most useful when placed near observed activity without becoming a contamination risk. Do not assume that one strip will protect a whole room or eliminate an infestation. Record where strips are placed and inspect them routinely. A rising catch count can signal that sanitation, screening, waste handling or professional inspection needs attention. Keep a simple date and location record when using several traps across a hotel, shop or warehouse. INTEGRATED FLY MANAGEMENT Physical traps are only one part of effective fly management. Keep rubbish in covered bins, remove food spills promptly, clean drains and waste areas, store food in closed containers and repair gaps in doors or window screens. Manage standing water and organic waste where appropriate. For persistent activity, identify the fly species and its breeding source before selecting further control measures. Chemical treatments, where necessary, should be chosen and applied only by competent persons in accordance with the product label and applicable Kenyan requirements. A sticky fly catcher should not be represented as a substitute for source reduction or a professional pest assessment.

Safety information

AND HYGIENE The supplier describes this product as a sticky paper trap and states that it contains no toxins. The listing does not provide a laboratory safety sheet, so users should not infer that it is suitable for every sensitive environment. Keep unused strips in their packaging and out of reach of children and pets. Prevent the adhesive from contacting skin, hair, clothing, food or food-contact surfaces. If adhesive contacts skin, follow the supplier's removal guidance and avoid using harsh solvents near eyes or irritated skin. Dispose of used strips in a sealed waste bag in accordance with site hygiene procedures. Wash hands after handling used traps.
