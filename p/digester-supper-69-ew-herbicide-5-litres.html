About Digester Supper 69 EW Herbicide – 5 Litres

Digester Supper 69 EW Herbicide (5 Litres) Digester Supper 69 EW contains fenoxaprop-p-ethyl 69 g/L and is a post-emergence herbicide for control of annual grass weeds in wheat. It is supplied in a 5 litre pack by Greenlife Crop Protection Africa, which suits wheat growers treating larger areas. KEY FEATURES • Fenoxaprop-p-ethyl 69 g/L in an EW (emulsion in water) formulation • Post-emergence herbicide • Controls annual grass weeds in wheat • 5 litre pack for larger acreages • Greenlife Crop Protection Africa • Delivered to all 47 counties WHY GRASS WEED CONTROL IN WHEAT MATTERS Annual grass weeds look similar to wheat in the early stages and compete strongly with it for light, water and nutrients. If left uncontrolled they reduce tillering, lower yield and make harvest harder, and weed seeds can contaminate harvested grain. A grass-weed herbicide that is safe on wheat when used as directed lets the crop grow with less competition. WHAT POST-EMERGENCE MEANS Post-emergence means the product is sprayed on weeds that have already come up and are actively growing. It is absorbed through the leaves, so weeds must be growing and have enough leaf area at the time of spraying. Timing matters: spray when the grass weeds are at the stage and the wheat at the growth stage shown on the label. HOW TO USE IT Follow the dose, water volume and timing on the label. Use a clean, well-calibrated sprayer so the field gets an even application, and do not overlap passes. Spray in calm weather, avoid windy or rainy conditions, and avoid spraying when the crop is stressed by drought, frost or waterlogging. Keep spray away from neighbouring grass crops such as maize, barley, sorghum and pasture grasses that you do not want to damage. THE 5 LITRE PACK The 5 litre container is suited to commercial wheat growers and farms that spray large areas. If you only need a small amount, store the remainder carefully in its original container. SAFETY Read the label before use and keep locked out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Do not let spray or rinse water reach streams, ponds or wells. Rinse the sprayer thoroughly away from crops and water sources. STORAGE Store in the original closed container in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
