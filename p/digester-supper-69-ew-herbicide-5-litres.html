About Digester Supper 69 EW Herbicide – 5 Litres

Digester Supper 69 EW Herbicide (5 Litres)

Digester Supper 69 EW is a post-emergence herbicide that controls annual grass weeds in wheat. Its 5 litre pack suits commercial wheat growers treating larger areas.

Key specifications & features

  • Active ingredient: Fenoxaprop-p-ethyl 69 g/L
  • Target: Annual grass weeds in wheat
  • Formulation: Emulsion in water (EW)
  • Application settings: Agricultural: wheat fields, larger acreages

Mixing & application instructions

1. Dilution rate: use the dose and water volume printed on the manufacturer's label for your crop and growth stage.

2. Application method: Use a clean, well-calibrated sprayer and do not overlap passes. Spray in calm weather and keep spray away from maize, barley, sorghum and pasture grasses.

3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Available in 5 Litres.

4. Spray when grass weeds and wheat are at the stages on the label; avoid crops stressed by drought, frost or waterlogging.

Safety & re-entry precautions

  • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.
