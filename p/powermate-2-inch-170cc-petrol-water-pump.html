Where & How to Use in Kenya

This pump may suit farm irrigation, filling tanks, moving water between reservoirs and general water transfer at homes or work sites. It is most suitable for applications where the required head is within the 28m maximum lift and the suction source is within the 7m maximum suction limit. It may be used for irrigation where the flow and pressure meet the needs of the chosen hose, sprinkler or distribution system.

It should not be selected for deep boreholes or high-elevation delivery beyond its stated lift. SUCTION AND DISCHARGE PLANNING Position the pump close to the water source and keep suction lift within the listed 7m limit. Use an airtight 2-inch suction hose and check all joints for leaks.

Follow the manufacturer's priming instructions. The listed 2-inch pipe water filter should be installed as directed and cleaned regularly. Use a compatible 2-inch discharge hose and avoid unnecessary reductions, sharp bends or long narrow lines that increase friction loss.

Secure hoses so they cannot move unexpectedly when the engine starts. PETROL ENGINE OPERATION Use the fuel and oil specified by the engine manufacturer. Check oil before each start and follow the recommended starting and priming procedure.

The automatic oiler is an additional engine feature, not a reason to skip routine checks. Operate the pump outdoors in a well-ventilated area because petrol exhaust gases are hazardous. Refuel only when the engine is stopped and cool.

Keep petrol away from flames, sparks and hot surfaces. Do not operate the engine in an enclosed room or poorly ventilated pit. INSTALLATION AND

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Set the pump on stable, level ground and protect it from flooding. Keep the engine cooling path clear. Secure suction and discharge hoses to prevent movement.

Direct discharged water to a safe location and avoid erosion, flooding or backflow. Do not run the pump dry if it requires priming or water in the casing. Keep hands, clothing and tools away from moving engine components.

Use suitable lifting assistance when transporting the unit and never lift it by its hoses. MAINTENANCE AND
