Product overview

PYRAMID TREK CITRIODIOL INSECT REPELLENT – 60ML IN KENYA

Pyramid Trek Citriodiol Insect Repellent is a portable personal repellent spray supplied in a 60ml pack for outdoor use. The product is made with Citriodiol, an ingredient derived from oil of lemon eucalyptus, and is intended to repel selected biting insects from treated exposed skin when used according to the label. Its compact bottle is convenient for travel, walking, camping and other outdoor activities. Always read the exact product label before use, especially when applying repellent to children.

WHAT CITRIODIOL REPELLENT IS FOR

Pyramid Trek Natural is marketed for protection against mosquitoes, midges, ticks, sand flies, black flies and stable flies. Supplier information for the 60ml product lists 40% Citriodiol, equivalent to 400g/kg in the formulation, and states that one application can provide up to 10 hours of protection under the conditions described by the manufacturer. Actual protection time can vary with activity, perspiration, weather and other factors. Follow the instructions on the bottle rather than treating the maximum stated duration as guaranteed.

HOW TO APPLY RESPONSIBLY

Apply only to exposed skin and only as directed on the product label. Avoid spraying directly into the eyes, mouth or onto broken or irritated skin. When applying to the face, spray a small amount onto the hands first and spread carefully, avoiding the eyes and mouth. Do not apply more often than the label allows. Wash treated skin when protection is no longer needed and follow the pack instructions for clothing or other surfaces. Do not use the product as an indoor surface insecticide.

CHILDREN AND SENSITIVE USERS

Manufacturer information for Pyramid Trek Natural states that the product is suitable for children from six months of age, but the actual bottle label supplied with the product should be checked before use in Kenya. Adults should apply repellent to children and avoid children's hands, eyes and mouth. If the label gives a different age restriction or special precaution, follow the label. Seek medical advice if irritation or an adverse reaction occurs.

OUTDOOR AND TRAVEL USE

A personal repellent can be useful during outdoor activities where mosquitoes and other biting insects are present. The 60ml bottle is a portable size for day trips and travel. Keep the cap closed when not in use and store the bottle away from heat and direct sunlight. Do not transfer the liquid into an unlabelled container. If travelling, check the destination's public-health guidance and use additional protection such as long sleeves, window screens or a suitable mosquito net where recommended.

WHAT REPELLENT DOES NOT DO

A skin repellent does not kill mosquitoes in the room, remove breeding sites or provide permanent protection after the product has been washed off or worn away. It should be used alongside measures that reduce exposure and mosquito breeding. Remove standing water from containers and gutters where practical, cover water storage and use intact screens on windows and doors. In areas with mosquito-borne disease risk, follow local public-health advice and use the full set of recommended prevention measures.
