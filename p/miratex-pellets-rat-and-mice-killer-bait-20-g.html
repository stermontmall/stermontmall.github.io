Where it helps

Rodents usually move along walls and through dark, quiet corners. They contaminate food, chew wires, damage sacks and furniture, and can spread disease, and because they breed fast, early baiting together with good hygiene is the sensible way to protect a home, a business and stored produce. Larger buildings need bait in many places and need it topped up over time, which is what the 240 g pack is for: grain stores, feed stores, poultry houses, workshops and godowns.

How to use it

Walk around first and look for droppings, gnaw marks, greasy rub marks along walls and burrows. Put bait close to those signs, along walls and near nesting holes, at points that children, pets and livestock cannot reach. Check the bait points often and replace pellets that have been eaten. Wearing gloves, remove dead rodents safely.

Before you start

  • Keep grain and animal feed in sealed containers, on raised pallets in stores
  • Block holes and gaps, and cut tall grass close to buildings
  • Keep rubbish and old materials away from the walls
  • Count the bait points you need before you choose a pack size

Safety

Check the PCPB registration number on the label and read the label before use. Place the bait where only rodents can reach it, and never near food for people or animals, or inside food containers. Keep it away from children, pets and livestock, and wash your hands after handling it.

Storage

Store the pellets in their closed pack, somewhere dry and secure, away from food and out of reach of children.
