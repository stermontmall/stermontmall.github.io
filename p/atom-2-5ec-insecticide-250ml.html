Product overview

Atom 2.5EC 250ml is an emulsifiable-concentrate agricultural insecticide containing deltamethrin 25 g/L. It is a pyrethroid insecticide intended for crop-protection programmes where the current Kenyan product label specifically permits its use. The 250ml pack is a practical option for farmers, horticultural growers and agricultural input retailers who need a clearly identified pack size for planned crop-protection work. Before purchase or application, check the original container label, batch details, expiry date and current Pest Control Products Board (PCPB) registration information. PRODUCT IDENTITY AND FORMULATION The active ingredient is deltamethrin at 25 grams per litre. The formulation type is EC, meaning emulsifiable concentrate, a liquid formulation designed to be measured and diluted in water according to the approved label. Deltamethrin belongs to the pyrethroid insecticide group, IRAC Group 3A. It affects insect nervous-system sodium-channel function and acts through contact and ingestion. This mode of action is different from products in many other insecticide groups, which is relevant when planning a responsible resistance-management programme. Do not assume that products with different brand names have different modes of action; check their active ingredients and IRAC group. REGISTERED USE AND RESPONSIBLE CROP PROTECTION The PCPB crop-products register identifies Atom 2.5EC as containing deltamethrin 25 g/L and lists its registered crop use for control of thrips and aphids on French beans, and caterpillars and aphids on roses. The register also gives a one-day pre-harvest interval and a 12-hour re-entry interval for the listed uses. These details are included to help buyers identify the product and understand why the label matters; always confirm that the current container label and current Kenyan registration still permit the intended use. Do not extend the product to another crop, pest, application method or rate simply because a general online description mentions it. For any crop-protection operation, first identify the pest correctly and assess whether treatment is necessary. Consider scouting records, crop growth stage, pest pressure, beneficial insects, weather and any buyer or export-market residue requirements. Where integrated pest management is practical, combine monitoring, field hygiene, crop rotation, resistant varieties and conservation of beneficial organisms with carefully selected chemical controls. Safety information

Wear the personal protective equipment specified on the product label when handling, mixing or applying the concentrate. Avoid skin and eye contact and avoid breathing spray mist. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling pesticides. Keep people and animals away from the treated area for the label-specified period. Avoid drift onto neighbouring crops, homes, livestock areas, water bodies and flowering vegetation. Deltamethrin products can be hazardous to aquatic organisms and non-target insects; do not contaminate streams, ponds, drains or water sources, and take particular care to protect pollinators and beneficial insects. If exposure, a spill or suspected poisoning occurs, follow the first-aid and emergency instructions on the container label and seek professional medical advice promptly. Do not induce vomiting unless a qualified medical professional or the label specifically instructs you to do so. Dispose of empty containers and leftover spray mixture only as directed by the label and applicable Kenyan requirements. Never reuse an empty pesticide container. RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT AND PRODUCT SELECTION Because deltamethrin is an IRAC Group 3A pyrethroid, repeated use of the same mode of action can contribute to resistance. Where the crop label and agronomist's advice allow, rotate with an effective product from a different mode-of-action group rather than repeatedly relying on Group 3A. Rotation must be based on the registered crop-pest use, not just on brand names. Follow any label limits on application frequency and intervals, and avoid tank mixtures unless the labels permit them.
