INSTALLATION AND CROP TRAINING Plan the support structure before sowing or transplanting so the net can be secured without disturbing established roots. Confirm net width, length, mesh opening and material with the supplier, as these details determine suitability for a particular crop and support span. Use posts or a frame strong enough for the mature crop and expected weather conditions. Fix the net evenly and check tension at intervals. Guide young vines gently through or along the mesh and avoid forcing stems that may break. Inspect ties and anchor points during growth, especially after strong wind or heavy rain. Harvest carefully to avoid pulling down the support.
