About Crop Support Net for Climbing Vegetables

Crop support netting is designed to support climbing and vining vegetable crops as they grow. It can help guide plant growth vertically, keep developing vines organised and make crop inspection and harvesting more convenient. It is suitable for use in compatible garden, greenhouse and farm setups. Confirm the net dimensions, mesh size and installation accessories with the supplier before purchase, and install the support structure securely for the crop load and site conditions.
