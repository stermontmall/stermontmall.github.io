About Infinito SC 687.5 Fungicide – 500 ml

Bayer Infinito SC 687.5 combines fluopicolide and propamocarb in a dual-action suspension concentrate fungicide for downy mildew and blight diseases in roses, tomatoes, potatoes, onions and vegetables. It comes in a 500 ml pack, and the label dose and pre-harvest interval should be followed.

Diseases it controls

  • Downy mildew: yellow patches on the upper side of leaves, with grey or purple growth underneath in humid weather
  • Late blight: dark, water-soaked patches on leaves, stems and fruits that can destroy tomato and potato crops quickly
  • Other blight and related diseases in vegetables, onions and roses

These diseases spread fast in cool, wet and humid conditions, and can ruin a crop in a few days if not managed. They are a major worry for tomato, potato and rose growers.

Dual-action protection

The product combines two active ingredients. Using two different actives together gives protection at more than one point and supports resistance management compared with using a single ingredient on its own. Follow the label for how often and how many times it can be used in a season.

How to use it

Follow the label dose, water volume, spray interval and maximum number of applications. Begin a protective programme before disease is widespread, and be alert when weather turns cool and wet. Shake well, half-fill the tank with water, add the measured product, top up and agitate throughout. Spray evenly and cover leaves, stems and fruits.

Good disease management

  • Plant healthy seed, tubers or seedlings
  • Space plants to allow airflow
  • Avoid overhead irrigation late in the day
  • Remove and destroy infected plants and debris
  • Rotate crops from season to season
  • Rotate fungicides from different groups

Safety

Read the label before use and keep out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Follow the pre-harvest interval on the label before harvest. Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and fish.

Storage

Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
