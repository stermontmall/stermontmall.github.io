Product Overview & Key Benefits

Dual Gold 960 EC 250ml is a Syngenta pre-emergence herbicide for planned weed management in crops listed on the Kenyan product label. It contains S-metolachlor at 960 g/L and is formulated as an emulsifiable concentrate (EC). Syngenta Kenya identifies it for control of annual grass weeds in beans, maize and sugarcane.

The 250ml bottle is a compact pack for growers who need a smaller quantity for a label-defined treatment area, rather than a larger farm or bulk pack. It is important to understand that this is a pre-emergence product: it is intended for use in the crop and weed-development window specified on the label, not as a general-purpose spray for every visible weed. The active ingredient is S-metolachlor, a selective herbicide used to manage susceptible annual grass weeds and certain broad-leaved weeds.

Syngenta describes the mode of action as inhibition of cell division. It should not be assumed to give the same result when used outside the approved crop stage or against weeds not listed on the label. The 250ml pack is convenient for a smaller purchase, but the area it can treat depends on the crop, approved rate, soil conditions and application method.

Always calculate the required amount from the current Kenyan label rather than estimating from the bottle size.
