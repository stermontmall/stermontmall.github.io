3M 8210 N95 DISPOSABLE DUST RESPIRATOR IN KENYA

The 3M 8210 N95 Disposable Dust Respirator is a particulate respirator intended to help reduce exposure to certain airborne non-oil particles when it is correctly selected, fitted and used. It is a cup-shaped respirator with a two-strap design, a nose clip and cushioning nose foam. The model is commonly selected for suitable dusty tasks such as sanding, sweeping and woodworking. It is not a general-purpose gas mask and must not be used for gases, vapours or oxygen-deficient atmospheres.

WHAT THE N95 CLASSIFICATION MEANS

3M identifies the 8210 as a NIOSH-approved N95 respirator with at least 95% filtration efficiency against certain non-oil-based particles under the applicable test conditions. This rating relates to particulate filtration; it does not mean the respirator blocks every airborne hazard or provides protection in every work environment. The product does not protect against gases, vapours, oil aerosols or low-oxygen conditions. For pesticide handling, chemical spraying or fumigation, select respiratory protection from the chemical label, safety data sheet and workplace risk assessment. A particulate respirator alone may be unsuitable for those tasks.

DESIGN AND FIT

The 8210 has a cup-shaped facepiece, welded dual-point strap attachments, a metal nose clip and soft nose foam. The two straps are designed to help hold the respirator in position and support a close face seal. A respirator only works as intended when it fits the individual wearer. Facial hair, incorrect strap placement, damage or poor adjustment can create leakage around the edges. Users should follow the official 3M fitting instructions and the applicable workplace respiratory-protection programme.

PUTTING ON AND CHECKING THE RESPIRATOR

Inspect the respirator before use for holes, tears, damaged straps, a bent nose clip or other defects. Place it over the nose and mouth with the lower edge under the chin, position the upper strap high on the back of the head and the lower strap below the ears around the neck. Shape the nose clip using both hands so it follows the bridge of the nose. Perform the user seal check described in the 3M instructions each time the respirator is donned. If air leaks around the face, readjust it; if a proper seal cannot be achieved, do not continue using that respirator for the task.

WORK TASKS AND LIMITATIONS

The 3M 8210 may be considered for suitable non-oil particulate exposures such as dust generated by sanding, sweeping or woodworking, subject to the hazard assessment and local requirements. It should not be selected solely because a task looks dusty. Some dusts are toxic, fibrogenic or otherwise require a formal respiratory-protection programme and a different respirator or supplied-air system. Do not use it in environments where oxygen is insufficient or where gases and vapours are present. Never assume it is appropriate for insecticide vapours, solvent vapours or thermal fogging.

FIT TESTING, TRAINING AND REPLACEMENT

Workplaces that require respirators should follow the relevant occupational-safety requirements, including medical evaluation, fit testing where required, training and a documented respiratory-protection programme. A user seal check is not a substitute for formal fit testing. Replace the respirator if it becomes damaged, contaminated, difficult to breathe through or unable to maintain a seal. The 8210 is a disposable respirator; do not wash it or attempt to disinfect it for reuse unless the manufacturer specifically authorises a method for the exact product.

Storage & handling

AND HANDLING

Keep unused respirators in their original packaging in a clean, dry place away from direct sunlight, chemicals, moisture and crushing. Check the manufacturer's shelf-life information and packaging condition before use. Store away from dust and contaminants so that the respirator remains clean before it is worn. Dispose of used respirators according to workplace procedures and the type of contaminant encountered.
