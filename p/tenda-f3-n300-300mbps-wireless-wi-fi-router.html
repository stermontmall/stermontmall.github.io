Product Overview & Key Benefits

Tenda F3 N300 is a home wireless router designed to share an internet connection over a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. The Kenyan Jumia listing identifies a 300Mbps wireless router with three external 5dBi antennas, an easy setup interface and IP-based bandwidth control. The listing showed KES 1,597 and in-stock availability at research time.

Listed box contents are one router, a patch cable, a power supply and a user manual. The retailer also states that this model does not have a USB port. The F3 is single-band 2.4GHz.

The advertised 300Mbps is a theoretical wireless link rate, not a guaranteed internet speed or single-device download rate. Real throughput depends on the internet plan, connected device, distance, walls, interference and the number of active devices. The 2.4GHz band is broadly supported by phones, laptops, smart TVs and many connected devices, but can be congested in apartment blocks and busy neighbourhoods.

Three external 5dBi antennas are listed. Antennas help distribute wireless signal, but coverage depends on building materials, router placement and interference. Position the router in an open, central location away from thick concrete walls and large metal objects.

IP-based bandwidth control is listed as a management feature for allocating network bandwidth among devices. The exact controls should be checked in the manual and administration interface.
