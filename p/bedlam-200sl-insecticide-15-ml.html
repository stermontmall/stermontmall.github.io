Where it helps

Bed bugs hide in mattress seams, bed frames, headboards and cracks in walls, and they bite at night. Cockroaches shelter in kitchens, drains, cupboards and behind appliances, and spread germs over food and surfaces. Fleas live in bedding, carpets, pet resting places and cracks in floors, and bite people and animals. The 15 ml pack is suited to treating one bedroom, a small kitchen area or a single piece of furniture.

Getting the best results

  • Clean first: wash bedding at a high temperature and vacuum mattresses, floors and corners
  • Dilute exactly as directed on the label
  • Spray cracks, crevices, mattress seams, bed frames, skirting boards and other hiding places
  • Keep people, pets and food away until the spray has dried
  • Repeat the treatment if the label advises it
  • Treat all affected rooms at the same time, so pests do not move from one room to another

Pets and fleas

If fleas are the problem, treat pets with a vet-approved product at the same time as the house. Wash pet bedding and vacuum carpets, then dispose of the vacuum contents outside.

Safety

Read and follow the label before use. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Wear gloves and a mask when mixing and spraying, and wash your hands afterwards. Do not pour leftover mixture into drains, streams or ponds, and do not reuse the empty bottle.

Storage

Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place.
