More about VectorFog DC20 Cordless ULV Fogger

Cordless ULV fogging for mobile treatment work

The VectorFog DC20 is a battery-powered ultra-low-volume (ULV) fogger for professional operators who need to move around a treatment area without a mains power cable. Its 12V operation and 2-litre tank make it a portable option for suitable pest-management and disinfection work. Choose a fogger only after checking the target site, treatment objective and application method approved on the product label.

Published DC20 specifications state a spray distance of about 2–7 metres and droplet sizes of 5–50 microns. Actual coverage depends on the liquid formulation, nozzle condition, airflow and site conditions. ULV fogging does not replace inspection, sanitation, exclusion or targeted residual treatment when those methods are required.

  • Cordless 12V operation and 2-litre tank.
  • Published droplet-size range: 5–50 microns.
  • Published spray-distance range: approximately 2–7 metres.

Battery, handling and safe application

The supplier lists up to 40 minutes of continuous operation and approximately 3 hours for charging. These are indicative figures; battery condition and operating conditions affect runtime. The listed weight is about 3.2 kg and dimensions are approximately 480 × 250 × 200 mm. Confirm the exact model, battery, charger, warranty and package contents before ordering.

Use only formulations whose labels explicitly approve ULV application. Follow the label and safety data sheet for dilution, personal protective equipment, ventilation, exposure controls and re-entry intervals. Keep people, animals, food and unprotected surfaces clear as directed; never assume every insecticide or disinfectant can be fogged.

  • Confirm battery, charger, warranty and spare-parts availability.
  • Follow the chemical label and safety data sheet for every application.

Choosing a cordless ULV fogger

A cordless ULV fogger is suited to work where mobility is important and the treatment protocol calls for a fine aerosol. It differs from a thermal fogger, which uses heat, and a conventional pressure sprayer, which generally applies larger droplets to selected surfaces. Match the machine to the pest, target site, approved chemical and operator controls.
