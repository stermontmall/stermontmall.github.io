Why Regular Deworming Matters for Dogs

Worms are common in dogs, especially puppies, dogs that hunt or scavenge, and dogs that eat raw meat or offal. Heavy worm burdens can cause weight loss, a dull coat, diarrhoea, vomiting, a swollen belly and weakness. Some worms, such as hydatid tapeworms, can also affect people, so keeping dogs dewormed also protects your family. Regular deworming, together with good hygiene, is part of responsible pet and farm dog care.

Tips for Dog Owners

Weigh your dog or estimate its weight carefully so you give the right number of tablets as shown on the label. Pick up and dispose of dog faeces regularly, wash food and water bowls, control fleas, and avoid feeding raw offal. Ask your veterinarian how often your dog should be dewormed, especially for puppies, pregnant or nursing dogs and dogs on farms where livestock are kept.
