Why Regular Deworming Matters for Dogs Worms are common in dogs, especially puppies, dogs that hunt or scavenge, and dogs that eat raw meat or offal. Heavy worm burdens can cause weight loss, a dull coat, diarrhoea, vomiting, a swollen belly and weakness.

Some worms, such as hydatid tapeworms, can also affect people, so keeping dogs dewormed also protects your family. Regular deworming, together with good hygiene, is part of responsible pet and farm dog care.
