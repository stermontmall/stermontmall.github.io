Safe setup and simple glass-top care

The included regulator, hose and clips are useful accessories, but correct LPG installation and compatibility matter. Have the connection checked by a qualified gas technician and follow the instructions supplied with the cooker and regulator. If you smell gas, do not light a burner or operate electrical switches; turn off the gas supply if it is safe to do so, ventilate the area and seek qualified assistance.
