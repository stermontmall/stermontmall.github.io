About Magnetic Mosquito Door Screen 100 x 210cm

OverviewOrdering & delivery

Product overview

Magnetic Mosquito Door Screen 100 x 210cm is a removable mesh curtain for a compatible doorway. Its fine mesh is intended to help reduce the entry of mosquitoes, flies and other flying insects through the screened opening while allowing airflow. This is a physical insect barrier, not an insecticide or chemical repellent. It does not kill insects already indoors and should be used as one part of a wider household prevention routine.

SIZE AND FIT

This listing is for one screen measuring 100 x 210 cm. Measure the doorway frame opening before purchase and compare the measurements with the curtain size and supplier guidance. Check whether the curtain requires overlap at the sides and top, and confirm there is enough flat frame surface for the supplied mounting system. Do not assume a 100 cm curtain will fit every 100 cm opening; frame shape, fastening allowance and product design affect the usable opening.

MAGNETIC CLOSURE

The split curtain is designed to open when someone passes through and close again as the magnetic edges meet. This hands-free arrangement can be convenient when carrying items between rooms or moving between an indoor area and a balcony or patio. For the closure to work properly, both panels must hang freely, the centre edges must align and the side borders must remain secured. Keep furniture and mats clear of the bottom edge.

SUITABLE LOCATIONS

A compatible screen may be used on bedroom, kitchen, dining-room, sitting-room or balcony doorways where a lightweight mesh curtain is appropriate. It is useful where occupants want ventilation without leaving the doorway completely open to flying insects. It only protects the doorway that it covers. Other openings such as windows and vents must be checked separately. The screen is not a security product, child-safety gate, pet containment barrier or replacement for a required rigid door.

FITTING INSTRUCTIONS

Read the instructions supplied with the actual product. Clean the mounting surface and let it dry fully. Remove dust, grease and loose material that may prevent the fixing strip from holding. Align the top edge before securing the sides. Attach the fastening system as directed, then check that the mesh is not twisted and that the centre magnets meet along the length. Inspect the top, sides and bottom for gaps. Test the screen by walking through it several times and observing whether it closes naturally. Do not stretch the mesh or attach it to a damp or unsuitable surface.

CLEANING AND CARE

Check the mesh, stitching, magnetic points and mounting strips periodically. Remove dust gently and follow the product care guidance. Avoid abrasive tools and strong solvents that could weaken the fibres or fastening. Repair holes and reattach loose edges promptly. If the magnetic closure no longer meets correctly, realign the panels before relying on the screen. Store the product dry if it is removed for a long period.
