Product overview

D-Amine 72 SL Herbicide 500ml is a soluble concentrate containing the dimethylamine salt of 2,4-D at 720 g/L, equivalent to 600 g/L of 2,4-D acid. Kenya's Pest Control Products Board crop register lists D-AMINE 72 SL under PCPB(CR)0753. The registered Kenyan uses are pre-emergence and post-emergence control of annual and perennial broad-leaved weeds in baby corn, and post-emergence control of broad-leaved weeds in wheat. The register also gives a 12-hour restricted-entry interval and a 30-day pre-harvest interval for wheat and baby corn. This listing is for one 500ml bottle and is intended for buyers who have confirmed that the exact crop, weed target and label directions fit the farm's weed-management plan. ACTIVE INGREDIENT AND FORMULATION The active ingredient is 2,4-D dimethylamine salt at 720 g/L, equivalent to 600 g/L acid. The SL designation identifies a soluble concentrate. The formulation and concentration matter when comparing herbicides: D-Amine 72 SL should not be confused with other 2,4-D products that have a different salt, acid equivalent, concentration or formulation. The product is a concentrate and is not ready to spray. Preparation, dilution, application method, timing, crop stage and treated area must follow the current approved Kenyan label. WEED-MANAGEMENT CONTEXT D-Amine 72 SL is a selective herbicide for broad-leaved weeds. It works as a synthetic auxin herbicide, disrupting normal growth processes in susceptible plants. Its registered use is not a replacement for correct crop identification, field scouting, weed identification and timing. Identify the weed species and growth stage before treatment. Avoid drift onto sensitive neighbouring crops, gardens, trees and ornamentals. Do not spray when wind conditions could carry droplets outside the target field. Safety information

Read and follow the complete, current product label and safety data sheet before opening or using this pesticide. Confirm that the product, formulation, intended crop or site, target pest or disease and application method are approved for the intended use. Do not guess a dose, dilution, tank mix, application interval or treatment method, and never use the product for an unlisted purpose.

Wear all personal protective equipment specified on the label and safety data sheet during handling, measuring, mixing, loading, application and equipment cleaning. Avoid contact with skin and eyes and avoid inhaling dust, vapour or spray mist. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. Wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly after work, and remove and launder contaminated clothing separately.

Keep children, animals and unprotected people away from the product and treated areas. Prevent spray drift, spills and run-off. Do not contaminate food, animal feed, medicines, drinking water, drains, ponds, rivers or neighbouring crops. Observe the exact restricted-entry interval and pre-harvest interval stated on the approved label; do not re-enter or harvest earlier.

If exposure, a spill or suspected poisoning occurs, stop work, follow the first-aid and emergency instructions on the product label and safety data sheet, and seek prompt medical or professional advice. Keep the original container and label available for medical personnel. Do not induce vomiting or administer treatment unless instructed by the label or a qualified medical professional.

Storage & handling

Keep the 500ml bottle tightly closed in its original container and store it in a secure, dry, well-ventilated chemical store. Protect it from heat, direct sunlight and physical damage. Keep it separate from food, feed, seed, medicines and household goods. Never transfer the concentrate into a beverage bottle or an unlabelled container. Do not reuse empty pesticide packaging. Follow the label and applicable Kenyan requirements for container disposal. Do not pour unused product or washings into drains, waterways or irrigation channels.
