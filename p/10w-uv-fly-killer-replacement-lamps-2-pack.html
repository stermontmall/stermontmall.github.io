Why UV replacement lamps matter

Insect light traps rely on a suitable light source to attract flying insects towards the capture mechanism. The correct replacement lamp helps restore the intended operating condition of a compatible unit. A replacement tube does not increase the rated coverage of the trap, change its electrical design, or replace glue boards and other consumables.

Use it only with a compatible insect light trap and follow the maintenance interval specified by the equipment maker.

Site planning and monitoring

Before installing equipment, review where flies enter, where waste is stored, and where staff or customers spend time. Keep a simple inspection record showing the installation location, cleaning date, lamp or adhesive replacement date, and any increase in insect activity. This helps a site manager notice changes and plan maintenance before equipment performance declines.

If the infestation continues despite a correctly maintained trap, investigate doors, windows, drains, refuse areas and other sources rather than relying on the device alone. Keep records accessible for routine audits.

Safety and responsible use

This equipment is one part of an integrated pest-management programme. Sanitation, waste control, exclusion, drainage repairs, monitoring and correct placement remain important. Electrical insect killers contain high-voltage components and should be installed where children and unauthorised people cannot reach them.

Do not touch internal grids or service a powered unit. UV lamps are not general-purpose room lighting and should not be viewed directly at close range. For pesticide fogging, use only products whose labels and the machine manual permit the intended site and equipment.

Never improvise a dilution or application rate.
