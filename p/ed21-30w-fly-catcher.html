About ED21 30W Electric Fly Catcher

The ED21 30W Electric Fly Catcher is a wall-mounted insect light trap designed for indoor flying-insect management in suitable commercial and service environments. The unit uses ultraviolet-A light tubes to attract flying insects towards an internal glue-board capture system. Unlike electric-grid zappers, the ED21 product specification describes flies being retained on adhesive boards. This makes it important to use the correct replacement glue boards and to service the unit regularly according to the manufacturer's instructions.

How the ED21 Fly Catcher works

The UVA tubes attract flying insects towards the unit. Insects that enter the capture area are retained on a replaceable adhesive glue board. The glue board provides a visible catch record that can help a facility monitor fly activity over time. The manufacturer listing describes a wall-mounted unit with a coverage figure of up to 90 square metres. Actual performance depends on room layout, light levels, competing light sources, airflow, insect pressure and correct installation. Do not treat the coverage figure as a guarantee for every room.

Where to install it

Potential locations include suitable indoor areas in hotels, restaurants, shops, offices, food-service support areas and other premises where flying-insect monitoring is required. The unit should be installed at a position recommended by the manufacturer and by the site's pest-management plan. In food-handling environments, glue-board insect light traps are generally selected and positioned to avoid attracting insects across exposed food or food-contact surfaces. Do not install directly above food-preparation areas, open ingredients or clean utensils. Confirm any applicable food-safety and inspection requirements before installation.

Wall-mounting and electrical safety

The ED21 is described as a wall-mounted unit. Use a stable mounting surface and have electrical installation carried out by a competent person where required. Check the power supply and clearance requirements in the manual. Keep the unit away from water, excessive moisture and locations where it can be struck by trolleys or equipment. Disconnect the power before opening the unit, cleaning it or replacing tubes and glue boards. Never attempt to service the electrical components while the unit is connected to power.

Glue board and tube maintenance

The front of the unit is designed to open for servicing of glue boards and tubes. Inspect the glue board at the interval set by the manufacturer and replace it when the adhesive is full, dusty or no longer effective. Use a replacement board that matches the ED21 model and dimensions; a board that is too small or too large may not fit or capture insects correctly. The product supplier lists shatter-resistant UVA tubes with an approximate lifespan of 8,000 hours. This is a stated tube-life figure, not a guarantee of the exact replacement date in every operating environment. Replace tubes as recommended and dispose of used components responsibly.

Monitoring and hygiene

A fly catcher should support, not replace, source control. Keep waste covered, clean food spills, maintain drains, remove decaying organic matter and repair gaps in doors or screens. In commercial premises, record glue-board replacement dates and insect counts. Repeated catches near a particular room may point to a nearby entry point, waste area or breeding source. Use the records to guide inspection and corrective action. If catches remain high, review placement and seek professional pest-management advice.

Product details to confirm

This listing identifies the ED21 30W Electric Fly Catcher as one unit. Supplier specifications describe wall mounting, adhesive board capture, UVA tubes, a detachable lead and a stated coverage of 90m². Confirm the finish, exact included tube configuration, glue board size, power requirements and accessories before purchase. The listing should not be assumed to include spare glue boards or replacement tubes unless stated.
