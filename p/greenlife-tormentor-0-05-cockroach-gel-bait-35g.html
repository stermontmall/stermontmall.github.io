Uses & applications

This is a targeted bait product, not a general-purpose insecticide spray, mosquito larvicide, crop pesticide or rodenticide. Use only for the pest and premises authorised by the current Kenyan label. Cockroaches can hide in warm, dark and damp locations, including gaps around cabinets, service ducts, equipment, appliances and other sheltered harbourages. A gel bait is designed for controlled placement at suitable points where cockroaches forage or shelter. It differs from a residual surface spray because it is applied as small bait deposits rather than a broad spray treatment. The 35g pack is intended for professional and commercial pest-management work where a technician needs a ready-to-use bait format for planned cockroach-control programmes. The product is suitable for consideration in commercial premises such as offices, warehouses, food-service premises, retail areas, hotels, institutions and other public places where cockroach activity has been confirmed and where the label permits treatment. Before selecting a bait, inspect the premises and identify the cockroach species, harbourages, food and water sources, and the extent of the infestation.

Safety information

Gel baits are most useful when placed where cockroaches are likely to encounter them and where they will remain undisturbed. Follow the placement instructions on the current label and avoid placing bait on food, food-contact surfaces, utensils, bedding, clothing, toys, or locations accessible to children and pets. Do not contaminate bait deposits with cleaning agents, insecticide sprays or other products unless the label specifically allows the combination. Avoid placing bait where it may be washed away or exposed to excessive heat, dust or moisture. Wear any protective equipment required by the label, avoid direct contact with the bait, wash hands after handling and keep the pack away from food, animal feed, medicines, children and pets. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product.

How to use & application

For a reliable programme, technicians should record the initial inspection, the locations where activity was found, bait placements and follow-up observations. Monitoring traps can help determine whether activity is reducing and whether additional inspection is needed. If cockroaches continue to appear, review sanitation, competing food sources, bait placement, pest identification and possible reinfestation before deciding on further treatment. Do not apply more bait or repeat treatment more frequently than the label permits. Check for the PCPB registration number PCPB(CR)1312 and ensure the product name and formulation match the current approved label. The product photograph is for identification only; the label supplied with the actual pack is the authority for the approved use, placement instructions, quantity, re-treatment interval, precautions, first aid and disposal. If the received label is missing, damaged or inconsistent with the product details, do not use the bait until the supplier or PCPB has clarified the discrepancy. Although the PCPB listing classifies the product as WHO Class III, slightly hazardous, all pesticide products must be handled responsibly.
