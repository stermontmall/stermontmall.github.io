Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

This is a pest-control or agricultural chemical product. Use it only for the crop, pest, site and application method expressly authorised on the current Kenya-approved product label. Read the full label before opening or use.

Follow the label exactly for personal protective equipment, dilution, application, ventilation, re-entry period, pre-harvest interval, environmental restrictions and any other precautions. Do not guess a dose, mix it with another product, or use it on food, animals, people or surfaces unless the label specifically permits that use. Keep the original container tightly closed and locked away from children, unauthorised persons and animals, and separate from food, drink and animal feed.

Prevent spills and contamination of drains, wells, streams and other water sources. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling; wash hands and exposed skin after handling. Product overview

Greenlife Tormentor 0.05% is a ready-to-use cockroach gel bait supplied in a 35g pack for public-health pest management. The Kenya Pest Control Products Board public-health register lists TORMENTOR 0.05% Gel under registration number PCPB(CR)1312 for control of cockroaches in public places and classifies it as WHO Class III, slightly hazardous, with a blue colour band. This is a targeted bait product, not a general-purpose insecticide spray, mosquito larvicide, crop pesticide or rodenticide.

Use only for the pest and premises authorised by the current Kenyan label. Cockroaches can hide in warm, dark and damp locations, including gaps around cabinets, service ducts, equipment, appliances and other sheltered harbourages. A gel bait is designed for controlled placement at suitable points where cockroaches forage or shelter.

It differs from a residual surface spray because it is applied as small bait deposits rather than a broad spray treatment. The 35g pack is intended for professional and commercial pest-management work where a technician needs a ready-to-use bait format for planned cockroach-control programmes. The product is suitable for consideration in commercial premises such as offices, warehouses, food-service premises, retail areas, hotels, institutions and other public places where cockroach activity has been confirmed and where the label permits treatment.

Before selecting a bait, inspect the premises and identify the cockroach species, harbourages, food and water sources, and the extent of the infestation. The product should be part of an integrated pest-management programme that also addresses sanitation, exclusion and monitoring. It is not a substitute for cleaning, repairing leaks, removing food residues or sealing suitable entry points.

Gel baits are most useful when placed where cockroaches are likely to encounter them and where they will remain undisturbed. Follow the placement instructions on the current label and avoid placing bait on food, food-contact surfaces, utensils, bedding, clothing, toys, or locations accessible to children and pets. Do not contaminate bait deposits with cleaning agents, insecticide sprays or other products unless the label specifically allows the combination.

Avoid placing bait where it may be washed away or exposed to excessive heat, dust or moisture. For a reliable programme, technicians should record the initial inspection, the locations where activity was found, bait placements and follow-up observations. Monitoring traps can help determine whether activity is reducing and whether additional inspection is needed.

If cockroaches continue to appear, review sanitation, competing food sources, bait placement, pest identification and possible reinfestation before deciding on further treatment. Do not apply more bait or repeat treatment more frequently than the label permits. Resistance management and rotation of control methods should be considered in recurring infestations.

If the received label is missing, damaged or inconsistent with the product details, do not use the bait until the supplier or PCPB has clarified the discrepancy. Although the PCPB listing classifies the product as WHO Class III, slightly hazardous, all pesticide products must be handled responsibly. Wear any protective equipment required by the label, avoid direct contact with the bait, wash hands after handling and keep the pack away from food, animal feed, medicines, children and pets.

Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling the product. Store it in its original packaging in a cool, dry and secure pesticide store. Never transfer the gel into an unlabelled container or reuse empty packaging.

Dispose of unused bait and packaging through approved local pesticide-waste arrangements. Do not discard product into drains, waterways, ponds, wells or places where non-target animals may access it. Keep the batch number, purchase record and expiry date for traceability.

For food-handling premises, hospitals, schools and other sensitive sites, treatment should be planned and carried out by appropriately trained pest-control personnel in accordance with the label and the site's hygiene requirements.
