Where & How to Use in Kenya

The current PCPB public-health register lists Air Chopper 7. 5% SC for control of bedbugs, cockroaches, ants, houseflies and fleas in domestic environs. These pests behave differently and should be correctly identified before selecting a product.

Bedbugs are usually associated with sleeping areas and furniture; cockroaches use concealed harbourage near food, moisture and warm equipment; ants follow trails and access points; houseflies are attracted to waste and exposed food; fleas may be associated with animal resting sites. An inspection helps determine which pest is present and whether the registered use applies to the site. This product should not be marketed as a general crop insecticide.

Its register entry is for public-health pest management in domestic environs. The presence of imidacloprid in the formulation is an additional reason to respect the site's restrictions and not promote open-field use. For commercial premises, food-handling areas, schools, hospitals, hotels or other sensitive locations, confirm the registered label and use a trained pest-control professional where required.
