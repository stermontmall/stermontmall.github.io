Product overview

Osho Bio-Nematon is a liquid biological nematicide supplied in a one-litre bottle. The Kenya Pest Control Products Board biopesticide register lists BIO-NEMATON Liquid spores and mycelial fragments under registration number PCPB(CR)1308. Its active biological ingredient is Paecilomyces lilacinus 1.5%, with a minimum concentration of 1 × 10^8 colony-forming units per millilitre.

The registered uses include control of root-knot, cyst and burrowing nematodes in roses, French beans and tomatoes, and cyst nematodes in potatoes. Always check the current Kenyan label supplied with the bottle for the approved crop, target nematode and directions. Paecilomyces lilacinus is a beneficial fungus used in biological nematode management.

It can parasitise nematode eggs and other susceptible stages. Spores attach to the host, germinate and produce fungal structures that penetrate and colonise the egg or nematode body. The fungus then develops and disrupts the nematode life cycle.

This biological mode of action differs from conventional chemical nematicides and can be useful in integrated soil-health programmes. Bio-Nematon is not a public-health insecticide, a household aerosol, a rodenticide, a herbicide or a foliar insecticide for every crop pest. The one-litre pack is suitable for commercial growers, greenhouse operators, nurseries and professional crop-protection teams who need a larger volume for planned soil or root-zone management.

The PCPB registration specifically identifies root-knot, cyst and burrowing nematodes in roses, French beans and tomatoes, and cyst nematodes in potatoes. Do not assume that registration for these crops automatically authorises use on other vegetables, ornamentals or field crops. Confirm the crop and target nematode on the current Kenyan label before ordering or applying the product.

Plant-parasitic nematodes live in soil and can damage roots, reducing water and nutrient uptake. Root-knot nematodes may cause galls or swellings on roots, stunting, yellowing, wilting during warm periods and uneven crop growth. Cyst and burrowing nematodes can cause other forms of root injury and reduced plant vigour.

These symptoms may also be caused by other soil problems, so accurate diagnosis is important. Where nematodes are suspected, inspect roots and use soil or root testing where necessary to confirm the species and infestation level before selecting a treatment. Bio-Nematon should be part of an integrated nematode-management programme.

Combine biological treatment with crop rotation, field sanitation, removal of heavily infested roots where appropriate, management of volunteer host plants, clean planting material and improvement of soil organic matter. Avoid moving infested soil or plant material between fields. Where practical, use resistant or tolerant varieties and maintain records of crop history, nematode pressure and treatment dates.

This helps identify recurring problem areas and evaluate whether the management plan is working. The product is a living biological formulation, so preparation and storage conditions matter. Follow the manufacturer’s and current Kenyan label instructions for storage temperature, shelf life, dilution, placement and application timing.

Keep the bottle in its original, tightly closed packaging and protect it from excessive heat, direct sunlight and freezing. Shake or mix the product only as directed. Use clean application equipment and prepare the suspension only as specified.

Do not store a prepared mixture longer than the label permits. Avoid contamination with fungicides, disinfectants or other products that may affect the viability of the biological fungus. The label determines the exact application rate, water volume, crop, target nematode, placement, interval, maximum number of treatments, re-entry conditions and any restrictions on tank mixing.
