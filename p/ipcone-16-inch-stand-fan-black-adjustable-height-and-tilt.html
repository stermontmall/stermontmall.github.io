Using the Fan

Assemble the fan on a flat floor, fit the base firmly and tighten the height lock so the pole does not slip. Place the fan a little away from the wall so air can flow freely, and point it towards the area you want to cool. Use a lower speed for sleeping and a higher speed when the room is very warm. Keep the fan on a dry floor and away from water.

Care and Cleaning

Unplug the fan before cleaning. Dust collects on the guard and the blades, so wipe them with a soft damp cloth every week or two. For a deeper clean, remove the front guard following the instructions in the user manual, then wipe each blade and dry everything before refitting. A clean fan moves more air and runs more quietly.

Safety Tips

Keep fingers, hair and objects away from the guard when the fan is running. Do not let children play with the fan, and do not cover the motor. If the fan makes unusual noises or smells, switch off and unplug it. Follow the user manual at all times.
