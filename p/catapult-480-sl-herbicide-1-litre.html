About Catapult 480SL Herbicide – 1 Litre

Greenlife Catapult 480SL Glyphosate Herbicide (1 Litre) Greenlife Catapult 480SL contains glyphosate 480 g/L and is a non-selective post-emergence herbicide. It controls annual and perennial weeds before planting maize, French beans and other crops, including under minimum or zero tillage. It is a soluble liquid (SL) supplied in a 1 litre pack. KEY FEATURES • Glyphosate 480 g/L soluble liquid • Non-selective post-emergence herbicide • Controls annual and perennial weeds • For use before planting maize, French beans and other crops • Suitable for minimum or zero tillage systems • 1 litre pack • Greenlife brand WHAT NON-SELECTIVE MEANS A non-selective herbicide can damage or kill most green plants it touches, including crops. This is why Catapult 480SL is used to clear land before planting, not over a growing crop. Take great care to keep spray off crops, trees, vegetables and other plants you want to keep. WHAT POST-EMERGENCE MEANS The herbicide is applied to weeds that are already growing. It is absorbed through the green leaves and moves through the plant, which is helpful for perennial weeds that regrow from roots, rhizomes and stolons. Weeds should have enough green leaf area and be actively growing when sprayed. Results show over several days to a few weeks as weeds yellow and die back. MINIMUM AND ZERO TILLAGE Under minimum or zero tillage, farmers plant with little or no ploughing. A burndown spray of glyphosate before planting clears weeds while saving soil, moisture, time and fuel. Plant into the cleared field after the waiting period given on the label. HOW TO USE IT Follow the dose, water volume and timing on the label. Use a clean sprayer with a suitable nozzle and a shield where possible, spray in calm, dry weather, and avoid spraying when rain is expected soon. Do not spray when weeds are covered in dust or are under drought stress. Avoid drift onto crops or neighbouring plots. SAFETY Read the label before use and keep out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying. Do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying, and wash your hands and face afterwards. Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Rinse the sprayer very well after use, away from crops and water sources. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
