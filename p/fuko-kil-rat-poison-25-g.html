Where it helps

Rats eat and spoil stored food, gnaw wires, packaging and furniture, and spread germs through droppings and urine. They breed fast, so a small problem soon becomes a large one. Moles push up mounds of soil and cut tunnels through lawns, gardens and fields, which matters on small farms and in kitchen gardens where young plants have shallow roots.

How to use it

Follow the label for placement and quantity. For rats, put small amounts of bait in protected spots near nesting holes, along walls and on runs where you see droppings and gnaw marks, using a covered tray or bait station where possible. For moles, follow the label's directions for placing the product in active runs, and do not spread it on the soil surface where other animals could find it. Replace bait that has been eaten or has got wet, and stop when the activity ends.

Before you start

  • Keep food, grain and feed in closed containers
  • Seal holes, gaps and cracks, and clear rubbish and long grass near buildings
  • Look for fresh mounds and runs so you know where the moles are active
  • Choose spots that children, pets and poultry cannot reach

Safety

Check the PCPB registration number on the label and read it before use. Keep Fuko-Kil away from children, pets, poultry and other animals, and never place it where food, feed or water can be contaminated. Collect dead rodents regularly, wearing gloves, and wash your hands after handling bait. If anyone swallows it, get medical help at once and show the pack; if an animal eats it, contact a vet immediately.

Storage

Close the pack tightly after each use and keep it locked away, cool and dry, away from food.
