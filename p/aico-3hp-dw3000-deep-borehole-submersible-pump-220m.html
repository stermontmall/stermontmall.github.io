The AICO Japan DW3000 3HP Deep Borehole Submersible Pump is an electric borehole pump listed for deep groundwater extraction. The supplier specifies a 3HP (2.2kW) motor, maximum head of 220 metres, stainless-steel AISI 304 housing and an outlet diameter range of 1.25 to 2 inches depending on configuration. The product page lists voltage options of 220–240V single phase or 380–415V three phase, and an immersion depth of up to 150 metres below static water level.

Where & How to Use in Kenya

This pump may suit deep boreholes, high-elevation water delivery, farm water storage and domestic or commercial systems where a high lifting head is needed. It can be considered where the water level is deep and the storage tank or delivery point is significantly above the source. For irrigation, calculate the required daily volume and the flow available at the operating head.

For homes and commercial buildings, compare the pump's expected flow with peak demand and storage capacity. A high head rating alone does not guarantee adequate delivery volume. BOREHOLE SIZING AND HYDRAULICS Confirm the casing's internal diameter and compare it with the exact pump diameter.

The supplier lists multiple outlet sizes and voltage options, so ask for the DW3000 variant being supplied. Measure the static water level and pumping water level, including drawdown. Calculate total dynamic head from the pumping water level to the delivery point and include friction losses in the rising main, elbows, valves and fittings.

Ask the supplier for a pump curve or written flow estimate at the intended operating point. Confirm that the pump remains within the listed 150m immersion limit. ELECTRICAL REQUIREMENTS The page lists both 220–240V single-phase and 380–415V three-phase variants.

These are not interchangeable. A qualified electrician should verify the actual motor nameplate, site supply, current rating, cable sizing, earthing, overload protection and control panel before installation. Confirm whether a starter or control box is included and whether the cable length is adequate.

Protect electrical joints from moisture and use appropriate isolation equipment. INSTALLATION AND

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Use qualified borehole installers to lower and secure the pump. Never lift it by its electrical cable; use suitable lifting equipment or a support rope. Secure the rising main so it does not strain the pump outlet.

Do not run the pump dry or outside the manufacturer's permitted immersion conditions. Check that the intake is not positioned in accumulated sediment. Isolate power before inspection or maintenance.
