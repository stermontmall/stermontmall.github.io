What You Can Cook

Boil water and tea, cook rice, pasta, eggs, noodles and porridge, fry sausages and chapati, or simmer sauces and stews. Use a low setting for slow simmering and a high setting for boiling or frying. Choose pots with a flat base that cover the coil well, so heat is used efficiently and food cooks evenly.

Using It Safely

Place the hot plate on a flat, stable, heat-resistant surface away from walls, curtains and anything that can burn. Do not use warped pots or very large pots that overhang. Do not leave the hot plate on while unattended, and turn the dial back to off before unplugging. The coil stays hot for a while after switching off, so keep children away and let it cool completely before moving or cleaning it.

Cleaning and Care

Unplug the hot plate and let it cool. Wipe the surface with a damp cloth, taking care not to let water enter the coil or the control. Remove burnt spills with a soft brush, and avoid putting water on the heating element.
