About Shenke Electric Mosquito Mats – 30 Pack

Shenke Electric Mosquito Mats are replacement consumable mats designed for use with a compatible electric mosquito mat heater. This listing identifies a 30-mat pack. The heater is sold separately and is not included. Each mat is intended to be inserted into a suitable electric heating unit, which warms the mat and releases its repellent vapour according to the product instructions. The exact active ingredient and formulation must be confirmed from the current pack label before use.

Check heater compatibility

Electric mosquito mats are not universal. Different heaters can use different mat dimensions, thicknesses and heating surfaces. Before ordering, compare the shape and size of the Shenke mat with the mat currently used in your heater and check the heater manufacturer's compatibility guidance. Do not trim, fold or modify a mat to make it fit. A poorly fitting mat may not heat evenly or may create a safety risk. This listing is for the 30-mat refill pack, not the electrical heater.

How to use the Mats

Follow the instructions supplied with the mats and compatible heater. Supplier information for this product type describes placing one mat flat on the heater's metal heating plate and switching on the unit according to its manual. Use the product in a well-ventilated area and follow the stated timing and replacement instructions. Do not leave a heater operating in a way that conflicts with the manufacturer's directions. If the mat changes colour or reaches the stated end of its service life, switch off the heater and replace it as directed.

Ventilation and electrical safety

Electric mat heaters use mains electricity and become warm during operation. Place the heater on a stable, heat-resistant surface away from curtains, bedding, paper, flammable materials and water. Keep the unit out of reach of children and pets. Do not touch the heating plate while it is hot and allow it to cool before replacing a mat. Switch off and unplug the heater when it is not in use. Do not use a damaged heater, frayed cable or loose electrical connection.

Precautions around people and animals

Read the full label for the active ingredient, permitted room size, ventilation requirements, exposure limits and any age restrictions. Some mosquito mat formulations may have specific precautions for fish, aquatic organisms, silkworms, pets or sensitive individuals. Do not assume that every electric mat product has the same ingredient or safety profile. Keep mats in their original foil packaging until required and store them away from food, children and animals. If irritation or an adverse reaction occurs, stop use and follow the label's first-aid advice.

Replacement and storage

The supplier guidance for Shenke mats indicates that a mat may change colour after a period of use and should be replaced according to the product instructions. Do not reuse a spent mat or place it back into the original pack. Store unused mats sealed in their foil packaging in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Dispose of used mats and empty packaging according to the label and local waste guidance.

Use with other Mosquito prevention measures

Electric mosquito mats are a room-use repellent product, not a complete mosquito-control programme. They do not remove breeding sites or prevent insects from entering through open doors and windows. Use window screens, close doors where practical and remove standing water from containers around the property. Follow local public-health guidance for additional mosquito prevention, especially in areas where mosquito-borne disease is a concern.
