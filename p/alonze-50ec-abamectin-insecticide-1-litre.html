Product details

ALONZE 50EC (Abamectin 50 g/L) is an insecticide, miticide and nematicide with contact and trans-laminar action. It controls spider mites in roses and carnations, thrips and leaf miners in beans, snow peas, broccoli, tomatoes and chilies, and nematodes in potatoes. Rate: 0.15 L/ha in 1000 L water (3 ml per 20 L). Potato nematodes: soil drench 240-300 ml/ha in 1000 L water. Apply at the first signs of pests. Repeat after 7 days, maximum 3 applications. Pre-harvest interval: chilies, broccoli, snow peas 7 days; runner beans, tomatoes, French beans 3 days; potatoes 14 days. Re-entry period: 12 hours. Always read the label before use.
