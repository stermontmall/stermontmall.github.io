About ALONZE 50EC Abamectin Insecticide – 1 Litre

ALONZE 50EC Abamectin Insecticide (1 Litre) ALONZE 50EC (Abamectin 50 g/L) is an insecticide, miticide and nematicide with contact and trans-laminar action. It controls spider mites in roses and carnations, thrips and leaf miners in beans, snow peas, broccoli, tomatoes and chilies, and nematodes in potatoes. It is sold in a 1 litre pack. KEY FEATURES • Abamectin 50 g/L in an emulsifiable concentrate (EC) formulation • Three-way action as insecticide, miticide and nematicide • Contact and trans-laminar action, so the product reaches pests on both sides of the leaf • Used on flowers and vegetables • 1 litre pack PESTS AND CROPS • Spider mites: roses and carnations • Thrips and leaf miners: beans, snow peas, broccoli, tomatoes and chilies • Nematodes: potatoes RATE AND TIMING Rate: 0.15 L/ha in 1000 L water (3 ml per 20 L). Potato nematodes: soil drench 240 to 300 ml/ha in 1000 L water. Apply at the first signs of pests. Repeat after 7 days, maximum 3 applications. Early application matters, because small pest populations are far easier to control than established ones. PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL AND RE-ENTRY • Chilies, broccoli and snow peas: 7 days • Runner beans, tomatoes and French beans: 3 days • Potatoes: 14 days • Re-entry period: 12 hours Do not harvest before the interval has passed. MIXING AND SPRAYING TIPS Half fill the tank with clean water, add the measured product, top up and agitate. Spray in calm weather with good coverage of leaf undersides, where mites and thrips hide. Limit applications to the maximum stated and rotate with products from other groups to help manage resistance. SAFETY Always read the label before use. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing. Keep away from children, pets, food and water, and keep spray away from streams, ponds and fish.
