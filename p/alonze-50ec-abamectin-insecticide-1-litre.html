About #1 Top ALONZE 50EC Abamectin Insecticide

ALONZE 50EC Abamectin Insecticide (25 ml, 50 ml, 100 ml, 250 ml, 1 Litre)

ALONZE 50EC is an abamectin insecticide, miticide and nematicide with contact and translaminar action. It controls spider mites, thrips and leaf miners on flowers and vegetables, and nematodes in potatoes.

Key specifications & features

  • Active ingredient: Abamectin 50 g/L
  • Target: Spider mites in roses and carnations; thrips and leaf miners in vegetables; nematodes in potatoes
  • Formulation: Emulsifiable concentrate (EC)
  • Application settings: Agricultural: flowers, vegetables and potatoes

Mixing & application instructions

1. Dilution rate: Foliar spray: 0.15 L per hectare in 1000 L of water (3 ml per 20 L). Potato nematodes: soil drench of 240 to 300 ml per hectare in 1000 L of water.

2. Application method: Half-fill the tank with clean water, add the measured product, top up and agitate. Spray in calm weather with good coverage of leaf undersides, where mites and thrips hide.

3. Coverage: depends on the dose and spray volume on the label. Available in 25 ml, 50 ml, 100 ml, 250 ml and 1 Litre.

4. Apply at the first signs of pests, repeat after 7 days, and do not exceed 3 applications.

5. Rotate with products from other groups and keep spray away from streams, ponds and fish.

Safety & re-entry precautions

  • Wear gloves, a mask, goggles, overalls and boots when mixing and spraying.
  • Pre-harvest interval: chilies, broccoli and snow peas 7 days; runner beans, tomatoes and French beans 3 days; potatoes 14 days. Do not harvest before the interval has passed.
  • Re-entry period: 12 hours. Keep people, children and animals out of the sprayed area until the spray has dried, and follow the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on the label.
  • Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and wells. Store in the original closed container, cool, dry and locked, away from food and feed.
