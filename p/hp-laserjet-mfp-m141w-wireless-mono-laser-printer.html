About HP LaserJet MFP M141w Wireless Monochrome All-in-One Laser Printer

Product overview

HP LASERJET MFP M141W WIRELESS MONOCHROME ALL-IN-ONE PRINTER IN KENYA The HP LaserJet MFP M141w is a compact monochrome laser multifunction printer designed for everyday black-and-white printing, copying and scanning. It combines three common document tasks in one desktop device, making it a practical option for a home office, small business, shop, school office or work-from-home setup that needs printed paperwork without requiring a large floor-standing machine. The M141w is the wireless version of the M141 series and supports both USB and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi connectivity, subject to the network and device configuration. PRINT, COPY AND SCAN WITH ONE DEVICE The M141w is a multifunction printer, often shortened to MFP. Its print function produces black text and monochrome documents, while the flatbed scanner can capture documents for compatible computer or mobile workflows. The copy function can reproduce paper documents without first creating a separate digital file. This combination is useful for routine office work such as invoices, quotations, letters, forms, school notes, delivery records and business paperwork. HP lists black A4 print speeds of up to 20 pages per minute under its stated test conditions. Actual output depends on the document, software, connection, driver and printer state. The rated print resolution is up to 600 x 600 dpi for black output. These specifications make the unit suitable for text-focused documents and everyday office printing; it is not a colour printer and should not be selected when colour graphics or photo printing are required. WIRELESS AND USB CONNECTIVITY The M141w supports Hi-Speed USB and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Wireless connectivity can help place the printer where it is convenient rather than directly beside one computer. Supported mobile-printing options include the HP app, Apple AirPrint, Mopria and Wi-Fi Direct, subject to compatible phones, operating systems, network conditions and software. Wireless performance depends on the distance from the router and the physical environment. The printer does not provide a guarantee that every phone or application will connect automatically; install the recommended software and follow HP's setup steps for the operating system in use. For a shared office, confirm that the printer and computers are connected to the intended network and that the correct printer driver is installed. If wireless printing is not available in a particular environment, the USB connection provides a direct connection option. A USB cable is not listed as included in the standard box contents, so check whether you need to purchase one separately. PAPER HANDLING AND WORKLOAD HP specifies a 150-sheet input tray and a 100-sheet output bin. The printer supports common media such as plain paper, envelopes, postcards and labels within the supported sizes and weights. Check the paper guide and product manual before loading specialty media. The recommended monthly print volume is 100 to 1,000 pages, while the maximum monthly duty cycle is listed as up to 8,000 pages. The recommended range is the more useful guide for regular use; the maximum duty-cycle figure should not be treated as a target for routine monthly printing. This model does not provide automatic two-sided printing. If you need duplex output, you will need to turn pages manually and follow the driver prompts. The flatbed scanner supports common file formats including JPG, PNG, TIFF and PDF through compatible software. Confirm the document workflow and scan settings you need before purchase. TONER, MAINTENANCE AND RUNNING COSTS The M141w uses one black toner cartridge. HP identifies the HP 150A Black Original LaserJet Toner Cartridge, model W1500A, as the replacement supply for this printer series. The actual number of pages from a cartridge varies with document coverage, print settings and usage. A page containing dense graphics or large areas of black can use toner more quickly than a page with ordinary text. Check the cartridge packaging and HP's current yield information when estimating ongoing printing costs. Keep the printer on a stable, level surface with room for ventilation and access to the paper tray. Use suitable paper, remove jammed sheets carefully and follow HP's instructions for cleaning and replacing toner. Do not touch internal components that may be hot immediately after printing. Use the printer in a clean, dry environment and avoid placing liquids on or near the device. WHO SHOULD CONSIDER THE M141W?
