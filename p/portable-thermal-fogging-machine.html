Storage & handling

Allow the machine to cool fully before cleaning, inspection or storage. Follow the manufacturer's instructions for flushing the liquid pathway, cleaning the nozzle and checking hoses, seals, clamps and electrical components. Inspect the barrel and protective casing for damage before each use.
