About Chariot 500SC Fungicide – 1 Litre

Greenlife Chariot 500SC Fungicide (1 Litre) Greenlife Chariot 500SC contains carbendazim 500 g/L and is a fungicide for control of botrytis on roses and angular leaf spot on French beans. It is a suspension concentrate (SC) supplied in a 1 litre pack. KEY FEATURES • Carbendazim 500 g/L in a suspension concentrate • Controls botrytis on roses • Controls angular leaf spot on French beans • 1 litre pack • Greenlife brand • Delivered to all 47 counties DISEASES IT CONTROLS Botrytis, also called grey mould, attacks flowers, buds and stems of roses. It shows as grey, fuzzy mould and brown, water-soaked patches on petals and stems, and it spreads quickly in cool, humid, still conditions such as greenhouses and packing areas. It can ruin stems before and after harvest, so it is a serious concern for cut-flower growers. Angular leaf spot affects French beans and shows as angular, brown spots on leaves that are limited by the leaf veins. In severe cases leaves dry out and fall early, and spots can reach the pods and lower quality. HOW TO USE IT Follow the dose, water volume, spray interval and maximum number of applications on the label. Shake the container well, half-fill the tank with water, add the measured product, top up and agitate throughout spraying. Spray evenly and cover leaves, buds and stems well. Begin when conditions favour disease, rather than waiting for heavy damage. GOOD DISEASE MANAGEMENT • Improve airflow in greenhouses and between plants • Avoid overhead watering late in the day and keep foliage dry • Remove and destroy infected flowers, leaves and plant debris • Rotate crops and use clean seed for French beans • Avoid bruising stems at harvest RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT Rotate with fungicides from other chemical groups and keep to the number of sprays on the label so the product keeps working. SAFETY Read the label before use and keep out of reach of children. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Follow the pre-harvest interval on the label before picking French beans. Keep spray and rinse water away from streams, ponds and fish. STORAGE Store in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
