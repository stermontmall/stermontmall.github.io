About Russell IPM Optiroll Yellow Sticky Trap Roll – 30cm x 100m

Russell IPM Optiroll Yellow is a colour-optimised adhesive roller trap used to monitor selected flying crop pests, particularly whiteflies and aphids. This listing identifies one 30cm-wide by 100m-long roll. The yellow surface is designed to attract small flying insects, which are retained on the high-tack adhesive. The wide roll format provides a large monitoring surface for suitable greenhouse, nursery and crop-growing environments where regular insect monitoring is part of the production routine.

Target pests and purpose

Russell IPM describes its Optiroll Yellow range for monitoring whiteflies, aphids and other small flying pests. Depending on the exact variant, yellow rolls may also catch thrips, fungus gnats, fruit flies and leaf-miner flies. The standard Optiroll Yellow, Super Yellow and Super Plus Yellow versions are not identical: some variants have additional black patterns or an encapsulated pheromone component. This listing names the 30cm x 100m Optiroll Yellow roll, so confirm the precise variant printed on the packaging before assuming that it has any special pattern or pheromone feature.

Why monitoring matters

Small flying pests can build up before damage is obvious across a crop. Sticky rolls help growers detect activity, compare catches over time and identify areas that may need closer inspection. They are not a complete pest-control programme and do not identify every insect automatically. A catch should be considered alongside crop scouting, leaf inspection, plant symptoms, weather and crop stage. Consistent monitoring helps growers make more informed decisions instead of relying on occasional visual checks.

Suitable crops and environments

Optiroll Yellow products are used in vegetable crops such as tomatoes and peppers, soft-fruit production, ornamental crops and nursery or propagation areas. The 30cm width may suit larger monitoring zones where a wider adhesive surface is useful. The exact number of rolls and installation points depends on the greenhouse layout, crop size, pest pressure and the manufacturer's guidance. Extra attention may be given to entrances, vents and crop edges where insects can enter, but placement height should be adapted to the crop and target pest.

Installation and positioning

Plan the installation before unrolling the adhesive. Use compatible tape brackets or supports where recommended so the roll can be suspended securely and positioned correctly in relation to the crop. Keep the adhesive away from leaves, irrigation lines, workers and equipment. Avoid stretching it across walkways or where it could catch clothing. Keep unused portions protected from dust and direct contact. Follow the supplier's instructions for installation, spacing and replacement rather than applying a universal metres-per-area figure to every crop.

Inspect and record catches

Inspect the roll regularly. Record the date, greenhouse zone, crop, height and insects observed. Replace the exposed section when it becomes covered with insects, dust or debris or when its adhesive performance is reduced. A rising catch should prompt closer inspection of the crop and nearby entry points. Do not make pesticide decisions from one trap count. Confirm pest identity and consult an agronomist or crop-protection adviser when needed.

Integrated pest management

Sticky rolls can support integrated pest management by providing early-warning information and helping track pest populations. Combine monitoring with inspection of incoming plants, removal of infested plant material where appropriate, weed management and good greenhouse hygiene. Russell IPM describes its Optiroll Yellow range as suitable for use alongside beneficial insects and biological-control programmes; confirm compatibility for the exact variant and beneficial species being used. If a pesticide is considered, verify that it is registered and labelled for the crop and target pest and follow all label directions and pre-harvest intervals.

Storage and product details

This listing identifies one yellow sticky roll measuring 30cm x 100m. Russell IPM product information describes the range as UV-resistant and having a high-tack adhesive layer. Confirm exact packaging, material and variant details from the current product label. Store unused roll material in its original packaging in a cool, dry place and protect it from dust and sunlight.
