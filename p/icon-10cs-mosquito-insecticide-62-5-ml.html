Syngenta Icon 10CS Mosquito Insecticide (62.5 ml)

Where it helps

In a capsule suspension the active ingredient is held in tiny capsules in a liquid. After spraying, the capsules stay on the treated surface and release the active ingredient slowly, which is designed to give a longer-lasting residual effect than a spray that is released all at once.

How to use it

Shake the bottle well, measure carefully as the label directs and mix thoroughly in clean water in the sprayer. Spray the surfaces where insects rest or walk: walls, door and window frames, eaves, shaded places, drains, and the cracks and corners where cockroaches hide. Treat the whole house in one session so insects do not move to an untreated room. For large buildings, consider a licensed pest control operator.

Before you start

  • Remove or cover food, dishes and water containers
  • Keep people and pets out of the treated rooms until the spray has dried
  • Clear stagnant water, rubbish and blocked drains to cut breeding sites
  • Fit mosquito nets and window screens for added protection

Safety

Read the label before use. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Lambda-cyhalothrin is very toxic to fish and bees, so never rinse sprayers or containers into drains, streams, ponds or fish tanks.

Storage

Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
