About Icon 10CS Mosquito Insecticide – 62.5 ml

Syngenta Icon 10CS Mosquito Insecticide (62.5 ml) Syngenta Icon 10CS contains lambda-cyhalothrin 100 g/L and is a long-lasting residual capsule suspension insecticide for control of public health insects, including mosquitoes, flies and cockroaches. It comes in a 62.5 ml pack and is diluted and sprayed as directed on the label. KEY FEATURES • Lambda-cyhalothrin 100 g/L • Capsule suspension (CS) formulation • Long-lasting residual action • Controls mosquitoes, flies and cockroaches • For public health and household pest control • 62.5 ml pack • Syngenta brand WHAT CAPSULE SUSPENSION MEANS In a capsule suspension, the active ingredient is held in tiny capsules in a liquid. After spraying, the capsules stay on the treated surface and release the active ingredient slowly. This is designed to give a longer-lasting residual effect than a spray that is released all at once, so insects that land on or walk across the surface continue to pick up the insecticide. PESTS IT CONTROLS • Mosquitoes: bite at dusk and night and can carry diseases such as malaria • Flies: land on food, rubbish and waste and spread germs • Cockroaches: hide in cracks and kitchens and contaminate food and surfaces HOW TO USE IT Dilute and spray as directed on the label. Apply to surfaces where insects rest or walk, such as walls, door and window frames, eaves, shaded places, drains and the cracks and corners where cockroaches hide. Shake the bottle well before use, measure carefully and mix thoroughly in clean water in the sprayer. A 62.5 ml pack is a convenient size for treating a house, a few rooms or a small business. GOOD PRACTICE • Remove or cover food, dishes and water containers before spraying • Keep people and pets out of treated rooms until the spray has dried • Clear stagnant water, rubbish and drains around the home to cut breeding sites • Use mosquito nets and window screens for added protection • For large buildings, consider a licensed pest control operator SAFETY Read the label before use. Wear gloves, a mask, goggles and protective clothing when mixing and spraying, and wash well afterwards. Keep away from children, pets, food and water. Lambda-cyhalothrin is very toxic to fish and bees, so never rinse sprayers or containers into drains, streams, ponds or fish tanks. STORAGE Keep in the original closed bottle in a cool, dry, locked place away from food and feed.
