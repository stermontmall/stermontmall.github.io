Choose suitable containers and heat food evenly

Use containers marked as microwave-safe, such as suitable glass or ceramic dishes, and avoid metal utensils, aluminium foil and dishes with metallic decoration. If you are unsure about a container, do not place it in the microwave. Arrange food in an even layer where possible and stir or rotate it during heating when appropriate so hot and cool spots are reduced. Allow food to stand briefly after heating and check that it is hot throughout before serving.

Mechanical dials make it straightforward to set a time and power level, but the correct setting depends on the food, quantity and starting temperature. Begin with a shorter heating time and add more if needed rather than overheating food. Follow the instructions supplied with the exact Roch model for defrosting and power-level guidance.

Position, clean and maintain the microwave safely

Place the microwave on a stable, level surface and leave the ventilation clearance specified in its manual. Keep the air openings unobstructed and avoid placing the appliance near water or a source of excessive heat. Do not operate it empty, and stop using it if the door, hinges, latch or seal appear damaged.

Unplug the appliance before cleaning the exterior or moving it. Wipe food splashes from the cavity with a soft damp cloth and dry it afterwards; avoid abrasive pads and harsh chemicals. Never remove the outer casing or attempt to repair internal microwave components yourself, because high-voltage parts can remain dangerous even when unplugged.
