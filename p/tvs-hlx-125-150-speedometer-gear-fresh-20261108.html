About TVS HLX 125/150 Speedometer Drive Gear

The TVS HLX 125/150 speedometer drive gear is part of the mechanical speedometer drive system. It transfers wheel rotation to the speedometer cable, which then drives the instrument cluster. If the drive gear is worn, damaged, incorrectly seated or incompatible with the wheel hub, the speedometer may stop registering or show inconsistent movement. This listing is described for TVS HLX 125cc and 150cc applications, but the correct gear must be confirmed against the exact motorcycle and the part removed from it.

Before ordering, compare the gear profile, tooth shape, diameter, mounting points, locating tabs and cable connection with the original component. Check the model year, front wheel or hub arrangement and the exact drive assembly used on your motorcycle. Similar speedometer gears can look alike while differing in tooth count or fit. Do not order solely because a listing mentions HLX 125 or HLX 150. If possible, ask a mechanic to inspect the old gear and confirm the required replacement. The product photo is a visual guide; check the physical part and its mating components before installation.

The speedometer drive gear works together with the cable, wheel-side drive housing and instrument cluster. If the speedometer has stopped working, inspect the whole system before assuming the gear is the only cause. A broken cable, loose connection, damaged drive tabs or faulty instrument can produce similar symptoms. During installation, clean the housing, check for abnormal wear and fit the gear in the correct orientation according to the service information. Do not force a gear into place or use an unapproved lubricant. After assembly, rotate the wheel safely and confirm that the drive turns the cable smoothly. If the needle remains erratic, continue diagnosis of the cable and instrument.
