Where & How to Use in Kenya

Agropests positions Navigator 100SC for public-health pest-control contexts and names bedbugs, cockroaches and mosquitoes. These pests can occur in different environments and require different inspection and management approaches. Bedbug work, for example, should begin with careful inspection of sleeping areas and furniture; cockroach management often requires attention to harbourage, sanitation and entry points; mosquito management depends on identifying breeding and resting sites as well as the appropriate control method.

A chemical product alone does not replace inspection, sanitation, exclusion, monitoring or an integrated pest-management plan. The correct product and treatment method depend on the pest species, the site, the people using the premises, nearby animals, food-handling conditions and the label's permitted use.
