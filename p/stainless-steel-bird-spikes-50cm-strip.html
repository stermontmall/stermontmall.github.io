# Stainless Steel Bird & Pigeon Spikes – 50cm Strip

Product Overview & Key Benefits

Help discourage pigeons and other nuisance birds from perching or roosting on exposed ledges with this 50 cm bird-deterrent spike strip. It is designed for targeted bird-proofing around buildings and outdoor structures where droppings, nesting and repeated perching create cleaning or maintenance problems. The product is a physical deterrent, not a poison or chemical treatment.

Technical Specifications & Pack Details

  • Pack: one 50 cm strip
  • Base: polycarbonate
  • Spike material: stainless steel wire
  • Spike length: 11.5 cm
  • Protection width: approximately 7 cm, tip to tip
  • Base dimensions: 50 cm long × 2.2 cm wide
  • Intended birds listed by supplier: pigeons, gulls, starlings, Indian mynas and sparrows

Suitable placement and installation

The supplier lists use on ledges, roofs, chimneys, gutters, fences, beams, balconies and other elevated perching points. Clean and dry the mounting surface first. The supplier recommends suitable outdoor adhesive or clips; it also describes fixing options such as screws, straps or cable ties where the surface and installation method permit.

Fix every section securely and inspect it after installation. Use protective gloves when handling the pointed wires, keep the product away from children and do not install it where it could injure people or non-target animals.
