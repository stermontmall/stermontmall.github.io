What You Can Cook

Use the menus to cook rice, porridge, soups, stews, beans, meat, chicken and more, and use the timer to delay or set the cooking time. It is also good for steaming vegetables and cooking pilau and biryani. The keep-warm function holds the food at a pleasant temperature until you are ready to serve.

Using It Safely

Always follow the user manual. Make sure the lid is locked before cooking, never fill the pot beyond the maximum line, and take care with foods that foam or swell, such as beans and rice.
