Why Choose This Pressure Cooker

Foods such as beans, githeri, ndengu, tough beef and goat meat can take hours on a jiko or gas cooker. A pressure cooker cuts the time sharply and uses less fuel, which can save you money on gas and electricity. Because this model is electric and has preset menus, you can set it and get on with your day. The 5 litre pot suits a family of four to six.

What You Can Cook

Use the menus to cook rice, porridge, soups, stews, beans, meat, chicken and more, and use the timer to delay or set the cooking time. It is also good for steaming vegetables and cooking pilau and biryani. The keep-warm function holds the food at a pleasant temperature until you are ready to serve.

Using It Safely

Always follow the user manual. Make sure the lid is locked before cooking, never fill the pot beyond the maximum line, and take care with foods that foam or swell, such as beans and rice. Release pressure carefully using the method shown in the manual and keep your face and hands away from the steam outlet. Do not open the lid until the pressure has been fully released.

Cleaning and Care

Unplug and let the cooker cool. Wash the inner pot with a soft sponge and mild soap to protect the non-stick surface, and wipe the lid, seal ring and steam valve clean after each use. Dry all the parts before putting them back together.
