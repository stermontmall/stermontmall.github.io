Product overview

Panthenon 100 CS is a public-health insecticide supplied in a 62.5ml pack. The product contains lambda-cyhalothrin 100 g/L in a capsule-suspension formulation and is registered in Kenya under PCPB(CR)1396. The Kenya Pest Control Products Board public-health register lists Panthenon 100 CS for cockroach control in public places and classifies it as WHO Class III, slightly hazardous, with a blue colour band.

Panthenon 100 CS is intended for use by trained pest-control operators in authorised premises and treatment areas.
