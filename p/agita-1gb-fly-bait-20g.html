Where it helps

Flies land on food, manure and waste and then walk across kitchen surfaces, milk equipment and food displays, spreading germs. On farms they gather around animal sheds, manure heaps and feed. A bait draws them in so that you control them where they gather, instead of chasing them around the room.

How to use it

Read the label and place the bait where flies gather, such as near bins, drains, waste points and animal sheds, in the way the label directs. Keep it out of reach of children, pets and livestock, and away from food and food preparation surfaces. Check the bait regularly and replace it as the label directs.

Before you start

  • Cover bins and empty them daily
  • Clean up spills and food waste quickly
  • Keep floors and drains dry
  • Choose a pack size that matches the area you want to protect

Safety

Read the label before use. Keep away from children, pets, livestock, food and water, and wash your hands after handling. Dispose of used bait and empty packs as the label directs.

Storage

Keep in the original, tightly closed pack in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, food, animal feed and children.
