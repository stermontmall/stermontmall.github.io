Biosecurity Tips for Poultry and Livestock Farms Disease prevention is cheaper than treatment. Clean away litter, manure and feed residues before disinfecting, because dirt can reduce the effect of any disinfectant. Disinfect houses between batches, and leave them empty and dry for a rest period. Place a foot bath at the entrance of every house, keep visitors and vehicles to a minimum and give clean overalls and boots to staff. Disinfect feeders, drinkers, crates, equipment and vehicles regularly, and keep wild birds and rodents out.
