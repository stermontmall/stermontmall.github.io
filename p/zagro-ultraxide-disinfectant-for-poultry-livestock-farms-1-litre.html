About Zagro Ultraxide Disinfectant 1L – 1 Litre

One sick batch can wipe out a season's profit. Zagro Ultraxide is a broad-spectrum disinfectant that combines glutaraldehyde and a quaternary ammonium compound to kill bacteria, viruses and fungi on poultry houses, pig pens, dairy parlours, equipment and vehicles. One litre makes about 150 litres of ready-to-use solution, so it costs very little per square metre.

Key benefits

  • Broad spectrum: bacteria, viruses and fungi; tested against African Swine Fever virus at 1:100, 1:200 and 1:300
  • Concentrated: 1 litre makes about 150 L at the standard farm rate
  • Many jobs: hatcheries, livestock buildings, wash rooms, egg stores, foot and wheel dips, vehicles
  • Residual activity stays stable for up to a week on treated surfaces
  • Pleasant smell and easy one-step mixing

Technical specs

  • Active ingredients: Glutaraldehyde 150 g/L + Benzalkonium chloride 100 g/L
  • Manufacturer: Zagro
  • Pack: 1 litre
  • Use: veterinary and farm disinfection

MIXING AND APPLICATION DOSAGE (per 20 L)

  • Livestock buildings, equipment, hatching and wash rooms: 1:150, about 133 ml
  • General strong disinfection: 1:100, about 200 ml
  • Egg store: 1:320, about 62 ml
  • Vehicles: 1:400, about 50 ml
  • Drinking water: 1:3000, about 7 ml

One litre makes about 150 L of solution at 1:150.

1. Remove birds, animals, feed and bedding, then scrub off dung and dirt first.

2. Fill the knapsack half full, add Ultraxide, top up and mix.

3. Spray all surfaces until thoroughly wet, then let them dry before restocking.

4. For foot and wheel dips, use the building rate and refresh the solution often.

Safety

For veterinary use. Wear gloves, goggles and a mask when handling the concentrate. Avoid contact with eyes and skin. Rinse equipment after use.

Each litre contains Glutaraldehyde 150 g/L and Benzalkonium chloride 100 g/L. It is made by Zagro and sold here in a 1 litre pack.

How much water does 1 litre of Zagro Ultraxide make?
