Product Overview & Key Benefits

The 3-Inch Diesel Water Pump with 8HP Engine is a portable engine-driven pump listed for high-volume water transfer. The supplier specifies an 8HP air-cooled, four-stroke diesel engine, 3-inch (80mm) inlet and outlet, maximum flow rate of 60,000 litres per hour, maximum lift head of 28 metres and suction depth of 7–8 metres. It is intended for buyers who need to move substantial quantities of water for irrigation, construction or drainage without depending on a fixed electrical supply.

The diesel engine and 3-inch ports make it a different class of equipment from small domestic electric booster pumps. The 8HP engine provides the mechanical power for the pump, while the 3-inch connections allow a large-volume water path. The supplier lists a maximum flow of 60,000L/h, equivalent to 60m³/h.

The stated 28m maximum head describes the maximum lifting height under specified conditions, and the 7–8m suction depth is the listed maximum suction lift. Flow will reduce as total head and pipe resistance increase, so the maximum flow should not be assumed at the maximum head. The air-cooled, four-stroke diesel engine is designed for fuel-powered operation and does not require a mains electrical supply to run the pump.

Where & How to Use in Kenya

The pump may suit farm irrigation, water transfer between reservoirs, filling large storage tanks, construction-site water movement and drainage where the water is compatible with the pump. The high stated flow can be useful when the goal is to move a large volume over a relatively modest lift. It may be less appropriate where the installation needs a very high vertical head or a small, carefully controlled flow.

Check the actual water source, required volume and discharge route before selecting the pump. SUCTION AND DISCHARGE PLANNING The supplier lists a suction depth of 7–8m. This is a maximum figure, not a recommended operating target for every installation.

Keep the pump close to the source and use a suitable suction hose with airtight connections. Any air leak on the suction side can prevent priming or reduce performance. Fit an appropriate strainer at the intake to reduce the chance of debris entering the pump, following the manufacturer's instructions.

Use a compatible 3-inch discharge hose and avoid unnecessary reductions that increase resistance. Secure the hose so it cannot whip or move dangerously when the pump starts. DIESEL ENGINE OPERATION Use the fuel and engine oil specified by the manufacturer.

Check oil before starting and follow the correct priming and start-up procedure. Diesel engines produce exhaust gases and must be operated outdoors in a well-ventilated area. Keep the pump away from enclosed spaces, ignition sources and flammable materials.

Refuel only when the engine is stopped and cool. Do not touch hot engine or exhaust parts. If the pump is used on a farm or construction site, assign an operator who understands the controls and routine checks.

INSTALLATION AND

Safety, Storage & Handling Guidelines

Place the unit on stable, level ground and protect it from flooding. Keep the suction line short and supported. Ensure the discharge point is safe and will not erode soil, flood neighbouring property or return water to the source.

Do not run the pump dry if the manufacturer requires priming or water in the pump chamber. Keep hands and loose clothing clear of moving engine parts. Use suitable lifting assistance when moving the machine and never lift it by hoses or cables.
