About Hybrid Onion Jambar Seeds

Onion growers are paid for bulbs that are uniform, firm and able to travel, so the seed you plant sets the ceiling for your profit. Hybrid Onion Jambar F1 from Seminis is a commercial onion seed known for uniform, globe-shaped bulbs with deep red skins, a pungent flavour and tight neck closure. A tight neck helps with post-harvest handling and reduces moisture loss during storage and transport to distant markets. This pack is a 500g tin.

  • Seminis hybrid onion, Jambar F1
  • Uniform, globe-shaped bulbs with deep red skins
  • Pungent flavour
  • Tight neck closure for better handling and storage
  • Seed treated with protective fungicidal dressings, including Fludioxonil and Metalaxyl, to guard young seedlings against soil-borne pathogens
  • 500g tin for commercial growers

Nursery and transplanting

1. Prepare a nursery bed with fine tilth, good levelling and good drainage.

2. Sow the seeds and keep the bed moist while seedlings establish.

3. Transplant healthy, pencil-thick seedlings after about six to eight weeks into a well-fertilized, weed-free field.

4. Keep proper plant-to-plant spacing so bulbs expand and size evenly.

Crop care

Bulb development is when yield is made. Give regular irrigation, keep weeds under control and use balanced nutrition through the bulbing phase. Rotate with crops that are not in the onion family and scout often for insects and diseases so problems are caught early. Follow the agronomist's advice and the seed label for your area.

Harvest and storage

Cure harvested bulbs in a shaded, well-ventilated place before selling or storing them. Good curing, together with the tight neck of this variety, helps the bulbs keep well. Store unused seed in its tightly sealed container in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, food and children.
