About Hybrid Onion Jambar Seeds

Hybrid Onion Jambar Seeds from Seminis represent top-tier genetics for commercial onion growers seeking exceptional bulb uniformity, high yield potential, and excellent storage qualities across diverse farming environments in Kenya. Jambar F1 is widely recognized for producing uniform, globe-shaped bulbs with deep red skins, pungent flavor, and tight neck closure, which significantly improves post-harvest handling and reduces moisture loss during extended storage and transit to distant markets.

The seeds are treated with protective fungicidal seed dressings, including Fludioxonil and Metalaxyl, to safeguard young seedlings against soil-borne pathogens during early establishment in the nursery bed. For optimal results, sow the seeds in well-prepared nursery beds with fine tilth, proper leveling, and good drainage. Transplant healthy, pencil-thick seedlings into a well-fertilized, weed-free field after approximately six to eight weeks, maintaining appropriate plant-to-plant spacing to encourage uniform bulb expansion and sizing.

Regular irrigation, proper weed control, and balanced nutrient management are crucial during the bulb development phase to attain maximum harvest potential and commercial profitability. Proper crop rotation and pest monitoring further protect your investment from soil-borne diseases and insect pressures. Cure harvested bulbs properly in a shaded, well-ventilated area before marketing or long-term storage. Store seed containers tightly sealed in a cool, dry location away from direct sunlight, food, and children. Always follow safety instructions on the product label.
