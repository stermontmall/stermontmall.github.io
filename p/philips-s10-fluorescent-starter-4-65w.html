COMPATIBILITY

Use only where the fitting specifies an S10-type starter and has a compatible electromagnetic ballast. This starter is not suitable for fittings with an electronic ballast or LED-only replacement tubes. Insect-trap fittings vary: confirm that the unit actually has a replaceable glow-starter socket before ordering. A 30W UV tube alone does not prove starter compatibility.

INSTALLATION AND

Safety information

Switch off and isolate the fitting from mains power before replacing a starter. Allow the lamp to cool, remove the old starter using the fitting manufacturer’s instructions, and fit the matching replacement without forcing it. If the fitting has damaged wiring, a burnt holder, repeated failed starts or an electronic ballast, have it checked by a qualified electrician. Never open or service an energized fitting.
