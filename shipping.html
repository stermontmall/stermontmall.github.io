How your order works

  1. Place your order. Add your items to the cart on our website and place your order.
  2. We confirm the details. We check stock and send you the total, the delivery fee and the delivery time for your location.
  3. You pay by M-Pesa. We process your order once payment is received.
  4. We deliver. Your order is sent to the location you gave us.

Delivery time

Most orders arrive within 1 to 3 days. Remote areas can take longer. We confirm the delivery time for your location before you pay.

Delivery fee

The fee depends on your location and on the size and weight of your items. We tell you the exact fee before you pay, so the total you agree to is the total you pay.

Payment

We accept M-Pesa. We show the payment details on your order confirmation and can also send them on WhatsApp.

Stay safe. Only pay using the details we give you on our website or in our chat, and check the name shown on M-Pesa before you confirm. We will never ask for your M-Pesa PIN.

What we need from you

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your county and town, with a landmark or a short description of the exact place
  • A phone number that will be answered on the day of delivery

When your order arrives

Please check your items when they arrive. If something is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, message us within 48 hours with photos. Read more on our returns and refunds page.

Collect from our shop

You are welcome to collect your order at The Sarit Centre (Westlands), Karuna Road, Nairobi. Message us first so we can confirm your items are ready.
