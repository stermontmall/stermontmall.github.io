When we will replace or refund

If your order arrives and any of the following is true, we will arrange a replacement or a refund:

  • The item is faulty or damaged.
  • The item is different from what you ordered.
  • Part of your order is missing.

How to report a problem

  1. Message us within 48 hours of delivery. Use WhatsApp on +254 748 888 230.
  2. Send clear photos. Show the item, the packaging and the problem.
  3. Give us your details. Your name, phone number and what you ordered.
  4. We review and arrange a fix. We organise a replacement or a refund.

Refunds

Refunds are sent by M-Pesa to the number you paid from, or to another number you give us. We confirm the amount with you before we send it.

Please keep in mind

  • Keep the item and its original packaging until we have replied.
  • For agrovet products such as seeds, chemicals, feeds and animal medicines, please do not open or use more than you need to check the product. For health and safety reasons, opened products can only be returned if they are faulty or not what you ordered.
  • Damage caused by misuse, wrong storage or wrong use is not covered.
  • Some products come with a manufacturer warranty. Ask us on WhatsApp before you buy if this matters to you.

Changed your mind?

Message us as soon as you can, before your order is dispatched, and we will do our best to change or cancel it.
