Privacy policy

This page explains what information we collect when you shop with us, why we need it and how we look after it.

1. What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and delivery details when you place an order.
  • The items you ordered and the order total.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, by email or through our website, including your phone number if you ask for a stock or price alert.
  • Your login details, if you create an account.
  • Anonymous visit information, such as which pages are viewed and roughly where in the world visitors are, collected by Google Analytics.

2. How we use it

  • To process, confirm and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your payment, delivery or a return.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To keep records of our sales, as the law requires.
  • To understand how people use our website, so we can improve it.

We do not sell your information. We do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

3. Who handles your information

We use trusted services to run our shop. Your order details may pass through them:

  • Supabase stores our order and stock records.
  • Web3Forms emails us your orders and messages.
  • WhatsApp carries your chat with us, if you message us there.
  • M-Pesa handles your payment. We never see your PIN.
  • Delivery partners receive only what they need to deliver to you: your name, phone number and address.
  • Google Analytics counts visits to our website. It receives anonymous usage information such as pages viewed. We do not send it your name, phone number or order details.

4. Cookies and storage on your device

Our website keeps your cart and login on your own device, so they are still there when you come back. Google Analytics also uses cookies to count visits. If you have "Do Not Track" switched on in your browser, we do not run analytics. We do not use your information to track you on other websites.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to deliver your order, handle returns and meet legal and accounting requirements. After that, we delete or anonymise them.

6. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you,
  • correct it if it is wrong,
  • delete it when we no longer need it, and
  • stop using it in ways you object to.

Contact us using the details below and we will reply as soon as we can. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

7. Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No online service is completely secure, so please only share payment details through the channels we confirm in your chat.

8. Changes to this page

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
