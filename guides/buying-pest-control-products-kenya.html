How to Choose Pest Control Products in Kenya

Kenya buyer guidance · Updated 18 November 2026

Pest control products are designed for different pests and treatment settings. A product intended for crawling insects may not control rodents, and a treatment labelled for outdoor use may not be suitable indoors or around food. Identify the pest and site first, then compare products whose labels specifically cover that use. For large premises or specialist fumigation, engage a licensed pest control operator.

Identify the pest and site

Confirm whether you are dealing with bed bugs, cockroaches, ants, mosquitoes, flies, termites or rodents. Note whether the site is a home, hotel, kitchen, warehouse, farm, animal house or outdoor area. Correct identification helps avoid buying an unsuitable product.

Match the label to target and location

Read the full label for target pest, permitted surfaces, indoor or outdoor use, application method, dilution, protective equipment, re-entry period and restrictions around food, water, people or animals. Check registration details with the relevant Kenyan authority. Never use a product outside its label directions.

Compare product forms and pack sizes

Sprays, concentrates, gels, baits, dusts and traps work in different ways. Choose only a form and use listed for the target pest and site. Compare pack size with the area or job, and do not assume a larger container is better value if it cannot be used or stored safely.

Protect people and know when to call a professional

Follow label precautions, keep children, pets, food and animal feed away as directed, and never mix chemicals or transfer them into unlabelled containers. Large infestations, sensitive premises, food-handling environments and fumigation jobs may require a licensed pest control operator.

Before you order: buyer checklist

  • Pest identified accurately
  • Product label names the target pest and site
  • Registration and directions checked
  • Pack size and product form suit the job
  • Safety measures and professional support considered
