How to Buy Agrochemicals in Kenya: A Practical Buyer’s Guide

Kenya buyer guidance · Updated 18 November 2026

Buying agrochemicals is not simply a matter of choosing the lowest price. The correct product depends on the crop, target pest or disease, growth stage and the use stated on the label. Identify the problem accurately, then compare only products whose labels cover that crop and target.

Identify the crop problem

Confirm whether the issue is a weed, insect, mite, fungal disease or another problem. If the cause is uncertain, ask an agronomist or qualified crop adviser before buying. A product used successfully on another farm may not be suitable for a different crop, pest or growth stage.

Read the complete label

Check the active ingredient, formulation, target crop, target pest or disease, application method, rate, pre-harvest interval, re-entry interval and safety directions. Verify current registration with the relevant Kenyan authority. Never assume products with similar names have the same formulation or permitted use.

Compare pack size and unit cost

Compare like-for-like pack sizes and calculate cost per gram, millilitre or kilogram where relevant. A larger pack may lower unit cost but may be unsuitable if it exceeds your planned requirement or cannot be stored correctly. Confirm whether the listing is for a sachet, bottle, jar or bulk container.

Plan safe use and resistance management

Do not mix products unless the label or a qualified adviser confirms compatibility. Rotate modes of action where recommended, keep products in original labelled containers and store them securely away from children, food, animal feed and water. Use the protective equipment stated on the label.

Before you order: buyer checklist

  • Crop and target problem identified
  • Label lists the crop, pest and intended application
  • Registration and active ingredient checked
  • Pack size and cost per unit compared
  • Safety and storage instructions understood
