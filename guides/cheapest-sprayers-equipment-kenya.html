Knapsack sprayers: choosing, checking and maintaining equipment

Select a sprayer by its intended use, tank capacity, operator comfort, available service parts and compatibility with the liquid being applied. A 16-litre knapsack sprayer may suit routine small-area work, but the correct nozzle, pressure and application method depend on the product label and job. Before filling, inspect the tank, hose, lance, seals and nozzle. Test the assembled sprayer with clean water, check for leaks and release pressure before maintenance. Rinse and store equipment as directed, and never mix chemicals or change application rates based on guesswork.
