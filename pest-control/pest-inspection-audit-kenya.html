A useful pest inspection is more than a quick look for insects. It records evidence, likely causes, risk areas, structural defects and corrective actions so property owners can prioritise the work that will make a difference.

What an inspection covers

The scope may include internal rooms, kitchens, washrooms, plant rooms, roof void access, basements, waste holding areas, loading bays, perimeter vegetation, drains and storage zones. The exact areas depend on property use and access.

Evidence and identification

Inspectors look for live pests, droppings, cast skins, egg cases, gnawing, tracks, nests, webbing, damaged packaging, moisture and conditions that support pest activity. Photographs and location notes help distinguish confirmed evidence from suspected activity.

Risk-based findings

Findings should be ranked by urgency and operational impact. A rodent sign near food storage, a leaking drain attracting flies and an isolated outdoor ant trail do not necessarily carry the same risk or require the same response.

Corrective action plan

A report can group actions into immediate control, housekeeping, maintenance, proofing, staff practice and monitoring. Each action should have a responsible person and a review date so that defects are not simply recorded and forgotten.

Repeat inspections

Follow-up checks help confirm whether activity has reduced and whether recommended repairs were completed. For businesses, a recurring inspection schedule can reveal seasonal patterns and repeat entry routes.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the treatment plan?

The pest identified, extent of activity, property use, access, occupants, food or stock present and the product directions all influence the scope.

Will one visit solve every infestation?

Not always. Hidden harbourage, pest life cycles, adjoining units and unresolved building defects can require monitoring, repeat work or repairs.

What should I send for a quotation?

Share your town, property type, approximate size, pest evidence, affected areas and any sensitive occupants, stock or equipment.
