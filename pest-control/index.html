How our process works

We identify the pest and conditions supporting it, agree a site-specific scope, explain preparation and safety requirements, complete the authorized work and recommend monitoring or corrective actions. Methods are selected for the site and used in line with product labels and applicable requirements.

We do not promise instant or permanent elimination. Some infestations require repeat visits, building repairs, sanitation changes or cooperation from neighbouring premises.

Need help identifying a pest?

Tell us what you have seen, where you found it and your town. We can help determine whether an inspection is needed.
