Poultry Farm Pest Management in Kenya

Pest-risk planning for poultry houses, feed stores, egg handling areas and farm surroundings.

Request a site-specific quote

Poultry facilities can attract flies, rodents, darkling beetles and other pests when feed, litter, water and waste are not well managed. A farm programme must protect birds and workers while respecting veterinary and biosecurity controls.

Key risk areas

Inspect feed stores, spilled feed beneath feeders, litter, manure handling, drinker leaks, egg rooms, dead-bird disposal points, drains, vegetation and building gaps. Pest pressure often increases where moisture and organic matter accumulate.

Rodent management

Store feed in secure containers, clean spills, maintain vegetation, proof suitable entry points and use a monitoring plan that prevents access by birds and non-target animals. Any baiting must be placed and managed to prevent accidental exposure and secondary risks.

Fly and beetle pressure

Manage manure and litter according to farm protocols, repair leaking drinkers, remove wet patches and keep waste handling areas controlled. Treatment should be selected for the pest and the presence of birds, eggs, feed and equipment.

Biosecurity coordination

Coordinate any pest intervention with the farm manager or veterinarian. Confirm whether birds must be moved, which areas can be accessed, what cleaning is required and how the treatment affects the farm's biosecurity plan.

Monitoring and records

Keep a log of rodent signs, fly activity, beetle observations, repairs, cleaning, treatment and follow-up. Review trends by poultry house and production stage rather than treating the farm as one undifferentiated area.

Request a farm assessment

Share the farm location, number and type of poultry houses, production stage, pest concerns, feed storage arrangements and veterinary or biosecurity requirements.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the treatment plan?

The pest identified, extent of activity, property use, access, occupants, food or stock present and the product directions all influence the scope.

Will one visit solve every infestation?

Not always. Hidden harbourage, pest life cycles, adjoining units and unresolved building defects can require monitoring, repeat work or repairs.

What should I send for a quotation?

Share your town, property type, approximate size, pest evidence, affected areas and any sensitive occupants, stock or equipment.
