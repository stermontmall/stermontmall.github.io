Bed Bug Prevention and Treatment for Hotels in Kenya

How hospitality teams can inspect rooms and reduce repeat incidents.

Ask SterMont about your pest issue

A guest report should trigger a consistent response. Record the room and observations, avoid moving furniture through guest corridors, and arrange a focused inspection. Bed bugs hide near sleeping areas, including mattress seams, bed frames, headboards and bedside furniture.

What to check first

A room-level plan identifies affected and adjoining rooms, preparation tasks, treatment approach, monitoring and release criteria. Housekeeping teams need clear guidance on linen handling and preventing transfer of infested items.

Practical next steps

Prevention includes routine checks, staff training, rapid reporting and documented follow-up. Avoid promising a one-visit cure: hidden harbourages and eggs can make monitoring or repeat treatment necessary.

When to call a pest professional

Arrange an assessment when pest activity is widespread, keeps returning, affects food or business operations, may involve structural damage, or cannot be identified confidently. A professional should explain the evidence, treatment options, preparation, safety controls and whether follow-up is needed.
