Restaurant Pest Control Audit Checklist for Kenya

A restaurant pest programme must protect food, customers, workers and stock while addressing the conditions that attract pests.

Receiving and storage

Inspect deliveries for damaged packaging and pest evidence. Store dry goods off the floor, rotate stock and keep receiving doors closed when not in use. Separate suspect stock for assessment.

Food preparation and wash-up

Clean crumbs, grease and food residues, maintain equipment voids, repair leaks and keep drains in good condition. Check behind cookers, refrigerators, dishwashers and fixed counters.

Waste management

Use covered bins, clean bin holding areas, arrange reliable collection and prevent spills around external waste points. Keep waste away from doors and food deliveries.

Building proofing

Check door sweeps, screens, pipe penetrations, wall-floor joints, drains and loading bays. Record repairs with an owner and completion date.

Treatment coordination

Plan any treatment around food, utensils, staff and customer access. Follow product label requirements for protection, cleaning, ventilation and re-entry. Do not apply a pesticide to food or food-contact surfaces unless the label explicitly permits it.

Keep an audit trail

Maintain pest sighting logs, inspection reports, corrective actions, treatment records and follow-up results. This helps management understand repeat problems and demonstrate that issues are being addressed.
