How to Control Cockroaches in Kenyan Kitchens

Cockroaches thrive where food, water and sheltered harbourage overlap. A lasting plan starts with identifying the species and locating harbourage rather than spraying every visible surface.

Understand the signs

Cockroaches thrive where food, water and sheltered harbourage overlap. A lasting plan starts with identifying the species and locating harbourage rather than spraying every visible surface.

Inspect and identify the source

Begin with a night-time inspection around sinks, refrigerators, cookers, cupboards and drain routes. Look for droppings, shed skins, egg cases and small nymphs. German cockroaches often remain close to warm appliances and food preparation areas, while larger species may enter from drains or outside spaces.

Prevention starts with the environment

Remove crumbs and grease, dry sinks overnight, repair leaking pipes, store food in sealed containers and empty bins. Reduce cardboard clutter and seal gaps around pipe penetrations. These steps reduce the resources that support an infestation.

Choosing an appropriate response

Professional treatment may involve targeted bait placements, crack-and-crevice applications or other labelled methods selected after inspection. Do not spray household insecticide over gel bait: it can contaminate bait and reduce feeding. Follow all label and re-entry directions.

Monitor and follow up

If activity continues after cleaning and targeted treatment, arrange a follow-up inspection. Multi-unit buildings may need coordinated action because pests can move between adjoining rooms and service voids.
