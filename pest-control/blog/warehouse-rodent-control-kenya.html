Warehouse Rodent Control: Protect Stock and Operations

A monitoring-led approach for godowns, factories and distribution centres.

Ask SterMont about your pest issue

Rodents contaminate stock, damage packaging and wiring, and create operational risk. Map loading bays, doors, drains, staff areas, racking and waste points. Record droppings, gnaw marks, runways and damaged packaging to understand activity.

What to check first

A programme combines proofing, housekeeping, stock rotation, waste management and carefully positioned monitoring devices. Bait stations, where justified, must be secured, identified and checked by trained personnel away from food and feed.

Practical next steps

Review activity trends, close gaps, maintain door sweeps, remove clutter and control vegetation near buildings. Documented reports help operations teams assign corrective actions and track progress.

When to call a pest professional

Arrange an assessment when pest activity is widespread, keeps returning, affects food or business operations, may involve structural damage, or cannot be identified confidently. A professional should explain the evidence, treatment options, preparation, safety controls and whether follow-up is needed.
