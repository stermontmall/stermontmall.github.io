Fumigation Prices in Kenya: What Determines a Professional Quote?

There is no responsible one-price-fits-all answer for fumigation. A quotation depends on the pest, property, treatment method, access, preparation and follow-up needed.

Property size and layout

A bedsitter, multi-bedroom house, restaurant kitchen and multi-storey warehouse have different treatment areas, access needs and operating constraints. Floor area matters, but layout and infestation spread also influence the scope.

Pest type and infestation extent

Bed bugs, termites, rodents, cockroaches and stored-product insects require different inspections and methods. A localised issue may need a different plan from activity across multiple rooms or floors.

Preparation and access

Moving furniture, protecting food, arranging after-hours access, isolating rooms and coordinating with tenants or staff can affect the work plan. The client should receive clear preparation instructions before the visit.

Treatment method and product

A targeted bait programme, residual application, monitoring plan and specialist commodity fumigation are not equivalent services. Ask the provider to state the method and what is included.

Follow-up and prevention

Some pests need repeat inspection or treatment because of life cycles, hidden harbourage or reinfestation from adjoining spaces. Proofing repairs, sanitation and monitoring may be separate parts of the quote.

How to request an accurate quote

Share your town, property type, approximate size, pest, affected rooms, urgency and whether food, children, pets or sensitive equipment are present. Ask for written scope, preparation, re-entry and any guarantee terms.

Need help with a pest issue?

Share your location, property type and what you have observed. We can help you identify the next practical step and confirm service availability.
