Mosquito Control for Homes and Estates in Kenya

Reduce mosquito pressure by targeting breeding sites and entry routes.

Ask SterMont about your pest issue

Mosquitoes need water to complete their life cycle. Inspect gutters, water-storage containers, discarded tyres, plant saucers, blocked drains and other places where water collects. Empty, cover or maintain these sites as appropriate.

What to check first

Screens, repaired window mesh, closed doors and maintained outdoor spaces reduce entry. Any treatment should reflect the site, target species, label directions and public-health guidance. Fogging does not remove breeding sources.

Practical next steps

Create a weekly inspection routine after rain and assign responsibility for water-storage covers, gutters and drains. Seek professional advice when mosquito pressure persists across a larger estate.

When to call a pest professional

Arrange an assessment when pest activity is widespread, keeps returning, affects food or business operations, may involve structural damage, or cannot be identified confidently. A professional should explain the evidence, treatment options, preparation, safety controls and whether follow-up is needed.
