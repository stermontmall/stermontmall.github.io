Hotel Pest Management: A Room-by-Room Checklist for Kenyan Hotels

Hotels need a response that protects guests while coordinating housekeeping, maintenance, reception and management. A room-level checklist helps teams identify problems early and document action.

Guest rooms

Inspect mattress seams, bed frames, headboards, bedside furniture, curtains, luggage racks and skirting when there is a bed bug concern. For other pests, check bathrooms, bins, mini-fridges and service penetrations.

Kitchens and food stores

Inspect receiving areas, dry stores, waste rooms, drains, equipment voids and staff facilities. Keep food sealed and record pest evidence by location rather than relying on general reports such as 'cockroaches in kitchen'.

Laundry and waste routes

Review linen movement, laundry holding areas, refuse rooms, loading bays and service corridors. These areas can connect guest rooms to external pest sources.

Incident response

Record the room, date, pest evidence, guest impact and immediate containment steps. Do not move potentially infested furniture through the property without a plan. Coordinate with a competent pest professional and follow the agreed treatment instructions.

Staff training and prevention

Train housekeeping to report signs rather than conceal them. Give maintenance responsibility for door sweeps, screens, leaks and wall gaps. Review recurring findings at management meetings.

Measure performance

Track repeated sightings by room and floor, time to close maintenance actions, treatment follow-up and guest reports. A contract should include reporting and escalation, not just scheduled visits.
