Disinfection, Sanitisation, Fogging and Fumigation: What Is the Difference?

These terms are often used interchangeably in everyday conversation, but they describe different processes. Choosing the correct service starts with the problem you want to solve.

Understand the signs

These terms are often used interchangeably in everyday conversation, but they describe different processes. Choosing the correct service starts with the problem you want to solve.

Inspect and identify the source

Cleaning removes dirt and organic matter. Disinfection uses a product intended to inactivate specified microorganisms on suitable surfaces when used at the correct dilution and contact time. Sanitisation generally refers to reducing contamination to a defined level for a particular use.

Prevention starts with the environment

Fogging disperses droplets into an area and should only be used when the product label and site conditions allow it. It does not replace cleaning of surfaces or targeted pest management. True gas fumigation is a specialist operation with strict controls.

Choosing an appropriate response

Ask the provider which target is being addressed, what product category is proposed, which surfaces are included, how long contact time takes and when people may safely re-enter. Never mix chemicals or improvise concentrations.

Monitor and follow up

A pest infestation needs pest identification and an appropriate pest-management plan; disinfecting a room does not eliminate bed bugs, termites or rodents.
