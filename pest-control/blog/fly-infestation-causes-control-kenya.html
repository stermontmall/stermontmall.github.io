Why Flies Keep Returning: A Practical Control Guide for Kenya

Recurring flies usually point to an unresolved breeding or feeding source. The right response starts by identifying the fly and tracing the conditions that support it.

Identify the type of fly

Houseflies are often linked to exposed refuse, animal waste or decaying organic matter. Fruit flies are attracted to fermenting fruit, beverage residues and damp organic deposits. Drain flies are associated with wet organic build-up in drains and similar sites. Different species need different inspections.

Map activity instead of guessing

Note where flies gather, the time of day, whether activity is indoors or outdoors and what happens nearby. Check bins, floor drains, grease traps, produce storage, loading bays, animal areas and waste collection points. A cluster at a window may simply be where flies gather, not where they breed.

Remove the breeding source

Clean food and beverage residues, manage waste containers, maintain drains and remove wet organic material. Empty and wash bins on a schedule. Repair leaks and eliminate standing water. Where drain build-up is suspected, use an appropriate drain-cleaning and maintenance procedure rather than relying on room spraying.

Use controls suited to the site

Traps and targeted products may help reduce adult activity, but should complement source removal. Food premises need careful placement away from exposed food and food-contact surfaces. Any chemical use must follow the label, including restrictions, ventilation and re-entry instructions.

Build a repeatable fly-prevention routine

Assign responsibility for bins, drains, spill clean-up and external waste areas. Check screens and door closers. Keep a simple sighting log and record corrective actions. If numbers rise after rain, waste collection changes or a new delivery pattern, investigate that trigger.

When to arrange an assessment

Persistent fly activity across multiple rooms, near food preparation, around drains or in a facility with recurring complaints deserves a structured inspection. Share the affected areas, operating hours and recent changes so the source can be investigated efficiently.
