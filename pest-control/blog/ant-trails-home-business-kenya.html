Why Ants Keep Returning to Your Home or Business

Repeated ant trails usually indicate an accessible food or water source, a nearby nest, or an entry route that has not been addressed.

Understand the signs

Repeated ant trails usually indicate an accessible food or water source, a nearby nest, or an entry route that has not been addressed.

Inspect and identify the source

Observe the trail before cleaning it away. Ants may travel along skirting, window frames, cables, paving joints or branches touching a building. Follow the route to likely entry points without disturbing nests unnecessarily.

Prevention starts with the environment

Store sugar, cereals and pet food in sealed containers. Clean sticky spills promptly, repair leaks and trim vegetation away from walls. Wipe indoor trails with soapy water and keep waste containers closed.

Choosing an appropriate response

Baiting can be useful when the product matches the ant species and is placed where workers can reach it safely. Broad spraying may scatter some colonies or leave the nest untouched. Never place bait where children or pets can access it.

Monitor and follow up

Persistent trails in several rooms or around food premises warrant a site assessment and a prevention plan that includes neighbouring entry routes.
