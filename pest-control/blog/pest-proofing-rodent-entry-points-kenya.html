How to Pest-Proof a Kenyan Home or Business Against Rodents

Rodent control works best when trapping or other control measures are paired with repairs that stop new animals entering and remove food and shelter.

Find the routes

Inspect gaps around pipes, damaged vents, door thresholds, roof edges, ceiling voids, drains and loading doors. Look for droppings, rub marks, gnawing, nesting material and repeated tracks along walls.

Remove food access

Store food and animal feed in secure containers, clean spills, close bins and avoid leaving pet food out overnight. In warehouses, inspect incoming stock and remove damaged packaging promptly.

Repair openings properly

Use durable materials suited to the building and the size of the opening. Protect vents and drains with suitable barriers and maintain door sweeps. Expanding foam by itself may not withstand rodent gnawing.

Avoid unsafe bait placement

Rodenticide must never be left loose where children, pets, livestock or non-target wildlife can reach it. Use only products and placements permitted by the label and the site risk assessment. Consider trapping and exclusion where appropriate.

Coordinate with neighbours and facilities teams

In apartment blocks, shopping centres and warehouses, rodents can move through shared service routes. A single unit may not resolve a building-wide source. Coordinate waste, drainage and proofing repairs.

Monitor after repairs

Continue checking for fresh droppings, gnawing and trap activity. Close the loop on every identified opening and record where signs persist.
