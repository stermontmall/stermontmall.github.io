Integrated Pest Management for Schools, Hospitals and Businesses

Integrated Pest Management is a planned approach that combines inspection, prevention, monitoring and carefully selected controls. It is especially useful where people, food, equipment or sensitive operations must be protected.

Start with a baseline

Map the facility, identify pest evidence and note food, water, waste, moisture and access points. Consider the people using each area and the consequences of pest activity in that space.

Prioritise by risk

A kitchen, medicine store, classroom, patient area, waste room and external garden do not have identical risks. Agree which findings need immediate containment and which require maintenance or housekeeping action.

Prevent before treating

Proofing, waste management, cleaning, moisture correction, stock rotation and staff awareness reduce the conditions pests need. Assign owners and due dates so recommendations become completed work.

Use targeted controls

Where control is needed, select measures for the pest and location, protect occupants and sensitive materials, and follow label directions. Avoid routine blanket applications without a clear reason.

Monitor and review

Use traps, inspections, sighting reports and trend reviews where appropriate. Repeated activity in the same place should trigger investigation of the underlying source.

Document the programme

Keep inspection findings, pest trends, corrective actions, treatments, product details where applicable and follow-up results. Good records make the programme easier to manage and improve.
