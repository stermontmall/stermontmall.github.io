Container Fumigation in Kenya: Planning, Safety and Documentation

Container fumigation is a specialist cargo operation. Correct planning protects workers, cargo, carriers and the integrity of the supply chain.

Start with cargo information

Identify the cargo, packaging, quantity, container size, loading status and target pest. Some goods may be sensitive to the proposed treatment. Confirm buyer, carrier, port and destination requirements before a booking is made.

Confirm the actual method

Ask whether the proposal is gas fumigation, a surface treatment or another pest-control method. These are not interchangeable. The quotation should identify the scope and any documentation included.

Control access and exposure

Gas fumigation requires competent authorised personnel, a site-specific plan, exclusion zones, warning arrangements, monitoring and emergency procedures. Workers and members of the public must not be exposed to fumigant gas.

Do not shortcut release

A container must not be opened or entered solely because a time period has elapsed. Release depends on the specified procedure and appropriate monitoring confirming safe conditions. Follow the responsible specialist's clearance instructions.

Keep the records

Retain treatment details, cargo identifiers, dates, monitoring and clearance documentation, and any required certificate. Make sure the document matches the actual container and consignment.

Questions to ask the provider

Ask who is responsible for the treatment, what method is proposed, which product and label apply, how access is controlled, how clearance is verified and what documentation will be issued.
