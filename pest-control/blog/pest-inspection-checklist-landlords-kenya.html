A Pest Inspection Checklist for Kenyan Landlords and Property Managers

Routine inspections can identify pest risks before tenants report a serious infestation. A simple checklist helps teams spot patterns and assign corrective actions.

Understand the signs

Routine inspections can identify pest risks before tenants report a serious infestation. A simple checklist helps teams spot patterns and assign corrective actions.

Inspect and identify the source

Inspect kitchens, waste rooms, plumbing risers, ceiling void access points, basements, gardens, shared laundry areas and refuse collection zones. Look for droppings, gnaw marks, insect skins, dampness, standing water and gaps around service lines.

Prevention starts with the environment

Record the date, location, evidence, photographs taken from a safe distance, action owner and target completion date. Keep tenant reports confidential and avoid blaming occupants before the source is established.

Choosing an appropriate response

Prioritise leaking pipes, damaged door sweeps, unsealed penetrations, overflowing waste and clutter in shared spaces. Arrange a professional assessment when evidence persists or the pest cannot be safely identified.

Monitor and follow up

In multi-tenant properties, coordinated treatment and communication often work better than isolated room-by-room responses.
