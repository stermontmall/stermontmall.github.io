Professional healthcare facility pest management

Healthcare Facility Pest Management supports clinics, hospitals, laboratories, pharmacies and care homes dealing with cockroaches, rodents, flies, ants and mosquitoes. We begin by understanding the property, pest evidence and operating conditions, then agree a proportionate plan. The service may include inspection, monitoring, targeted treatment, exclusion advice, sanitation recommendations and follow-up checks where required.

What the service includes

Every assignment is scoped to the property and the pest evidence. We confirm target areas, inspection findings, preparation tasks, access arrangements and proposed treatment or prevention measures. The scope can include monitoring points, proofing recommendations, staff or occupant guidance and scheduled reviews.

How we plan the work

Tell us the town, property type, approximate size, pest history and urgency. An inspection or preliminary assessment helps determine the work required. Before treatment, we explain the plan and quotation, identify areas that must remain clear, and agree timing with the responsible person. After the visit, we provide aftercare and monitoring guidance.

Safety and responsible treatment

Methods must be selected for the identified pest and site, and used strictly according to approved product labels and applicable Kenyan requirements. Occupants, pets, food, water, sensitive equipment and non-target organisms must be protected. Evacuation, ventilation, cleaning and re-entry instructions are specific to the method and must be followed exactly.

Coverage and quotation

SterMont is based in Nairobi and can discuss service requests in other Kenyan towns subject to scheduling and confirmation. Share your town and property details for an initial discussion. Final scope and price depend on inspection findings, access, method and follow-up needs; we do not invent one fixed price for every property.

Frequently asked questions

Do you inspect before treatment?

Yes. The scope should be based on pest evidence and property conditions. We confirm whether a site inspection is required.

How much does the service cost?

Cost depends on property size, target pest, infestation level, access, treatment method and visits. Contact us for a quotation.

Is one treatment always enough?

No. Some infestations need monitoring, proofing, sanitation changes or repeat visits. The technician explains the expected plan.

Can people remain inside during treatment?

That depends on the method and product label. We provide site-specific instructions; never assume a treated area is immediately safe to occupy.
