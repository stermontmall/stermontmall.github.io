Cockroach Control Services in Kenya should begin with understanding the pest, the property and the conditions that allow activity to continue. SterMont provides enquiry-led service planning for residential, commercial and institutional customers. Availability outside Nairobi is confirmed when you share your location.

What this service addresses

This service is intended for situations involving german cockroaches, american cockroaches and other common species. The correct response depends on accurate identification, the extent of activity, the building layout and the people or operations using the space.

Where we look

An assessment may include kitchen cupboards, appliance motors, drains, service ducts, storage rooms and cracks. We look for visible evidence, entry routes, moisture, food sources and sheltered harbourage. Not every sign means an active infestation, so findings should be interpreted in context.

How a site-specific plan is developed

Inspection of harbourage and food/water sources; targeted gel baiting or labelled residual treatment where appropriate; sanitation and proofing recommendations; follow-up based on activity and product label.

Prevention and preparation

Store food in sealed containers, clean grease and crumbs, repair leaks, empty bins regularly and seal gaps around pipes. Avoid spraying over bait placements.

Safety and realistic expectations

Treatment choice, preparation, access restrictions and re-entry instructions vary by product and site. Follow the written instructions supplied for the actual job. Some infestations need monitoring, repeat treatment or building repairs; no single visit can responsibly be represented as a universal permanent solution.

What to include when requesting a quotation

Share your town or estate, property type, approximate size, pest observed, affected rooms, how long the issue has been present and any photos taken safely. Mention children, pets, food handling, aquariums or sensitive equipment so the assessment can account for them.
