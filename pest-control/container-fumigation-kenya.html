Container Fumigation Services in Kenya

Assessment-led container pest treatment planning for cargo, shipping and storage operators.

Request a site-specific quote

A container is an enclosed transport environment, but it is not automatically suitable for fumigation. Cargo type, packaging, container condition, target pest, destination requirements and worker safety all influence the correct method.

When container treatment is considered

Treatment may be requested for insects associated with timber, agricultural produce, packaging, stored commodities or other cargo. Some consignments have buyer, carrier, port or phytosanitary requirements that must be confirmed before work is planned.

Pre-treatment assessment

Identify the cargo and packaging, inspect the container for damage and leakage, review loading density, check whether the goods can tolerate the proposed method and confirm the target pest. Confirm the applicable import, export, carrier and destination documentation requirements with the responsible parties.

Controlled work and exclusion

Any gas fumigation must be performed by appropriately qualified and authorised personnel under a site-specific plan. The area must be controlled against unauthorised access, with required warnings, monitoring, emergency arrangements and protective measures.

Aeration and release

A container must not be opened, entered, moved or released merely because a stated number of hours has passed. Re-entry and release depend on the approved procedure, monitoring results and the relevant product and regulatory requirements. Required records should be retained.

What to confirm before booking

Provide container number, size, cargo description, packaging, loading status, origin and destination, target pest, requested standard or certificate and timing. Never load or seal cargo in a way that prevents the competent fumigation provider from carrying out the approved procedure.

Important distinction

Ordinary insect spraying, space fogging and gas fumigation are different operations. A quotation should name the actual method and scope rather than use the word fumigation as a generic label.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the treatment plan?

The pest identified, extent of activity, property use, access, occupants, food or stock present and the product directions all influence the scope.

Will one visit solve every infestation?

Not always. Hidden harbourage, pest life cycles, adjoining units and unresolved building defects can require monitoring, repeat work or repairs.

What should I send for a quotation?

Share your town, property type, approximate size, pest evidence, affected areas and any sensitive occupants, stock or equipment.
