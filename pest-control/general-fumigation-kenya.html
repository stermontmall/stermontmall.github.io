General Fumigation and Pest Treatment in Kenya should begin with understanding the pest, the property and the conditions that allow activity to continue. SterMont provides enquiry-led service planning for residential, commercial and institutional customers. Availability outside Nairobi is confirmed when you share your location.

What this service addresses

This service is intended for situations involving depends on inspection: crawling insects, flying insects, rodents or other identified pests. The correct response depends on accurate identification, the extent of activity, the building layout and the people or operations using the space.

Where we look

An assessment may include homes, offices, retail units, hospitality premises and selected storage facilities. We look for visible evidence, entry routes, moisture, food sources and sheltered harbourage. Not every sign means an active infestation, so findings should be interpreted in context.

How a site-specific plan is developed

Confirm pest identity and scope; assess occupants, food, sensitive equipment and ventilation; select a method suited to the site; explain preparation, exclusion and re-entry instructions; document recommendations.

Prevention and preparation

The term fumigation is often used broadly. True gas fumigation is a specialist, tightly controlled process and is not interchangeable with ordinary spraying or fogging.

Safety and realistic expectations

Treatment choice, preparation, access restrictions and re-entry instructions vary by product and site. Follow the written instructions supplied for the actual job. Some infestations need monitoring, repeat treatment or building repairs; no single visit can responsibly be represented as a universal permanent solution.

What to include when requesting a quotation

Share your town or estate, property type, approximate size, pest observed, affected rooms, how long the issue has been present and any photos taken safely. Mention children, pets, food handling, aquariums or sensitive equipment so the assessment can account for them.
