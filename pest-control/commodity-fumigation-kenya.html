Commodity Fumigation and Stored Grain Treatment in Kenya

Specialist assessment for stored commodities where a regulated treatment is required to manage internal or concealed pest infestation.

Request a site-specific quote

Commodity fumigation is not the same as spraying an empty room. The treatment interacts with the commodity, packaging, storage structure, target pest and intended market, so it must be planned as a controlled technical operation.

Commodities and target pests

Requests may involve bagged or bulk grains, pulses, oilseeds, animal feed, dried produce or other stored materials. The likely pests include internal and external feeders, beetles, weevils, moths and mites. The commodity and pest must be identified before a method is selected.

Scope and compatibility

The assessment should establish commodity type, quantity, moisture and temperature conditions, packaging, stack or bulk arrangement, infestation evidence, storage construction and whether the goods are destined for local use or export. Product directions and destination requirements must be checked.

Safety-critical planning

Where a fumigant is proposed, the work requires a competent specialist, an appropriate containment and exclusion plan, access control, monitoring, emergency arrangements and a documented clearance process. No person should enter or handle a treated enclosure until the responsible specialist confirms it is safe.

After treatment

Follow the specified aeration, testing, release and documentation process. Treatment does not replace store cleaning, stock rotation, moisture management or inspection of new deliveries.

Records and traceability

Maintain lot numbers, quantity, treatment date, target pest, product and method records, monitoring results, clearance documentation and any required certificate. These records are important for quality assurance and supply-chain communication.

Request a technical review

Share the commodity, volume, packaging, location, pest evidence and intended destination. We can clarify the service scope and any documentation or specialist requirements before confirming a quotation.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the treatment plan?

The pest identified, extent of activity, property use, access, occupants, food or stock present and the product directions all influence the scope.

Will one visit solve every infestation?

Not always. Hidden harbourage, pest life cycles, adjoining units and unresolved building defects can require monitoring, repeat work or repairs.

What should I send for a quotation?

Share your town, property type, approximate size, pest evidence, affected areas and any sensitive occupants, stock or equipment.
