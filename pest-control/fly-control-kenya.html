Fly Control Services in Kenya

Targeted fly management for kitchens, food premises, homes, institutions and commercial facilities.

Request a site-specific quote

Fly activity is often a symptom of an accessible breeding or feeding source. A durable plan investigates waste handling, drains, standing organic matter, doors, windows and incoming goods—not just the adult flies visible in a room.

Common fly problems

Houseflies around refuse and food; fruit flies around fermenting produce and beverage residues; drain or moth flies around wet organic build-up; and blowflies around animal waste or decaying material. Correct identification matters because each group uses different breeding sites.

Inspection priorities

Review refuse holding areas, bin washing points, floor drains, grease traps, food preparation zones, loading bays, compost, animal areas and exterior lighting. Check whether doors close properly and whether insect screens are damaged. Record where activity is highest and when it peaks.

Control approach

Remove breeding material and clean affected drains using an appropriate maintenance method. Improve bin lids, collection frequency and waste segregation. Where suitable, use labelled targeted products, traps or other controls selected for the fly species and operating environment. Fogging alone does not remove breeding sources.

Food premises and sensitive sites

Coordinate treatment around food handling, stock, utensils, customers and staff. Protect exposed food and food-contact surfaces as directed, and observe product label requirements for application, ventilation and re-entry. Maintain a written sighting and corrective-action log.

Prevention checklist

Keep refuse covered; clean spills immediately; remove wet organic waste; maintain drains; repair screens; fit door sweeps where appropriate; reduce standing water; and inspect incoming produce or waste streams.

What SterMont needs to quote

Share the property type, town, affected areas, time of day when flies are most active, nearby waste or drainage conditions and whether food is prepared on site. This helps scope the inspection and any required follow-up.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the treatment plan?

The pest identified, extent of activity, property use, access, occupants, food or stock present and the product directions all influence the scope.

Will one visit solve every infestation?

Not always. Hidden harbourage, pest life cycles, adjoining units and unresolved building defects can require monitoring, repeat work or repairs.

What should I send for a quotation?

Share your town, property type, approximate size, pest evidence, affected areas and any sensitive occupants, stock or equipment.
