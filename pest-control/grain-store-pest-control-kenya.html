Grain Store Pest Control and Fumigation in Kenya

Practical protection for maize, beans, cereals, seed and other stored commodities in farm stores and commercial godowns.

Request a site-specific quote

A grain store needs a programme that protects the building, the bags and the grain itself. Treating only visible insects while leaving residues, moisture and infested incoming stock untouched often leads to repeat problems.

Before grain enters the store

Remove old grain and dust, clean cracks and ledges, repair roof leaks, inspect pallets and check that the store can be kept dry and ventilated. Separate new deliveries from older stock until their condition is checked.

During storage

Keep bags off the floor and away from damp walls, leave inspection access between stacks, identify each lot and use a first-in-first-out system. Check for torn bags, webbing, live insects, frass, rodents, dampness and unusual odours at planned intervals.

Integrated pest prevention

Combine store hygiene, building proofing, moisture management, rodent exclusion, stock rotation and inspection. Avoid placing grain directly against walls or allowing spilled kernels to accumulate beneath stacks.

When treatment may be needed

The treatment depends on whether pests are on the building, packaging or within the commodity. Commodity treatment must use a product and method specifically authorised for that commodity and purpose. Gas fumigation requires specialist competence, controlled access, gas monitoring and verified clearance before re-entry.

Records for farms and businesses

Document supplier, harvest or purchase date, variety or commodity, quantity, stack location, inspections, moisture concerns, pest findings and action taken. These records help trace recurring infestation and manage stock quality.

Prepare for a quotation

Tell us whether the store contains bagged or bulk grain, the commodity and quantity, the store location and dimensions, the pest signs and whether stock is currently present. We will clarify whether the request is for store treatment, pest monitoring or specialist commodity fumigation.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the treatment plan?

The pest identified, extent of activity, property use, access, occupants, food or stock present and the product directions all influence the scope.

Will one visit solve every infestation?

Not always. Hidden harbourage, pest life cycles, adjoining units and unresolved building defects can require monitoring, repeat work or repairs.

What should I send for a quotation?

Share your town, property type, approximate size, pest evidence, affected areas and any sensitive occupants, stock or equipment.
