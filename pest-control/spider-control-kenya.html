Spider Control and Web Management in Kenya

Inspection and prevention for spider activity in homes, offices, stores and commercial buildings.

Request a site-specific quote

Spiders often appear where insects are plentiful and undisturbed corners provide shelter. Reducing webs alone may improve appearance temporarily, but lasting management also looks at insect entry, lighting, clutter and building gaps.

Where spiders gather

Check ceiling corners, roof spaces, stores, window frames, external lights, garden sheds, unused rooms and behind stored materials. Web patterns and egg sacs can help identify activity zones, but do not handle unfamiliar spiders.

Reduce the food source

Manage flies and other insects attracted by waste, moisture and external lighting. Keep screens intact, close doors, clean food spills and maintain waste areas. Fewer insects can mean fewer spiders around a building.

Housekeeping and exclusion

Remove webs using suitable cleaning equipment, reduce clutter and seal suitable gaps around windows, doors and service penetrations. Keep outdoor vegetation from touching walls where practical.

Treatment considerations

Targeted treatment may be considered after identifying the species and location. Avoid indiscriminate application to ceilings, bedding, food-contact surfaces or areas where people and pets may contact residues. Follow the actual product label.

When to seek help

Arrange an assessment if spider activity is widespread, recurring or involves a suspected medically significant species. Do not attempt to catch or handle an unidentified spider for identification.

Frequently asked questions

What affects the treatment plan?

The pest identified, extent of activity, property use, access, occupants, food or stock present and the product directions all influence the scope.

Will one visit solve every infestation?

Not always. Hidden harbourage, pest life cycles, adjoining units and unresolved building defects can require monitoring, repeat work or repairs.

What should I send for a quotation?

Share your town, property type, approximate size, pest evidence, affected areas and any sensitive occupants, stock or equipment.
